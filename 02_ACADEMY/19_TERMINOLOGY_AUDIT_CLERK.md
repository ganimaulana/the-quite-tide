## 2. THE TWO-NAMESPACE PRINCIPLE [PROPOSED]

The audit applies the ruling through two namespaces that must not be confused:

- **Namespace 1 — institutional naming** (the ruling's target): curriculum, division, classification, assignment, job titles in-world. Here "Clerk" is **forbidden** as a formal name. The curriculum is INTELLIGENCE OPERATIONS. Full stop.
- **Namespace 2 — character-DNA / descriptive language** (verdict §4's domain): "the clerk's method" (observe→hypothesize→test→pay), "the clerk's epistemology," "the clerk" as insult. The verdict *mandates* this language as character DNA ("the clerk material becomes character DNA only"). It is grandfathered and explicitly permitted by the ruling's own exceptions (insult/social label + descriptive term).

A usage is a violation only if it sits in Namespace 1. Everything in Namespace 2 is [KEEP].
