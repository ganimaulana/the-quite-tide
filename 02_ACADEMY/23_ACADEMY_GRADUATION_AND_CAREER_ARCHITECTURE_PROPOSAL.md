**What the Academy must plausibly prepare cadets for [CANON / PROPOSED]:** military service **[CANON — the Academy's defined purpose]**; technical/support roles **[PROPOSED]**; research **[PROPOSED]**; government/institutional roles **[PROPOSED]**; and other professional pathways **[PROPOSED]**. ## 2. Division

**Definition:** a Division is the cadet's organizational home **[CANON — L-16]** and professional formation environment **[PROPOSED]**. It is where the cadet bunks, drills, eats, and is socially filed **[CANON — L6-9]**, where teams are housed, and from which the mission board draws deployable units **[CANON — LOCK 6]**. Divisions are named by *function*, not power type **[PROPOSED]**.

**What a Division is not [PROPOSED]:** not a reward, power ranking, permanent caste, future-rank declaration, exclusive classroom, or Classification. DIVISION ≠ CURRICULUM ≠ CLASSIFICATION **[CANON — L-16]**; Irregular ≠ Division **[CANON — LOCK 5]**.

**The principle as institutional fact:** the Academy's files must be able to say of any Division: *"you trained here; that does not decide where you serve."* Division supplies professional exposure and a posting history, not a career contract. Division prestige is a Division-level fact **[PROPOSED]**; the cadet's posting prestige is determined separately by stamp-routing and recommendations (§7–§8). This extends the locks' status logic — rank ≠ leadership; classification as filing status **[CANON — LOCK 6; L5-3]** — from *status* to *career*.

**Formal inventory status:** the only formally named curriculum in authoritative canon is **INTELLIGENCE OPERATIONS** — never "Clerk" **[CANON — L-17]**. **The IO ladder is a Curriculum progression, not an Academy progression [CANON — LOCK 3, L3-0]:** Records & Archives → Information Analysis → Operational Intelligence → Anomaly Intelligence → Field Intelligence → Special Investigation → unique role. ## 4. Practicum

**Definition [PROPOSED — substance attested, name invented]:** practicum is the cadet's Division-situated work — postings, details, maintenance rotations, records shifts — graded through the existing review machinery (Division training emphases and mission functions attested; assignment-board postings **[PROPOSED]**; **after-action review is the graded record** **[CANON]**). "Division practicum" as a distinct named layer remains **[PROPOSED]** — used here as a design label, not canon.

**What practicum does [PROPOSED]:** it exposes breadth — systems, procedures, failure points, equipment types, institutional patterns — priced by the world's budget (hours, duty rosters). **What it does not do:** grant proficiency. KNOWLEDGE ≠ SKILL **[PROPOSED]** — a cadet who handled weapons in maintenance understands construction and failure points; that is not marksmanship, tactical mastery, or a combat qualification.

**Practicum's role:** the *practical-record generator*. The practicum record — postings held, systems touched, AARs filed — feeds graduation placement (§7). It is not a ranking and not a second curriculum: it is a history.

## 5. Academic evaluation

**Definition [PROPOSED]:** the academic record is the graded account of classroom and theoretical performance — examinations, coursework, instructor assessments, board notes. Illustrative subjects (design direction, **not locked**): Military History, Military Ethics, Basic Tactics, Academy Regulations, Physical Conditioning, Basic Weapon Safety, Anomaly Awareness, Field Safety, Communication, Emergency Response **[PROPOSED — career proposal §2A]**. Whether common cross-Division classes exist is **[UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED]** — attested cohort mixing runs through exercises, missions, rivalries, and Joint Teams **[CANON / PROPOSED]**; this document assumes evaluation records exist under either model.

**Form of evaluation — the anti-game guardrail [PROPOSED]:** evaluation is institutional, not ludic — examination bands, instructor assessments, board minutes; never XP, never levels, never numeric ranks-as-score. **Asymmetric performance is permitted and expected** **[PROPOSED — career proposal §8]:** a cadet may be weak in some bands and unusually strong in others (Arthur's locked profile: low Esper output, ordinary-to-good physicality, strong analytical and anomaly-reasoning bands — the EAR already encodes this **[CANON — L4-5]**). An occasional top-band placement in a single class should read as surprising against general standing, never as a trend **[PROPOSED]**.

**Hard separations (no-collapse rules) [CANON unless noted]:**

- ACADEMIC RECORD ≠ ESPER RANK. The EAR "does not measure, reassess, or revise Esper Rank" **[CANON — L4-4]**; excellent coursework does not raise a Rank, and a low Rank does not invalidate coursework.
- ACADEMIC RECORD ≠ LADDER RUNG. The IO ladder moves on institutional triggers (L3-T1–L3-T6), not examination scores **[CANON — L3-0]**.
- ACADEMIC RECORD ≠ MISSION RECORD. Classroom and field competence are separate histories (§6); either may inform the other, but neither derives from the other.
- The EAR is Division-agnostic **[CANON — L4-5]** and changes nothing — no classification, division, curriculum, or assignment **[CANON — L4-4]**. Academic evaluation, however exceptional, grants no standing by itself.

## 6. Mission evaluation

**Definition [PROPOSED]:** the mission record is the graded account of performance under operational or near-operational conditions — practicum missions, mixed exercises, Joint Team deployments. **After-action review is the graded record** **[CANON]**; exercise results and Joint Mission Command reviews feed the same file.

**Three distinct evaluation contexts [CANON / PROPOSED]:**

1. **Practicum missions** — Division-situated, supervisor-graded, reviewed **[PROPOSED]**.
2. **Mixed practical exercises** — cross-Division training assemblies: instructor-supervised, graded, not operationally consequential, dissolved afterward **[PROPOSED — reconciled with LOCK 7]**.
3. **Joint Team missions** — temporary, mission-specific, multi-Division, operational, dissolved after the mission **[CANON — LOCK 7, L-20]**.

**ERROR/Classification grants nothing** **[CANON — LOCK 7 principles 7–9]**: no mission authority, no command authority, no automatic eligibility. A stamped cadet's record grows only through: mission requirement → intelligence requirement → personnel selection → demonstrated usefulness → increased responsibility **[CANON]**.

**Form of evaluation [PROPOSED]:** mission records are written in institutional language — AAR findings, commander endorsements, documented patterns — not scores. The locked ladder already establishes the vocabulary: L3-T2's trigger is literally "AAR-documented pattern of load-bearing pre-mission assessments" **[CANON — L3-2]**. Placement (§7–§8) draws on the same records but **uses them as a file is used — read, weighed, vouched for — not as points are added**. No mission leaderboard is canon; the division exercise scoreboard is **[PROPOSED]** and belongs to exercises only.

## 7. Graduation

**Definition [PROPOSED]:** graduation certifies that a cadet has completed the course — Division service, Curriculum requirements, practicum history, Year-4 cadet-officer phase **[PROPOSED — `14_ACADEMY_RESTRUCTURE.md` §4.1]** — and is eligible for service placement. It is not a life binary:

> GRADUATE ≠ AUTOMATIC PRESTIGIOUS CAREER · NON-GRADUATE ≠ LIFE OVER **[PROPOSED — career proposal §20.4]**

Graduation opens or improves access to professional routes without guaranteeing them; non-graduation does not end a working life (§9).

**What graduation certifies [PROPOSED]:** the filed record — Division served, Curriculum completed, practicum postings, exercise and mission record, EAR result where applicable — activating the **six-year National Esper Service Obligation** **[PROPOSED]**. It does **not** revise Esper Rank, re-stamp Classification, or move the IO ladder: separate machines, staying separate.

### 7.1 Two placement currencies — reconciling a conflict

**[CONFLICTING — neither locked:]** `17_INTER_DIVISION_RIVALRY.md` §2 documents placement as recommendation-driven — *"recommendations … are the real currency"* **[PROPOSED]** — while the career proposal §12/§14 proposes a graduation **placement examination** with primary/secondary mechanism **[PROPOSED — NOT ESTABLISHED elsewhere]**.

**Reconciliation [PROPOSED]:** the two are complementary — **the examination is the auditable formal gate** (a filed result answering "why this cadet, why this field"); **the recommendation is the political currency** (who vouches determines which doors the result opens). *A passing score without a vouching officer is a filed paper; a vouching officer without a passing score is a favor the institution will not cash.* Both are game-proof: a band is not XP, a recommendation is a name on a record, not a score. Adopting either — or this reconciliation — is **[AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED]** (reconciliation decision 5).

### 7.2 What placement depends on

Graduation placement is a function of a combination, never a single number **[PROPOSED]**:

- academic record (§5)
- practical/practicum record (§4)
- aptitude (as assessed, not as Rank)
- demonstrated competence (mission record, §6)
- mission evaluations (AARs, endorsements)
- institutional requirements (which fields need bodies)
- available positions (quotas exist in the world; specific numbers are **[UNKNOWN]**)
- examinations / selection procedures (§7.1)

No single factor is dispositive. The weighting is itself an institutional judgment — where patronage, politics, and the recommendation economy live. Intentional: the Academy should feel like a real career gateway, where merit is real but mediated — not a scoreboard.

### 7.3 Early-chapter establishment (pacing)

Early chapters establish only three facts **[PROPOSED]**: (1) the Academy is a military career gateway — cadets graduate into service; (2) Division is a practicum home, not a destiny (shown through ordinary scenes, not speeches); (3) graduation carries a service bond and a posting — the future is constrained but not written.

Acceptable foreshadowing (design examples, not mandatory prose): *"If I fail placement, I'll apply for contractor work."* / *"Your Academy record won't be the only thing that determines where you work."* The full machinery (§7.1–§7.2) is never exposited; it surfaces through later placement beats.

### 7.4 IO-ladder interface — compatibility check

**Requirement:** graduation mechanics must not duplicate or contradict the IO ladder's institutional triggers (L3-T1–L3-T6) **[CANON — L3-2–L3-7]**.

**Check performed:** L3-T1 (filed Contract-holder bulletin), L3-T2 (AAR-documented assessment pattern), and L3-T3 (documentary proof of a misclassified closed incident) each move Arthur between rungs of the IO Curriculum; graduation placement *reads* their documentary output as evidence in the practical/mission record but never re-triggers, re-scores, or re-names the movement. L3-T4–T6 (field-deployment necessity, doctrinal failure, framework-breaking crisis) are career-scale institutional events occurring *in service*, not at the Academy exit — a different altitude from graduation entirely. The no-choice bar (L3-8) governs both: formal recognition and formal posting follow demonstrated dependence, never anticipate it. L3-9's Arc I ladder patience is matched here: graduation is not scheduled in Arc I.

**Interface rule [PROPOSED]:** the ladder is *curriculum-internal*; graduation is *Academy-exit*. A rung movement and a placement may coincide in story time, but one never causes the other by rule — where they touch, graduation *reads the ladder's documentary output as evidence*, never re-triggering, re-scoring, or renaming a rung.

## 8. Placement

**The placement chain [PROPOSED — `21_ACADEMY_CAREER_AND_DIVISION_ARCHITECTURE_PROPOSAL.md` §12]:**

ACADEMY DIVISION → PRACTICUM EXPERIENCE → ACADEMIC RECORD → PRACTICAL RECORD → GRADUATION → MILITARY PLACEMENT EXAMINATION → TARGET MILITARY FIELD → RESULT + RECORDS → FINAL MILITARY ASSIGNMENT

**Reading the chain:** Division is the first link and the *weakest causal* link — it contributes practicum experience and a posting history, not a destination. Passing the placement examination for a field outside the cadet's Division is **possible and not automatic**; performance still matters; requirements and positions constrain every attempt **[PROPOSED — §13]**. **AGENCY + CONSEQUENCE, not FREE CHOICE = GUARANTEED CAREER** **[PROPOSED]**.

**Destination fields [PROPOSED — `14_ACADEMY_RESTRUCTURE.md` §4.4; `17_INTER_DIVISION_RIVALRY.md` §2]:** SMD Operations arm, prefectural threshold agencies, Sweeper-contract liaison, Compact secondments; SMD Census Administration and Liaison; Meridian Institute state contracts (RES-001); the Academy's examiner/faculty track; Erosion-care system, White Reed-affiliated clinics, field-medical units; SMD operations support, Sweeper-contract logistics, containment-levy administration; hunter guilds, research institutes, command tracks; quiet desks, archive services, examiner-assistant tracks, cold-case review, Seep-site monitoring. **No new organizations are created by this listing** — it inventories destinations already present in design material.

**Stamped-cadet constraint [PROPOSED — `17_INTER_DIVISION_RIVALRY.md` §2]:** Irregular-classified cadets' recommendations are *initially* limited to records, archives, and support tracks — "the bureaucratically logical placement": the graduation-scale expression of L5-2's Grade-3 routing **[CANON]** and the standing constraint behind Arthur's Records & Archives posting **[CANON — L-17]**. **Initial, not eternal:** the rivalry file's own "disruptive" note anticipates the first Irregular recommended for a *field* track on aptitude grounds as a political event — the constraint can be *surpassed*, and the surpassing is story. This proposal schedules that surpassing for no one.

**Optional primary/secondary mechanism [PROPOSED — NOT ESTABLISHED]:** a cadet submits primary and secondary target fields and sits the relevant assessments; failure on the primary leaves the secondary viable **[PROPOSED — career proposal §14]**. Adoption is **[AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED]**; compatible with both §7.1 currencies.

## 9. Career mobility

**Core principle [PROPOSED — career proposal §20]:**

> ACADEMY DIVISION ≠ PERMANENT CAREER
> ACADEMY GRADUATION ≠ GUARANTEED MILITARY PLACEMENT
> ACADEMY FAILURE ≠ END OF CAREER

The Academy is one major institutional gateway among several possible paths — not the entire world, and not a life binary.

**In-Academy mobility [RECOMMENDATION-level — `06_DIVISION_SYSTEM.md` §13 item 3]:** division transfers exist on paper, requiring both division chiefs' sign-off; socially read as promotion (into Combat/Tactical) or disgrace (away from high prestige). EAR-recommended reassignment: same standing **[RECOMMENDATION — NEEDS DECISION]**. This proposal adds no new transfer machinery and assumes the paper mechanism stays rare, political, and visible — a transfer is always *noticed*, which makes it a story beat rather than an administrative convenience. Formalization beyond the recommendation is **[AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED]**.

**Post-graduation mobility [PROPOSED]:** the wider world contains multiple legitimate career paths — military and specialist military/government service; civil service; research or technical organizations; private or corporate service; private security; licensed mercenary or independent contractor work; other professional organizations; civilian careers **[PROPOSED — career proposal §20.2; illustrative, not locked]**. "Mercenary" is not automatically criminal; the world may distinguish licensed/legal work, independent contracting, private security, and illegal armed activity — the legal framework is **not** defined here **[PROPOSED — §20.5]**. Retraining applications, reassignment requests, examiner-loan desks, and the guild labor market give a serving officer lateral options; the service bond constrains *when*, not *whether*, a career bends.

**Pacing rule [PROPOSED]:** this ecosystem must not become an exposition dump. Early chapters show only the three facts from §7.3, revealed gradually through brief natural references — a cadet's sibling in contract logistics, a teacher's offhand line, a posting board glimpsed in passing. The Academy arc never lectures on the labor market; the labor market arrives when a cadet first chooses within it.

## 11. Risks

1. **Game-ification drift [PROPOSED risk].** Future material may quietly convert institutional records into a scoring system — exercise points as "levels," bands as "stats," recommendations as "reputation points." Guardrail: this document's vocabulary is *filed records, bands, AARs, recommendations* — none of which aggregate. Any proposal introducing aggregating scores must be flagged against this section.
2. **Exposition-dump risk [PROPOSED risk].** A career architecture invites the author to explain it. The §7.3/§9 pacing rules exist to prevent this: the machinery is *used* (placement beats, transfer rumors, posting boards), never lectured. A paragraph explaining the placement chain in a chapter draft is a candidate for deletion.
3. **Ladder-duplication risk [PROPOSED risk].** Failure mode: an examination that "counts" a ladder trigger as a score, or a beat treating graduation as a rung movement. Guardrail: rung movements are curriculum-internal with trigger-shaped causes; placements are Academy-exit with record-shaped causes.
4. **Recommendation-cynicism risk [PROPOSED risk].** A recommendation economy can curdle into pure patronage fiction. Design intent is *merit mediated by politics* — the examination gate (§7.1) keeps the bureaucracy honest enough that competence matters. If recommendations override examinations without consequence, the Academy becomes a court, not a gateway.
5. **Stamped-cadet caste risk [PROPOSED risk].** The initial recommendation constraint (§8) must read as a *standing institutional posture* — one the rivalry file anticipates being surpassed as a political event — not a permanent caste. If no stamped cadet tests the boundary, the classification is decorative; if the boundary collapses without cost, the constraint was never real.
6. **Master-of-Weapon inevitability risk [PROPOSED risk].** Failure mode: architecture prose treating broad weapon competence as Arthur's *scheduled* outcome — converting exposure into destiny and violating the core principle. Exposure is an input; the outcome belongs to the story.
7. **Placement-subplot risk [PROPOSED risk].** No chapters about examination preparation, no placement-board procedural arcs, unless a beat directly serves the story. Placement appears as *beats* — a result slip, a vouching conversation, a posting board — not as arcs.
