## 8. CHARACTER GENERATION RULES

1. The pre-migration Japanese naming set is retired from active canon; migration is governed by the Western/British world baseline.
2. Arthur Reed and the Western Academy roster are the active naming baseline.
3. Do not create duplicate surnames without a deliberate family relationship.
4. International characters should not be reduced to nationality stereotypes.
5. Character design follows: background → experience → worldview → behavior.
6. Not every named character is a rival.
7. Not every named character needs an unusual Ability.
8. Do not give every new character a tragic backstory.
9. Do not make international students automatically exceptional.
10. Prefer approachable junior staff, cadets, technicians, clerks, and ordinary instructors over constant use of elite officials for exposition.
11. Character functions must remain compatible with the Academy's current identity as a state-recognized specialist academy rather than a primarily military officer school.
12. No character in this register changes the Contract System, Motion Sense, six-slot Contract limit, or artifact-marker canon.

## 10. FOUR-YEAR CHARACTER GENERATION TARGET — AUTHOR LOCK 2026-09-26

Named recurring character generation is planned across four Academy cohorts:

| Cohort | Minimum | Recommended working range | Core distinction |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **Year 1** | 5 | 7–8 | Foundation and social formation |
| **Year 2** | 5 | 6–7 | Specialization and reliability |
| **Year 3** | 5 | 5–6 | Field readiness and experience |
| **Year 4** | 5 | 5 | Professional transition and external placement |
| **Total** | **20** | **23–26** | Four-stage Academy ecosystem |

Year 4 characters should normally have lower campus visibility because many are already undertaking approved external placements, internships, supervised missions, research assignments, exchanges, or similar professional-facing work. They remain cadets until graduation.

This target is a **named-character planning minimum**, not the size of an in-world cohort. Many additional cadets may remain unnamed.

The authoritative progression rules are maintained in `33_ACADEMY_YEAR_PROGRESSION_AND_COHORT_STRUCTURE.md`.
