## §3. L7-2 — FORMATION TRIGGER [LOCKED — canon-preserved]

The formal trigger is the **mission requirement profile** (MMS §2, five axes: threat tier filed / environment / anomaly class / civilian exposure risk / information sensitivity). The assembly chain:

1. The **mission board** posts the profile (filed, auditable).
2. The profile selects the **division slot template** (MMS §3).
3. The board's matching algorithm fills slots (**filed tier + EAR aptitude profile** — the competence-match; sealed-profile rule: the board matches against data the cadet cannot see).
4. **Joint Mission Command** assembles the formation from Divisions/Teams/Classifications.
5. The **supervising instructor** holds a pre-deployment veto window (filed; patterns reviewable).
6. **Political placement** (7th observer slot or justified swap) is a visible, filed interference — never business as usual.

Authority is **distributed by design**: no single selector exists in canon, and none is invented. [KEEP — the split (JMC assembles Joint Teams; scheduling office owns stable teams; the board posts missions) is consistent across TEAM_DOCTRINE T-7, MMS, and ARTHUR_ACADEMY_ENTRY.]

3. **Mandatory friction:** formal and functional command diverge by design; every mixed mission stages ≥1 formal-vs-functional conflict (anti-formula rule 12).
4. **Instructor override:** the supervising instructor may reassign command mid-mission — filed, graded, political.
5. **Disputed command:** both versions filed; the after-action review reconciles them.

**Relations:** a Home Team leader holds no authority inside a Joint Team — the JMC-designated formal commander does. Instructors hold **overwatch, not intervention** (the instructor grades, not saves; a saved team fails the exercise). Instructor authority vs cadet operational command is a filed boundary, not a gray zone.

## §12. L7-11 — FAILURE / CONSEQUENCE [LOCKED]

Failure is priced, not cosmetic, and not permanently punishing:

- Every mission ends in a **graded, filed after-action review** (five sections: objective outcome 40%, method log 30%, command assessment 20%, aptitude notes, remainder/disposition). Permanent, readable by cleared staff.
- **Responsibility attribution:** command reconciliation (formal vs functional); political-placement justifications re-audited; veto patterns reviewable.
- **Consequence channels:** individual ledger (demerits, aptitude-note deltas, stand-down eligibility), division ledger (quarterly standings → resource/prestige stakes), instructor evaluation, and the remainder/disposition filing that chains into future missions.
- Failure alters future expectations: a note that says "profile unresolved, not low" is a recognition-as-access event; aptitude deltas feed the matching machinery. Exceptions to quotas and assignments are fileable — the system bends on the record, never silently.
