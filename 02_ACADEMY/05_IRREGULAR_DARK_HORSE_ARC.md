## §2. THE 7-PHASE DARK-HORSE PROGRESSION [PROPOSED]

Directive Part X. For each phase: (a) the division's **[SUPERSEDED — read "the Irregular cohort's" per LOCK 5: Irregular = classification, not a division; C-14-15 repair 2026-09-23]** ACTUAL capability state, (b) what the academy BELIEVES, (c) the visible event(s) forcing the transition, (d) the driving slots, (e) Arthur's leadership phase (directive Part VII).

**Phase-transition rule [PROPOSED]:** a phase advances only on a WITNESSED, RECORDED event satisfying the §5 gating rule of 18_SOCIAL_HIERARCHY_PROGRESSION.md (≥2 divisions witness; public record; ≥2 Irregular-classified cadets credited). No phase is skipped. The institution's contempt stage (18_SOCIAL_HIERARCHY_PROGRESSION.md §4) and perception rung (§5) advance in lockstep — the three ladders are one movement seen from three angles.

### Phase 1 — Lowest status
- **Actual capability:** unassessed and mutually distrustful. Ten strangers with incompatible Abilities, no shared doctrine, no coordinator. Their only common property is the system's rejection.
- **Academy believes:** "Garbage Division" (rung 1; contempt stage 1). Irregular is a budget line.
- **Transition event → Phase 2:** the Special Classification Examination (directive Part IV) returns *aptitude* data the academy has never had — not higher ranks (no rank changes, ever), but the first accurate profiles. The data is briefed; it does not change anyone's mind, but it creates the *paper* Arthur will later use.
- **Driving members:** none yet — this phase demonstrates weaknesses only (each rostered cadet fails on-page at least once, per §0 ensemble rules).
- **Arthur's leadership:** Phase 1–2 (member → observations). He is another Irregular-classified cadet; he watches, files, and says nothing. His Records & Archives posting gives him legitimate access to everyone's files — the observation phase is *institutional*, not sneaky.

### Phase 2 — Occasional surprises
- **Actual capability:** still uncoordinated — but Arthur has begun *quietly* matching members to micro-tasks inside exercises (unofficial, unattributed). The improvement is in match quality, not in members (§3).
- **Academy believes:** luck. "The exercise was easy" / "the lane was miscalibrated." (Contempt stage 1→2: forced proximity has begun via mixed exercises.)
- **Transition event → Phase 3:** a mixed-division exercise where an Irregular member's hyper-specialized Ability (Clarke's fiber-memory) solves a lane no one else could attempt — on the public score sheet, witnessed. The academy *must* explain it; it chooses "coincidence."
- **Driving members:** Clarke (the solve), Foster (the anchor — the dry body holds the operation's floor so the solve isn't a fluke-with-casualties).
- **Arthur's leadership:** Phase 2–3 (observations → unofficial coordinator). His coordination is visible to Irregular members, invisible to the institution. The members begin to notice that "the F-rank's suggestions keep working."

### Phase 3 — Dismissed as coincidence
- **Actual capability:** Arthur is now *openly* coordinating inside Irregular (the members know; the institution doesn't care). First synergy pairings tested: Owen's flare predicted by Arthur's analysis and covered by Foster's steadiness; Owen's failure-probes mapped.

Attribution can no longer be laundered through the mixed team.

- **Arthur's leadership:** Phase 3 (unofficial coordinator). His coordination is now *visible* to mixed-team partners ("the F-rank kept reassigning people and it kept working") but still unattributed by the institution.

### Phase 4 — Repeated success
- **Actual capability:** the division now has *doctrine* — Arthur's role-assignment method, written down (the first Irregular field manual: six pages, unofficial, photocopied — Blake's handwriting, Arthur's method, per §3's planner's paradox). Synergy pairings are rehearsed. - **Arthur's leadership:** Phase 4 (voluntary following). Irregular members now follow his assignments *openly and by choice*. The photocopied manual is the artifact of this phase.

### Phase 5 — Studied
- **Actual capability:** unchanged in ceilings — but the division now has *reputation*, which is a capability multiplier (better mixed-team partners request them; the quartermaster stops shorting their draw out of self-interest). Actual team output rises because the *environment* stops taxing them.

- **Arthur's leadership:** Phase 5 (instructor notices). The institution identifies the coordinator. This is the most dangerous leadership phase — being noticed by the institution is what the cultivated cover (face-slap file §1) was *for*, and its price comes due here (Form 10, institutional surveillance, begins).

### Phase 7 — Genuine dark horse
- **Actual capability:** the division at its academy-movement peak — same ceilings as Phase 1, unrecognizable output. The improvement is 100% deployment + doctrine + resourcing + synergy. (If any reader can point to a member and say "they got stronger," the arc has failed — see §3 guardrails.)

## §3. THE MECHANISM OF IMPROVEMENT — why they get better without getting stronger [PROPOSED]

Directive Part X's thesis: *"They were never individually perfect. They simply never had someone who knew how to use them."* This section makes that thesis mechanical. Five vectors; none of them raises a ceiling.

### Vector 1 — Correct deployment for the first time
The academy's assignment system sorts cadets by filed Esper rank and tier. Irregular-classified cadets were therefore systematically assigned to mission/exercise shapes that mismatch their Abilities — Clarke sent to general lanes, Bennett teamed conventionally, Owen never deployed during flare windows because no one tracked them, Blake scored on output he cannot produce under fire instead of the planning he can. Arthur's Records & Archives posting gives him legitimate access to incident files and exercise briefs; his method matches *member-shape to mission-shape* before deployment. **The members don't change; the match quality does.** This is the arc's primary engine and must be *shown* as paperwork: the reader sees Arthur reading a brief, reassigning two names, and the exercise going differently — fair-play, every time.

### Vector 2 — Role-assignment (directive Part VII's method)
"Arthur does not have to be the strongest person in the room. He has to understand what everyone in the room can do." The method, staged: observe (exam data + exercise observation) → profile strengths/weaknesses per member → assign roles per mission shape → adapt the plan mid-execution when a member's weakness triggers. The method is *writable* (the field manual, Phase 4) — which means it is *stealable*, which is a later plot engine (the institution studies it in Phase 5).

### Vector 3 — Synergy protocols (complementary weaknesses, designed)
Pairings where A's weakness is covered by B's strength, rehearsed until reliable. Four canonical pairings [PROPOSED] (none may be removed without redesigning the phase that stages them):
- **Predictor + Sensor (Arthur + Owen):** Arthur's analysis of flare conditions (records patterns, Tide tables, incident history) predicts Owen's flicker windows; Owen delivers the highest-fidelity reading in the cadet body during the window; Foster covers Owen's medical cost on the back end. The weakness (uncontrollable timing + cost) is converted into a *scheduled* capability. This is the arc's anchor pairing — the first one Arthur builds (Phase 2) and the first one he teaches (Phase 4's manual).
- **Dry + Disruptor (Foster + Bennett):** Bennett's Choir suggestion is aimed phenomena-side; Foster is the tested-immune partner — the immunity test is itself a Phase 3 beat (his dry channel holds; the rebound finds no Tide to travel through). The weakness (can't be teamed) becomes a weapon with exactly one safe teammate. Immunity is *tested on-page*, never assumed — if the test had failed, the pairing would be cut, not hand-waved.
- **Probe + Reader (Owen + Rowan):** Owen's misfire maps the Rule's boundary by failing against it; Rowan's slow object-reading documents what failed, at whatever pace the phenomenon allows. Failure is *instrumentalized* — the arc's purest expression of the thesis. The academy's instruments read this as "two low-ranks staring at a wall"; the after-action reads it as a boundary map.
- **Brace + Burst (Mercer + Hart):** Mercer's sense-lending steadies Hart's targeting; Hart's need for a brace answers Mercer's partner-dependence. The pairing is filed with an abort condition — the standing rule for any Hart deployment (the roster file flags his destructive potential): if the brace slips, the burst does not fire. The pairing that proves C-rank output is a *deployment* problem, not a power problem.

**The planner's paradox (the fifth protocol, structural):** Blake writes the plans he cannot execute (his freeze is the fixed weakness). Foster executes the physical line Blake's Ability can't walk. The Phase 4 manual is in Blake's handwriting; the method is Arthur's. This is the arc's proof that the improvement is in the *division*, not in Arthur: the manual survives his absence.

**The forbidden pairing (designed temptation):** Bennett + Ward. Ward's self-harming healing could soak Bennett's rebound cost — the manual *forbids* this explicitly (ethics boundary, written by Blake at Arthur's insistence). The temptation must appear on-page at least once (Phase 5 or 6) and be *refused at cost* — the refusal is itself a priced demonstration that the arc's currency is doctrine, not power.

### Vector 4 — Aptitude data (the institution finally sees them)
The Special Classification Examination (directive Part IV) measures *aptitude* — analytical/tactical reasoning, anomaly interpretation, pattern recognition, information management, decision under uncertainty — not Esper output. Irregular, assembled from the academy's discards, posts anomalously high *unit-level* aptitude. **Nothing about their Abilities changes; the institution's instrument changes.** This vector is the only one the academy itself provides, and it is why Phase 5 (studied) is possible: the institution has data it cannot unsee.

### Vector 5 — Anomaly-understanding applied to Abilities
Arthur treats each member's strange Ability as a *phenomenon with a Rule*: observe → hypothesize → test → document. The Ability doesn't change; the *manual* for it is written for the first time — operating procedures, flare-condition checklists, interference-aiming protocols, failure-probe patterns. This is the clerk's method (observe→hypothesize→test→pay [CANON-adjacent]) applied to people, and it is why Arthur — not a stronger Esper — is the arc's catalyst.

### Anti-"secretly OP" guardrails [PROPOSED] (load-bearing — violate none)

1. **The ceiling rule:** no Irregular member's Esper Rank rises during the academy movement. Not once. Any draft implying a rank-up is a design error — flag and rewrite. (Their *aptitude* standing rises; rank and aptitude are distinct per directive Part IV. The distinction must be shown on-page at least once, or readers will misread aptitude as rank.)
2. **Demonstrated limits first:** every member's weakness is shown on-page in Phase 1 before any synergy beat. The reader must *know* Owen collapses, Hart's bursts go nowhere, Clarke blanks every general board, Bennett rebounds, Blake freezes, Whitaker cannot be briefed — so that later successes read as *arrangement*, not revelation.
3. **No solo carries:** every scored Irregular success credits ≥2 members by name (the §5 gating rule's prong (c)). A beat where one member wins alone is cut or rewritten as a *setup* for a later team beat.
4. **Visible losses:** the division loses on-page at least once per phase in Phases 2–5. Loss conditions are structural: Arthur absent (the plan exists but no one adapts it mid-execution); mission-shape mismatch (the wrong members for the shape — deployment fails); flare mistiming (Owen's cost arrives mid-operation); interference unmanageable (the Foster–Bennett immunity fails under stress); the abort condition fires (Hart's burst is *correctly* cancelled — a priced non-win). Losses are *priced*, not humiliating — the arc is not a humiliation farm (face-slap guardrail 6 [KEEP]).
5. **Arthur-plans, they-execute:** at least once per phase from Phase 4, a success must occur with Arthur *planning only* — not present, not improvising. This proves the improvement is in the division, not in him. (If every win needs him in the room, the arc is secretly a Arthur-power story.)
6. **The thesis test:** any reader asked "did anyone get stronger?" at the arc's end must answer "no — they got *used right*." If the answer is ambiguous, the phase's beats are rewritten until it isn't.

## §4. WHAT THIS ARC IS NOT [PROPOSED]

Stated explicitly because each of these is the genre-default failure mode (cf. directive Part XVI stress tests):

- **Not a tournament arc.** There is no bracket, no championship, no final match. Scored exercises appear as *measurement instruments* (they move the ladders), never as the arc's purpose. (Anti-tournament guardrails: 17_INTER_DIVISION_RIVALRY.md §2.)
- **Not a training arc.** There are no training montages, no "three months later they're stronger." Improvement comes from deployment-match quality, role-assignment, synergy protocol, aptitude data, and manuals — not from getting stronger. A scene of Irregular "training hard" played for power-growth is a design error.
- **Not everyone-gets-a-power-up.** Ceilings are fixed (§3, guardrail 1). The Hyper-Specialist's one thing stays one thing. The Failed Awakener stays failed. What grows is *arrangement*.
- **Not Arthur-solos.** Prong (c) of the gating rule; guardrail 5. Arthur's contribution is analysis, assignment, and adaptation. The arc's climax must be visibly collective.
- **Not instant.** Seven phases, each gated by a witnessed, recorded, multi-credited event. No phase skipped. The institution's contempt stage never skips either (18_SOCIAL_HIERARCHY_PROGRESSION.md §4).
- **Not a secret-elite reveal.** Nobody was hiding S-rank power. Nobody "was actually strong all along." The true-label doctrine ([KEEP]) applies to every slot: the academy's instrument was correct about their Esper output and wrong about their *use*.
- **Not a friendship-makes-us-strong story.** Trust matters (Phase 4's voluntary following), but trust is not the mechanism — *assignment* is. Sentiment without the manual is just morale; the arc's currency is doctrine.
