## 4. Four-Year Quick Reference

| Year | Stage | Core competence question | Campus presence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Year 1** | Foundation | Can the cadet perform the fundamentals safely? | Very high |
| **Year 2** | Developing Specialization | Can the cadet perform the task reliably? | High |
| **Year 3** | Field Readiness | Can the cadet operate under field conditions? | Moderate |
| **Year 4** | Professional Transition | Can the cadet make professional decisions? | Low |

Year 4 remains a student cohort. Lower campus presence is caused by approved professional placements, internships, supervised missions, research assignments, exchanges, and similar external work.

## 5. Character Generation Quick Reference

Minimum **five named recurring characters per year**:

| Cohort | Minimum | Recommended |
|---|---:|---:|
| Year 1 | 5 | 7–8 |
| Year 2 | 5 | 6–7 |
| Year 3 | 5 | 5–6 |
| Year 4 | 5 | 5 |
| **Total** | **20** | **23–26** |

This is a named-character planning target, not cohort population.

Character design order:

**Background → Experience → Worldview → Behavior → Role → Relationship → Trajectory**

Do not design characters as:

**Name → Rank → Ability → Personality**

Rank is one attribute, not the character's identity.

## 7. Canon Separation Rules

Always keep these concepts separate:

**YEAR ≠ ESPER RANK**  
**YEAR ≠ APR**  
**YEAR ≠ DIVISION**  
**YEAR ≠ POWER LEVEL**  
**DIVISION ≠ CAREER DESTINATION**  
**APR ≠ COMBAT POWER**  
**ESPER RANK ≠ COMBAT OUTCOME**

The four-year model describes institutional development. It does not create a hidden RPG level system.

Existing named cadets remain active. New characters must be added around the existing roster rather than replacing it.

## 9. Maintenance Rule

When a new Academy canon rule is created:

1. Put the rule in the **smallest coherent domain module**.
2. Add or update the cross-reference in this index.
3. Update `35_CANON_DATABASE.md` if the rule has a canonical entity/system ID or master-canon significance.
4. Update affected chapter continuity states only when the rule changes their active canon.
5. Mark conflicting historical proposals rather than silently rewriting them.

Do not create a new MD for every isolated fact.

**Core repository principle:**

> **One MD = one coherent knowledge domain.**

Not:

> one MD = one tiny fact.
