## J. WHY ACADEMY ACCELERATES RATHER THAN SLOWS THE STORY

1. **The tripwire pair: ch5 impossible record, ch6 anomaly clue.** Both are planning gates — orientation is delivered *inside* the records beats, never as standalone chapters. The academy is a pressure vessel, not a campus tour.
2. **Every chapter carries ≥1 driver (target ≥2).** The driver-quota rule cuts 0-driver chapters at planning — the structural guarantee against school-story drift (stress test A).
3. **The institution is a filing machine that notices him.** Sealed Review results, the stamp on his file, the denial ledger, the inclusion-request aggregates, the audit at ch26 — the academy's paperwork is the *mechanism* by which the plot advances. Bureaucracy here is plot.
4. **Joint Teams are the recurring engine, not a school event.** Requirement-generated composition, rotation quotas preventing repetition, dissolution after every mission — the Part XII loop (ACADEMY → MISSION → ANOMALY → CONTRACT → RIVALRY → NEW RIVALRY) never stalls at ACADEMY, because the team is never the same twice.
5. **The Contract plot and the academy plot are one plot.** The knock's presence-only condition manufactures the knock-witness mechanic: using the Contract inside a witnessed, graded, filed environment *is* the cost (stress test J).
6. **Progression is visible without power climb.** The Intelligence Operations ladder gives the reader a progress bar (assignment, clearance, filed rung movements) while the Esper rank letter never moves — investment without inflation (stress test L).
7. **The rivalry is scored on ledgers, not brackets.** No tournament arc; Joint Team outcomes and quarterly ledgers move reputation — rivalry produces *institutional consequences* every arc (stress test M).
