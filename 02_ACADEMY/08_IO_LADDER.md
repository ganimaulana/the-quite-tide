## §1. THE LADDER PRINCIPLE — trigger-first, access + responsibility + cost

### 1.1 The user's bar, restated as design law

The user forbade the administrative level-up: *"kita tentukan dulu apa yang harus Arthur lakukan sehingga institusi tidak punya pilihan selain mengubah status operasionalnya"* — determine first what Arthur must do so that the institution has no choice but to change his operational status.

The design law derived from that bar:

> **A rung movement happens when the institution discovers it is already depending on Arthur in a capacity the paperwork doesn't cover. The rung is the paperwork catching up — and the delay between dependence and formalization is itself a story beat.**

No rung is a reward for being impressive. Every rung is the formalization of a dependence that already exists and has become riskier *unformalized* than formalized.

### 1.2 Progression = access + responsibility + cost

Each rung changes three things simultaneously — never just one:

- **ACCESS** — information access is the real currency. Each rung widens what he may read, request, author, or task. Access is what the reader *feels* as progression.
- **RESPONSIBILITY** — each rung gives him something he can now be *blamed* for. Signed memos, cited assessments, opened investigations, overridden plans. Blameability is the institution's real marker of a rung — a promotion you can't be fired from isn't a promotion.
- **COST** — each rung narrows something: anonymity, deniability, the luxury of time, the protection of obscurity. Every rung makes him more visible, more legible to factions, and more exposed to his own failure modes.

### 1.3 What never moves

- **Esper Rank stays F, permanently** (LOCK 1; verdict standing constraint). The ladder climbs *beside* the rank letter, never through it. Every file must keep the letter visible — the letter's immobility is the undervalue engine's fuel (stress test D).
- **Classification stays IRREGULAR.** His ERROR never clears (L-11 [RECOMMENDATION]), so he is the one cadet the system can never re-sort — the rung changes his *function*, never his *stamp*. The ladder and the classification are two separate instruments; the design's answer to "progression without power climb" is that they never converge.
- **No chapter numbers are assigned to any rung movement in this file.** Chapter placement follows the trigger event, never precedes it (§4 names candidate soils, not assignments). This is the trigger-first rule, enforced structurally.

### 1.4 The anti-gaming discipline

Every trigger below carries a falsification check: *could this trigger be manufactured, faked, or granted as a favor?* The institution's defense is always the same shape — **cross-division witnessing + documentary evidence + institutional need** — and if a draft can't meet the bar, the rung doesn't happen. A rung that can be gamed is an admin level-up wearing a trigger's clothes.

## §3. THE SIX TRANSITIONS — triggers, perception, failure modes

Each transition is specified in four parts: **(a)** what the rung IS institutionally — posting, access, authority, responsibility, cost; **(b)** the NARRATIVE TRIGGER — the species of event that forces the institution's hand, with the no-choice logic made explicit; **(c)** perception beats across the four lenses (F-rank / Irregular / just Records / not field-suitable — File 8); **(d)** the new failure modes the rung opens. Every rung must create new risks, not just new powers.

### T1 — Records & Archives → Information Analysis (Records Cadet → Anomaly Analyst)

**(a) What the rung IS institutionally.** Posting: Cadet-Records Grade 3 → **Analyst-Trainee** (Intelligence Division), attached to the Annex's analysis cell, off the filing rotation. ACCESS: retrieval-only becomes **read-write** — he can author *analysis memoranda* that attach to incident records (his name on the document); he can submit cross-division Records requests in his own name (not through the chief clerk); he gains read access to sanitized after-action reviews (not raw field data). AUTHORITY: he can file a **re-examination request** on a *closed* incident's documentation (the paperwork, not the investigation) — which the annex must log and answer. RESPONSIBILITY: his memos are signed and auditable; a wrong memo costs the annex credibility — **he can now be blamed**, which is the rung's real marker. COST: visibility. The "clerk" contempt loses its truth; he enters the named-personnel economy — instructors who liked him invisible start auditing him; his Contract use now has an audience with forms.

**(b) The NARRATIVE TRIGGER — the Contract-holder field bulletin.** After Contract #1 is bound, the academy faces a concrete institutional problem: a cadet holds a Contract whose conditions, costs, and misfire modes govern Joint Team safety — and **no one else can authoritatively document what it does**. The institution needs a filed, auditable safety/conditions bulletin for any mission deploying near knock-adjacent phenomena, and the only qualified author is Arthur. The trigger is not "he impressed someone" — it is **an unfillable institutional need**: command cannot in good conscience brief a team without a document only he can write. The rung is the paperwork catching up — you cannot have a cadet authoring mission-safety documentation while his posting says "filing rotation, retrieval only." The re-examination request is the secondary forcing surface: his first memo will likely *reopen a closed file's documentation* (the impossible-record species), which a mere filer has no standing to do.

*No-choice logic:* the choice facing command is not "reward him or not" — it is "let the bulletin be authored by the one person who understands the Contract, or brief teams without it." Refusing the rung means accepting unaudited, unsigned Arthur-reads circulating anyway — the riskier option. *Anti-gaming:* the bulletin need must be witnessed cross-division — a Joint Team's after-action or a briefing officer's formal request, never one instructor's favor. And a bulletin that is wrong gets teams hurt, so the institution will test it — which is why the rung is earned, not granted.

**(c) Perception (four lenses).**
- *F-rank:* "The F-rank wrote the safety bulletin?" — contempt curdles into unease; the letter is still true on paper, which makes it worse.
- *Irregular:* the stamp doesn't move — and that becomes the thing people start noticing ("why is the kid writing our briefs still stamped Irregular?").
- *Records:* "he's not just Records anymore" — admin cadets lose their favorite insult and reach for new ones.
- *Suitability:* instructor schism begins — some see a named asset; others see an untested cadet holding a pen that now signs truth-claims.

**(d) New failure modes.**
- A signed memo can be **wrong**: felt-certainty (Contract #1's failure mode) can infect his analyses — he can now be confidently wrong with an institutionally blessed pen, and the blast radius is a mission, not a filing cart.
- **Withholding becomes a crime:** access creates the liability of omission — what he knew and didn't file.
- The bulletin makes his Contract's conditions **legible to everyone**, including people who will design around them — adversaries read the bulletin too.

### T2 — Information Analysis → Operational Intelligence (Anomaly Analyst → field-analysis authority)

**(a) What the rung IS institutionally.** Posting: Analyst-Trainee → **junior Operational Intelligence analyst**, attached to Joint Mission Command's intelligence cell. ACCESS: live operational data feeds (sanitized), pre-mission brief packets *before* they're finalized, the right to **task** Records requests across divisions (pull, not just read) — and read access to *pending* anomaly assessments, not just closed ones. AUTHORITY: his threat assessments can attach to a Joint Team's mission plan as a named appendix — **the plan cites him**. RESPONSIBILITY: if his assessment misses the operative condition, the team walks in blind *with his name on the plan* — he is now a named dependency. COST: he becomes schedule-bound — briefings have deadlines, and the archive's luxury (time to be right) is gone; he must now be right **on time**, which is a different skill from being right eventually.

**(b) The NARRATIVE TRIGGER — the mission that turned on his assessment.** A Joint Team deploys on an anomaly-adjacent mission; his pre-mission assessment names the operative condition; the outcome turns on it — and critically, there is a **near-miss or partial failure he predicted but the plan couldn't fully accommodate**, so the after-action proves his analysis was load-bearing, not decorative. The forcing logic: command discovers it is **already depending** on his products — teams are informally routing around the official intel cell to get his read first. The rung formalizes a dependence that already exists; refusing to formalize it means the next mission runs on unaccountable, unaudited Arthur-reads anyway. The institution formalizes him because *informal dependence is the riskier option*.

*No-choice logic:* same shape as T1, one level up — the choice is "audit him or let him operate unaudited," not "promote him or not." *Anti-gaming:* the dependence must be documented across **multiple missions and multiple teams** (not one grateful commander), and the after-action review is the evidentiary instrument (File 7 §5.4's five-section AAR). One lucky call is a story beat; a pattern across AARs is a rung.

**(c) Perception (four lenses).**
- *F-rank:* cracks among operators — "I don't care what his letter is" (the letter stays; the caring changes).
- *Irregular:* still stamped (his ERROR never clears — L-11) — the stamp starts looking like the institution's problem, not his.
- *Records:* dead as an insult among anyone who's deployed; persists among cadets who haven't — the contempt surface migrates to the inexperienced.
- *Suitability:* open instructor schism — the "not field-suitable" camp vs. the "his reads keep my teams alive" camp.

**(d) New failure modes.**
- **Dependency is a leash:** he can be tasked to *bless* missions he hasn't actually solved — rubber-stamping under time pressure, where saying "I don't know" costs him the rung's justification.
- His Contract's **felt-certainty becomes an institutional hazard**: a Contract-holder's analysis carries hidden confidence that its readers can't see.
- **Compartmentalization:** he now produces assessments at clearances he can't fully read — he learns fragments he cannot act on, and the DECLARE/CONCEAL custody structure means some of his best products are restricted from the very people who need them.

### T3 — Operational Intelligence → Anomaly Intelligence (field-analysis authority → Special Investigator)

**(a) What the rung IS institutionally.** Posting: junior analyst → **Special Investigator** (Intelligence Division), anomaly desk. ACCESS: the **anomaly registry** — including the unfileable backlog (L-14's Irregular functions) and sealed-incident documentation at clearance; cold-case files; the right to request *phenomenon-adjacent* materials, not just reports about them. AUTHORITY: he can **open investigations** — recommend an incident's anomaly status be reclassified; lead a records-side investigation cell with tasking power over Records requests. RESPONSIBILITY: opening an investigation spends institutional resources and political capital; a wrong opening burns both — and the reopened file's original closers are now his institutional opponents. COST: visibility to the Quiet Desk and the institutional-attention machinery — the *"Why do they keep putting Arthur on these missions?"* beat lives here; his name enters faction sightlines (the 28-faction web starts keeping a file).

**(b) The NARRATIVE TRIGGER — the closed file that was wrong.** He proves — through Records-discrepancy work, his native vantage — that a **closed** incident's anomaly classification was wrong, and the proof is **documentary** (not his Contract, not a hunch): the files convict themselves. The forcing logic: the institution's own records prove its classifications can fail; the person who proved it must be given the tool (reopening authority), because the next such case will come to him anyway — and the alternative is the institution formally *refusing to know*, which no commander will sign after the proof is on the record. Secondary forcing species: a "closed" phenomenon **recurs** because the first solve was wrong — recurrence is the institution's nightmare, and it makes reopening authority a safety instrument, not a reward.

*No-choice logic:* the institution cannot un-know what its own files now say; the rung is the difference between "we have a documented classification failure and no one empowered to act on the next one" and "we have an investigator." *Anti-gaming:* the proof must be **documentary and reproducible by a second analyst** — his method must survive someone else re-running it (the manual's-appendix beat is this trigger's seed); a single lucky reopening is a story beat, the *class* of error is the rung.

**(c) Perception (four lenses).**
- *F-rank:* openly debated in the mess hall — the letter vs. the name, argued as a paradox.
- *Irregular:* the stamp stays, which becomes the scandal — "why is *he* still Irregular?" (L-11's designed hope/denial beat ignites here).
- *Records:* inverted — "Records" becomes a threat-assessment ("he's Records — check what he filed about this").
- *Suitability:* "not field-suitable" is contested — he's investigating, not filing; the camp that holds the line looks increasingly political rather than professional.

**(d) New failure modes.**
- Reopening authority means he can be **blamed for reopening what should have stayed shut** — some files are closed for political reasons, not factual ones; the phenomena tier re-affirmed by the Disclosure Protocol carries criminal penalties, so some truths are *illegal to file*.
- His Contract use becomes an investigation tool → Laggard-masking and contract-use reads create **audit exposure** (the read-as-fluctuation cover gets stress-tested).
- Enemies can now **feed him poisoned records** — the archive is his battlefield, and a forged discrepancy is a weapon aimed at the analyst, not at the phenomenon.

### T4 — Anomaly Intelligence → Field Intelligence (Special Investigator → Field Consultant)

**(a) What the rung IS institutionally.** Posting: desk investigator → **Field Intelligence officer**, deployable with Joint Teams. ACCESS: deployed sensorium feeds, on-site evidence before it's filed, **immediate post-incident debrief authority** (he debriefs the team before the paperwork cools). AUTHORITY: the **functional-command doctrine** (File 7 §5.3 — command follows the problem) formally attaches to his role: in anomaly-active phases, his read can **override** the tactical lead's plan — on the record, with his name. RESPONSIBILITY: lives, directly. The first time his override is wrong, the doctrine dies with his credibility — and the team knows it. COST: physical exposure (still F-rank, still the trained-human body — the capability ceiling is real and now load-bearing); his Contract's toll-marks accumulate **in view of others**; every deployment is a witness event.

**(b) The NARRATIVE TRIGGER — the incident that couldn't wait for the desk.** A phenomenon mutates faster than the reporting cycle — desk analysis arrives correct and *too late*; or a Joint Team on the ground hits a knock-adjacent (or Contract-relevant) condition where **only the Contract-holder's field-present read** can prevent a catastrophe. The forcing logic, stated as the commander's dilemma: the asset exists; **withholding** it from the field is now a liability decision the command must sign for — "we knew the one person who could read it and we kept him at a desk" is the sentence no after-action survives. The inversion of the earlier beat: operators begin *requesting* him — "Why do they keep putting Arthur on these missions?" asked by outsiders, asked by insiders as a demand. The institution deploys him because **not deploying him has become the indefensible option**.

*No-choice logic:* liability, not merit — the rung is what the commander signs to avoid signing the other sentence. *Anti-gaming:* the deployment must be **requested by operators, not volunteered by him** — the demand has to come from the people whose lives are at stake, on the record, with dissenters present. This is also the bullying-reversal engine's fuel: the people who mocked the F-rank now have to ask for him in writing.

**(c) Perception (four lenses).**
- *Suitability:* breaks — "not field-suitable" becomes an open institutional argument, and the people making it are now arguing against mission records.
- *F-rank:* category crisis among combat cadets — the letter says one thing, the deployment orders say another.
- *Irregular:* the stamp is now openly called an embarrassment by his own division's officers.
- *Records:* dead — replaced by a new contempt surface: **"the asset"** — dehumanizing in the other direction. He's becoming a tool people fight over, which is its own story.

**(d) New failure modes.**
- Field presence means his Contract is **witnessed** — the knock-witness mechanic (File 7 §7.6) becomes a public fact; containment of knowledge about his capability ends.
- His ordinary body is a **single point of failure** the team must protect — a liability they resent, and a lever for anyone who wants to hurt the team by hurting him.
- **Felt-certainty in the field is lethal** (the ch27 beat is this rung's warning label).
- The functional-command doctrine makes him the person everyone blames when the anomaly wins — **override authority is blame authority**.

### T5 — Field Intelligence → Special Investigation (Field Consultant → Anomaly Specialist → Contract Holder)

**(a) What the rung IS institutionally.** Posting: field officer → **Special Investigator** with a cross-division case docket; direct line to the institute-level anomaly apparatus. ACCESS: **cross-division tasking authority** for anomaly investigations; his own docket; sealed materials at the investigator's clearance (the highest clearance a cadet can hold). AUTHORITY: he can **certify** anomaly assessments — his signature on a truth-claim about a phenomenon, which other divisions must treat as authoritative input (not orders — weighted assessments). RESPONSIBILITY: certification propagates — a wrong certification is read by every team that touches the phenomenon. **His name is now on the academy's epistemology.** COST: legibility to every faction — the 28-faction web can see him clearly now; his methods become teachable (the manual's appendix grows into a chapter) → replicable → **no longer exclusively his**.

**(b) The NARRATIVE TRIGGER — the doctrinal failure.** An incident that **no division's doctrine can frame** — Combat, Recon, and Research all bring their playbooks and all fail — where his solve is the only thing that worked, **witnessed at scale** (not a quiet records win, not a deniable field assist). The forcing logic: the academy must either admit its doctrine has a hole shaped like him, or keep losing the same way. The rung is the institution **naming the hole** rather than pretending the playbook covered it. This is deliberately distant — it belongs to a later arc's climax, not Arc I; designing its exact shape now would be false precision (the user's rule against inventing what isn't needed).

*No-choice logic:* the choice is "admit the hole or repeat the loss" — institutional self-preservation, not recognition. *Anti-gaming:* "witnessed at scale" means **cross-division witnesses with conflicting interests** — a Combat officer and a Research lead must both sign the after-action. No single patron can manufacture this rung.

**(c) Perception (four lenses).**
- The four lenses **openly contradict each other** — F-rank letter vs. the name everyone knows; the contradiction itself becomes the academy's open secret, then its open joke, then its open problem.
- *Irregular:* the institution's embarrassment is now formal — "why is he still Irregular?" is asked in meetings, and L-11's answer (his ERROR never clears) is why the system can't fix it.
- Hierarchy stage: respect/fear — File 8's stage 7, **strategic concern**.

**(d) New failure modes.**
- Certification authority is the **felt-certainty hazard at institutional scale** — his confident misreading now ships academy-wide.
- Factions move from watching to **recruiting or coercing** (the coerced-contract risk sharpens — R-28's territory).
- A taught method can be **turned against him**: an adversary who reads his manual knows how he thinks — his transparency becomes his attack surface.
- The **DECLARE/CONCEAL custody structure collides with his visibility**: what the public must not know vs. what his role requires people to know — the secrecy architecture starts tearing at this rung.

### T6 — Special Investigation → unique role (Contract Holder → Unclassified / Outside Conventional Classification)

**(a) What the rung IS institutionally.** Not a promotion — **a recognition of category failure**. The system stops trying to place him. ACCESS: whatever he can justify under a **charter exception**; the role is defined by exception, not by ladder. AUTHORITY: **sui generis** — his assessments travel on their own letterhead, outside the division sign-off chain. RESPONSIBILITY: he becomes the **precedent** — how the institution treats him sets the template for every future Irregular. His handling is now policy. COST: **outside classification = outside protection.** No division owns him; no division defends him. His failures are his alone; his successes belong to whoever claims them. This is the most dangerous rung, not the most powerful — the file must never frame it as a coronation.

**(b) The NARRATIVE TRIGGER — the crisis where the rules forbid the only working solution.** He has to break the framework — or the institution has to suspend it — to solve it, and the solution works **in front of witnesses the institution can't silence**. The forcing logic: the framework itself is on trial; the institution's choice is to excommunicate him or rewrite itself around the exception. This is endgame-of-an-era material. Its exact shape is **[UNKNOWN] by design** — specifying it now would be false precision and would pre-empt the arcs that must earn it. The trigger-species is named (a *framework-breaking crisis survived in public*); the event is not.

*No-choice logic:* the institution cannot un-witness what was witnessed; it can only choose the *form* of its response — and "rewrite around the exception" is the form that preserves the institution. *Anti-gaming:* ungameable by construction — it requires a crisis no one would manufacture and witnesses no one controls.

**(c) Perception.** The lenses collapse into one — **"Arthur" as his own category**. The F-rank letter becomes an irony the institution maintains on paper. Fear/strategic concern as the steady state — not an aura, but the documented fact that he changes mission outcomes.

**(d) New failure modes.**
- Being the precedent means **every Irregular after him is measured against him** — a burden on others, and a political weapon for anyone who wants the precedent *gone*.
- **Disavowability:** the institution can cut him loose cleanly — the charter exception is also an ejection seat.
- His Contract portfolio (whatever it becomes) is now the only thing between him and irrelevance — the dependency the whole design warns against. The guardrail is LOCK 2's principle: **capacity ≠ acquisition** — he must never *need* the next Contract to matter.

- **T4–T6:** no Arc I soil is claimed. Claiming it would violate trigger-first. Their triggers are named as species of event (§3.4–§3.6), and their chapters will be whatever chapters contain those events.

**Restated as law:** chapter placement follows the trigger event, never precedes it. If a draft reaches the end of Arc I with T1 untriggered, the correct response is not to force T1 — it is to check whether the Arc I plan actually produced the *need* the trigger requires, and if not, to let the ladder wait. An unclimbed ladder is not a pacing failure; a forced rung is a design failure.

## §6. HONEST WEAKNESSES — what is thin, and what would fix it

1. **T1's trigger is the strongest and the most Contract-#1-dependent.** If LOCK 8 picks a different Contract #1, the bulletin's shape changes and T1 must be re-derived. This is the file's load-bearing dependency — stated here so it can't surprise anyone later.
