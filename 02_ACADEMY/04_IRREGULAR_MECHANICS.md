## L5-1 — Classification Authority **[PROPOSED]**

- **Initial classifier:** the **Sorting Board** (chaired by the Deputy Commandant, Sorting), acting through its **intake exception clause** at entry. The stamp is **provisional** at intake — confirmed, or rarely revised, at the **Stage-4 board at end of Year 1** [PROPOSED — from IRREGULAR_CLASSIFICATION §1.6].
- **Mid-course stamping:** on **division-chief petition** to the Sorting Board (washouts, reliability failures, medical/legal flags, compatibility failures discovered in training — Blake's door).
- **Review authority:** the Sorting Board. Once per year, a cadet may **petition** for re-examination and removal of the stamp, or an **instructor may refer** them. The bar is deliberately conservative: removal requires a division chief willing to *own* the cadet unstamped — division chiefs do not volunteer for damaged goods. In practice: **rare, not impossible** (the rarity is itself the plot engine).
- **Permanence:** permanence is **NOT assumed**. The mechanism is *conditionally revisable* [PROPOSED]. **Arthur is the exception by fact, not by rule:** his ERROR never clears (LOCK 3; IO_LADDER L-11), so he is the one cadet the system can never re-sort. The ladder and the classification are two separate instruments — they never converge. Distinguish always: *revisable mechanism, irreversible case.*
- **Appeal mechanism:** [LOCKED 2026-09-23] conservative review chain — Provisional → Stage-4 Confirmation → Conditional Review → reclassification only if the conventional framework can reliably process the subject, requiring NEW MEASURABLE EVIDENCE (not subjective argument). Conditionally revisable ≠ likely to be revised. No appeal court; re-examination rights (annual petition, instructor referral) feed the chain.

## L5-2 — Institutional Meaning **[PROPOSED]**

"What does the Academy do differently because a cadet is Irregular?" — separated by domain:

- **Administrative:** the stamp is printed on the file header, uniform record tag, and Sorting Board roll — visible to every instructor, quartermaster, scheduler, and mission board *before* they see the cadet. Assignment routing is bureaucratic, not chartered: low-prestige, low-clearance assignments (records, archives, monitoring, transcription); **Clearance Grade 3** (Arthur's case). Stamped cadets are disproportionately posted where the state cannot leave work unfilled.
- **Training:** stamped cadets attend their **own divisions' curricula** — there is no Irregular curriculum, no Irregular instruction beyond Hayes's cross-division advisory hours (counseling and triage, not curriculum). Stamp-adjusted priority only: ranges at off-hours, classroom priority below unstamped cadets; curriculum *bars* attach to individual risk profiles (Hart: barred from the combat track), never to the stamp wholesale.
- **Safety:** risk-profile flags per cadet (medical: Owen's side effects, Hart's Erosion accrual, Bennett's Erosion-risk, Ward's self-harm economics; legal: Whitaker's tribunal-warrant bar; reliability: Blake's freeze record). The stamp brings *visibility*; the flags bring *specific protocols*.
- **Mission:** the stamp makes command reach for stamped cadets last — and makes every inclusion a story event. Discipline (MIXED_MISSION_SYSTEM §4.5 [KEEP]): **the deployment's trigger must be stated aloud on the record** ("Irregular attached per records-flag 7-C"); a deployment with no statable trigger is a design error. The justification's insult is always load-bearing later.
- **Information access:** the low-pile paradox [INFERENCE]: low clearance plus high volume means stamped records-posted cadets see every file *nobody senior bothers to read* — where discrepancies go to hide. This is the institutional reason Arthur's Records beats find him, not the other way around.
- **None of this is privilege.** The stamp is the system's honest confession that it cannot use the cadet conventionally, filed as routing logic. Reading any of §1.4's mechanics as prestige is a design error — flag and rewrite.

## L5-3 — Rights and Restrictions **[PROPOSED]**

**The mechanism [PROPOSED]:** restrictions attach to the **individual risk profile**, not to the stamp uniformly. The stamp brings routing and visibility; the *flags* bring restrictions. This is what keeps the classification from being a blanket punishment and what makes each roster member's cage a different shape:

| Restriction type | Example (different cadets, different cages) |
|---|---|
| Equipment | Quartermaster penalty tier (classification-wide policy: last-priority issue, training-grade gear, salvage pool); no warded kit / no personal sidearms for F-ranks (Arthur's tier) |
| Mission access | Combat-track bar (Hart); cadet-grade tasking limits, tribunal-warrant bar (Whitaker); zero (Foster: no Ability to bar, so no bar — his restriction is absence itself) |
| Additional evaluations | EAR automatic trigger for ERROR readings; instructor-referral and cadet-petition paths for the rest (SPECIAL_CLASSIFICATION_EXAM §-interface [KEEP]); sealed by default |
| Safety protocols | Erosion-accrual monitoring (Hart, Bennett, Ward); medical side-effect flags (Owen) |
| Documentation | The sealed aptitude profile; mission-board inclusion justifications; annual census notes |
| Ordinary cadet rights | **Retained in full.** The classification is a filing status, not a legal demotion: no separate legal status, no reduced service rights, no shortened bond. The stamp changes where the file routes, not what the law says the cadet is |

**Explicitly rejected [PROPOSED]:** universal restriction ("all Irregulars barred from X") — any such rule in worker prose is a design error. If a restriction applies to all stamped cadets (the quartermaster penalty tier), it is *quartermaster policy*, not classification law, and it can be petitioned like any other supply-tier decision.

Perception runs through four lenses (IRREGULAR_CLASSIFICATION §1.7 [KEEP]): Combat cadets see **F-rank** (weakness); elites see **Irregular** (institutionally damaged goods); admin cadets see **"just Records"** (boring); instructors see **"not field-suitable"** (a planning constraint).

Combat cadets can reach rivalry while instructors are still at dismissal; the asynchrony is the realism. No lens advances without an earned visible event. "Everyone respects him now" is the failure mode (stress test K) — flag and rewrite.
- **The classification's thesis is social, not martial:** "the system measured the wrong thing" — played through Foster first, then through the roster as a set.

## L5-6 — Operational Utility **[PROPOSED]**

**The classification grants no power. Ever.** Any advantage a stamped cadet brings comes only from:

- **specialization** (Clarke's forgery detection — a human mass spectrometer for paper),
- **unusual compatibility** (Mercer's zero-emission sense-lending link),
- **adaptability** (Blake's doctrine-trained planning from the rear),
- **mission fit** (Ward's austere-condition triage; Rowan's post-incident object forensics),
- **team synergy** (the combination logic — IRREGULAR_CLASSIFICATION §4 [KEEP]: investigation cell, breach team, silent recon link, aftermath team; weaknesses interlock visibly *before* Arthur's leadership prices them),
- **demonstrated performance** (the dark-horse measurement: the stamped roll's aggregate reputation — quartermaster penalty-tier reviews, assignment-routing patterns, mission-board reach rates, census notes [KEEP from IRREGULAR_DARK_HORSE_ARC §245]).

**Guardrails [KEEP]:** no Irregular member's Esper Rank rises during the academy movement (ceiling rule — a draft implying rank-up is a design error); no member solos a mission; no combination is secretly overpowered (every combination has a priced weakness); other divisions are not stupid for missing the combinations (they only work because Arthur *prices* the weaknesses — Records-born cost-accounting no conventional doctrine teaches). Improvement comes from deployment-match quality, role-assignment, synergy, aptitude data, and manuals — **not from getting stronger** (a "training hard for power-growth" scene is a design error).

## L5-9 — Story Function **[PROPOSED]**

Irregular classification **must function as:**
- **social friction** — the stamp is visible before the cadet; every room starts with the file's opinion;
- **institutional ambiguity** — the state keeps what it cannot place, and the keeping is the story;
- **mission-composition variable** — stamped cadets deploy from their divisions; inclusion is always a story event, justified aloud on the record;
- **a source of expectations** — low expectations are the dark-horse engine's fuel (every success is a surprise, every failure priced in);
- **a source of misunderstanding** — the stamp is misread as verdict by characters who should misread it;
- **eventually, a framework the Academy is forced to reconsider** — the long arc: the classification's thesis ("measured by the wrong framework") must one day be answered by the institution, not just by the reader.

It **must NOT function as:**
- instant protagonist privilege (the stamp buys nothing — the quartermaster penalty tier is policy);
- a hidden SSS rank (ceiling rule: no rank rises; strength is decoupled from utility);
- an excuse for arbitrary power (every combination has a priced weakness);
- a substitute for character development (each roster member's arc is earned or it is decorative — Bennett and Ward must be allowed to refuse Arthur's deployments);
- a substitute for Contract progression (Arc I without Contract #2 stands; the classification is orthogonal to the Contract ledger).
