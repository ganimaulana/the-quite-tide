## 1. Executive Summary

The Seven-Division Audit established that:

- Logistics is the only documented Category-A structural fit for the "low prestige but high utility" model (documented lowest-prestige conventional division; real military utility; existing ordinary-background cadet population; Rank-F Irregular precedent: Daniel Foster, IR-08).
- Research is Category B (socially mocked, peer-low-status plausible; low-rank cadets its backbone; no records/equipment breadth).
- Intelligence is Category D for this direction: the author direction excludes it, and Arthur's placement there is locked (LOCK 1 L-17; LOCK 3 L3-0; LOCK 4 L4-4/L4-7).
- Chapter 001 is FINAL; Chapter 002 Stage 10 is PLAN READY, Stage 11 not started.

This document compares two architectures without choosing between them:

- **Option A — Preserve:** Arthur stays Division Intelligence / Curriculum Intelligence Operations / Assignment Records & Archives. Test whether the new Academy direction can be achieved without moving him.
- **Option B — Move to Logistics:** hypothetical. Arthur becomes Division Logistics; Curriculum and Assignment UNDEFINED (not invented). Identify required changes only; redesign nothing.

Core finding pattern (neutral): Option A requires zero lock changes but cannot deliver equipment/logistics breadth from Records & Archives (no canon support) and cannot deliver cross-Division classes or a placement exam (not established for either option). Option B delivers equipment/logistics breadth natively but cannot be adopted without revising three locked items plus reopening Chapter 001, and it removes the records access the IO ladder and several mystery-economy beats assume.

## 10. Risks and Unknowns

1. **Option A — equipment-breadth gap.** The proposal's signature payoff beats (weapon-maintenance recognition) have no canon support under Records & Archives. If the author wants those beats, they require cross-Division mechanisms that are themselves only [PROPOSED] (mixed exercises) or undefined.
2. **Option B — archive-access gap.** Arthur's investigation method and the IO ladder's T3 trigger assume records access. Logistics has no documented records function. The mystery economy must be re-authored or an assignment carve-out invented (invention = new canon, author decision).
3. **Option B — lock-revision precedent.** Revising three user-approved locks (LOCK 1, 3, 4) plus reopening a FINAL chapter is the heaviest revision class in this project. The audit does not assess whether it is warranted — only the author can.
4. **Sorting rationale.** Under current documented logic, Arthur sorts "deterministically" to Intelligence/IO/Records. A Logistics placement needs a revised, documented sorting rationale ("reasonable based on the initial information available to the Academy" — proposal §4) that survives the "Academy is not stupid" principle.
5. **Division names are [PROPOSED].** A Logistics assignment would reference a working name whose official British formal designation is still pending user lock.
6. **Foster precedent limits.** Daniel Foster (Logistics, Rank F, no Ability, Irregular) proves the combination is institutionally possible, but his placement rationale (kendo captaincy — "reliable bodies") does not transfer to Arthur automatically.
7. **Equal unknowns for both options:** common cross-Division classes; graduation placement exam; primary/secondary field mechanism; formal curricula for six divisions; division quotas; transfer mechanics.
8. **Chapter 002 timing.** Stage 11 has not started. If the author leans toward Option B, holding Stage 11 avoids building on a profile that may change; if toward Option A, Stage 11 can proceed on the locked profile.
