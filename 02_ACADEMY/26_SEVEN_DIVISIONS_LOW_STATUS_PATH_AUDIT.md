## 5. Curriculum Analysis

- **The only formally named curriculum in authoritative canon: INTELLIGENCE OPERATIONS** — "formal curriculum name — never 'Clerk'" (`06_DIVISION_SYSTEM.md` coordinator amendment). Lives in Intelligence Division alongside analysis, counter-infiltration, and informant-handling tracks. Arthur's curriculum under LOCK 1 (L-17).
- **All six other divisions' formal curriculum names: UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED.** The amendment states only "each division teaches multiple curricula" and that other divisions' curricula are "unchanged in function" — the division chapters describe *training emphases* (e.g., Logistics: quartermaster craft, field engineering; Recon: observation logging), not formal curriculum inventories. No formal curriculum list was ever established.
- **The distinction is locked:** DIVISION ≠ CURRICULUM ≠ CLASSIFICATION (L-16, LOCK 1). "Sorting outputs five fields, not one" (`14_ACADEMY_RESTRUCTURE.md`).
- **Arthur-relevant curriculum constraint:** LOCK 3 L3-0 locks the IO progression ladder sequence (Records & Archives → Information Analysis → Operational Intelligence → Anomaly Intelligence → Field Intelligence → Special Investigation → unique role; order fixed; no rung skipped). A non-Intelligence start removes the locked base rung. `08_IO_LADDER.md` §6.6 permits rung-*title* translation for secondment outside Intelligence, but not sequence revision.
- **Assignment ≠ curriculum:** Arthur's Records & Archives is an *assignment* (posting), not his curriculum. His curriculum (Intelligence Operations) is what he is trained to perform; the assignment is where he is posted to perform it (`06_DIVISION_SYSTEM.md` amendment).
- **Curriculum compatibility verdict:** any low-status Division other than Intelligence lacks a formal curriculum name and has no locked ladder. This is not a contradiction — it is missing material: a new Division assignment for Arthur would need either a curriculum assignment within that Division or an author decision on how the IO ladder maps.
