### RM1. Mission scores
- **Institution:** every practicum and field mission is scored on a posted rubric. The rubric weights conventional output (neutralizations, area secured, time-to-objective) at ~80% and interpretation/documentation at ~20%. Scores are cumulative per cadet and per division, posted weekly.

### RM2. Academy rankings
