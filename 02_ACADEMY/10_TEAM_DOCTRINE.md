## §3. L6-1 — PURPOSE [LOCKED 2026-09-23]

A Home Team's institutional functions (built from canon fragments; all [PROPOSED] unless noted):

1. **Recurring training and exercises** — the unit that drills together; the venue where division-specific preparation happens (division-owned, instructor-supervised).
2. **Routine Academy assignments** — the work-crew level of academy life: filing rotations, quartermaster details, duty rosters. (Arthur's assignment board: *filing rotation* — ARTHUR_ACADEMY_ENTRY §216.)
3. **Relationship continuity** — the stable cast against which every dissolving Joint Team is contrasted. Trust and distrust accumulate here because the faces don't change.
4. **Instructor evaluation substrate** — graded exercises and practicums run through teams; the after-action review grades individuals, but instructors *watch* teams. (The review machinery is [CANON]; its use as the instructor's observation ground is [INFERENCE].)
5. **Preparation for Joint Team missions** — teams are the pipeline the mission board draws from; team-level competence is what the competence-match machinery (MIXED_MISSION_SYSTEM §4.2) has to work with.
6. **Division socialization** — a cadet learns what their division *is* (prestige, stereotypes, contempt) at the team level before ever deploying.

**Explicitly NOT:** a miniature military unit with standing operational authority. The Academy is a military *academy* (LOCK 4 schooling model); operational command belongs to instructors, the mission board, and Joint Mission Command. Teams do not self-deploy, do not hold missions, and do not outrank any filed authority.

---

**Load-bearing principle [LOCKED 2026-09-23]:** *Home Team is a continuity mechanism, not a power-progression mechanism.* Home Team exists so the reader learns who works with Arthur, who doubts him, how his reputation changes, how chemistry develops, and how others judge his capability. The Joint Team is where operational consequence is tested.

**Continuity engine [LOCKED]:**

HOME TEAM (relationship + expectation)
        ↓
JOINT TEAM (pressure + unfamiliar personnel)
        ↓
MISSION (actual competence)
        ↓
AFTER-ACTION REVIEW (institutional consequence)
        ↓
HOME TEAM (new reputation / new expectation)

This chain is the guardrail against school-life filler.

**Leadership doctrine [LOCKED 2026-09-23]:** *Rank does not automatically determine Team leadership; demonstrated competence may establish leadership regardless of Rank, but no protagonist-based promotion.* Leadership is a consequence of demonstrated competence + institutional recognition.

## §7. L6-5 — EVALUATION [LOCKED 2026-09-23]

**Canon machinery:** graded after-action review per mission (objective outcome 40%, method log 30%, command assessment 20%, aptitude notes, remainder/disposition); individual + division reputation ledgers, public; recognition arrives as **access, never rank**; division ledger drives material stakes (training resources, equipment priority, instructor prestige postings, practicum priority).

**Proposed Home Team evaluation layer:**

- Teams are evaluated *through* their members' exercise grades and mission after-actions — there is **no separate team points system** (no canon supports one; a generic points/ranking system is explicitly not invented here).
- The division ledger's quarterly posting is the public face of team performance; a team's reputation is the aggregate its members carry into Joint Team selection (the competence-match machinery reads the same files).
- Evaluation consequences are canon-consistent: reputation, rivalry (the public ledger is the rivalry engine's ambient fuel), instructor attention, resource consequences (quartermaster draw order), future mission selection (selection rules weight aptitude-fit and ledger standing).
- **Tournament-arc guardrail** [CANON — MIXED_MISSION_SYSTEM amendment]: Joint Teams exist for missions with real remainders, never for bracket entertainment. Home Team exercises are *graded*, not *ranked as spectacle*. The scoreboard feeds rivalry; it is never the story.

## §8. L6-6 — SOCIAL FUNCTION [LOCKED 2026-09-23]

The Home Team is the story's relationship substrate:

- **Recurring relationships** — the only cast that persists between dissolving Joint Teams; the contrast (stable bunk vs re-assembled strangers) is itself a story surface: *who do you trust when the formation changes but the bunk doesn't?*
- **Trust/distrust, friction, role expectations** — team roles (who briefs, who carries, who files) become social facts through repetition.
- **Social hierarchy at close range** — the team's internal pecking order is where contempt is *lived* (mess, barracks, drill), distinct from the institution's filed hierarchy.
- **Interpersonal growth and earned leadership** — the venue where functional authority converts into followed authority (L6-4).
- **Bullying guardrails** [CANON — LOCK 1 standing constraints]: allowed and required to appear (contempt, exclusion, humiliation, rivalry, patronizing praise, equipment bias, authority bias), with varied expression (never the same noun twice within 15 chapters per SHP's variety rule) — but **not every team member is hostile, not every member admires him, and there is no instant found-family.** Dissenters and neutrals are required by the existing design (named dissenters in L5-5's social mechanics).
