# DR. MIRIAM KESSLER — INSTITUTE LIAISON

**Status:** AUTHOR-APPROVED CHARACTER LOCK — ACTIVE 2026-09-26

## Core identity

- Name: **Dr. Miriam Kessler**
- Affiliation: Albion Institute of Esper Studies
- Role: research liaison / anomalous-materials researcher
- Primary function in Arthur's story: institutional bridge between Ravenscroft and the Institute
- Not Arthur's mentor, guardian, or automatic ally.

## Function

Kessler is interested in the distinction between a phenomenon being **unresolved** and being **poorly understood**. She recognizes that Arthur's field reports repeatedly produce useful distinctions without pretending that he has solved the larger mystery.

She can provide Institute-side context, controlled research proposals, historical transfer information when authorized, practical explanation of why the Institute wants access, and pressure from institutional priorities.

She cannot reveal restricted answers without authorization, explain the origin of Arthur's artifact, identify the previous holder without evidence, explain Contract #2 or future Contracts, or act as an omniscient exposition device.

## Relationship with Arthur

Their relationship is professional and transactional.

Arthur wants evidence.

Kessler wants controlled access to unusual material and reliable field methodology.

They can cooperate when their objectives overlap and disagree when institutional interests diverge.

She respects Arthur because he distinguishes observation from interpretation.

## Characterization

- calm;
- precise;
- patient with evidence;
- willing to admit uncertainty;
- capable of institutional pressure without theatrical villainy;
- does not underestimate Arthur because of Rank F;
- does not romanticize his irregularity.

## Hard rules

1. Kessler is not omniscient.
2. She does not solve Arthur's mysteries for him.
3. She does not automatically protect Arthur from the Institute.
4. She may disagree with Arthur's conclusions while respecting his method.
5. Her Institute affiliation creates legitimate institutional pressure.
