# THE QUIET TIDE — ACADEMY REFORM & INTERNATIONALIZATION

**Status:** AUTHOR-APPROVED WORLD AMENDMENT — ACTIVE 2026-09-25  
**Scope:** Academy institutional identity, admissions, reputation, leadership, reform, cohort composition, and long-term Academy progression.  
**Authority:** This amendment supersedes older design/proposal language that describes the Academy primarily as a national military academy. It does not alter the seven-Division model, Irregular mechanics, Contract System, or Arthur's Contract canon.

---

## 1. Core Institutional Identity

The Academy is a **state-recognized specialist academy for Esper and anomaly-related disciplines**, not a school whose primary purpose is to produce national military officers.

It is publicly chartered and nationally regulated, but its graduates may enter government, research, medical/support, intelligence, investigative, field, private/corporate, and military pathways.

Military service remains an important destination, but it is one destination rather than the Academy's defining identity.

The Academy therefore retains disciplined, practical training without functioning as a national military boot school.

## 2. National Reputation

The Academy is **not an elite national institution**. Its reputation is:

> **legitimate, useful, improving, but historically second-tier.**

National evaluations generally place it in the **lower half of recognized specialist academies**, with its exact position changing by year and evaluation criteria.

It is respected enough to receive state recognition and professional accreditation, but it does not have the prestige, funding, alumni network, or recruitment power of the leading institutions.

This is not a garbage school. Its weakness is institutional reputation and historical performance, not the worth of its students.

## 3. The Reform

The Academy entered a significant reform period shortly before Arthur's cohort began. A newly appointed **Director** is the institutional force behind the reform. The Director's personal history remains deliberately underdeveloped until needed by the story.

The reform principle is:

> **The Academy does not need to imitate the elite institutions. It needs to become useful in ways the elite institutions are not.**

### Historical model

- bureaucratic
- conservative
- division-siloed
- examination-heavy
- slow to adapt curriculum
- focused on conventional professional pathways

### Reform model

- cross-Division education
- practical evaluation
- critical thinking
- research literacy
- teamwork
- documentation quality
- field-readiness
- medical/support awareness
- interdisciplinary problem solving
- broader professional pathways

The reform is incomplete when Arthur enters. That incompleteness is a continuing source of institutional conflict.

## 4. Internationalization

The Academy is nationally chartered but **internationally accessible**. Most students are from Asian countries and neighboring regions; a smaller number come from other regions.

International admissions may occur through:

- bilateral education agreements
- specialist scholarships
- transfer arrangements
- protected-student or humanitarian placements
- research-track admissions
- exchange programs

Foreign students are not automatically exceptional. Most are ordinary students with ordinary reasons for attending.

## 5. Cohort Diversity

The cohort can contain local students, students from other Asian countries, scholarship students, children of professionals, merchant families, medical families, research families, military/security families, politically displaced students, and ordinary students with no remarkable lineage.

At least one future recurring student may have been displaced from their home country because of political circumstances. Their history should be revealed gradually and should not be reduced to a simple good-government/bad-government explanation.

At least one future Medical/Support student may have substantial family knowledge of traditional Eastern medicine. This is practical cultural expertise, not automatic supernatural authority, and may coexist or conflict with modern medical training.

## 6. Cultural Balance

The Academy is **Asian-majority, internationally connected**.

Character design follows:

> **background → experience → worldview → behavior**

not nationality → personality.

Arthur remains British-coded and culturally grounded while the cohort contains multiple Asian and international backgrounds.

## 7. Academy Social Ecosystem

The Academy should feel like a functioning institution rather than a military barracks.

Cadets share common academic instruction, practical evaluations, Division-specific training, Team activities, residential life, research/library access, inter-Division exercises, and institutional assessments.

**COHORT = social/academic generation**  
**DIVISION = organizational/professional home**  
**TEAM = recurring working group**  
**JOINT TEAM = temporary mission formation**

## 8. Evaluation Culture

The reform broadens student evaluation. Relevant dimensions may include physical performance, combat application, observation, critical thinking, research, documentation, teamwork, problem solving, field judgment, and specialist competence.

These are institutional measurements, not RPG stats.

Arthur's asymmetric profile is therefore useful: physically capable, observant, analytical, weak in conventional Esper output, initially inexperienced in combat, and secretly developing a Contract System the Academy does not know exists. His active professional placement is **Operations Support — Field Support Specialist**, allowing the reform-era Academy to test his value through interdisciplinary exercises and eventual field assignments rather than confining him to an administrative role.

## 9. Reform Conflict

Reformists believe practical competence matters more than inherited prestige. Traditionalists believe the Academy's decline came from relaxing established standards. Neither side is universally correct.

The conflict should surface through instructors, curriculum changes, evaluation rules, funding, student selection, and inter-Academy comparisons rather than exposition dumps.

## 10. Long-Term World Expansion

World expansion proceeds in layers:

1. **Academy:** cohort, instructors, divisions, ranking, reform, rivalry.
2. **Other Academies:** stronger competitors, exchanges, different philosophies.
3. **Local institutions:** hospitals, research centers, businesses, government offices, field organizations.
4. **National systems:** regulators, funding, politics, national evaluation.
5. **International systems:** foreign institutions, multinational organizations, regional anomaly cooperation.

The story should earn each layer before introducing the next.

## 11A. Four-Year Cohort Progression — AUTHOR LOCK 2026-09-26

Ravenscroft Academy uses a four-year development structure. The cohorts are differentiated by professional maturity, not treated as simple power tiers:

1. **Year 1 — Foundation:** learn and safely perform fundamentals.
2. **Year 2 — Developing Specialization:** perform trained tasks reliably and develop professional identity.
3. **Year 3 — Field Readiness:** operate under supervised field conditions and carry greater operational responsibility.
4. **Year 4 — Professional Transition:** remain cadets while increasingly undertaking approved external placements, internships, supervised missions, research assignments, exchanges, and other professional-facing work.

Year 4 therefore has substantially lower routine campus presence than the earlier cohorts. This is a structural feature of the Academy, not a reason for fourth-year characters to disappear from the story.

The authoritative year-progression, campus-presence, competence, and character-generation rules are maintained in `33_ACADEMY_YEAR_PROGRESSION_AND_COHORT_STRUCTURE.md`.
