## §2. PURPOSE

The Sorting Board has already sorted Arthur: **Irregular / Operations Support / {Curriculum TBD} / Records & Archives / F / ERROR**. The conventional instruments are done with him. The Review exists because the military **hates wasting talent it has already bought** — the state paid to Awaken, register, house, and feed every cadet; an F-rank with a commander's brain is still cheaper than a new cadet. The Review answers one question for the file: *is this unclassifiable mind operationally valuable, and at what risk?* It answers it without touching the rank instrument — the decoupling lets the institution extract the value (his analysis) while withholding the price (rank, pay, access). The system is not being fair to him. It is being efficient.

## §3. STRUCTURE (adopted from the mechanics file, locked here)

- **Format:** one day, nine stations (§0 C-3's mechanics file holds the full designs). No channeling required at any station — this is a design constraint, not an accommodation: an F-rank must be able to sit the exam.
- **Output:** an **Aptitude profile** in **bands, not scores** (Band 1: exceptional → Band 5: below cohort mean); a composite Operational Aptitude Index in bands. False precision would invite rank-thinking; the institution refuses it.
- **Filing:** **sealed by default.** The full profile goes to the Sorting Board and the SMD liaison desk's quiet file. The cadet receives band summaries only — enough to be useful, not enough to be a weapon. The seal is what makes the rumor possible: "they say the Error's Tokurei file is sealed" becomes barracks gossip — institutional ambiguity, manufactured by procedure.
- **The doctrine sentence** (printed on the charter page): **"Rank measures what the Tide does through you. Aptitude measures what you do with what you notice."**

### §3.1 The three dimensions (the user's A/B/C)

The nine stations are grouped into the three conceptual dimensions the Review reports:

**A. COGNITIVE APTITUDE** — analytical reasoning, pattern recognition, information synthesis, contradiction detection.
*Stations:* 1 (the box — contradictory-source synthesis), 4 (the strips — signal detection with false-positive discipline), 5 (the cart — archive cross-indexing; **the planted discrepancy**).
*Institutional rubric:* can the cadet build a true account from hostile material, name what cannot be resolved, and notice what does not belong?

**B. OPERATIONAL APTITUDE** — decision-making under uncertainty, tactical reasoning, prioritization, resource allocation, consequence analysis.
*Stations:* 2 (sand-table — planning with a missing critical fact), 7 (the clock — a call under time pressure with stated error bars), 8 (degradation curve — what fails first under stress), 9 (the strangers — unled group planning).
*Institutional rubric:* can the cadet make a call with missing data, state the uncertainty honestly, and degrade gracefully?

**C. ANOMALY APTITUDE** — interpreting abnormal phenomena, distinguishing signal from noise, hypothesis formation, falsification, recognizing rule-like behavior in unexplained events.
*Stations:* 3 (the sealed files — state the phenomenon's Rule in falsifiable form), 6 (kill the favorite — design the cheapest test that destroys the examiner's held hypothesis).
*Institutional rubric:* can the cadet extract a falsifiable Rule from redacted material and attack a belief efficiently? **This is the strangest dimension** — the one with the thinnest calibration population, and the one the institution trusts least and needs most.

## §5. INSTITUTIONAL INTERPRETATION — what three offices do with the sealed file

- **The Intelligence Division observer: asset.** "Give him to analysis. He's a natural. The Records office is wasting him on filing carts — I want that discrepancy-flagging reflex pointed at real incidents." The first institutional *pull* on Arthur — gentle, flattering, a leash with better furniture. It moves nothing: the board already gets the asset at F-rank prices.
- **Hayes (faculty advisor, standing proctor): liability.** "Clever F-ranks get people killed. The Tokurei measures test-taking under supervision, not judgment under fire. File it and watch him." His contempt is professional and *earned* — the hardest position in the room to dismiss.
- **The Records & Archives supervisor: inventory.** "Good — keep him where he is. His reflex makes my office's output better, and I am not volunteering him upward." Institutional hoarding: the office that benefits from him has no incentive to release him — a quiet brake on the Intelligence observer's pull.
- **The Sorting Board: "observe."** Covering sentence: *"Exceptional aptitude does not revise measurement."* Aptitude exceptional; rank unchanged; re-examination in one year; the sealed file to the quiet desk.

**The informal outcome:** three offices now have a *reason* to watch the Error. The sealed file is a quiet magnet. Nothing happens fast — which is why, when things start happening, they happen *through* the watchers.

## §6. LIMITATIONS — what the Review cannot measure or conclude

1. **The ERROR causal boundary [LOAD-BEARING]:** the examination **cannot determine whether Arthur's aptitudes have any causal connection to his ERROR status.** The instruments measure output (analysis quality); they cannot see the mechanism. Whether the aptitude is *his* or *the ERROR's* is outside the charter — and outside the science. The result is **institutional ambiguity, not mystery solved.** "What exactly is Arthur?" remains unanswered, by design of the instrument, not by authorial evasion.
2. **Snapshot bias:** one day, supervised, no live stakes. The Review measures test-taking under supervision, not judgment under fire (Hayes's objection is legitimate).
3. **Command blindness:** the Review cannot distinguish "doesn't want the room" from "can't take the room" — the profile hedges, and the story should too.
4. **Calibration thinness on Dimension C:** the "insufficient reference data" annotation is honest — the institution has few subjects who do falsifiable Rule-extraction, so Band 1 on C is a flagged measurement, not a clean one. This is the seam future skeptics will attack.
5. **Band granularity is coarse by design:** bands refuse the false precision that would let anyone treat the profile as a second rank system.

## §7. CONNECTION TO T1/T2 — latent pressure, never promotion

The Review creates **no standing, no access, no authority.** What it creates is a *documented capacity* the institution can later cite without inventing justification:

- **Toward T1 (knock-adjacent safety bulletin):** when command needs an auditable bulletin only Arthur can author, the sealed file is what lets the paper trail say "the cadet's sealed aptitude profile (4 Sep 2024) documents the relevant capacity" instead of "we panicked and used the F-rank." The Review does not *trigger* T1; it makes T1's paperwork survivable.
- **Toward T2 (missions turning on his assessment):** when AARs start citing his pre-mission reads, the profile is the pre-existing institutional memory that lets observers say "we already knew" rather than "this came from nowhere." The No-Choice Bar remains the only thing that moves a rung.
- **The rule:** no chapter, no rung movement, no authority change is ever justified *by the Review alone*. The Review is the file; the field is the trigger.

- **Presiding:** the Deputy Commandant (Sorting) — the Academy's own authority, four weeks into the job, running inherited procedures by the book.
- **Examiners:** **SMD seconded examiners** (the Census's examiner corps — [INFERENCE] from ACADEMY_RESTRUCTURE §3.6's quiet-desk examiner loans [CANON-adjacent: 06_GOVERNMENTS]); **Lantern examiners** for the sealed-material stations (their methodology is the institution's).

- **Standing proctor:** Hayes (faculty advisor; 03_IRREGULAR_CLASSIFICATION.md) — the "liability" voice, on the record.
- **Materials:** real redacted incidents from the legacy pipeline — **standing constraint: none may touch the 9/18 UNDETERMINED** (fence).

The board's newness is texture, not incompetence: the examiners know their stations; the institution is still learning how to *file* the results. That filing-uncertainty is part of why Arthur's profile ends up sealed in the quiet desk rather than routed cleanly — procedure, not plot armor.

## §9. THE SCHOOLING MODEL [NEEDS DECISION]

**Locked facts:** DOB 20 Sep 2006; Academy entry 1 Sep 2024 (age 17, mid-final-school-year — British cohort graduates March 2025); First Standard Intake April 2025; Academy admits ages 15–18 (ACADEMY_RESTRUCTURE §4.1).

**Audit result:** no existing REBOOT_DESIGN canon and no bible canon states how the Academy handles unfinished secondary schooling. The bible's closest principle (15_EDUCATION, Albion's First Tide protocol) emphasizes *not* removing the child from schooling — continuity as care. The Academy's 15–18 admission window already implies it must handle schooling internally for the youngest entrants; nothing contradicts an integrated model.

**The three options:**

1. **Academy replaces ordinary schooling.** The state's conscription-education apparatus absorbs the cadet fully; no separate school.
2. **Dual enrollment.** The 17-year-old attends ordinary final-year school alongside cadet training — *rejected by the user*: it forces a two-institution subplot the story doesn't need.
3. **[RECOMMENDED] Integrated military + formal education institution.** The Academy carries accredited foundational education internally: **September 2024 – March 2025** = final academic/cadet cohort phase; **March 2025** = formal completion of foundational education (matching Albion's school-year end — diegetic elegance, and satisfying the bible's continuity-of-schooling principle by keeping schooling inside the institution); **after March 2025** = advanced military/operational training.

**Consequences of option 3:**
- The founding intake's March 2025 "graduation" is **foundational-education completion**, not Academy graduation (the Academy is four years; Arthur remains a Year-1/Year-2 cadet).
- The **April 2025 First Standard Intake** arrives as a new Year-1 cohort one month later — no conflict: the founding cohort proceeds to advanced training while the new cohort begins. The founding-vs-standard cohort social friction (already locked in T-17) gains an education-status dimension: the founding cadets are *ahead*, not merely earlier.
- No ordinary-school subplot is ever required; Arthur's schoolboy past survives as character DNA (futsal, port-road walks) without a dual-life mechanic.
- **What it costs:** the Academy must be written as carrying real accredited schooling for under-18 cadets — a line in the charter, not a subplot.

**Status: [LOCKED 2026-09-23]** — approved by the user's "LOCK 4 — FINAL" directive: integrated military + formal education; Mar 2025 = Foundational Education Completion (not Academy graduation).
