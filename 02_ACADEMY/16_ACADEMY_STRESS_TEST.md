## A. ACADEMY BECOMES A BORING SCHOOL STORY
**Directive Part XVI-A.**

**Manifestation in this design:** the academy's barracks, mess hall, and class schedule are vivid — and vividness is seductive. The failure looks like: quiet chapters multiplying into dorm comedy, festival arcs, food chapters, and training montages with no driver attached. The mission engine idles while the story "breathes." The Part XII loop stalls at ACADEMY and never reaches MISSION.

**Early-warning signs:** (1) an outline chapter whose driver count is zero (the 30-chapter plan requires ≥1, target ≥2 — a 0-driver chapter is already a breach); (2) two consecutive chapters with no anomaly-mystery or contract-progression movement; (3) "breather" used as a chapter's justification in the batch record without naming which driver the breather *pays*.

**Structural fix:** the **driver-quota rule** (directive Part XI, operationalized): every chapter plan must name its drivers (≥1, target ≥2); a chapter with 0 drivers is **cut at batch planning** — not rewritten, cut. Quiet chapters are budgeted, not improvised: the ≥2-per-10 quiet-chapter quota is a *ceiling* as well as a floor — a third quiet chapter in any 10-chapter batch requires naming, in the batch record, which plot chapter it replaces and why the ledger needs it.

## B. EVERY CHARACTER SECRETLY BECOMES OP
**Directive Part XVI-B.**

**Manifestation in this design:** the dark-horse engine (Part X) is the most tempting place to cheat. The failure looks like: every Irregular cadet turns out to have a hidden S-tier mode — the "useless" Ability was actually devastating, the unstable Awakening stabilizes into a trump card, the F-rank was mismeasured. Victories get re-read as hidden-stats reveals, and the synergy thesis ("never individually perfect") becomes a lie the text told. It also infects rivals: the elite rival who lost suddenly reveals a second Ability; the instructor was S-rank all along.

**Early-warning signs:** (1) an Irregular victory whose after-action review cannot cite the *combination* (which complementary weaknesses paired, whose aptitude covered whose gap); (2) any character's Ability description gaining new combat applications not present at introduction; (3) the division's win rate decoupling from preparation shown on-page (wins without the mapping work).

**Structural fix:** the **synergy ledger rule**: every Irregular victory's review must attribute the win to a *named combination* — cadet A's weakness covered by cadet B's aptitude, positioned by Arthur's mapping — never to an individual's hidden stat. An individual-power victory is **flagged as a design error at batch audit** and rewritten to combination or cut. Corollary: ALL Ability sheets (Irregular, rivals, instructors) are **locked at introduction** in the series bible; new applications require a CHANGELOG entry — the "hidden mode" becomes expensive to improvise. Applies academy-wide, not just to Irregular.

## C. IRREGULAR BECOMES A GENERIC UNDERDOG TEAM
**Directive Part XVI-C.**

**Manifestation in this design:** distinct from B (secretly OP) — here the members stay weak but become *interchangeable*: the plucky misfits who win through "heart," "teamwork," and "never giving up." The failure looks like: victories attributed to morale rather than method; cadets swappable without changing the outcome; the division's identity collapsing to "the lovable losers" — a sports-manga template wearing anomaly-fiction clothes.

**Early-warning signs:** (1) a victory outline that would work identically if any two roster members swapped roles; (2) the words "heart," "bonds," or "spirit" appearing as causal explanations in a batch document; (3) two consecutive Irregular wins using the same tactical shape.

**Structural fix:** the **non-interchangeability rule**: every roster member's §3 profile (03_IRREGULAR_CLASSIFICATION.md) names their *load-bearing function* — the specific thing only they can do. A mission plan must show which function each deployed cadet serves; a cadet deployed without their function engaged is **re-blocked at planning** (why are they there?). Victories must be *tactic-specific*: the after-action review names the tactic, and no tactic may repeat within 15 chapters (the face-slap matrix's column rule, extended to tactics). Morale is allowed as *fuel*, never as *cause*.

## D. ARTHUR SOLVES EVERYTHING ALONE
**Directive Part XVI-D.**

**Manifestation in this design:** Arthur's analysis is the sharpest tool in every room, so drafts let him carry every mission while teammates carry equipment. The failure looks like: method logs where only Arthur's entries contain decisions; a synergy victory re-staged as his solo plan executed by porters; teammates' aptitudes never the causal difference.

**Early-warning signs:** (1) a mission outline with no named decision attributed to a non-Arthur cadet; (2) the phrase "Arthur's plan" in a batch document without a teammate's load-bearing amendment; (3) after-action reviews where the aptitude-notes section is empty for everyone but him.

**Structural fix:** the **decision-quota**: every mission's after-action review must attribute **≥1 load-bearing decision to a non-Arthur cadet** — a decision without which the outcome changes. Arthur's analysis is *advisory* until the leadership phases earn command — and even then, the quota holds: leadership is *using* the team, not replacing it. A mission with zero non-Arthur decisions is flagged at batch audit and re-blocked.

## E. OTHER DIVISIONS BECOME CARTOONISHLY STUPID
**Directive Part XVI-E.**

**Manifestation in this design:** to make Irregular's wins satisfying, drafts make Combat/Recon/Tactical cadets arrogant *and* incompetent — charging blindly, ignoring their own doctrine, missing obvious clues. The failure looks like: rivals who exist to be wrong; instructors who teach nothing; elite methods that fail because the plot needs them to. Wins against idiots are not satisfying; they are condescending, and they teach the reader that the academy's hierarchy is fake — which kills the bullying's sting too.

**Early-warning signs:** (1) a rival's plan that their own division's training (06_DIVISION_SYSTEM.md) would have prevented; (2) an elite cadet with no on-page win to their name by ch30; (3) Arthur's analysis succeeding against a mistake no competent operator would make.

**Structural fix:** the **competence floor**: every named rival and every division must have **≥1 on-page win** (a mission, exercise, or deduction they carry cleanly) before Irregular beats them at anything that matters. Elite methods must be *sound* — Arthur wins by exploiting *conditions* (terrain, Rule-interference, information asymmetry), never by exploiting *stupidity*. The batch audit checks: "would this opponent's own manual endorse what they just did?" If yes and they still lost, the win is earned. If no, the opponent is rewritten, not the outcome.

## F. BULLYING BECOMES REPETITIVE
**Directive Part XVI-F.**

**Manifestation in this design:** the contempt beats that powered ch2–ch10 keep firing the same shape: a Combat cadet sneers "F-rank," Arthur stays quiet, the reader waits. The failure looks like: the same actor × same structure × same corridor, chapter after chapter; contempt as wallpaper. (Distinct from mode I's treadmill: this is *repetition of the contempt itself*, not of the reversal cycle.)

**Early-warning signs:** (1) the same (actor × bullying-form) cell firing twice within 15 chapters; (2) contempt beats with no new information (no new cost, no new witness, no new institutional consequence); (3) readers being able to predict the contempt scene's blocking before it starts.

**Structural fix:** the **contempt-scheduling rule** (18_SOCIAL_HIERARCHY_PROGRESSION.md §3, operationalized): the 10 bullying forms rotate on a compelled schedule — no (actor × form) cell repeats within 15 chapters, no form repeats within 8; every contempt beat must carry a *new* cost, witness, or institutional consequence (if nothing new is at stake, the beat is cut, not repeated). The 7-stage evolution (CONTEMPT → … → FEAR/STRATEGIC CONCERN) is the macro-guardrail: contempt that has not *evolved* in form within 10 chapters is flagged as stalled and re-blocked at the next stage.

## G. MIXED TEAMS BECOME RANDOM AND MEANINGLESS
**Directive Part XVI-G.**

**Manifestation in this design:** the mixed-division promise decays into "grab five names": teams with no requirement logic, Irregular included as a token, divisions present but functionally absent. The failure looks like: a Medical cadet with nothing medical to do; a Recon cadet whose recon never matters; team composition that could be reshuffled without changing the mission. The engine's whole point — *forced cooperation across institutional cultures* — evaporates.

**Early-warning signs:** (1) a mission outline where a deployed division's function is never engaged; (2) team composition with no stated requirement logic in the batch record; (3) Irregular inclusion with no trigger named (inclusion as fiat).

**Structural fix:** the **requirement-logic rule** (12_MIXED_MISSION_SYSTEM.md §§2–4, operationalized): every team is generated from a *mission requirement profile* → *division slot template* → *selection rules* (rotation quotas, competence-match, political placement, instructor veto, and one of the five Irregular-inclusion triggers — records-flag, aptitude-match, expendability posting, instructor request, incident overflow — stated aloud at briefing). A team with an unengaged division or an untriggered Irregular is **re-blocked at planning**. The anti-formula is structural: the requirement profile changes the template every mission, so composition cannot default.

## H. DIVISION RIVALRY OVERWHELMS ANOMALY MYSTERY
**Directive Part XVI-H.**

**Manifestation in this design:** the rivalry engine is fun to write — scores, ledgers, humiliated rivals — and it eats the mystery. The failure looks like: three consecutive chapters of pure inter-division maneuvering with the anomaly as set dressing; the Part XII loop stalling at NEW RIVALRY and never cycling back to ANOMALY; readers tracking the leaderboard instead of the phenomenon.

**Early-warning signs:** (1) two consecutive chapters advancing rivalry with zero anomaly-mystery movement; (2) a rivalry beat whose stakes are purely reputational with no mystery payload attached; (3) the anomaly of the arc solvable without any division's involvement.

**Structural fix:** the **rivalry budget + pairing rule**: rivalry may consume **at most half** the chapters of any arc's middle movement, and every rivalry beat must be *paired* with a mystery payload (a clue, a Rule-interference, a filing consequence) — rivalry scenes are where information *moves*, not where it pauses. The Part XII loop order is enforced at batch audit: any arc whose loop stalls at RIVALRY without reaching ANOMALY is re-blocked. The reputation ledger (FILE 7 §5.5) is the permitted scoreboard *because* its entries are filed next to mission reports — rivalry and mystery share the same paperwork.

## I. ANOMALY MYSTERY OVERWHELMS CHARACTER DEVELOPMENT
**Directive Part XVI-I.**

**Manifestation in this design:** the inverse of H — the phenomena are fascinating, the Rule-deduction is intricate, and the cadets become camera operators for the mystery. The failure looks like: a five-chapter investigation where no relationship changes, no roster member's arc moves, Arthur's leadership phases stall; readers can summarize the anomaly but not what it *cost* anyone.

**Early-warning signs:** (1) an investigation arc with no per-cadet arc movement in the batch record; (2) Arthur's leadership phase unchanged across 15 chapters; (3) a solved anomaly that leaves every relationship exactly where it was.

**Structural fix:** the **character-driver quota**: every mystery chapter must advance ≥1 *character* driver (character progression or relationship development — two of the six Part-XI drivers), not just the mystery driver. The batch plan names, per chapter, whose arc moves and how. The dark-horse arc's phase-gating (05_IRREGULAR_DARK_HORSE_ARC.md) is the macro-enforcement: division phases cannot advance on mystery results alone — each phase transition requires a *named relationship or leadership event* (a voluntary following, a trust extended, a cost absorbed for a teammate).

## J. CONTRACT SYSTEM BECOMES DISCONNECTED FROM ACADEMY STORY
**Directive Part XVI-J.**

**Manifestation in this design:** the academy plot (divisions, rivalry, missions) and the Contract plot (Seal, knock, costs) run as two serials sharing a protagonist. The failure looks like: five academy chapters with no Contract movement and no Seal presence; the knock usable only "off-campus"; Contract costs never intersecting academy stakes (grades, assignments, reputation).

**Early-warning signs:** (1) a 5-chapter stretch with no Contract-progression driver; (2) an invocation whose cost is payable entirely outside academy stakes; (3) the Seal unmentioned for >10 chapters.

**Structural fix:** the **integration rule** (12_MIXED_MISSION_SYSTEM.md §6, operationalized): "the Seal hunts where the system misfiles him" — academy missions are *the* Contract-acquisition surface, not a separate track. Every arc must contain ≥1 Contract-progression beat *priced in academy currency* (a cost paid in access, standing, or filing exposure — never abstract). The knock's presence-only rule is the designed bridge: using it inside the academy's witnessed, graded, filed environment is itself the cost (the knock-witness problem, FILE 7 §6). Contract beats are scheduled in the 30-chapter plan, not left to "when the mystery needs one."

## K. ARTHUR BECOMES TOO RESPECTED TOO QUICKLY
**Directive Part XVI-K.**

**Manifestation in this design:** the reversals work *too* well — by ch12 the Combat elites are asking his opinion, instructors defer to him, the contempt evaporates. The failure looks like: stage-skipping on the 7-stage evolution (CONTEMPT → RESPECT in one arc); sealed exam results leaking into corridor gossip; his F-rank treated as a technicality everyone winks at. The undervalue engine loses its fuel chapters early.

**Early-warning signs:** (1) a contempt-actor converting before their second contempt beat; (2) respect expressed as *rank-talk* ("he's really an A") rather than *access* ("give him the file"); (3) the 7-stage evolution advancing >2 stages in one arc.

**Structural fix:** the **stage-gate rule**: the 7-stage evolution (18_SOCIAL_HIERARCHY_PROGRESSION.md §4) advances **at most one stage per arc**, gated by a *priced* visible event — and respect is denominated in *access*, never rank (two-ladder doctrine). The exam's sealed-by-default results are the structural brake: the institution *cannot* respect what it cannot file, so corridor respect stays deniable and reversible. Any draft showing stage-skipping is re-blocked to the correct stage; the skipped stage's event must still be *earned* on-page before advancing.

## L. ARTHUR BECOMES TOO WEAK FOR READERS TO REMAIN INVESTED
**Directive Part XVI-L.**

**Manifestation in this design:** the flip side of K — the F-rank denials, the sealed exam, the staged contempt all work, and Arthur spends fifteen chapters being denied, dismissed, and filed away. The failure looks like: no demonstration beats; the reader's competence-hunger starved; "trust the slow burn" as the only promise. Undervaluation without demonstration is just bullying the protagonist.

**Early-warning signs:** (1) >6 consecutive chapters with no DEMONSTRATION beat (Part XIV cycle); (2) the operational ladder showing no visible rung movement across an arc; (3) beta-reader shorthand: "when does he *do* something?"

**Structural fix:** the **demonstration quota**: every 5-chapter window must contain ≥1 demonstration beat — not a reversal, not a victory, just *visible competence* (a correct call, a filed discrepancy, a Rule named before anyone else). The operational ladder (Part XIII) is the visible progression instrument: Arthur enters at rung 1 (Records Cadet), so he *progresses* operationally from ch1 while his rank letter never moves. **Honest gap:** the first formal rung movement (Records Cadet → Anomaly Analyst) is not yet scheduled in the 30-chapter plan — the plan has unofficial-leadership phases (ch22) but no filed rung change. The rung movement must be placed at chapter planning (a natural home: the ch23 review or the ch30 ledger), denominated in access and responsibility, never in rank. Weakness must always be *priced weakness with visible method* — the reader invests in the method, not the suffering.

## M. ACADEMY POLITICS BECOMES FILLER
**Directive Part XVI-M.**

**Manifestation in this design:** board meetings, instructor maneuvering, SMD liaison memos, budget fights — all correctly institutional, all dramatically inert. The failure looks like: political chapters that change nothing on any ledger; politics as *texture* ("the academy is political, you see") rather than *mechanism*; readers skipping to the mission chapters.

**Early-warning signs:** (1) a political beat whose removal changes no score, assignment, resource, or relationship; (2) politics discussed only by instructors/officials with no cadet stakes attached; (3) two consecutive political chapters with no mission or mystery consequence.

**Structural fix:** the **ledger-movement rule**: every political beat must move ≥1 filed ledger — mission scores, equipment priority, division reputation, an assignment, a budget line, a personnel file. Politics is *how the academy allocates reality*; a political scene that allocates nothing is cut. The corollary: political beats are always *paired* with a cadet-stake (whose deployment, whose equipment, whose file) — politics is never staged above the cadets' heads. The reputation ledger (FILE 7 §5.5) is the audit instrument: if the ledger didn't move, the politics was filler.

## SUPPLEMENTARY MODES N–T (Worker 4's reconstructions — kept, condensed)

These name real risks the directive implies but does not enumerate. Each keeps the worker's original diagnosis in brief form.

**N. CONVENTIONAL-ESPER ARTHUR (was C).** He wins by output, not understanding — the identity rule violated in spirit while the rank letter stays F. **Fix:** the output-null rule — his Esper output is mechanically zero; any draft beat where his output decides an outcome fails chapter audit and is rewritten to understanding-leverage; bodily capability capped at evasion/endurance/positioning, every bodily victory exposure-priced.

**O. RANK-LADDER COLLAPSE (was D).** The two ladders merge — recognition arrives as rank-talk. **Fix:** recognition as access, never rank; any change to his rank letter requires a CHANGELOG entry (expensive by design); re-exams return ERROR on schedule.

**P. SANITIZED HIERARCHY (was E).** Contempt softened to keep the academy pleasant — the face-slap engine fires blanks. **Fix:** contempt quota + matrix compulsion (no actor×structure cell repeats within 40 chapters); every contempt beat names its cost.

**Q. DELAYED FIRST ANOMALY (was F).** The entrance arc becomes orientation. **Fix:** the tripwire pair (revised per the Organizational Revision's ch1–7 pacing) — by ch5, the **impossible record** must be on the page (the records-mystery payload); by ch6, the **anomaly clue** (the Seal's pointing) must have fired. If either is missing at its chapter, the batch plan is rejected at planning, not revised.

**R. HOLLOW F-RANK (was K).** The label has no teeth — denials never enforced. **Fix:** the denial ledger — every arc contains ≥1 concrete, filed denial with its cost shown.

**S. EXAM-AS-RANK-UP (was L).** The Classification Exam treated as a promotion path. **Fix:** the exam's charter prints "no rank effect" diegetically; the exam board has no rank jurisdiction.

**T. SEAL/CONTRACT SOFTENING (was M).** Invocation costs waived for academy convenience. **Fix:** per-arc price audit; the epistemic invariant (no conclusion from a contract rather than the loop); felt-certainty must bite on-page ≥1 per arc.
