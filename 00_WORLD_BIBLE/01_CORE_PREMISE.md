## Why civilization hasn't collapsed

> CANON: Civilization endures because of seven interlocking facts, not one miracle:

1. **Rarity.** ~96â€“98% of humanity has a strong **Veil Reflex** (called "the Quiet" in civilian slang): a subconscious cognitive immune response that rationalizes, degrades, or deletes memories of Seep exposure within hours. Most people literally cannot retain what they saw.
2. **Rot.** Recordings of active Seeps degrade â€” "static rot." Undertow-structured information does not persist cleanly in Membrane media. The stronger the Seep, the faster the footage corrupts.
3. **Noise.** Since the 1990s, intelligence-adjacent media operations have flooded the internet with fake paranormal content. Real leaks drown in ten thousand fakes. Since ~2020, the **deepfake dividend** has done the rest: nobody trusts video anymore, which is the best secrecy ally ever invented.
4. **Weakness of the median Seep.** The overwhelming majority of Seeps are T0â€“T2: harmless, useless, or merely strange. Civilization-threatening Seeps are rare and draw immediate multi-faction response.
5. **Eighty years of infrastructure.** Since the 1949 Meridian Accords, a global apparatus exists: the Seismograph detection network, Sweeper teams, Quiet Insurance, containment engineering, the Meridian Compact's arbitration courts. This machine has had three generations to professionalize.
6. **Erosion.** Channeling power destroys the channeler. The strongest Attuned burn out fastest â€” a built-in ceiling on any individual's ability to dominate. There is no immortal god-king because the price of godhood is self-dissolution ("Drowning").
7. **No adversary.** The Undertow does not want anything. There is no intelligent enemy trying to end the world â€” only pressure, and humans exploiting it. (Whether this is *true* is a Layer-5 mystery; see `33_MYSTERIES.md`. What every expert *believes* is that there is no adversary.)

> STATUS: THEORY (in-world expert consensus): "The masquerade can hold indefinitely with sufficient funding." A growing minority of analysts inside the Compact privately believe it cannot survive the High Tide.

## Tone and atmosphere

- **Oppressive hidden organizations** (Black Haze register): the world is administered by tired, competent, morally gray bureaucracies that file paperwork about impossible things.
- **Abilities with costs** (Darker than Black register): every power is a contract with an installment plan, and the installments are paid in selfhood. Operators are professionals, not heroes.
- **Horror inside working life** (workplace-horror register): the supernatural exists next to shift schedules, rent, insurance claims, and office politics. A containment operation can close your street while you are buying coffee two blocks away.

## AUTHOR-APPROVED SYSTEM INTEGRATION — 2026-09-26

The Academy now uses an official **Academy Performance Record (APR)** and cumulative **Cohort Points** system. This does not replace the separate Esper Rank system. APR measures demonstrated cadet performance across theory, physical ability, Resonance control, combat, field operations, teamwork, practical assignments, and sanctioned exercises. Cohort standings are socially visible and may be used for team selection, exercise seeding, and inter-Academy representation.

**Esper Rank remains separate.** It is system-generated, formally reassessed once per academic year, and cannot be manually changed by instructors or administrators. Personnel may report discrepancies or request review but cannot assign a replacement rank.

Arthur's Rank F / ERROR / UNDEFINED / Irregular status is therefore an institutional classification problem, not a teacher's personal decision. His later improvement may make his actual combat capability dramatically exceed what his Rank suggests. Annual reassessment can move the official Rank only when the classification system itself produces a different result. A later Rank E result does not imply that Arthur's practical capability is merely E-level.

**Resonance integration:** Awakened Espers can use Internal Resonance to reinforce the body and weapons, while individual Abilities may also express externally. Arthur's early Esper combat expression is primarily internal enhancement of physical movement and weapon handling; his dagger remains his primary weapon. External Ability output is weak or absent in the early Academy period.

**System-interference mystery:** Arthur's private Contract System may interfere with the classification infrastructure, producing the ERROR / UNDEFINED state. The exact causal relationship is unresolved and must not be explained early. The classification terminal may visibly return an error without the Academy's entire software infrastructure being generally defective.
