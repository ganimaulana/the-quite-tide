# 30 — POWER HIERARCHY: T0–T7 in Practical Terms

> CANON: Tier measures **maximum sustainable output** — nothing else. Not skill, not danger, not Erosion rate, not intelligence, not preparation. This file is the practical manual: what each tier can and cannot do, what power *costs* at each tier, and — the load-bearing section — **how the weak survive the strong**, because the entire novel runs on that mechanic. System foundations in [04_POWER_SYSTEM.md](04_POWER_SYSTEM.md).

## 30.1 The tiers, practically

| Tier | Name | Can (sustained) | Cannot | The one-line truth |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T0 | Quiet | Everything ordinary humans can do — including killing an Attuned with a rifle | Channel deliberately | The tier that wins wars: 8 billion people, logistics, law |
| T1 | Spark | Party tricks; light a cigarette with a thought; sense pressure; minor mending | Anything decisive; anything unnoticed at scale | Inconvenient or charming; the hidden world's plankton |
| T2 | Lantern | Professional-grade work: read a room's Tide-marks, shatter a door, heal a fracture, calm a crowd, wade briefly | Decide a battle; act at distance; sustain output for hours | The working class of the hidden world — 90,000 professionals |
| T3 | Surge | Decide a firefight or a boardroom; breach wards; slip across a city; steer a room's emotions | Act without being Seismograph-visible; fight an army; hide the cost | Registered, watched, licensed — power with paperwork |
| T4 | Tempest | National-asset effects: weather-working a county, leveling a building, crowd-scale suggestion, intercity slip | Sustain it (minutes, not hours); survive the response; outrun the Erosion bill | Every use is a career decision |
| T5 | Cataclysm | City-scale consequences; a single afternoon can redraw a map | Do it twice; do it secretly; survive the decade | ~40 alive; rarely past 50; every use is a geopolitical event |
| T6 | Worldtide | Continental consequences (three confirmed alive — CHAR-009/010/011 per Drafter A's registry: Okonkwo, Reyes, Shirakawa) | Anything without Compact observation; anything without dying by inches — all past 70 Fathoms | Living deterrents; the weapons that degrade themselves |
| T7 | Unknown | Reserved for "we don't know what that was" | Be confirmed (0 confirmed) | The designation exists because it has been needed |

> CANON: **Tier is not destiny in a fight.** Raw tier wins *unprepared* encounters. Preparation is the great equalizer — which is why intelligence, not output, is the true currency of the hidden world.

### What tier can't buy (hard limits, all tiers)

- **Time.** No tier alters the past ([02_WORLD_RULES.md](02_WORLD_RULES.md)). Causal bubbles (line-of-sight, minutes, ruinous Erosion) are the ceiling, and only some disciplines reach it.
- **Minds.** No tier performs direct mind control. A T6 Choir can steer a *crowd's* emotions; it cannot command an *individual's* obedience — suggestion seeding remains resistible, backlash-prone, and unreliable at every tier.
- **Permanence.** Borrowed matter reverts ≤72h at every tier. A T5 can build you a bridge; it cannot build you an empire.
- **Self.** No tier reverses Erosion — not even (especially not) on oneself. The healer's paradox: aggressive Bone healing *transfers* Erosion-risk to the healer.

## 30.2 Erosion economics per tier

> CANON: Output scales linearly; Erosion scales **exponentially past ~40 Fathoms**. The table below is the Compact medical directorate's actuarial summary — the numbers every handler, insurer, and general knows by heart.

| Tier | Typical cost per serious use | Sustainable career | What "one fight" costs | Life expectancy (active) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Negligible (<0.1 Fathom) | Decades; most never notice | Nothing | Normal |
| T2 | 0.5–2 Fathoms | 15–25 years of professional work at discipline hygiene | A bad week of migraines | Near-normal with care |
| T3 | 3–8 Fathoms | 8–15 years; the licensing tier exists because of this row | Months of recovery; visible thinning | Reduced; most retire by 55 |
| T4 | 10–25 Fathoms | 3–8 years at operational tempo; then desk, clinic, or Drowning | A year of self, minimum | Rarely past 60 active |
| T5 | 25–60 Fathoms | 1–3 major uses *total* past 60 Fathoms; the cruel arithmetic | Possibly everything | Rarely past 50 |
| T6 | 40+ Fathoms | Measured in *uses*, not years — all three confirmed are past 70 | The afternoon, and then the long dying | Dying by inches under observation |

**The three economic laws:**

1. **Peak output is cheap to buy and ruinous to use.** A T4 *can* level a building. The question their handler asks is never "can you" but "is the building worth a year of you."
2. **Endurance beats peak in every engagement longer than ninety seconds.** Conventional forces, wards, and prepared T2s win by *lasting* — the strong burn, the weak persist. This is why militaries still matter ([04_POWER_SYSTEM.md](04_POWER_SYSTEM.md)).
3. **Information is cheaper than Fathoms.** A Lantern T2 who knows a T4's Rule spends *zero* Fathoms to spoil a channeling worth 20. The entire hidden economy prices knowledge above output ([02_WORLD_RULES.md](02_WORLD_RULES.md)).

## 30.3 How the weak survive the strong

> CANON: Seven mechanisms. Every "weak defeats strong" scene in the novel must use at least one; the best use three.

**1. Rule interference.** Resonances interact through *conditions*, not power levels ([04_POWER_SYSTEM.md](04_POWER_SYSTEM.md)). Every ability has a Rule with requirements — line-of-sight, a name, a threshold, an exception clause. Attack the condition, not the channeler: break line-of-sight, flood the area with Tide-noise, trigger the exception. A T2 who has read the file beats a T4 who hasn't read the room.

**2. Preparation.** The strong improvise; the weak *prepare*. Wards take hours to raise and seconds to matter. Stillwater gas, Anchor restraints, documented Tide-marks, a building's blueprints, the target's schedule — preparation converts tier difference into a solved equation. The Compact's internal proverb: *"A T4 with a plan beats a T5 with a mood."*

**3. Terrain.** Thin places, warded zones, and mundane geography all favor the defender who chose them. A warded containment cell shreds unrecognized Slipping arrivals; a stairwell with a counting Rule eats the thirteenth pursuer regardless of tier. Home ground is a force multiplier the classification system doesn't measure.

**4. Tide-noise.** Artificial pressure static jams detection *and* channeling. Military-grade noise generators are the great equalizer of the modern battlefield (proven 2012, [EVENT-074](25_TIMEmessaging app.md)): a T0 squad with a jammer fights a T3 on nearly even terms for ninety seconds — and ninety seconds is usually enough.

**5. Information advantage.** Knowing the enemy's Rule, Fathom depth, handler, family, and schedule is worth more than two tiers. Lantern work, records work, *clerk* work — Arthur's whole survival strategy is this mechanism. The Ledger (INTL-003) prices it; the Red Ledger (SUP-004) auctions it.

**6. Erosion attrition.** The strong are on clocks; the weak are not. Every minute a T4 spends at full output is a minute off their life. Strategy: *don't win — last*. Make them spend. The Drowned leave Seeps; the patient inherit the field.

**7. Social and legal constraints.** The strong live in the world too. A T5 has a mother, a bank account, a Census file, a handler with a phone number. Choir use without a license is prison in 140+ countries; unregistered T3+ channeling is Seismograph-visible globally within minutes. The law is a leash the weak can pull — Arthur's documentation is meaningful precisely because the Tribunal (INTL-002) exists.

> CANON: These mechanisms are *why* the setting supports long-form storytelling without power escalation collapsing it. The ceiling is not "a bigger punch" — it is *a better-prepared nobody*.

## 30.4 Worked scenarios

### Scenario 1 — Prepared T2 vs arrogant T4 (the classic)
**Setup:** A T4 Tide debt-collector (67 Fathoms, accustomed to obedience) comes for a T2 Lantern archivist who has had three days' warning.
**The weak's play:** The archivist (a) floods her office with a commercial Tide-noise generator (mechanism 4), (b) breaks the Tide's line-of-sight Rule with a maze of bookshelves and a documented exception — the collector's Resonance requires *seeing* the target's hands (mechanism 1; she read his Tribunal file — mechanism 5), (c) fights from a warded reading room (mechanism 3).
**The strong's failure:** The collector burns 15 Fathoms in four minutes trying to force channeling through noise, hits nothing, and faces the arithmetic: one more push risks 80+ and pre-Drowning. He leaves. He will bill the client for the Fathoms.
**Lesson:** *Arrogance is an Erosion accelerant.* The T4 lost to paperwork.

### Scenario 2 — T0 Loud clerk vs T3 Choir interrogator
**Setup:** A licensed Choir T3 (corporate security) "interviews" a Loud records clerk about a leak. Direct mind control is impossible; suggestion seeding is the tool.
**The weak's play:** The clerk (a) *knows* he's being seeded — awareness is resistance ([02_WORLD_RULES.md](02_WORLD_RULES.md)); (b) writes everything down in real time, in triplicate, timestamps it — external memory the Choir cannot edit (mechanism 5 + 7); (c) reminds the interrogator, politely, that unlicensed Choir use is prison in 140+ countries and that *this interview is being logged by his union rep* (mechanism 7 — the licensed interrogator has more to lose).
**The strong's failure:** Seeding produces backlash — the interrogator leaves with a distorted echo of his own implanted impulse and a headache. The clerk leaves with notes.
**Lesson:** *Stubbornness is a countermeasure.* The file is armor.

### Scenario 3 — Sweeper team (T0s + one T1) vs a T4 Object Seep
**Setup:** "The Gilded Cradle" (ANOMALY-015, object/T4, per Drafter C's registry) — the Erosion-transfer Rule: an infant placed in the cradle sleeps one year and wakes carrying the rocker's transferred Fathoms; eleven uses, eleven children, and a Tribunal standing prohibition on a twelfth. A desperate T4 (71 Fathoms, six months from Drowning) has stolen a *twelfth* infant and is rocking the cradle in a Jakarta apartment block.
**The weak's play:** The Sweepers don't fight the object — they fight its *Rule*. (a) The Tribunal prohibition order makes the *use* the crime — secure the perimeter and invoke the order by radio before entering (legal mechanism; the rocker is now committing a Tribunal offense, not a rescue); (b) the Rule needs an infant *placed* and a rocker who *sleeps the full hour and wakes* — deny the placement: evacuate the infant's family first, so there is no second placement if the first is interrupted (mechanism 1); (c) the transfer completes only when the rocker *wakes* — interrupt the rocking before the hour completes: cut power, flood the flat with light and noise, drag the rocker out mid-sleep (mechanism 2+3); (d) document the Rule for the Tribunal's containment desk — the interrupted twelfth use is the evidence the prohibition order needs for renewal.
**Outcome:** Zero casualties, one contained object, one arrested rocker, one infant who never slept. A very boring after-action report. Sweepers consider boring reports the highest art.
**Lesson:** *The exterminator model.* Professionals don't duel anomalies; they process them. The Tribunal's prohibition order is a weapon the Sweepers can pick up and use.

### Scenario 4 — T5 vs a city (why the T5 loses)
**Setup:** A grieving T5 Tide (88 Fathoms) decides a city's government will answer for a coverup. Maximum output, no restraint.
**What happens:** Minute 0–3: catastrophic local effects; the Seismograph network lights up globally. Minute 3–10: the Joint Containment Command (INTL-006) declares; Tide-noise saturation begins; Drowndust munitions are authorized (banned, stockpiled, used — [EVENT-083](25_TIMEmessaging app.md) is the precedent everyone pretends isn't). Minute 10–30: the T5's Erosion curve goes vertical — 88 to 100 is *one afternoon* at maximum output. The city survives because it is *large* and *patient*; the T5 Drowns, usually before the army even arrives, leaving a new Seep the Sweepers will process by Thursday.
**The deeper loss:** The T5's family lived in that city. Their Census file is now a memorial. Social constraints (mechanism 7) are why this scenario is rare — the strong know the arithmetic.
**Lesson:** *A T5 can't fight long.* Endurance beats peak; the city is the ultimate weak survivor.

### Scenario 5 — Arthur (T0, Loud, Laggard) vs a T2 Hollow tracker
**Setup:** A Cartographer-contracted T2 Hollow is paid to follow Arthur and report his routine. Hollow tracking: reading his "wake" through thin places.
**The weak's play:** Arthur (a) has read the tracker's discipline from NQA files — Hollow tracking needs *his wake*, which the Laggard *eats* (the tether drinks ambient information; his wake is 3 seconds stale and wrong — mechanism 1, accidental); (b) prepares terrain: he walks his route through a warded old-quarter in Ravenhurst, where unrecognized Hollow work shreds (mechanism 3); (c) when cornered, steps into the lag — 3 seconds out-of-phase, unseen — and simply *leaves* (mechanism: the anomaly, §B.11 use 5).
**The strong's failure:** The tracker reports that the target "has no wake" — and bills for a wasted week. She never sees the shadow step.
**Lesson:** *The Laggard's weakness is camouflage.* Being filed T1 is the best ward Arthur owns.

### Scenario 6 — T4 vs a corporation (the foreclosure)
**Setup:** A T4 Bone enforcer decides Halcyon Dynamics (CORP-001) owes him a debt. He can win any fight in the building.
**The corporation's play:** Halcyon doesn't fight. It (a) freezes his accounts via Ledger (INTL-003) compliance flags (mechanism 7); (b) leaks his Census file and Fathom depth to the Tribunal's press desk — *registered, 71 Fathoms, unlicensed Bone augmentation* (mechanism 5+7); (c) offers his handler a better contract; (d) waits. The enforcer can win any *fight*. There is no fight. There is paperwork, and then there is a cell.
**Lesson:** *Institutions are the apex predator of individuals.* Erosion kills the strong; bureaucracy buries them.
