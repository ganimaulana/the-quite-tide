# 02 — WORLD RULES

These are the load-bearing rules. Everything in this bible — every faction plan, every ability, every economy — must obey them. Rules marked CANON-LOCKED cannot be changed without a CHANGELOG entry explaining the contradiction that forced the change.

## I. Metaphysical hard rules (CANON-LOCKED)

> CANON-LOCKED: **No true resurrection.** Death collapses a person's pattern. Anything that "returns" from the Undertow wearing a dead person's shape is an **echo**: it has memories up to the point of pattern-capture, but it is not the person. Echoes degrade. Every faction's science and every religion's theology has independently confirmed this at least once, usually at terrible cost.

> CANON-LOCKED: **No permanent created matter.** Matter "borrowed" from the Undertow **reverts** within 72 hours (usually much sooner for complex structures). You can build a bridge of borrowed steel to escape a burning building; you cannot build a borrowed-steel empire.

> CANON-LOCKED: **The past cannot be changed.** The Undertow has no time. Its "memories" of the past — **Tide-marks** — are impressions only: viewable, never editable. Any ability, ritual, or anomaly claiming to alter the past is either viewing Tide-marks, creating a local causal bubble (see below), or lying.

> CANON-LOCKED: **No direct mind control.** A mind is a Membrane phenomenon; the Undertow cannot grip it directly. What *is* possible is **suggestion seeding** through the informational layer: planting impulses the target experiences as their own thoughts. It is resisted by awareness, strong identity, training, and (ironically) stubbornness; it always produces **backlash** — the seeder experiences a distorted echo of the implanted impulse. **Mass reliable mind control is logistically impossible; per-link unreliability compounds geometrically, and each link pays backlash.**

> CANON-LOCKED: **No power copying.** A Resonance is a unique neural pattern. "Copying" an ability requires consuming the source's pattern — i.e., killing them in a specific, difficult, Drowning-adjacent manner — and even then the thief's Erosion accelerates catastrophically. There are no power-sponges walking around with forty abilities. (There are three corpses in Compact vaults that prove why.)

> CANON-LOCKED: **Patterns can only be captured during collapse.** The theft above is possible only in the moment of pattern collapse (the Drowning-adjacent window); a stable, living pattern cannot be taken, sampled, or siphoned. The procedure requires a **T3+ Lantern examiner**, is **Seismograph-visible** (it reads as a T4+ event), and is lawful only with the **subject's consent or a Tribunal warrant** — without one it is murder and pattern-theft, the two charges the Tribunal has never once declined to prosecute.

> CANON-LOCKED: **Slipping (teleportation) has anchors.** Crossing the Undertow to relocate is possible, but arrival requires **recognition**: you can only arrive somewhere you have been, or somewhere held strongly and accurately in the living memory of **someone who knows you** — *someone* meaning **another living person**, not yourself, not an echo, not a recording. You cannot slip into a vault you have never seen, a country you cannot picture, or "behind" a person. Each slip costs Erosion proportional to distance and unfamiliarity. Warded/contained zones shred unrecognized arrivals. **Hardened wards shred all non-whitelisted arrivals** — recognized or not, the ward checks the whitelist, not the memory. And **recognition decays**: arrival-grade memory fades with time and inaccuracy — a place you visited twenty years ago and barely remember is not an anchor. **Slip-courier chains** (relaying cargo Slip-to-Slip) are legal but **economically dead**: each link pays the full Erosion cost of its leg, and the chain's price compounds past any cargo's value within three hops — except for emergencies, where the price is the point.

> CANON-LOCKED: **Erosion is irreversible.** Channeling wears the Veil. Stillness practices, drugs, and rest can *slow* further Erosion; nothing reverses it. At 100 Fathoms the user **Drowns** — pattern dissolution, usually leaving a new Seep behind.

> CANON-LOCKED: **Causal bubbles are small, brief, and expensive.** Local time dilation/acceleration (a room where seconds stretch) by **Attuned channeling** is possible. It cannot extend beyond line-of-sight scale, cannot last more than minutes, and costs Erosion at a ruinous rate. There are no time-loop fortresses.

> CANON-LOCKED: **Anomaly Rules may exceed the Attuned channeling caps — and inherit proportional costs.** A Seep's Rule can sustain a larger or longer causal bubble than any Attuned could, but the excess is priced into the anomaly itself: larger Tide pressure, wider Seismograph visibility, faster Undertow attention, harder containment. Nothing gets the effect for free; the bill is just denominated differently.

## II. Economic hard rules (CANON-LOCKED)

> CANON-LOCKED: **Anomalies are non-fungible.** Every Seep is unique. There is no commodity market in "anomaly units" — only auctions of specific items with specific Rules. This single fact prevents most infinite-wealth schemes: you cannot scale what you cannot reproduce.

> CANON-LOCKED: **The Seismograph network sees large Tide events.** Any channeling above ~T3, any unregistered Slipping, any active T4+ Seep triggers detectors on every continent within minutes. Unregistered large-scale use is therefore *visible* — the question is only who responds first.

> CANON-LOCKED: **Borrowed wealth reverts.** Because created matter reverts (Rule I), no fortune can be built on conjured gold, conjured oil, or conjured anything. Financial-precognition abilities exist at small scales and are the reason every major exchange runs Lantern-screened compliance — and the reason "lucky" traders keep Drowning.

> CANON: **Licensed conjuration contracting is a real trade.** Temporary tooling — formwork, molds, scaffolding, shoring — conjured to shape *real* matter is legal under license in 60+ signatories: it is Seismograph-visible at scale, Erosion-priced by the job, and regulated like explosives handling. It survives because the economics *usually* favor mundane alternatives — conjured tooling is a premium product for sites where cranes can't go and time costs more than Fathoms. Unlicensed conjuration contracting is the misdemeanor the trade's inspectors actually spend their time on.

> CANON-LOCKED: **Information is the scarce resource, not power.** Raw power is common enough to be cheap; *reliable, specific, actionable knowledge* about Rules, Tide-marks, and faction intentions is what money actually buys.

## III. Secrecy hard rules (CANON-LOCKED)

> CANON-LOCKED: **The Veil Reflex is universal and involuntary.** ~96–98% of humans rationalize or forget Seep exposure. It cannot be "trained away" in ordinary people; removing it requires becoming Loud, which requires either birth or a Seep event — both rare.

> CANON-LOCKED: **Static rot is proportional to intensity.** Recordings of active Seeps corrupt at a rate proportional to the Seep's Tide pressure. A T1's party trick films fine. A T5 event leaves melted sensors and four seconds of screaming static. This is why "undeniable footage" keeps failing to materialize.

> CANON-LOCKED: **The deepfake dividend cuts both ways.** Synthetic media has destroyed public trust in video evidence. This protects the masquerade — but it also means *factions cannot prove things to each other either*, which is why the Compact's closed verification protocols (witnessed, multi-sensor, in-person) are the only currency of proof that matters.

## IV. Narrative rules (CANON)

> CANON: **Power is legible.** Every ability and anomaly follows a Rule that can, in principle, be discovered through observation. There are no "it just works" powers. If Arthur cannot eventually figure out *why* something happened, it should not have happened.

> CANON: **Competence is distributed.** Governments, corporations, and factions employ intelligent professionals. "Why didn't they just…?" must always have an answer: incentives, constraints, cost, law, politics, or Erosion.

> CANON: **The world is indifferent.** The Undertow is not evil. Factions are not evil-by-job-description. Catastrophes come from incentives and pressure, not malice quotas.

> CANON: **Mystery is budgeted.** Five mystery layers exist (see `33_MYSTERIES.md`). A mystery labeled INTENTIONAL MYSTERY is a promise to the reader; a mystery that is merely an unexplained gap is a bug. Never relabel a bug as a mystery.

## V. Rule-change policy

- CANON-LOCKED rules change only via CHANGELOG entry with: the contradiction found, three considered solutions, the chosen solution, and its secondary consequences.
- New CANON must not contradict CANON-LOCKED. PROVISIONAL entries are re-audited each cycle.
- In-world characters may *believe* false rules. Those beliefs are labeled `STATUS: THEORY` or `STATUS: IN-WORLD MISCONCEPTION`, never stated as plain CANON.
