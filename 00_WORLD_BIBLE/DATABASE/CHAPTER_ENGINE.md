## §1. Purpose & scope

**What it is.** The formal production framework turning the story architecture — the 12-arc spine (~ch. 1–700) and the Approach band (~ch. 700–1000+) — into chapter *plans*, one chapter at a time, in disciplined batches. It specifies the chapter's components (§2), what chapters can do (§§3–4), the test separating chapter from filler (§5), rhythm rules for a thousand-chapter serial (§15), the causality law binding chapters (§18), ordinary-life accounting (§19), the archive doctrine (§20), scale bands (§21), the production protocol (§22), the plan schema (§23), quality diagnostics (§24), and the audits and failure modes catching drift before prose (§§25–27, §30).

**What it is not.** Not an outline of any chapter; it assigns no mystery, event, beat, or revelation to any chapter number. It does not resolve the four open preconditions (§31) — it is *built to survive them being resolved either way*. It invents no lore, characters, factions, organizations, anomalies, events, or IDs; every reference is a pointer to a canon database. It does not decide what happens — only *how* what happens is shaped, checked, and kept honest.

**The governing constraint.** Arthur's arc is UNFILED→ANSWERABLE, never WEAK→STRONG (STORY_ENGINE.md §3; MEMORY.md's character-arc principle). The engine must produce ~1,000 chapters around a protagonist who stays ordinary. The default chapter payoff is therefore: **something was documented, priced, understood, or answered — by a clerk.**

**Pipeline.** Story architecture (Phase 8) → Chapter Engine (this file) → batch chapter planning (§22) → prose. No stage skips its predecessor. Prose begins only after a batch of *plans* passes the value test (§5), the quality diagnostic (§24), and the continuity audit (§22 step 10).

## §2. The 13 chapter components (A–M)

Every chapter plan has thirteen components. Any component may be marked UNCHANGED — stillness is legal — but **no chapter may leave all thirteen unchanged**: every chapter needs ≥1 meaningful function (§5).

- **A. CHAPTER PURPOSE.** The chapter's single structural job, from the §3 taxonomy (one primary, at most two). If it can't be stated in one taxonomy sentence, the chapter is unplanned.
- **B. PRIMARY OBJECTIVE.** What Arthur (or the driving actor) wants *in his own terms*: not "advance the mystery" but "authenticate the page before the hammer falls." It must be something a night-shift records clerk could hold in his head.
- **C. PRIMARY CONFLICT.** The opposing force — an instantiation of the antagonist gradient (STORY_ENGINE.md §6), the arc's palette kind, or ordinary life itself (fatigue, rent, the Tuesday asymmetry). No opposing force = a transition chapter by definition; label it (§15) or plan a conflict.
- **D. CHARACTER MOVEMENT.** What changes inside the people present — Arthur's internal-arc ledger first (STORY_ENGINE.md §3; the four assets: attention→devotion, memory→testimony, honesty→telling, showing-up→commitment), then the cast. "He notices X" isn't movement; a shifted belief, habit, or cost-threshold is. Personality is load-bearing canon (ROMANCE_ARCHITECTURE.md §1) — reallocation, never replacement (§30.9).
- **E. INFORMATION MOVEMENT.** What reader and Arthur each learn, tracked separately against INFORMATION_KNOWLEDGE_MAP.md's three states (●/◐/○ across R, D, AL, AN, GF). Includes *negative* movement: information lost (redacted, Veil-edited) or confirmed *unavailable* (§20).
- **F. MYSTERY MOVEMENT.** MYSTERY-IDs touched and how: seed, advance, partially resolve, resolve, or deliberately *withhold* (a question loaded but unasked — the margin note's date paradox is the canon model: fair-play clue first, handwriting check later). Checked against REVEAL_ORDER.md (D-table's 24 edges, L5 schedule): no movement outside its window, no L5 resolution sharing an arc (ARC_DEPENDENCY_GRAPH.md §6), question-debt ≤2/arc.
- **G. POWER MOVEMENT.** Changes in Arthur's relation to ANOMALY-001 and the five disciplines — almost always *understanding and price*, almost never *new capability*. Governed by POWER_PROGRESSION.md (12 Uses in discovery order, 7 stages, the ratchet), the 16-item no-shortcut list, the 8 brakes. Every Use priced (ratchet fraction, physiology, exposure); every option via observe→hypothesize→test→pay.
- **H. FACTION MOVEMENT.** Position changes in the 28 factions / 88 org IDs — *their* movement, never Arthur's. The 15 Arthur-instanced conflicts are machines he instantiates, never owns (ARC_DEPENDENCY_GRAPH.md §6); the 102 ACTIVE conflicts run Arthur-independent trajectories. A chapter may *reveal* an off-page change (repricing after a reveal: every major reveal reprices ≥2 factions) but never decides a faction's strategy. Respects the PEAK discipline (ARC_ARCHITECTURE.md §13.3).
- **I. RELATIONSHIP MOVEMENT.** Changes between Arthur and others — against the romance gates/rules *and* the non-romantic layer (Okada, Hasegawa, Saitō, Night Clerks, Nadia, Aisha, Eleanor). Selected-configuration beats gated by mystery-evidence windows (15 links, ARC_DEPENDENCY_GRAPH.md §5) and the dinner rule (Rule 5). All movement through the trust engine — showing-up, file-sharing, the telling, the dinner — never declaration. Unselected configurations keep moving in friendship/professional forms (STORY_ENGINE.md §7).
- **J. WORLD MOVEMENT.** Changes independent of Arthur: the Compact's model, the Choir's schism, the Drowndust market, tide-pressure readings. Texture in P1–P2, load-bearing from P3. May be UNCHANGED per chapter; no arc may leave it unchanged throughout.
- **K. CONSEQUENCE.** What the chapter *costs*: tether use priced (the ratchet), shared facts invoiced (exposure), ordinary choices calendared/monetized. Activated in chapter *n*, consequences must be paid, remembered, or compounded by *n+k* (§30.15). They compound, never cancel — the escrow's ~30% failure rate, masking fatigue, the hiring flag are *carried* costs.
- **L. END STATE.** The closing delta — what is true now that wasn't (or was differently true) at the start (§27: "WHAT IS DIFFERENT AFTER THIS CHAPTER?"). Empty everywhere = filler by definition (§5).
- **M. NEXT-CHAPTER HOOK.** What pulls the reader onward — *derived from this chapter's consequences*, not stapled on. Artificial cliffhangers (§30.11) are hooks imported from nowhere.

## §§3–4. Chapter purpose taxonomy

One primary purpose per chapter (at most two), from this taxonomy. Illustrations show what each purpose looks like in this series' machinery — engine illustrations, NOT chapter plans; nothing new invented.

- **Introduce.** A new element enters the working set. *E.g.:* the 03:00 curb as a place where something is *decided*; the Night Clerks' Discord log as a source before it's evidence.
- **Investigate.** The clerk's method applied: appraisal, collation, authentication. *E.g.:* the revision history on Nadia's softened filings; holding *both* files at the jurisdiction seam (SECRET-020).
- **Reveal.** A scheduled truth delivered. *E.g.:* the tether reveal's grammar flip (ARC II); the page surfacing worse than the bidding (ARC VIII). Obeys the reveal budget (§31); reprices ≥2 factions.
- **Misdirect.** A genuine red herring (RED_HERRINGS.md) or an honest wrong theory doing work. *E.g.:* the Pale Court's collecting doctrine — *wrong in exactly the way that makes it dangerous*. Real evidence; wrong conclusion.
- **Escalate.** Raise an existing pressure without resolving it. *E.g.:* the Factor's buyer inquiry established (EVENT-093) — the price on his head before publication.
- **Complicate.** A legitimate obstacle to an in-progress objective. *E.g.:* CG-044's mole hunt making Nadia's routine *look* deliberate.
- **Recover.** Breathe after a peak: the invoice arriving. *E.g.:* recalculating the escrow's reliability model after testing — the true price of the old threat, written down.
- **Develop character.** The internal ledger moves. *E.g.:* the ARC-III question ("the arrow may have reversed, and he has no test that falsifies it") made to cost him something specific.
- **Develop relationship.** A trust engine advances toward its next gate. *E.g.:* Configuration A's file-sharing beat — his files for her manual and its revision history. Intimacy as mutual evidence.
- **Develop faction.** A faction's machinery on its own logic; Arthur may be absent. *E.g.:* the Desk applying watch-not-act to a non-Arthur case.
- **Establish setting.** A place made load-bearing. *E.g.:* B2–B3 as the one room where the camouflage thesis is *true* (ARC I); the curb as the ordinary place become exposed (ARC V). Never travelogue — the place serves a §19 function or holds a mystery mechanism.
- **Provide information.** Necessary facts, no reveal drama — the plumbing. *E.g.:* the Ledger's charter 14 §14.3(2) read as law before it's used as a weapon. Must pass §20: facts via investigation, interpretation, verification, risk — never lecture.
- **Create uncertainty.** Open a question honestly or wobble a certainty. *E.g.:* the mirror tripwire — someone else mentions the mirror. Uncertainty must be *priced*: the reader feels what not-knowing costs.
- **Create consequence.** Activate a cost. *E.g.:* the leak breaking — Nadia reads what her hands did, with him present. Nothing new revealed; the *consequence of her knowing* is created.
- **Transition.** Honest movement across time, place, state. *E.g.:* the ordinary week between the pitch's refusal and the leak's break. Labeled honestly; a transition in mystery's clothes is filler.
- **Prepare.** Stage conditions without firing them — the tripwire, not the trap. *E.g.:* the demographers' flag *filed* chapters before detonation.
- **Confront.** Forced direct meeting: interrogation, negotiation, the pitch, the auction, the gala question. *E.g.:* the recruitment pitch (ARC VI) — refusing costs more than accepting.
- **Resolve.** Close a scheduled question/thread/conflict. *E.g.:* the suppressor named with motive (ARC IX); the auction voided — physics voids it, law admits the crime (ARC V). Gated by the D-table and L5 schedule; a resolution outside its window is a leak (§30.2).
- **Recontextualize.** New meaning for known facts, no new facts. *E.g.:* 1977 re-read as the storm's *fuel* (ARC IX); the smile's nature (ARC XII). The endgame's primary weapon — how L5-3 is handled: recontextualized, never resolved.

## §5. The formal VALUE TEST

The removal test. Remove the planned chapter; ask what is *lost* on each axis:

1. **Information** — reader/Arthur permanently less informed, or a scheduled reveal landing later/weaker/never? (D-table, reveal budget.)
2. **Character** — Arthur's ledger poorer: belief unforced, asset unreallocated, cost unpriced?
3. **Relationship** — a trust engine stalled: gate unapproached, friendship's honest floor unbuilt?
4. **Mystery** — a MYSTERY-ID flattened: seed unplanted, thread untightened, fair-play clue unfelt?
5. **Power** — the price-structure vaguer: Use unpriced, ratchet fraction unmeasured, no-shortcut discipline unenforced?
6. **Faction** — a trajectory blurred: repricing unregistered, autonomy verdict weakened, tripwire unfired?
7. **World** — the world-state thinner: checkpoint thread dropped, masquerade texture worn smooth?
8. **Emotional** — felt stakes diminished: the cost-of-presence feeling the series is actually about?
9. **Consequence** — an invoice unpaid: debt forgotten, exposure unpriced, fatigue uncarried?

**Verdict.** Nothing lost on all nine = filler by construction; rejected. Meaningful loss on ≥1 axis = PASS — "meaningful" = specific (nameable, traceable to a canon ID or field delta), not atmospheric. **FLAG AS POTENTIAL FILLER:** only emotional loss or world-texture loss survives → the anti-filler audit (§25). Texture alone never carries a chapter, though it can *justify* one passing elsewhere.

**The value test is the engine's single load-bearing gate.** §§15–27 make chapters that pass it; §30 catches chapters pretending to pass it.

## §15. Rhythm model

**The seven-beat option.** HOOK → CONTEXT → DEVELOPMENT → COMPLICATION → TURN → CONSEQUENCE → HOOK is the default skeleton — an *option*, never a mandate. It fits Arthur's reality: rooms that already have paperwork; the paperwork complicating his objective; something he read changing what the room means (the turn); the change invoiced (the consequence); the invoice pulling him into the next room (the hook). Departures must be *nameable in the plan* (an aftermath may run HOOK→CONTEXT→CONSEQUENCE→REST; a discovery may run COMPLICATION→TURN with the discovery *as* consequence). The model makes departures legible; it doesn't forbid them.

**The eleven chapter types.** *Investigation* — the clerk's method (appraisal, collation, authentication; testing reserved for ARC XII's scale); must end with something *filed*, even "inconclusive, filed anyway." *Character* — the internal ledger moves; slowest, most failure-prone (§25); needs a specific, nameable change. *Action* — bodies in motion; series rule: Arthur's action chapters are *evasion and endurance*; victories are documentation-shaped (§30.13). *Mystery* — a MYSTERY-ID moves; capped by the reveal budget (§31), gated by the D-table. *Relationship* — a trust engine advances or stalls honestly; obeys the gates, six rules, dinner rule (§31). *Faction* — a faction's machinery visible, Arthur optional; autonomy respected. *Aftermath* — the invoice arrives (escrow recalculated, ratchet re-measured); skipping it is how consequences get forgotten (§30.15). *Discovery* — something *found* by work (the wrong file next to the right file); must show the finding's *mechanism* or it's archive omniscience (§30.16). *Transition* — honest movement across time/place/state; labeled, never disguised. *Ordinary-life* — the five components (§19) are the chapter; may be *simply ordinary*, but each beat names its §19 function. *Tension* — nothing happens but pressure; cheapest to write, easiest to fake — pressure must trace to a canon mechanism (a named tripwire, a priced ratchet, a filed flag), never mood alone.

**Run-length guidance.** ≤3 consecutive chapters of one type without a plan-named reason; 4+ is a §26 red flag. Every batch's type sequence is verified at §22 step 9. The mix respects the arc's palette kind: an *auction — PAPER (48)* arc (ARC IV) runs heavy on investigation/mystery/faction; a *truth commission (51)* arc (ARC IX) on discovery/aftermath/recontextualization. The kind-per-arc rule (§31) constrains the arc; type discipline constrains the *rhythm within* it. The P2 adjacency watch (ARCs I–II both in the 60 family — audit §10) is the model: same-family neighbors legal only if *structures* differ (private self-appraisal vs. provenance contest; character vs. relationship chapter). **Approach band:** world-scale convergence legitimately shifts the mix toward faction/aftermath/tension at large scale; the engine holds ordinary-life types at *Arthur's* scale (the 1K is still the 1K) — scale control is a rhythm discipline, not just a theme.

## §18. Chapter-to-chapter causality

**The law: EVENT → CONSEQUENCE → RESPONSE → NEXT EVENT.** Every KEY EVENT (§23) produces a CONSEQUENCE (component K); the next chapter's event traces to a *response* — by Arthur, a faction, or the world. Responses may be latent for arcs (the demographers' flag is the canon model), but the link must be *nameable in the plan*. "And then" is forbidden; "therefore / because / in response to" mandatory. The NEXT CHAPTER DEPENDENCY field (§23) records the link.

**No-episodic-reset.** No chapter begins as if prior consequences didn't happen: Arthur doesn't wake un-tired; the escrow's model doesn't heal; Nadia doesn't un-learn the revision history; the ratchet doesn't shorten. The ordinary-life engine (§19) is the usual casualty — fatigue, money, the shift calendar, workplace consequences are *the* continuity of an ordinary man's life.

**Don't-force-artificial-continuity.** The mirror rule, equally load-bearing: not every chapter ends on a cliffhanger; consequences may lie latent; mysteries may simmer; a chapter may end in *rest* (component L: "nothing changed except the invoice was filed"). Artificial continuity — the stapled-on hook, the imported threat — is §30.11. The test: **NEXT CHAPTER DEPENDENCY must be derivable from this chapter's actual consequences.** If the hook needs a fact the chapter didn't establish, replace it with a consequence-derived hook or label the chapter a transition and let it rest.

**Latency is legal; amnesia is not.** Consequences persist on- or off-page (no reset) and surface only when the architecture's timing earns it (no forcing). CONSEQUENCE tracks *activation state* (latent / compounding / due / paid); the §22-step-10 audit checks every consequence sits in a justifiable state.

## §19. Ordinary-life balance

The ordinary-life engine (STORY_ENGINE.md §4) is a load-bearing system, tracked like mysteries. The five canon components are mandatory planning axes, each with granular sub-axes. A batch leaving a component untracked is flagged.

1. **Night shift (22:00–06:00, B2–B3).** Sub-axes: work shifts (which nights, overtime, absences — and who notices), workplace consequences (the hiring flag's trail; Okada's scheduling as the attendance record noticing absences first; Hasegawa teaching the cover story's seams), fatigue (cumulative, never reset — §18). Per-arc position (advantage → liability → gone/compromised — STORY_ENGINE.md §4.1) is a planning invariant.
2. **Family dinner rule (every other Sunday, Willowmere).** Sub-axes: meals (the load-bearing peace), social (Eleanor's careful silences; the civilian immune system — salt by the door, *protective charm*), the annual *New Year* audit ("bring them home or explain why not"). The commitment technology in every configuration (Rule 5, §31); commitment-adjacent beats account for the dinner calendar.
3. **Conbini geography (03:00 break; the curb).** Sub-axes: commute (three streets over, a Sweeper team processes someone's worst night), meals (£600 breakfasts — the romance's early economy), errands (what makes the 1K livable), ambient intake (the bottleneck: the Vietnamese intern's counting dream; street-level early warning before any faction's file). From ARC V the curb is a *known* meeting point — plans track who knows.
4. **Clara Whitmore (CHAR-036) and Henry Lawson (CHAR-037).** Sub-axes: social (small mercies; kindness as cover), workplace consequences (their trust as what deep use near work *spends*). The Quiet witnesses he must not spend — plans price the trust-cost explicitly.
5. **Night Clerks' Ravenhurst cell (IND-004; futsal, Discord).** Sub-axes: social (futsal by the bay; sighting logs — never briefings), bottleneck (shared glances at wrong files; the channel the Desk can't fully model). Every arc leaning on the cell *spends* it — the blast radius is people who play futsal.

**The four functions, per chapter.** Every ordinary beat is tagged **rest / contrast / stakes-grounding / information bottleneck**; the engine rule (STORY_ENGINE.md §4) is enforced per batch: no arc runs beyond its share without all four firing ≥1. Stakes without rest = misery; information without bottlenecks = omniscience; contrast without the ordinary = the forbidden power fantasy.

**Collision cadence.** P1–P2: collisions are *incidents* (the ordinary is camouflage; the supernatural intrudes). P3: collisions are *prices* (the ordinary is spent — the curb a meeting point, the shift a liability). P4: collisions are *tests* (the ordinary is what the stakes are *for* — the dinner as what the High Tide threatens). Checked per batch vs. the arc's phase position (§22 step 9).

**Ordinary scenes may simply be ordinary.** The 03:00 rice ball can be just the 03:00 rice ball; Eleanor's *Are you eating properly?* just a mother's question. The plan shows *which function* the ordinariness serves — not a stapled-on supernatural hook. The moment every ordinary scene must "pay off" in hidden-world terms, the ordinary stops being ordinary, and the series loses its stakes' ground.

**Apartment and finances as continuity.** The 1K (~£48,000/mo) and ~£230,000/mo salary (ROMANCE_ARCHITECTURE.md §1) are hard continuity: rent, the dawn-convenience store dating economy, absence costs, the escrow's construction price. His poverty isn't flavor — it's why romance runs on £600 breakfasts and why "against being *fired*" is a genuine-powerlessness condition. Plans spending money he lacks, or forgetting rent, fail continuity.

## §20. Archive engine

Arthur never extracts truth by force; he finds it in files. The ten sources are operations of the clerk's method (STORY_ENGINE.md §5: appraisal, collation, authentication, testing), each bound by the ARCHIVE ≠ OMNISCIENCE doctrine.

**The ten archive sources.** *Old files* — records predating the question, answering sideways: Ilsa's journals (dormant 28 years: *holding is not knowing*, SECRET-006); the 1977 budget line (098). *Missing records* — absence as evidence: the torn last page (SECRET-007); the unreported 2017 transfer (SECRET-020). The plan names *who benefits from the absence* and *what its shape implies*. *Damaged documents* — partial legibility as mechanism: the redacted signature (SECRET-009); revision history showing *what changed* but not *by whose order*. Damage informs on the damager. *Incorrect dates* — the unfitting date is the fair-play clue: the margin note's date paradox (SECRET-024; the reader may notice before Arthur's handwriting check). Pure *appraisal* fuel. *Duplicate records* — two disagreeing copies: the SMD's thin copy vs. BPF Jakarta's filing (SECRET-020's seam — Arthur first to hold *both*); Nadia's softened filings vs. originals. The difference *is* the story — collation fuel. *Classified material* — the classification *level* is metadata (who was scared enough?); the classifier's identity a lead; the declassification schedule a clock. *Forgotten incidents* — filed, never read: the Hollow Men's bureaucracy eating their own diver's warning (SECRET-011 — institutional knowledge without institutional attention). The thesis restated: *archives are not attention*. *Administrative anomalies* — paperwork that shouldn't exist: the flagged hiring file approved anyway (EVENT-087); the budget line for an unclosed internment (098). Where the system's lies leave fingerprints. *Historical inconsistencies* — two official histories that can't both hold: the Compact's founding narrative vs. 1977 (ARC IX); the Choir's doctrine vs. the preacher's method (ARC VII). *Recontextualization* fuel (§3). *Archival metadata* — the file *about* the file: access logs, checkout records, whose terminal requested the copy. How a clerk without clearance reads a secret's *shape*. The ordinary clerk's lockpick.

**ARCHIVE ≠ OMNISCIENCE.** Every archive-sourced revelation must satisfy all five — a missing one flags archive omniscience (§30.16): **(1) Investigation** — the file is *sought* (pulled, requested, noticed as the wrong file next to the right file, followed via metadata); files don't fall off shelves. **(2) Interpretation** — the file is *read*, and reading is work (clerk's eye, handwriting check, dating, duplicate comparison); the obvious-on-sight file is exposition in a folder. **(3) Verification** — checked against something outside itself (second copy, witness, date, physical fact). Canon standard: SECRET-019 — the 2021 examination's method was *specific and limited*, never "the system sees everything." "The file says so" isn't verification. **(4) Risk** — reading *costs*: exposure (who sees him pull it), the invoice (whose attention the request draws), the ordinary price (hours, noticed absences). The access making him dangerous is revocable — every deep dive prices the employment risk. **(5) External information** — the file alone never suffices: Loud recall, a conversation, a street observation, the intern's counting dream. The bottleneck (§19) as hard rule: the archive gives paper; the world gives what the paper means.

**The doctrine's positive form.** All five holding = the series at its best: authentication as weapon (ARC IV — only a holder authenticates; he sets the terms); collation at institutional scale (ARC IX — the founding crime's budget line read like the wrong file next to the right file); the jurisdiction seam (SECRET-020 — nobody with clearance connected the desks; the clerk without clearance did, because he *read*).

## §21. Chapter scale guidelines

Four bands — **guidelines, not rigid requirements**. Ratios matter; the absolute unit is a prose-production decision (pages or word-count equivalent):

- **Micro (3–7).** One beat, cleanly executed: a measurement taken, a file pulled, a one-thing conversation, honest rest. Connective tissue — the demographers' flag *filed*, the tenth-of-a-second measurement written down. Several per batch allowed; nothing-but-micros dissolves the serial into incident reports.
- **Short (8–15).** The standard chapter: one §3 purpose fully executed, consequence priced, hook set. Most chapters, most batches. The value test's (§5) calibration unit — a short chapter that can't pass it should be a micro or merged.
- **Medium (16–30).** Two full purposes, or one at unusual depth: the authentication set-piece (ARC IV's climax), the leak breaking (ARC VI), the deep-lag session (ARC VIII). The plan justifies the length: two named purposes, or one genuinely needing the room.
- **Major (31–60+).** Structural peaks only: arc climaxes, the page read (ARC VIII), the paper read aloud (ARC IX), the test's design (ARC XII). A major that isn't a checkpoint event (§28) is two chapters in a trench coat — split it. ≤1 major per batch; no arc holds more majors than its START/MIDPOINT/CLIMAX/END names.

**Scale discipline.** Scale ≠ importance — a micro (the tenth-of-a-second measurement) can outweigh a medium (a well-executed inessential confrontation). CHAPTER PURPOSE (§23), not the band, carries importance. Scale creep — every chapter drifting one band past its purpose — is caught by §24's PACING and §25.

## §22. Batch generation protocol

Chapters planned in batches of **10** — large enough to check rhythm, causality, arc-position; small enough to audit by hand. **Never generate hundreds blindly**: quality control is per-batch. 8–12 acceptable when a checkpoint (§28) falls mid-batch and the batch should close on it.

**The 12-step sequence** — all twelve, in order, no skips; prose only after step 12:

1. **Read previous state.** Outgoing batch's end states (component L), outstanding consequences and activation states (latent/compounding/due/paid), the ordinary-life ledger (shift calendar, finances, fatigue, dinner calendar), the last chapter's NEXT CHAPTER DEPENDENCY. The new batch's first chapter satisfies it or explicitly re-plans it, reason named.
2. **Arc architecture.** Locate the batch in the spine (ARC_ARCHITECTURE.md): arc(s), §28 checkpoints crossed, palette kind, central conflict, Arthur's objective, per-arc fields (turning point, climax, aftermath, unresolved, new questions). Chapters map *between* checkpoints, never independent of the arc.
3. **Active mysteries.** MYSTERY-IDs whose REVEAL_ORDER windows cover the batch: live, due, protected (L5 schedule, 12 intentional UNKNOWNs, endgame-unknown ledger). Verify movements vs. the D-table's 24 edges — nothing outside its window.
4. **Active foreshadowing.** FORESHADOWING-IDs the batch may *pay toward* (usually fractions: a glance, a recurrence, a tightening). Check RED_HERRINGS.md: no herring accidentally resolved or unredeemably planted.
5. **Character states.** Arthur's internal-arc position (STORY_ENGINE.md §3 row; which of the four assets reallocating) + every recurring character touched — vs. CHARACTERS.md and the romance gates (ARC_DEPENDENCY_GRAPH.md §5).
6. **Faction states.** 28 factions / 88 org IDs per FACTIONS_AND_CONFLICTS.md and the interleave map (ARC_ARCHITECTURE.md §13): SIMMER/ACTIVE/PEAK, armed tripwires, propagating repricings. Verify autonomy: no faction's movement decided by Arthur.
7. **Romance states.** Selected configuration's (§31) trust-engine position and gates; unselected configurations' friendship/professional forms; the dinner calendar (a Willowmere dinner inside the batch constrains commitment-adjacent beats); six rules + 7 independence watches as active checks.
8. **Power state.** Arthur's stage (POWER_PROGRESSION.md's 7-stage curve; the arc's stage per ARC_DEPENDENCY_GRAPH.md §3), priced Uses available, the ratchet's measurement, the escrow's reliability model, no-shortcut list + 8 brakes as applicable. Power movements pre-checked here.
9. **Generate plans.** The 10 plans in the §23 schema — plans only, no prose. Assign §15 types; check type sequence for run-length violations; check collision cadence (§19) vs. phase position; verify value test (§5) and end-state deltas (§27) per chapter.
10. **Continuity audit.** Vs. all canon databases: IDs exist (retcon scan — audit §12); timeline legal (EVENTS.md; EVENT-079 March 2017 fixed anchor); knowledge-map three-states hold (no leakage — §30.3); factions respect autonomy + PEAK discipline; romance respects gates; power respects brakes; ordinary-life axes continuous (no reset — §18).
11. **Patch planning errors.** In the plan, before prose. Prefer demotion over deletion (a failing chapter is usually a micro or merged beat — deletion breaks causality); prefer moving a beat to its legal window over rewriting it (windows are load-bearing; content isn't); never invent a new fact to cover an old error (that's a retcon — audit §12).
12. **Prose allowed.** Strictly from patched plans — one plan per chapter, no new plot at prose stage. Prose-stage discoveries return as *plan patches* (back to step 11), never improvised into canon. At the prose stage, the NON-NATIVE READABILITY & ACCESSIBILITY STANDARD applies as an editorial constraint (DATABASE/FAST_PACE_DEEP_MYSTERY_RETENTION_SOP.md, Part II §§37–50): easy words + deep ideas — simplify the delivery, never the mystery. At blueprint and prose stage, the NARRATIVE MOVEMENT VARIETY rules also apply (retention SOP, Part III §§51–60): dominant movement tracked (§51), investigation method varied (§52), anti-formula comparison vs. the previous three chapters (§57), serial pacing floors met (§59).

**Batch records.** Each batch's plans, audit results, and patches are filed as the batch record — the next batch's step 1 reads the record, never memory.

## §23. Chapter plan schema

All twenty-four fields filled. **No prose in the planning stage** — fields hold specifications, IDs, deltas, decisions, never page-ready sentences. A plan containing prose is a draft smuggled past the audit; rejected.

- **CHAPTER ID.** Position in the ~1–1000+ sequence. Sequential, immutable once a batch locks — renumbering is a continuity event, not an edit.
- **TITLE.** Working title. Production metadata, not canon; the plan's TITLE is what the batch record and cross-references use.
- **ARC.** Which of the 12 arcs (I–XII) per ARC_ARCHITECTURE.md. Boundary chapters name both and satisfy both checkpoint states (§28).
- **TIMEmessaging app.** In-world date/time to needed precision (the 22:00–06:00 window makes hours matter). Vs. EVENTS.md; EVENT-079 (March 2017) is the fixed anchor.
- **LOCATION.** Where, at needed precision. Continuity: who knows the place (§19's curb rule), its four-function history, whether compromised.
- **POV.** Viewpoint — default Arthur-limited. Deviations named and justified: what this POV knows that Arthur can't, and why the chapter needs it. Never carries L5-adjacent information Arthur lacks (reader-lead rule, §31).
- **CHAPTER PURPOSE.** One primary §3 purpose (≤2). The plan's thesis sentence; everything serves it.
- **PRIMARY CONFLICT.** The opposing force as canon instantiation (faction interest, antagonist move, ordinary-life pressure, Arthur's filing instinct). Identifiable, not atmospheric.
- **SECONDARY CONFLICT.** The second pressure — often the ordinary-life counterweight to a supernatural primary or vice versa. Optional, recommended: the series' texture lives here.
- **CHARACTERS PRESENT.** Everyone appearing or acting by reference. Names only — no new characters (retcon scan, audit §12); plot-weightless anonymous functionaries permitted.
- **CHARACTER STATE CHANGES.** Component-D deltas per present character. Arthur stated vs. the internal-arc ledger (STORY_ENGINE.md §3) and four assets. "None" legal for minor characters; never for Arthur in a character-purpose chapter.
- **INFORMATION REVEALED.** What reader and Arthur each learn — facts, not vibes, stated separately, each tagged with its knowledge-map delta (e.g., "D: ○→◐ on SECRET-024's date paradox").
- **INFORMATION WITHHELD.** Deliberately unrevealed though expected: loaded-but-unasked questions, the file pulled unread, the tripwire armed unfired. Planned withholding, not omission.
- **MYSTERY IDs.** IDs touched, each with verb (seed / advance / partial / resolve / withhold) and REVEAL_ORDER window cited. Question-debt contribution stated: 0, 1, or 2 new threads — never more.
- **FORESHADOWING IDs.** IDs planted/serviced with service fraction (planted / glanced / tightened / paid). Cross-checked vs. RED_HERRINGS.md where applicable.
- **POWER ELEMENTS.** Uses spent/priced/discovered/threatened — price stated (ratchet fraction, physiology, exposure), stage/brake check cited. "None" is the most common legal value.
- **FACTION ELEMENTS.** Factions/org IDs moving or revealed moving — as *their* action or repricing, never Arthur's decision for them. Interleave intensity (SIMMER/ACTIVE/PEAK) cited.
- **ROMANCE ELEMENTS.** Trust-engine movement for the selected configuration + unselected configurations' friendship/professional forms. Gate check (ARC_DEPENDENCY_GRAPH.md §5); applicable rules (six rules, dinner rule, watches) cited. "None" legal.
- **KEY EVENT.** The EVENT→CONSEQUENCE→RESPONSE chain (§18): what happens, what it costs, who responds — one line each. The chapter's causal spine.
- **TURNING POINT.** Where the chapter's meaning changes — the handwriting check, the revision history, the mirror mentioned by another mouth. "The invoice arrived" is an honest aftermath turn. No turn at all = a transition; label it (§15).
- **CONSEQUENCE.** Component K with activation state (latent / compounding / due / paid) and price. Every consequence names its creditor: the ratchet, the exposure ledger, the calendar, a faction's memory. Creditorless consequences are forgotten by the next batch (§30.15).
- **ENDING TYPE.** hook (consequence-derived, §18) / rest (honest aftermath) / turn / transition. Must match type and purpose — a mystery chapter ending in rest signals a mislabeled movement.
- **NEXT CHAPTER DEPENDENCY.** The onward causal link, derivable from this chapter's consequences (§18): "Next opens with Arthur responding to X by doing Y" — X the consequence, Y a defensible response.
- **CONTINUITY RISKS.** What could break: strained knowledge-map states, touched timeline edges, approached autonomy, spent ordinary axes, early-risked foreshadowing. The plan's honesty box — the step-10 audit starts here.

## §24. Quality diagnostic checklist

Eleven dimensions, each **PASS** (numeric score **≥9.0/10** and evidence present) / **WATCH** (below 9.0; repair required before approval) / **FAIL** (broken; back to §22 step 11 before prose). **No overall averaging**: a chapter cannot compensate for a weak dimension with a stronger one. The 9.0 floor is a minimum quality floor, not a requirement that every chapter be loud, action-heavy, or a peak chapter.

- **FUNCTION.** ≥1 meaningful thing done? PASS: §5 finds specific, traceable loss on ≥1 axis. WATCH: only emotional/world-texture loss survives (FLAG AS POTENTIAL FILLER — §25). FAIL: removal finds nothing.
- **CAUSALITY.** Bound to neighbors? PASS: KEY EVENT's chain (§18) derives from the previous NEXT CHAPTER DEPENDENCY and genuinely constrains the next. WATCH: legal but thin (latent consequences only). FAIL: episodic reset or artificial continuity (hook needs an unestablished fact).
- **CHARACTER.** Anyone moves — and Arthur moves like Arthur? PASS: component-D deltas specific, consistent with the internal-arc ledger and personality canon (meticulous, dry-humored, conflict-avoidant but stubborn about facts; reallocation, never replacement). WATCH: movement generic (fits any protagonist). FAIL: personality drift (§30.9) or no movement in a character-purpose chapter.
- **INFORMATION.** Movement honest? PASS: revealed/withheld precise; knowledge-map deltas legal (three-state separation; reader-lead ≤1 phase except the two ironies); archive beats meet the five requirements (§20). WATCH: legal but inefficient (a reveal that could've been a discovery). FAIL: leakage, archive omniscience, exposition dumping (§30.3/16/12).
- **MYSTERY.** Architecture obeyed? PASS: every movement inside its REVEAL_ORDER window; D-table edges hold; L5 intact; debt ≤2. WATCH: legal but early/late in-window (timing strain recorded). FAIL: premature reveal, window violation, L5 mishandling, debt breach (§30.2).
- **CONSEQUENCE.** Everything priced? PASS: every Use, revelation, exposure, and ordinary spend has a named creditor and activation state; outstanding consequences in justified states. WATCH: creditor vague ("the Desk will notice" — which desk, which file, when). FAIL: unpriced spend or forgotten consequence (§30.4/15).
- **CONTINUITY.** Fits the canon? PASS: IDs exist (retcon scan); timeline legal; factions autonomous with correct intensity; romance gated; ordinary axes continuous (no reset). WATCH: a §23 honesty-box risk uncleared — recorded for next batch. FAIL: hard contradiction (wrong EVENT number, impossible date, faction against its verdict, Arthur knowing the unknowable).
- **PACING.** Scale and rhythm-place earned? PASS: §21 band fits the purpose; §15 type doesn't extend a run-length violation; batch type-sequence varied; collision cadence (§19) fits the phase. WATCH: legal but drifting (three same-type chapters, thin justification). FAIL: purposeless scale creep, a fourth consecutive identical type, a major that isn't a checkpoint event, or a fourth consecutive chapter sharing the same dominant movement category without story-architecture mandate (retention SOP §51; movement check also runs at the §35 gate per §60).
- **ORIGINALITY.** Mechanism new or reskin? PASS: structure, conflict shape, and mystery mechanism differ from recent chapters on §26's axes — or repeat deliberately as *motif* (named). WATCH: one axis repeats unjustified. FAIL: multi-axis repetition (§30.10).
- **EMOTIONAL VALUE.** Costs someone something felt? PASS: the consequence lands on a named attachment (the dinner's peace, Okada's trust, the £600-breakfast economy, the mirror he won't write down) — cost-of-presence specific. WATCH: stakes real but abstract (ledgers without a human creditor). FAIL: all mechanism, no attachment.
- **READABILITY.** Understandable without a dictionary, hard to forget? The prose-stage enforcement of the NON-NATIVE READABILITY & ACCESSIBILITY STANDARD (DATABASE/FAST_PACE_DEEP_MYSTERY_RETENTION_SOP.md, Part II §§37–50) — applies at Blueprint → Prose Generation → Revision → Narrative Audit → Full Narrative Audit for every future chapter (021+). PASS: §50's READABILITY AUDIT CHECKLIST passes; everyday actions in simple English, specialized terms necessary and contextually clear, no stacked difficulties, figurative language selective, mystery and atmosphere intact. WATCH: local friction (one underexplained specialized term, a stacked difficult sentence) — repaired at prose stage, plan unchanged. FAIL: the chapter needs outside vocabulary to follow the story, or prose more literary than readable — back to §22 step 11 for prose-level repair (§45's gate pairing with §35: both must pass).

### §24A. 9+ QUALITY FLOOR — NON-COMPENSATORY

The chapter-quality floor is **9.0/10 minimum on every applicable quality dimension**. This is a gate, not a ranking system.

The score must be evidence-based and accompanied by a short reason. The reviewer must not raise a score merely because a chapter is exciting, beautiful, mysterious, or important elsewhere. Likewise, a quiet chapter may score 9+ when its movement is precise, its consequence is meaningful, and its purpose cannot be removed without loss.

**Required approval conditions:**

1. **Quality floor:** every applicable §24 dimension is ≥9.0.
2. **Narrative value floor:** the chapter produces a specific, traceable delta on **at least two** of the following axes: plot/information, mystery, character, relationship, consequence, world/faction, ordinary life/emotional state.
3. **End-state integrity:** §27's "WHAT IS DIFFERENT AFTER THIS CHAPTER?" answer is concrete and agrees with the §5 removal test.
4. **Anti-formula integrity:** the chapter has a named structural or experiential variation when recent chapters repeat on multiple axes (§26; Retention SOP §§51–57).
5. **Canon safety:** canon/continuity remains a hard gate. A canon contradiction is never offset by a high craft score.

**Quiet-chapter rule:** 9+ does **not** mean "more events." A recovery, relationship, ordinary-life, aftermath, or tension chapter can pass at 9+ if the chapter creates meaningful movement through the appropriate axis and earns its space. Do not inject action, revelations, or artificial cliffhangers solely to raise a score.

**Peak-chapter rule:** not every chapter should be a peak. Peak chapters may score higher, but the production target is consistent 9+ quality with deliberate variation in intensity, movement, and ending type.

**Scoring discipline:** use the following interpretation for each applicable dimension: **9 = excellent and fully controlled; 9.5 = exceptional with unusually strong execution; 10 = exemplary and essentially free of material weakness.** Scores below 9 are repair states, not publishable states. Do not use decimal precision to manufacture false confidence; **9.0 means the minimum pass line.**

## §25. Anti-filler audit

The value test (§5) catches functionless chapters. This catches the harder case: **slow chapters that might be meaningful anyway.** Slowness isn't filler; emptiness is.

**SLOW BUT MEANINGFUL** — all three required: (1) passes the value test on ≥1 non-plot axis (character, relationship, emotional, consequence — plot not required); (2) end-state delta (§27) specific and traceable (named belief changed, named trust advanced, named cost priced); (3) slowness *structural* — the purpose requires it (aftermath registering the invoice; trust built one showing-up at a time; rest performed), and the plan names why faster execution breaks the purpose.

**SLOW AND STRUCTURALLY EMPTY** — any one fails it: (1) only emotional loss, stated abstractly ("the mood deepens"), no named attachment; (2) end-state delta empty or circular ("Arthur reflects" — reflection without a changed belief isn't a delta); (3) slowness *default* — nothing planned, atmosphere doing the plan's work.

**The seven acceptable quiet-chapter contributions** (a low-velocity chapter is legitimate making ≥1, plan-named): **(1) Rest with texture** — ordinary beats performing rest (§19) with specific continuous detail (shift rituals, rice ball Fridays, futsal); rest that *restores* capacity to feel the next stakes. **(2) Trust built incrementally** — one honest trust-engine increment short of a gate; recurrence is the engine's fuel and needs chapters. **(3) Bottleneck enforced** — showing what Arthur does *not* know and *can't* learn yet; the reader feeling his knowledge's edges is what makes revelations land. **(4) Ordinary economics advanced** — the 1K's ledger moved (rent, lost overtime, strained £600-breakfast economy, carried fatigue); grounding maintained. **(5) Contrast sharpened** — the ordinary beside the hidden so the hidden stays strange (three streets over, a Sweeper team processes someone's worst night while he chooses rice ball); the camouflage thesis made felt. **(6) Fair-play texture planted** — a small honest clue, no fanfare (the unfitting date, the wrong-year duplicate, the unfamiliar terminal in the access log); the archive engine's (§20) slow fuel. **(7) Latency registered** — a consequence quietly compounding (the tenth-of-a-second measurement written down; the hiring flag existing another chapter; the escrow at ~30% while everyone pretends otherwise); the invoice aging is a contribution.

A slow chapter making *none* of these isn't "atmospheric" — it's empty, and the FLAG AS POTENTIAL FILLER rule (§5) routes it here for verdict.

## §26. Anti-repetition audit

A thousand-chapter serial will repeat itself. Distinguish *motif* (deliberate recurrence with variation — the signature) from *mechanic* (unexamined reuse — the rot). Every batch's step-9 check runs the seven axes across the batch and trailing ~30 chapters:

1. **Structure** — the skeleton (§15's beats or the departure). Two investigation chapters both running HOOK→file-pulled→complication→handwriting-check→consequence repeat structurally even with different files.
2. **Conflict** — the opposition's shape (institutional vs. predatory vs. ordinary-life cost vs. internal). The gradient exists to rotate; pure institutional-pressure runs are gradient failures.
3. **Setting** — the rooms. B2–B3, the curb, the 1K, Willowmere are beloved, therefore most repeated. Legal only when the place's *meaning* changed (curb as free choice vs. curb as exposed meeting point).
4. **Emotional beat** — the engineered feeling. Three consecutive dread-engineered chapters aren't three times as dreadful — they're numb.
5. **Mystery mechanism** — *how* the mystery moves (another file pulled, another witness, another handwriting check). The ten sources (§20) exist to rotate the mechanism.
6. **Antagonist behavior** — what the opposition *does*. 102 ACTIVE conflicts exist so behavior varies; a faction that only "makes an offer" is a vending machine.
7. **Resolution** — how chapters close (another authentication, another voided transaction, another refusal). ENDING TYPE (§23) audited for variety; all-hooks means hooks stop hooking.

**Cross-reference.** The narrative-level counterpart is retention SOP §57 (ANTI-FORMULA CHAPTER RULE): the ten previous-three-chapters comparison dimensions at prose stage; §52 (INVESTIGATION REPETITION CONTROL) at method level; §51 (movement-variety) at purpose level. All three distinguish motif from mechanic the same way this section does.

**Motif vs. mechanic.** *Motif*: the plan names it deliberate and states the variation — the mirror avoided again, but this time costing a specific telling; the file pulled again, but the metadata is the discovery; the dinner again, but the silences heavier (ARC III→V). Requires naming *what's different this time* — recurrence with a delta. *Mechanic*: no nameable delta — same beat/shape/feeling because it worked before. Mechanics patched by varying ≥2 axes; motifs protected and tracked in the batch record (active motifs noted so later batches vary or retire them).

## §27. Chapter end-state rule

Every plan answers, in component L and the §23 end-state-adjacent fields: **"WHAT IS DIFFERENT AFTER THIS CHAPTER?"** — specific, traceable, as a delta. Honest "nothing" = filler (§5), except: "nothing changed *except* the invoice was filed and priced" is an aftermath chapter, and the filed invoice *is* the delta.

**The ten acceptable answers** (valid with ≥1, stated specifically): **(1)** New information held by Arthur — a knowledge-map delta (○→◐, ◐→●) on a named SECRET/fact (e.g., both files at the jurisdiction seam). **(2)** New information held by the reader but not Arthur — within the reader-lead rule (≤1 phase, except the two ironies) (e.g., the reader watches the page's bidding first). **(3)** A relationship changed — trust increment, gate approached/passed, friendship's honest floor built or cracked; named with its rule/watch. **(4)** A mystery advanced, seeded, or closed — ID moved with verb and window, including deliberate withholding (the question now *felt* as withheld). **(5)** A power position changed — Use priced, ratchet fraction measured, escrow model recalculated, brake respected at cost; understanding or price per §G. **(6)** A faction position shifted — repricing registered, tripwire fired/armed, autonomy verdict honored; the faction's movement. **(7)** A consequence activated, compounded, or paid — invoice moved between states, creditor named (escrow tested, fatigue carried, absence noticed). **(8)** The world-state changed — a checkpoint thread moved (Choir succession, Drowndust market, tide-pressure readings). **(9)** An emotional state changed — named belief/attachment/cost-threshold moved (guilt economy repriced, the dinner's peace made costlier, the mirror's avoidance given a new reason). **(10)** An ordinary-life position changed — shift calendar, finances, fatigue ledger, dinner calendar, the curb's exposure status; the 1K's continuity moved.

**The FLAG rule.** A plan whose delta maps to none of the ten — or maps abstractly ("things feel different") — is **FLAGGED**: back to §22 step 11 (demote to micro, merge with a neighbor, re-plan with a genuine delta). §5 asks what would be lost if the chapter were removed; §27 asks what the chapter *claims* changed; the two answers must agree.

## §30. Failure modes

Sixteen known modes. Each: **DETECTION** (which check, which file, which field) + **CORRECTION** (planning stage, §22 step 11 — never improvised at prose). All run in the step-10 audit and the §24 diagnostic; the responsible dimension is named.

1. **Filler inflation.** Chapters existing because the batch needed ten. *Detection:* the value test (§5); the §27 FLAG rule; early warning — >2 chapters WATCH on FUNCTION per batch. *Correction:* demote to micro, merge with a neighbor, or re-plan with a genuine function — never delete (deletion breaks causality, §18).
2. **Mystery reveal acceleration.** Reveals before their windows — spending fuel early. *Detection:* every movement vs. REVEAL_ORDER.md windows + D-table's 24 edges; the reveal budget (~1 major/25 ch P2–P3, ~1/40 P4); L5 protection windows. *Correction:* demote to the legal form (reveal → partial → seed → withheld-with-fair-play-clue); move the full reveal into its window; keep the §3 purpose with smaller mystery movement.
3. **Protagonist knowledge leakage.** Arthur knowing what he can't legally know — the most corrosive failure. *Detection:* the three-state separation (INFORMATION_KNOWLEDGE_MAP.md — every movement as ●/◐/○ across R/D/AL/AN/GF); the reader-lead rule (≤1 phase except SECRET-007/015); the five archive requirements (§20) — "he just knows" is leakage until proven otherwise. *Correction:* revert to the legal state and re-earn it legally (investigation, the wrong file next to the right file, a priced conversation) — or move the chapter past the legal discovery point.
4. **Power inflation.** Capabilities growing faster/cheaper/cleaner than allowed. *Detection:* the 8 brakes (STORY_ENGINE.md §10) per movement; the stage-vs-Use ledger (7 stages, 12 Uses in discovery order — ARC_DEPENDENCY_GRAPH.md §3 maps stages to arcs); the 16-item no-shortcut list. *Correction:* roll the Use to its legal stage window and price it, or convert "new capability" into its legal form — understanding, threat, information (the flare *threatened* is Stage-5-legal; *spent* is Stage-6-gated).
5. **Faction reset.** A faction forgetting its interests/history/verdicts for convenience. *Detection:* autonomy verdicts (28/28 autonomous, 0 Arthur-required; the Arthur-free test — ARC_DEPENDENCY_GRAPH.md §4); the PEAK discipline (ARC_ARCHITECTURE.md §13.3); each faction's stated doctrine. *Correction:* restore its independent trajectory — the beat becomes the faction pursuing its own interest with Arthur as instantiation point, never decision-maker; needed help becomes *priced and self-interested* (the Ledger does trades, never favors).
6. **Relationship reset.** Trust engines losing progress. *Detection:* six rules + dinner rule + 7 watches as state checks (ROMANCE_ARCHITECTURE.md §0; audit §16); the 15 gates (ARC_DEPENDENCY_GRAPH.md §5). *Correction:* restore the recorded trust position and write *from* it; needed regression must be a priced event (betrayal, revelation, choice) with its own consequence — never quiet forgetting.
7. **Continuity drift.** Small inconsistencies accumulating. *Detection:* steps 5–7 state reads vs. the §23 CHARACTER STATE CHANGES / FACTION ELEMENTS / ROMANCE ELEMENTS fields; the batch record as source of truth (never memory). *Correction:* patch the plan to the recorded state — the record always wins over the new plan's convenience.
8. **Timeline drift.** Chapters floating free of the calendar. *Detection:* §23 TIMEmessaging app vs. EVENTS.md, EVENT-079 (March 2017) as fixed anchor; shift/dinner/*New Year* calendars as secondary clocks. *Correction:* re-seat the chapter; if its beats need another date, move the chapter — never the canon date.
9. **Character personality drift.** Acting out of character — usually toward generic protagonist behavior. *Detection:* the personality canon (ROMANCE_ARCHITECTURE.md §1: meticulous, dry-humored, conflict-avoidant but stubborn about facts; "professionally unequipped to parse" romantic interest; brave the way clerks are brave — he will not run, he will *document*); the internal-arc invariants (STORY_ENGINE.md §3: no power-shaped growth; guilt economy → responsibility; masking fatigue permanent; the archivist's deformation managed, never cured; never a different person). *Correction:* re-voice through the clerk's epistemology — measure it, file it, avoid the confrontation, be stubborn about the fact later.
10. **Repeated chapter structures.** The serial in a groove. *Detection:* the §26 audit — seven axes across the batch + trailing ~30 chapters; the §15 run-length rule (≤3 consecutive identical types, named reason required). *Correction:* vary ≥2 axes, or name the repetition as motif with its delta (§26).
11. **Artificial cliffhangers.** Stapled-on hooks: the threat that wasn't in the room, the purposeless "meanwhile." *Detection:* the §18 test — NEXT CHAPTER DEPENDENCY derivable from actual consequences; the hook's raw material present in §23 TURNING POINT and CONSEQUENCE. *Correction:* replace with a consequence-derived hook (the invoice, the exposure, the forced decision) or label a transition and end in honest rest.
12. **Exposition dumping.** Information as lecture: the explaining character, the self-reading file. *Detection:* the §20 five requirements — any movement missing investigation, interpretation, verification, risk, or external information is dumping until proven otherwise; §24 INFORMATION flags "legal but inefficient" as WATCH. *Correction:* route through the clerk's method — appraisal, collation, authentication — with the seeking, the reading-work, and the price on the page.
13. **Action inflation.** Action beyond the series' grammar — Arthur winning fights, chases resolving mysteries, violence substituting for documentation. *Detection:* the §15 action rule (evasion and endurance; victories documentation-shaped); the brakes (beyond-stage capability is failure mode 4 in another coat). *Correction:* convert — the chase becomes the evasion costing the shift's plausible deniability; the confrontation becomes the authentication (he sets the terms; the room answers); the unwinnable fight becomes the file he *can* write.
14. **Artificial romance.** Romance beats before their evidence — intimacy as plot device. *Detection:* the 15 mystery gates (ARC_DEPENDENCY_GRAPH.md §5); the dinner rule (Rule 5); the six rules structurally (Rule 1: no deceived partner; Rule 4: the handler is always in the room). *Correction:* push the beat to its legal window, or re-resolve into the configuration's honest non-romantic form (STORY_ENGINE.md §7 — same rule as for unchosen configurations).
15. **Forgotten consequences.** Invoices unpaid, exposures unpriced, latencies dropped — the quietest failure. *Detection:* CONSEQUENCE activation-state tracking (component K; §23) — every activated consequence in a justified state (latent/compounding/due/paid) at each batch's step-1 read; the step-10 ledger reconciliation. *Correction:* re-attach — pay it (invoice arrives as aftermath), compound it (name the delay's price), or explicitly transfer it (another creditor picks it up — the transfer priced). Never silently dropped.
16. **Archive omniscience.** The archive delivering complete truth without the five requirements. *Detection:* the ARCHIVE ≠ OMNISCIENCE doctrine (§20) per revelation; the SECRET-019 standard (method specific and limited, never "the system sees everything") for institutional-knowledge beats. *Correction:* rebuild through the five requirements — the seeking, the reading-work, the outside check, the price (exposure, hours, revocable access), the external information. If the truth can't survive them, it wasn't earned — withhold honestly (§3: create uncertainty) until it can be.

## §31. Open parameters & inheritance

### The four open preconditions (NOT resolved here)
