# 14 — LAW ENFORCEMENT

> CANON: There are two polices in every signatory state: the one the public sees, and the one that reads Tide-marks. The first handles the aftermath; the second handles the cause. The handoff between them — a beat cop's report that the Veil will eat within hours — is the most failure-prone inch of the entire legal system.

## 14.1 Normal police vs. supernatural police

**Beat police** are first contact and know almost nothing. A patrol officer who walks into an active Seep will, within hours, remember *a weird call, probably nothing* — the Veil does the redacting. What survives: the body, the broken window, the insurance claim. Departments in high-seep cities (Jakarta, Lagos, São Paulo, Naples, Ravenhurst) train officers in **exposure drills**: *write it down immediately, call the quiet desk, do not discuss it at the station.* The notebook matters more than the memory.

**Albion's capillary network.** Albion's beat police are the world's most systematic first-contact layer: **local police boxes** put officers within walking distance of nearly every urban block, and the neighbourhood association reporting culture means the Division hears about a strange smell on the third floor before the building's own management does. Patrol officers file standardized suspicious-person/object reports; in high-seep precincts these are auto-copied to the **county quiet desk**, which copies the SMD — the local police box is the capillary, the quiet desk the vein, the Division the heart. **The mechanism (stated):** the neighbourhood association's **circular notice board** doubles as a low-grade information-control channel — the disaster-drill schedule on Arthur's apartment-block notice board is also a sweep schedule; the garbage-sorting rules encode what gets reported and to whom. Nobody is recruited, nobody is briefed — the neighborhood association does what it has always done, and the Division reads the board the way it reads everything else: as terrain. (This is also why the neighbourhood association notice board is the first thing a Sweeper team photographs on arrival.) (The Division's org-chart view — its three arms, Operations, Census Administration, Liaison — is in [06_GOVERNMENTS.md](06_GOVERNMENTS.md), GOV-006; this section is the street-level view of the same body.) SMD field officers hold judicial-police appointments — the arrest power the National Police Agency resents (see [Governments](06_GOVERNMENTS.md), GOV-006). By the standing compromise, the Division arrests on threshold grounds and hands ordinary criminal charges to the **county police**; the handoff is where the two chains fight, every time.

**Quiet desks / threshold units** are the bridge: detectives with Lantern support, classification authority, and a direct line to the national agency. They don't patrol; they *inherit* — every anomalous incident report in the precinct funnels to them within 24 hours, before the Veil finishes its work.

**National agencies** ([Governments](06_GOVERNMENTS.md)) handle T3+, cross-jurisdiction cases, and anything with a vector the quiet desk can't hold. The relationship is chronically tense: the agency thinks the police are slow; the police think the agency steals their cases and redacts the files.

**The chain-of-custody problem:** evidence that degrades (static rot — [World Rules](02_WORLD_RULES.md)) and witnesses who forget (the Veil) mean supernatural cases have the shortest evidence half-life in criminal law. The quiet desks' motto, stenciled in half the world's threshold offices: *"The scene is freshest when it's strangest."*

## 14.2 Anomaly investigation units & Lantern forensics

A standard AIU (Anomaly Investigation Unit): a lead detective, two Lantern forensic examiners, a field classifier with provisional-vector authority, and a containment liaison. Their toolkit:

- **Tide-mark reading.** Lantern examiners read the residual impressions of the past at a scene ([Supernatural System](03_SUPERNATURAL_SYSTEM.md)). What they read is **residue and low-fidelity surface impressions** — the Undertow's associative memory of the event, not a recording: they show what the Undertow "remembers," which is associative, not chronological. An examiner can tell you *a man bled here feeling betrayed*; they cannot always tell you the man's name. Deep, high-fidelity replay does not exist — no examiner has ever pulled more than the surface, and the examiners who claim otherwise are the forensics scandals of the hidden world.
- **Rot-dating.** Static rot is proportional to Tide pressure ([World Rules](02_WORLD_RULES.md)) — forensic labs use the *corruption gradient* in seized electronics to estimate the event's intensity. A melted sensor is a measurement.
- **Erosion toxicology.** Fathom estimation from the accused or the victim (Stillwater metabolites, Veil-integrity markers). Standard in Resonance-assisted crime; the results go to sentencing ([CASE-003](#145-landmark-cases)).

**Limits examiners will testify to under oath:** Tide-marks can't be replayed, can't be subpoenaed twice (each reading degrades the impression), and can't distinguish a memory of the event from a memory of *fearing* the event. **Archives store the examiner's written report, never the impression itself** — there is nothing to store; the impression degrades with every reading and is gone within days. The profession's ethics code — the **Lantern Oath** — requires examiners to state their uncertainty in Fathom-equivalent confidence bands. Examiners who don't are the forensics scandals of the hidden world.

## 14.3 The law, by subject

> CANON: **The Reed doctrine.** The threshold legal framework is complete and self-consistent — *except where the tether touches it*. Arthur Reed's existence is the case the law cannot process: the trafficking premise misdescribes the physics (14.3(2)), the succession vacuum admits no title, the C-A filing is an administrative convenience. The doctrine is **jurisprudence, not statute** — courts *recognize* the gap; legislatures are forbidden from "fixing" it (see the attempted-trafficking acknowledgment in 14.3(2) — the law criminalizes what the physics voids, and admits it). A future Tribunal that legislates the tether would end the theme; the law's completeness *breaking* at the protagonist is load-bearing by design.

**1. Ability use.** Licensing by discipline. Tide-combat licenses are the most restricted civil licenses on earth; Bone-medical licenses require clinical supervision; **Choir is the most regulated discipline on earth** — unlicensed Choir use is a prison offense in 140+ countries ([Power System](04_POWER_SYSTEM.md)). **Choir channeling in elections and referenda is an Accords offense prosecuted by the Tribunal** ([INTL-002](07_INTERNATIONAL_RELATIONS.md)): campaigns in signatory states are Lantern-monitored, debates are screened, and a candidate caught seeding faces disqualification *and* a Tribunal docket — the one electoral crime the Compact treats as an attack on the settlement system itself (see [09_CURRENCIES.md](09_CURRENCIES.md) 9.5 on market-manipulation penalties). Hollow channeling without registration is a strict-liability offense in most signatories (the Slipping risk). The general principle: *channeling is legal; unlicensed consequential channeling is not* — where "consequential" is defined by vector thresholds, so the [Classification Office](05_ANOMALY_CLASSIFICATION.md) indirectly writes criminal law.

**2. Anomaly possession, ownership, inheritance.** [Ledger](07_INTERNATIONAL_RELATIONS.md) title governs T2+/C-B+; below that, national law varies from licensed ownership (US, Indonesia) to state monopoly (China, Russia). **Person-attached anomalies cannot be Ledger-titled** — a tether like the Laggard ([ANOMALY-001](28_ANOMALIES.md)) is not ownable property: the holder retains full personhood, and any "sale" of the anomaly is in law a sale *of the holder*, prosecuted as **trafficking** under Accords law. Because the transfer cannot be engineered ([29_PROTAGONIST_ANOMALY.md](29_PROTAGONIST_ANOMALY.md) B.8), the offense is necessarily *attempted* trafficking — the physics voids every transaction the law criminalizes, which does not void the crime; completed harm to the holder is prosecuted as crimes against the person. Non-severable tether succession is *legally invisible*: the death-proximity transfer creates no title, no probate (CASE-004 excludes non-property), no license category, and no recipient reporting duty — the Division's C-A filing of the Laggard is an administrative convenience, not a legal regime, and future drafters must not "fix" this gap by legislating it. (Enforcement varies — the Red Ledger exploits the variance, which is why its Factor keeps a buyer list for things that cannot legally be bought.) **Inheritance** follows [CASE-004](#145-landmark-cases): anomalies are inheritable property, but the heir must qualify for the license the anomaly requires — a T3 object inherited by an unlicensed civilian is held in state custody until they qualify or sell. The probate courts of Geneva, Jakarta, and London handle more anomaly estates than anywhere else. "Finders" law: an unregistered anomaly you find is yours *after* you report it and it's vectored — failure to report is the offense, not the finding. **Penalty:** forfeit of the find plus an administrative fine scaled to the anomaly's class (C-B+: forfeit and fine; C-A and above: forfeit, fine, and referral — an unreported C-A is treated as an unlicensed containment operation). The penalty escalates to criminal prosecution if the unreported find causes harm — which is why the Gray Market's "found, not bought" defense fails in every jurisdiction that has tested it.

**4. Criminal rehabilitation (the missing mechanism).** The Compact has **no amnesty or rehabilitation protocol for criminal factions** — there is no *pentiti* regime, no truth-and-reconciliation track, no legal path by which a Gray Market network or syndicate "goes legitimate." The claims are claims: the Blackwater Syndicate's younger presidents *want* full legitimacy ([12_FACTIONS.md](12_FACTIONS.md), CRIM-007), the Koi Network's Precepts *order* a humanitarian pivot if the masquerade collapses (CRIM-001), the Lantern Bearers *bid* for legitimacy through the Accords Secretariat ([07_INTERNATIONAL_RELATIONS.md](07_INTERNATIONAL_RELATIONS.md), INTL-005) — and no one believes any of them, which is the story. The absence is deliberate: a real rehabilitation mechanism would require the Compact to admit the criminal economy is load-bearing, and no signatory's legislature will vote for that.

**3. Anomalous weapons.** Drowndust possession, manufacture, or use is a **war crime** under the 1949 Meridian Accords (ban made mandatory by the 1977 revision) — the hidden world's equivalent of chemical-weapons law, with the same enforcement gap. Borrowed-matter munitions sit in a legal gray zone the Compact has failed to close three times: the weapon reverts, the evidence reverts, and the law can't quite grip the 72 hours in between.

**4. Supernatural crime.** Resonance-assisted crime carries the **Tide uplift**: sentencing enhancements scaled to the tier of channeling used. The uplift is steepest for Choir offenses (the seeding statutes) and Hollow offenses (Slipping into a crime scene defeats every conventional security assumption). Corporate Resonance crime — precognition-assisted trading, seeded negotiations — is policed by Lantern-screened compliance regimes at every major exchange ([World Rules](02_WORLD_RULES.md)). > CANON: **Licensed commercial Choir use** (negotiation support, mediation, morale — the legal sliver) is subject to **pattern-of-outcome audits**: compliance Lanterns review deal outcomes statistically for the seeding signature (too-smooth concessions, too-uniform counterparty sentiment). A contract demonstrated to have been *influenced* — not merely touched — by unlicensed Choir use is **voidable** at the Tribunal's commercial chamber; the precedent line runs through [CASE-002](#145-landmark-cases) (Okafor): influence proven by backlash evidence unwinds the deal. The market prices this in: counterparty Choir-screening is a standard M&A warranty in 40+ jurisdictions.

**5. Civilian knowledge.** Possession of classified Tide knowledge is a strict-liability offense in most signatories — the "knowing too much" statutes. I-flag material is the hard core: possession of infohazard content is criminalized regardless of intent, because intent doesn't matter to an infohazard. Journalists, academics, and the Loud live in the penumbra of these laws; enforcement is selective, which is itself a policy instrument.

**6. Illegal experimentation.** Awakening experimentation is a **crime against humanity** under the 2017 Accords ([CASE-005](#145-landmark-cases)). The statute was written with the three known state programs in mind and applies to corporate and private actors equally. It is the only hidden-world offense for which the Tribunal claims universal jurisdiction — and the Vatican's Asylum Protocol is the only door out.

**7. Infohazards.** The "Ward Six" statutes (named for [ANOMALY-007](05_ANOMALY_CLASSIFICATION.md)): distribution of I-flag material is prosecuted as *reckless propagation*, with the V flag as an aggravator. Platforms have safe-harbor obligations — Pale Lantern Media's (CORP-006) noise-farming contracts are, legally, the compliance mechanism: the state pays a company to drown real leaks in fakes, and the company's liability shield depends on doing it well. The ugliest honest paragraph in media law.

## 14.4 Court admissibility: Tide-marks and seeding

**Tide-mark evidence** is admissible under the **two-source rule**: a Lantern examiner's reading is never sufficient alone; it must be corroborated by physical evidence, testimony, or a second examiner's independent reading. The rule exists because Tide-marks are impressions — and because the first decade of admissibility produced two wrongful convictions from examiners who read confidently and wrong. The Lantern Oath's confidence bands are now read to juries verbatim.

**Suggestion-seeding testimony** is presumptively **tainted**. Any testimony where seeding is alleged triggers a Lantern examination of the witness (warrant required; refusal draws an adverse inference but is not contempt — the compromise every jurisdiction reached independently). The **seeding defense** — *"I was seeded; the impulse wasn't mine"* — is the hidden world's insanity plea: recognized everywhere, successful almost nowhere, because [CASE-002](#145-landmark-cases) set the bar at *demonstrated* seeding with backlash evidence, and backlash evidence requires examining the *seeder*, who is usually absent, Drowned, or denying everything.

> CANON: The courts' working assumption — never stated in a judgment, visible in every acquittal rate — is that **Choir crime is under-punished by an order of magnitude**, because successful seeding is indistinguishable from ordinary bad judgment. The law knows it can't see the crime. It punishes what it can see and calls the rest deterrence.

**Seeding desks.** The deterrence that actually works is **friction, not punishment**: the major threshold agencies run *seeding desks* — small counter-seeding units attached to courts, command staffs, and corporate compliance departments ([13_MILITARY.md](13_MILITARY.md) 13.2). Their methodology: (1) **inoculation briefings** — training high-risk personnel to recognize the texture of an implanted impulse (the "is this mine?" drill); (2) **backlash-mark screening** — Lantern examination of *suspected seeders* for the distorted echo seeding always leaves ([02_WORLD_RULES.md](02_WORLD_RULES.md), I); (3) **post-incident audits** of decisions that smell seeded. Cases: the **2009 Okafor backlash-examination** (see [CASE-002](#145-landmark-cases) — the first court-ordered seeder examination); the **2017 Meridian House inoculation** (the Compact's own secretariat staff, briefed after a Choir-adjacent lobbying scandal); the **2020 Santara board audit** (corporate compliance desk, unlicensed — the Tribunal fined the company for *running* one without a warrant protocol, which tells you everything about the law's priorities).

> CANON: **Desk scale.** A seeding desk runs **4–8 Lanterns and clears ~200 screenings a year** — a rounding error against the caseload, which is the point: each examination costs the examiner Erosion-risk and the subject's trust, and the desk exists to make seeding *expensive to attempt*, not to catch every attempt.

## 14.5 Landmark cases

**CASE-001 — Halvorsen v. Bureau of Threshold Affairs (US, 2001).** The teacup case (see [Classification](05_ANOMALY_CLASSIFICATION.md), 5.6). Established the **Classification Appeals Panel**: any owner can challenge a vector within 90 days, with evidence standards for filings above T2. The hidden world's most-cited administrative-law precedent — and the reason every agency's classifiers file *up* with documentation instead of *down* with confidence.

**CASE-002 — R v. Okafor (UK, 2009).** A Choir T3 seeded a suggestion into a business partner during a negotiation; the partner later committed fraud and claimed the seeding defense. The court admitted backlash-evidence standards: the defense must demonstrate *the seeder's* backlash echo, not merely the defendant's impulse — which required examining Okafor, who had Drowned the previous year. Conviction upheld; the judgment's famous line: *"The law cannot acquit on the basis of a ghost's headache."* Every Commonwealth jurisdiction cites Okafor for the proposition that the seeding defense requires a living seeder.

**CASE-003 — People v. Marchetti (Tribunal, 2013).** The first Attuned extradition: an Italian Tide T4 who leveled a warehouse in Marseille during a Gray Market dispute, then fled to a non-extradition posture in the Sahel.

> CANON: **The leverage.** Marchetti was not caught — he was *sold*: his Sahel protectors traded him to a joint French threshold-unit / Ledger-contractor arrest team after the Ledger froze his soundings escrow (he couldn't pay his guards) and the Tribunal signaled it would seek a depth-adjusted sentence calibrated to Drown him outright. He surrendered in exchange for the calibrated sentence.

The Tribunal's judgment created **depth-adjusted sentencing**: Marchetti's Stillwater sentence was calibrated to cost him 22 Fathoms — the court heard medical evidence that 30 would have been a death sentence by installments, and 10 would have been "a sabbatical." The sealed Fathom registry of the convicted dates from this case. Defense counsel called it "punishment measured in selfhood"; the court agreed and did it anyway.

**CASE-004 — In re: The Widow's Teacup (Ledger Arbitration, 1996).** Not the Halvorsen teacup — an earlier, funnier-titled precedent: a Genoese widow's Object Seep (a thimble that mended what it touched, `MV-T2/C-A`) inherited by three children, one unlicensed. The Ledger's arbitration created modern anomaly probate: **title vests in the heir, custody vests in the qualified** — the unlicensed heir owns the thimble but the state holds it until they qualify or sell. Cited in every inheritance dispute since; the reason Arthur's employer maintains a probate desk.

**CASE-005 — State v. Chen (China, 2006).** A Vesper Pharmaceuticals (CORP-002) subcontractor ran Awakening trials on debtors in Guangdong — eleven subjects, nine Drownings. Prosecuted under Chinese law before the 2017 Accords codified the international offense; the case's evidence file became the drafting record for the crimes-against-humanity statute. Chen's defense — that the subjects consented — produced the statute's most important clause: **consent is no defense to Awakening experimentation**, because the Veil and the power imbalance make informed consent impossible. The Jade Office's hardliners cite Chen whenever the integrationists get idealistic.

**CASE-006 — The Surabaya Ledger Litigation (Indonesia, 2017–2020).** The civil aftermath of the Surabaya misclassification ([Classification](05_ANOMALY_CLASSIFICATION.md), 5.6): victims' families sued the BPF for the under-classification that killed fourteen people. The court's judgment is the hidden world's leading case on **state liability for vectors**: a classification is an administrative act, and a negligent one creates liability — but the court capped damages at the BPF's annual field budget, noting that bankrupting the agency would kill more people than the misclassification did. Ratna Kusuma (CHAR-005) framed the judgment and hung it in the budget office. The plaintiffs' lawyer called it "justice, discounted for poverty." Both readings are correct.

## 14.6 The street level

For Arthur's novel: the Ravenhurst street level is the **Ravenshire County Police** threshold unit — the "quiet desk" — working out of the county HQ annex with eight detectives, three Lantern examiners on rotation from the SMD's Ravenhurst field office (the loan arrangement both sides complain about), and a classification clerk who files provisional vectors between coffee runs. The local police-box network feeds them: every suspicious-person/object report with a Tide smell lands on their desk within 24 hours, which is why Ravenhurst clears a higher share of its caseload than any other county desk in Albion — the rest goes to NQA Ravenhurst's Sweeper contractors, or into the drawer where the D-class files live. When Arthur's story collides with law enforcement, it will be with *these* people — detectives who have seen his branch's claim forms, an SMD liaison officer who reads his Census file with professional interest, and local patrol officers who know his route by name. The SMD's arrest powers mean the Division can take a case off the county desk at will; the desk's revenge is paperwork — every handoff arrives with the files the Division needs and none of the context it wants.

**Secondary-country note.** The KNF's Liwanag quiet desk still works out of the field-office annex in Sentro with six detectives, two Lantern examiners (one of whom is 61 Fathoms deep and everyone knows it), and a classification clerk who files provisional vectors between coffee runs. They clear about 70% of their caseload — the rest goes to Nusantara Quiet Assurance's Sweeper contractors, to Aisha Rahman's (CHAR-034) KNF-contracted Sweeper team staging from Kalsada's van yard, or into the drawer where D-class files live. When the story touches the Philippines, it will be with these people: competent, tired, and underfunded.
