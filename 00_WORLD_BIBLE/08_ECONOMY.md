## 8.1 The shape of the hidden economy

> CANON: The supernatural economy does not sit *beside* the normal economy. It sits *inside* it, the way a parasite sits inside a host: insurance, pharma, logistics, telecom, defense, and media companies whose supernatural business lines are booked as ordinary revenue.

Three structural facts govern everything below:

1. **Anomalies are non-fungible** ([02_WORLD_RULES.md](02_WORLD_RULES.md), II). There is no commodity market in "anomaly units." Every high-value transaction is a bespoke auction or a bespoke contract. This keeps the market fragmented, relationship-driven, and permanently resistant to financialization â€” which is exactly why it hasn't been captured by Wall Street.
2. **Information is the scarce resource, not power** (02, II). The highest-margin business in the hidden world is not selling force; it is selling *answers*: What is this Seep's Rule? Who else knows? What did the Tide-marks show?
3. **Erosion is a depreciation schedule on human capital** ([04_POWER_SYSTEM.md](04_POWER_SYSTEM.md), 4.4). Every Attuned employee is an asset with a known, irreversible decay curve. This single fact shapes salaries, contracts, insurance, and military doctrine more than any law.

## 8.2 The ten supernatural sectors

### 8.2.1 Anomaly insurance â€” Quiet policies

> CANON: **Quiet Insurance** is the oldest and largest supernatural industry. It does not insure against Seeps the way fire insurance insures against fire. It insures against the *aftermath of exposure*: memory care, trauma treatment, property decontamination, legal liability when the Veil Reflex makes your witnesses useless, and "Quiet leave" (paid time off after exposure, standard in 40+ countries).

How it works:

- **Retail Quiet policies** are bundled into ordinary products: travel insurance, corporate liability, homeowner's riders, gig-worker platform coverage. Most policyholders never know the supernatural clause exists. [CORP-011](11_CORPORATIONS.md) Nusantara Quiet Assurance sells ~4 million such policies across Indonesia, mostly as riders nobody reads.
- **Claims** are the industry's secret engine. A spike in "gas leak / mass hysteria / vandalism" claims in one postal code is often the first signal of an unregistered Seep â€” which is why insurers run the best civilian detection analytics on earth (see [27_TECHNOLOGY.md](27_TECHNOLOGY.md): this is what Arthur's employer actually does with his night-shift records).
- **Reinsurance** concentrates at [CORP-004](11_CORPORATIONS.md) Meridian Re, which backstops ~60% of the world's Quiet risk. Meridian Re's claims database is arguably the most complete Seep registry in existence â€” and the most jealously guarded dataset on the planet.

> CANON: **The Meridian Re loss triangle** (the numbers every actuary in the hidden world can recite): ~2.1M Quiet claims per year globally; mean service-denominated severity ~$11k; annual aggregate loss ~$23B; primary-carrier expense and risk load â†’ ~$28â€“35B gross primary premium (the sector line in 8.1). The reinsurer's book is ~2â€“4% of the supernatural-adjacent economy, not a rival to it: reinsurance's power is *leverage* ($1 of Meridian Re capital backstops ~$8 of primary risk), not premium volume. ([11_CORPORATIONS.md](11_CORPORATIONS.md) CORP-004.)
- **Pricing** is actuarial and ruthless: premiums rise with local Tide pressure. Jakarta's premiums have risen ~40% since 2018 (High Tide pricing). Nobody at the retail level is told why.

> CANON: Quiet Insurance is load-bearing for the masquerade. Without it, every Seep leaves a trail of bankrupt witnesses, untreated trauma, and lawsuits â€” exactly the paper trail that would end secrecy.

> CANON: **Quiet payouts are service-denominated, not cash.** There are no jackpot payouts for seeing the impossible: a Quiet claim pays in *services* â€” memory care, trauma treatment, decontamination, legal liability cover, Quiet leave â€” booked and delivered through the insurer's provider network. > CANON: The fraud model polices *claimants* â€” but the bible's documented frauds are all broker- and underwriter-side: EVENT-084's auctioned Rule, the Factor's inquiry book, Meridian Re's quiet Gray Market retrocession ([11_CORPORATIONS.md](11_CORPORATIONS.md) CORP-004, secret 3). **How $140â€“200B hides from auditors (CANON).** It hides in plain sight, one layer at a time: **auditors audit *numbers*, not *causes*.** The Big Four sign off on Meridian Re's reserves because the numbers reconcile â€” *why* the claims cluster in certain postal codes is actuarial judgment, and actuarial judgment is not the auditor's job. ## 8.7 Why the powerful don't become billionaires

> CANON: There is no Attuned billionaire whose fortune comes from channeling. This is overdetermined â€” five independent mechanisms, any one of which would probably suffice:

1. **Erosion clocks** ([04_POWER_SYSTEM.md](04_POWER_SYSTEM.md) 4.4). Wealth compounds over decades; Erosion compounds faster. A T4 who channels for profit is spending irreplaceable selfhood for replaceable money. The rational strategy is to channel *as little as possible* â€” which caps income at professional, not plutocratic, levels. The few who ignored this are case studies, not role models: "lucky" traders keep Drowning (02, II).
2. **Seismograph visibility** (02, II). Any channeling above ~T3 is seen globally within minutes.
