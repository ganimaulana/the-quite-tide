# 07 — INTERNATIONAL RELATIONS

> CANON: The hidden world has a government. It is not a world government — it is a **treaty secretariat with a budget, a court, a bank-registry, and no army**. Everything the [Meridian Compact](07_INTERNATIONAL_RELATIONS.md) (INTL-001) achieves, it achieves because its members find compliance cheaper than defiance. When that arithmetic flips, the Compact loses. It has lost before.

## 7.1 The institutions

### INTL-001 — The Meridian Compact

**Nature:** A secret treaty organization headquartered in Geneva ("the Meridian House," a warded 19th-century banking palace on the Rue du Rhône — the address is known; what happens inside is not). Founded by the 1949 Meridian Accords; 191 signatories as of 2024. Three states are non-signatories: North Korea (COUNTRY-020), Eritrea, and Myanmar.

- **The great-power payoff (CANON):** the Accords hold because each great power gets something it cannot get alone. The **United States** gets Seismograph placement and overflight coverage over everyone else's territory (the network's eyes are American-hosted where it matters); **China** gets process control — committee chairs, classification-standard drafting, the veto-by-arrears that keeps new obligations from outrunning its Census capacity; **Russia** gets grandfathering — its legacy arrays, its closed cities, and its pre-1989 filings are exempt from the re-audit standards that apply to everyone else. Each payoff is unstated in the treaty text and unmistakable in the practice; each is also the reason no great power leaves — the price of exit is losing the payoff while everyone else keeps theirs.
**Structure:**
- **The Council of Signatories** — one vote per state; meets annually in closed session. Real decisions are made in weighted committees (budget, classification standards, sanctions), where the great powers hold de facto vetoes by threatening arrears.
- **The Secretariat** — Secretary-General **Fatoumata Diallo** (CHAR-008), Senegalese career diplomat. By convention the SG always comes from a small or neutral state — the great powers distrust each other too much to let one of their own hold the gavel. Diallo is the fourth SG; her predecessors were Uruguayan, Norwegian, and Ghanaian.
- **The Classification Office** — owns the [Meridian Vector](05_ANOMALY_CLASSIFICATION.md) standard; ratifies vectors; hears classification appeals (post-[CASE-001](14_LAW_ENFORCEMENT.md)).
- **The Medical Directorate** — maintains the Fathom scale, Stillwater protocols, Erosion-care standards; the only body whose authority rests on expertise rather than treaty text, which makes it the most trusted and least powerful organ.
- **The Arbitration Court** — binding arbitration between signatories on Ledger title, classification disputes, and cross-border incidents.
- **The Seismograph Authority** (INTL-004) — operates under Compact charter (see below).

**Powers:** Authorship of the Accords; classification ratification; [Ledger](07_INTERNATIONAL_RELATIONS.md) oversight; sanctions (see 7.5); arbitration with binding awards.

**Limits:** (1) **No standing army** — enforcement is borrowed (the [Joint Containment Command](07_INTERNATIONAL_RELATIONS.md), INTL-006, is contributed forces). (2) **No power over great powers in practice** — the US, China, and Russia comply when it suits them; the Compact's leverage is Ledger access and Seismograph data, both of which the great powers can partially replace. (3) **The budget** — dues are perpetually in arrears, and the largest debtor is the United States, which uses arrears as a policy instrument. (4) **Verification** — the deepfake dividend (see [World Rules](02_WORLD_RULES.md)) means the Compact cannot prove violations to anyone outside the closed verification protocols; most enforcement runs on witnessed, in-person, multi-sensor evidence that is expensive to produce. (5) **The High Tide** — Seep frequency has tripled since 2000; the Compact's staffing has grown 40%. The arithmetic is visible to everyone in the Meridian House, and discussed in the corridors, never in session.

### INTL-002 — The Tribunal

**Remit:** Extradition and trial of Attuned criminals across signatory borders. Seat: a sealed wing of the Peace Palace, The Hague.

**Procedure:** Built around the deepfake dividend — no video evidence is accepted without witnessed, in-person, multi-sensor corroboration. Defendants are held in Anchor restraints; Lantern examination of witnesses requires a warrant and is never the sole basis for conviction (the **two-source rule**, see [Law Enforcement](14_LAW_ENFORCEMENT.md)).

**Sentencing:** **Depth-adjusted.** The Tribunal's most original jurisprudence ([CASE-003](14_LAW_ENFORCEMENT.md), *People v. Marchetti*, 2013): punishment is calibrated to Erosion — a Stillwater sentence that costs a T2 five Fathoms is a fine; the same sentence that costs a T4 thirty Fathoms is a death sentence by installments. The Tribunal maintains a sealed Fathom registry of the convicted.

**Limits:** Jurisdiction covers signatories only. And it stops at the Vatican's door — the **Asylum Protocol** ([06_GOVERNMENTS.md](06_GOVERNMENTS.md), GOV-010) is the one carve-out the Tribunal cannot cross. Two Attuned war criminals currently shelter under it; the Tribunal's docket lists their cases as *"deferred — jurisdictional."* Everyone knows what that means.

### INTL-003 — The Ledger

**Remit:** The anomaly asset registry, Geneva. Every anomaly at **T2+/C-B+ or higher** must be registered: Rule description (redacted tiers), vector, title holder, containment location. The Ledger is simultaneously a property deed office, an auction house regulator, and — through its numbered accounts — the hidden world's private bank.

**What it does:** Adjudicates title (including inheritance — [CASE-004](14_LAW_ENFORCEMENT.md)); licenses auctions (the Trieste disaster, [EVENT-062](#76-historical-incidents), was an *unregistered* auction, which is why the raid was legal); maintains the unit of account for anomaly-asset transfers, **the sounding** (SND) — see [Currencies](09_CURRENCIES.md).

**The political economy:** Registration is mandatory for signatories and universal in practice — because unregistered anomalies cannot be insured, and uninsured anomalies cannot be legally held. The Ledger's real power is not its vaults but its **title system**: an anomaly without Ledger title is unsellable, uninsurable, and seizable. Gray Markets exist precisely in the gap between "seizable" and "seized."

### INTL-004 — The Seismograph Authority

**Remit:** Operation of the ~2,000-station global Tide-pressure detection network. Detects channeling above ~T3, unregistered Slipping, and active T4+ Seeps, worldwide, within minutes (see [World Rules](02_WORLD_RULES.md)).

**Data tiers:** Full members (dues current, sharing reciprocated) get the raw feed. Partial members get alerts. **Data cutoff is the Compact's most-used sanction** — silent, deniable, and immediately felt: a state that can't see T4 events in its own territory goes blind in days.

**The "who responds first" problem:** Detection is global and simultaneous; response is national and sequential. The Authority's alert goes to every signatory at once — which means covert operations above T3 are visible to everyone, and the diplomacy starts before the containment does. This is why large-scale Attuned combat is rare: not because it's impossible, but because it's *witnessed*.

### INTL-005 — The Accords Secretariat

Administers the treaty: revision conferences, signatory compliance reviews, the failed-state contingency fund (which pays for Somalia). The Secretariat's compliance reports are the hidden world's most boring and most consequential documents — a "yellow rating" precedes most sanctions by eighteen months.

### INTL-006 — Joint Containment Command (JCC)

Contributed forces — national Sweeper teams, containment engineers, and (at need) military units — under a rotating Compact command. Activation threshold: **T4+/Regional+**, or any E-class filing. Command rules: the territorial state leads unless it requests transfer; at T5+, JCC command is mandatory and the Council is notified. The JCC is the closest thing the hidden world has to an army, and every great power staffs its contribution with officers whose real job is watching the other contributors.

### INTL-007 — The Memorandum Group

Intelligence-sharing. The **Quiet Nine** (US, UK, Albion, Germany, France, Australia, Canada, South Korea, Singapore) share Lantern intelligence, Tide-mark archives, and Seismograph analysis at the highest tier. China and Russia run a **parallel bilateral** arrangement and share with the Group only through the Compact's formal floors. Compartmentalization is the point: the Group exists so that its members never have to show each other everything.

## 7.2 The treaty framework: four revisions

**1949 — The Meridian Accords (founding charter).** Written in six weeks by exhausted people who had just survived the 1940s (the war's unrecorded supernatural casualties are the Accords' silent preamble). Established: the secrecy obligation, national containment responsibility, the Census minimum, the Ledger, the Drowndust ban (in principle), the embryonic Tribunal, and the Compact itself. Mandated — but did not yet build — the Seismograph detection capability (constructed 1963–68). The founding compromise: *sovereignty first, coordination second.* Every limit the Compact has today was a price paid in 1949.

**1977 — The Quiet Night revision (mandatory political revision).** Forced by the **Quiet Night** (see [Terminology](36_TERMINOLOGY.md)) — the global near-breach that proved the 1949 framework couldn't see what it was supposed to contain. Made mandatory what 1949 had declared: universal Seismograph coverage (the built network, 1963–68, brought under binding reporting floors), the **Drowndust ban** (enforceable), the Tribunal's modernization begun — and, in an **unpublished protocol**, the Vatican's Asylum carve-out. The 1977 revision is why the modern system works; it is also when the system learned to keep secrets from itself.

**1979 — The Second Protocols (implementing).** The Quiet Night's implementing legislation, two years late. Completed the 1977 program: the Tribunal in its modern form, the [Joint Containment Command](07_INTERNATIONAL_RELATIONS.md) (INTL-006) chartered, Census recommendations hardened, the Drowndust ban re-ratified (and re-violated). The implementing protocols — not the 1977 declaration — are what field operators actually live under.

**2017 — The High Tide revision.** Forced by the numbers: Seep frequency tripling, Gray Markets industrializing, Sapient anomalies demanding (and sometimes seizing) standing. Added: information-sharing floors (widely evaded — see the Census table in [Governments](06_GOVERNMENTS.md)), **Sapient rights protocols** (bitterly controversial; Russia and China filed reservations), the Gray Market containment doctrine, and the criminalization of Awakening experimentation as a crime against humanity (see [CASE-005](14_LAW_ENFORCEMENT.md)). The 2017 revision is the one the great powers comply with least — and the one the small states cite most.

## 7.3 Cross-border anomaly protocol

> CANON: When a Seep moves across a border — or sits on one — jurisdiction follows four rules, in order:

1. **Territoriality.** The state of manifestation leads. Always the default, always the first argument.
2. **Arbitration.** Contested cases go to the Compact Arbitration Court, which rules within 30 days. The seep does not wait 30 days; interim control goes to whoever can physically hold the site, which is why rule 1 matters more than rule 2.
3. **Hot pursuit.** Sweeper teams may cross borders for **72 hours** with notification (not permission) to the territorial state, in active pursuit of a moving anomaly. Abuse of this rule is the single most common interstate complaint in the system.
4. **The flag follows the seep.** For continuously moving Seeps (drift ice, vessels, weather systems), the *origin* state retains lead unless it requests transfer — codified after the Bering Drift ([EVENT-041](#76-historical-incidents)). At T4+/Regional+, the JCC takes command regardless.

Sapient anomalies add a fifth, unwritten rule: *if it reaches a nunciature, the argument is over.* Nobody writes this down. Everybody knows it.

## 7.4 Covert wars and proxy conflicts

The hidden world has not had an open war between signatories. It has had everything else:

- **The Drowndust pipeline.** The Compact assesses — [STATUS: THEORY](00_INDEX.md), high confidence, officially unconfirmed — that four states stockpile Drowndust despite the 1977 ban. The accusations are ritualized: the US accuses Russia, Russia accuses the US, everyone accuses North Korea, and the precursors keep moving through the Somali corridor. The pipeline is both a weapons program and a diplomatic instrument — each side's stockpile justifies the other's.
- **Gray Market proxy fights.** The Ash Exchange (CRIM-003) and the Salt Road (CRIM-005) are battlegrounds where state buyers, corporate collectors, and criminal brokers collide under cutouts. The Trieste raid ([EVENT-062](#76-historical-incidents)) was the proxy war surfacing.
- **The consultant wars.** BTA franchise Sweepers, Jade Office work units, and Department 12 "advisors" keep meeting in third countries over the same anomalies, under different authorities, with incompatible rules of engagement. The JCC's after-action files have a standing category for it: *"multi-presence incident."*

> CANON: **No one nukes Seeps.** The proposal surfaces after every T5 — a warhead is cleaner than a regional Rule, runs the argument. The answer is [EVENT-031](25_TIMEmessaging app.md): **mass death creates thin places.** Hiroshima and Nagasaki tore thin places across both cities that took months of unacknowledged containment; every modeling run agrees a warhead on an active Seep trades one T5 for a *city-sized cluster of new ones*, plus the Tide-pressure of the mass casualty itself. It is Compact doctrine, great-power doctrine, and (per the 2017 revision's classified annex) *war-gamed doctrine that failed every test*: the cure is worse than the disease by orders of magnitude. The military option ends where containment begins — which is why the [Joint Containment Command](#76-historical-incidents) exists at all.
- **Choir diplomacy.** Suggestion-seeding in negotiations is the hidden world's open secret and the Memorandum Group's hardest problem: you cannot prove you weren't seeded, and you cannot prove the other side did it (backlash evidence is inadmissible in most forums — see [CASE-002](14_LAW_ENFORCEMENT.md)). The 2017 revision nearly collapsed over a seeding allegation in the Sapient-rights committee. The talks resumed after everyone agreed never to mention it again — which tells you how the hidden world handles its unsolvable problems.

## 7.5 Sanctions: the ladder

The Compact's coercive power, in escalating order:

1. **Censure** — a closed-session resolution. Costs nothing, signals everything.
2. **Ledger suspension** — the target's anomaly titles become uninsurable and unsellable. Economic, slow, devastating to Gray Market-adjacent states.
3. **Seismograph cutoff** — the target goes blind to T4+ events. Fast, frightening, and the reason most sanctions never need to go further.
4. **Tribunal referral** — for individuals; the Asylum Protocol excepted.
5. **JCC "assistance"** — the euphemism for intervention. Invoked twice: once in the Sahel (1990s, unacknowledged) and once — debated, never confirmed — in 2009. The threat is the policy.

> CANON: Sanctions have never been applied to a great power. The ladder is for small states, failed states, and individuals. Everyone in the Meridian House knows this; the small states resent it; the great powers rely on it. It is the system's load-bearing hypocrisy, and it holds because the alternative — a Compact that *tried* to sanction the United States — would not survive the attempt.

## 7.6 Historical incidents

> STATUS: PROVISIONAL — Drafter D owns the timeline ([25_TIMEmessaging app.md](25_TIMEmessaging app.md)). The EVENT IDs below are A's placeholders, claimed in the registry, pending D's audit.

**EVENT-041 — "The Bering Drift" (2009).** A T5 Place Seep — drift ice with a Rule (anything left on it for more than a day was *kept*: equipment, shelters, and eventually a listening post) — crossed the Bering Strait over eleven days. The BTA and Department 12 both claimed jurisdiction; both deployed; a US cutter and a Russian icebreaker spent nine days in a standoff neither government acknowledged. Resolved when the JCC took command under what became the "flag follows the seep" rule — the US, as origin state, retained lead, with a Russian liaison officer aboard the American command vessel, filing reports both governments pretended not to read. Consequences: the moving-seep protocol codified in 2017; a standing US–Russia deconfliction line for the Bering Strait that both sides still use and neither admits exists.

**EVENT-062 — "The Trieste Auction Raid" (2017).** An unregistered Ledger auction in Trieste's old port — fourteen lots, including two T4 objects — was raided under Compact authority six weeks after the 2017 revision. Present, under different authorities: BTA franchise Sweepers (contract authority), Department 12 operatives (hot pursuit, notified), Jade Office observers (observer status, creatively interpreted), and the sellers' private security. A firefight in a bonded warehouse; six dead; the catalog scattered — **two lots remain unaccounted for**, a fact the Compact's public-after-action report omits and its classified annex leads with. Consequences: the Gray Market containment doctrine in the 2017 Accords; Trieste's Gray Market moving further underground and further east; the two missing lots as the hidden world's most expensive unsolved problem.

**EVENT-073 — "The Sulu Transfer Dispute" (2021).** A Sapient organism Seep surfaced in disputed waters between Philippine, Malaysian, and Chinese claims. The Jade Office moved to contain; the Philippines requested JCC activation; the organism — demonstrating the novel problem-solving that earns the Sapient filing — reached a Vatican-flagged research vessel and **Monsignor Ferretti (CHAR-007) invoked the Asylum Protocol**. First open (inside the hidden world) use of the 1977 unpublished carve-out. The organism now resides in a Vatican extraterritorial facility; China has filed a Tribunal case that cannot proceed; the Philippines has filed a Ledger title claim the Ledger cannot adjudicate. The case sits in all three dockets as *"deferred."* It is the live crisis that will define Diallo's secretary-generalship — and the proof, if anyone needed it, that the 1977 compromise is still collecting its price.
