## 27.1 Why technology hasn't auto-discovered everything

> CANON: Four mechanisms, each sufficient alone, all operating together:

3. **The Seismograph data is classified.** The network *does* auto-detect â‰¥T3 events globally within minutes ([02_WORLD_RULES.md](02_WORLD_RULES.md), II). The data exists. It's just the most classified scientific dataset on earth. Civilian science can't discover what it can't see â€” and the few civilian researchers who've noticed the *gaps* (seismologists, mostly) get visited by polite people with publication advice.
4. **The Veil Reflex in researchers.** Scientists are human. The physicist whose detector recorded the impossible rationalizes it by morning â€” miscalibration, a glitch, "I'll re-run it." ## 27.3 AI + anomaly detection: what ML actually catches
