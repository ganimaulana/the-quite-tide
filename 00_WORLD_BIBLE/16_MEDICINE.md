# 16 — MEDICINE

Supernatural medicine: what happens to bodies and minds when the Undertow touches them, and the systems built to treat what normal medicine can't explain.

> CANON: Normal medicine works normally on supernatural injuries *except where the injury's Rule says otherwise*. A Tide burn is a burn — until the Rule says it only burns on Tuesdays, at which point dermatology yields to phenomenology.

## 16.1 Supernatural injuries

Field medicine recognizes five categories:

1. **Tide burns.** Direct Undertow-pressure tissue damage: cell death in patterns that ignore anatomy — spiral lesions, lesions that spell things, burns that *avoid* tattoos. Treatment: treat as thermal injury; debride; watch for Rule behavior (some Tide burns *recur* on schedule — the Rule, not the wound, is the disease).
2. **Rule-lacerations.** Injuries inflicted *by* a Seep's Rule: the stairwell that removes the thirteenth person's finger; the door that only opens for the bleeding. The wound is mundane; the *mechanism* isn't. Treatment is mundane; *prevention* is phenomenological (learn the Rule, don't be thirteenth).
3. **Reversion sickness.** Exposure to collapsing borrowed matter ([02_WORLD_RULES.md](02_WORLD_RULES.md), I): nausea, disorientation, and — in severe cases — partial pattern displacement (the patient's *shadow* reverts before they do; it grows back wrong). Rare, usually iatrogenic (someone built the borrowed bridge and stood on it too long).
4. **Erosion trauma (acute).** Channeling past one's safe depth in a single event: neural shearing, identity fragmentation, spontaneous Tide-mark bleed. The medical emergency of the hidden world — see 16.2.
5. **Contamination.** Prolonged proximity to high-pressure Seeps without channeling: the slow version of everything above. Sweeper teams' occupational disease; managed by rotation limits no one fully obeys.

> CANON: Emergency responders' rule of thumb — *"Treat the wound, respect the Rule."* Stabilize the patient with normal medicine first; call the Lantern second. Reversing the order kills people.

## 16.2 Erosion medicine: the Fathoms scale in clinical practice

> CANON: The **Fathoms scale** (0–100) is maintained as a clinical standard by the Compact's medical directorate, validated at [RES-005](15_EDUCATION.md) the Holloway Clinic. Measurement combines neural imaging (Hollow Pattern degradation), cognitive battery (pattern-integrity tests), and Lantern assessment (the "depth read" — a trained Lantern can estimate Fathoms within ±5).

### Clinical bands

| Fathoms | Band | Clinical picture | Management |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–20 | **Shallow** | Nosebleeds, migraines; full function | Outpatient; routine monitoring |
| 20–40 | **Working depth** | Memory gaps, emotional flattening, sleep disorders | Active management; Stillwater as needed; duty restrictions |
| 40–60 | **The thinning** | Personality erosion; Tide-mark bleed (remembering things that never happened to them) | Intensive outpatient; [ACAD-002](15_EDUCATION.md)-style stillness protocols; consider duty limitation |
| 60–80 | **Deep** | Identity instability; involuntary channeling; physical wrongness (reflections lag, shadows misbehave) | Residential care; [RES-005](15_EDUCATION.md) Holloway or equivalent; no operational duty |
| 80–100 | **Pre-Drowning** | Pattern dissolution in progress | Hospice; accompaniment ([REL-005](10_RELIGIONS.md) Choir rites or [REL-007](10_RELIGIONS.md) Garden houses — patient's choice, where possible) |

> CANON: Erosion is irreversible ([02_WORLD_RULES.md](02_WORLD_RULES.md), I). All treatment is *palliative and slowing*: stillness practices, Stillwater-class drugs, rest, and — the evidence is soft but consistent — *being known*: patients with stable, informed social bonds degrade slower. The Holloway's best-validated intervention after stillness is a regular visitor who isn't afraid.

> CANON: **The Vesper guardrail.** When Vesper ([CORP-002](11_CORPORATIONS.md)) says "treated," it means *slowed* — nothing more, ever. The theoretical ceiling is accumulation-reduction approaching, but never reaching, zero; there is no negative Erosion, no reversal, no buyback. Any claim of *reversal* is either fraud or a canon-break requiring a CHANGELOG entry. This is what keeps the setting's most expensive R&D bet *tragic* rather than *setting-breaking*: Vesper's scientists are the smartest people working on the problem, and the problem is bigger than they are.

> CANON: The macro liability is **~$2B/year** globally, buried in defense and health budgets worldwide — see [08_ECONOMY.md](08_ECONOMY.md) 8.4. No legislature has ever voted on it as a line item.

### The cruelest clinical fact

> CANON: The Fathoms scale measures the *self*, not the body. A patient at 70 Fathoms can have perfect bloodwork and be *someone else* than they were at 30. Families grieve the living. The psychiatric literature calls it "erosive bereavement"; the Garden houses just call it Tuesday.

> CANON: **Pastoral fraud controls.** Grieving families are the softest target in the hidden world: echo-phenomena ([02_WORLD_RULES.md](02_WORLD_RULES.md), I) are *not* the deceased, but fraudsters sell "echo visits" — claimed contact with the Drowned — to the bereaved. Controls: the Majelis Fenomena's 2001 ruling (echoes are not the deceased; rites are for the living — [10_RELIGIONS.md](10_RELIGIONS.md)) is the doctrinal firewall; hospice and Erosion-care chaplaincy licensing requires **Lantern-verified credentials** (unlicensed "echo mediums" are prosecuted as fraud, not as channelers); and Quiet insurers' service-denominated payouts ([08_ECONOMY.md](08_ECONOMY.md) 8.2.1) fund bereavement counseling through vetted providers — the payout can't be skimmed by a medium because it pays in *appointments*, not cash.

## 16.3 Stillwater drugs

The channeling-dampening drug class ([36_TERMINOLOGY.md](36_TERMINOLOGY.md)), [CORP-002](11_CORPORATIONS.md) Vesper's franchise:

> CANON: **The mundane indication.** Stillwater is approved by the FDA/EMA and equivalents as an **anxiolytic/sedative** (brand families vary by market) — a real, published, peer-reviewed indication with real trials, real prescriptions, and millions of ordinary patients. The channeling-dampening effect is the *same pharmacology*; the indication is the cover story that makes the manufacturing, distribution, and prescription volumes explainable. Every regulator that approved it saw the anxiolytic data. None of them was shown the Tide data.
- **Clinical Stillwater (Rx):** dampens channeling capacity by 40–70%; first-line for Erosion management, acute Erosion trauma, and involuntary-channeling episodes. Side effects: emotional blunting (dose-dependent), sleep architecture disruption, and — with chronic use — a flattening that patients describe as "watching my life through glass."
- **Graywater (street):** diluted/diverted Stillwater ([11_CORPORATIONS.md](11_CORPORATIONS.md) CORP-002). The hidden world's most abused drug: used by Attuned to take the edge off, by criminals to suppress witnesses, by the desperate to *stop remembering* (it doesn't — but the blunting feels like it does). A quiet public-health crisis with no public. **ER coding doctrine:** graywater overdoses are coded as **benzodiazepine-adjacent sedative overdoses** — the toxidrome matches closely enough that no emergency physician has ever needed a second code. The coding is the containment: the public-health statistics show a sedative-abuse curve, not a supernatural one.
- **Next-generation:** Vesper's Erosion-slowing pipeline ([11_CORPORATIONS.md](11_CORPORATIONS.md) CORP-002's hidden agenda) — and the shelved V-77 (Erosion *accelerator*, vaulted). The patent cliff (2027–2031) means generics are coming; the street market is about to get cheaper and stranger.

> CANON: Stillwater treats the *channeling*, not the *Erosion*. It slows further accumulation; it reverses nothing. Patients who misunderstand this stop their stillness practice, and do worse.

## 16.4 Anomalous diseases

Conditions with no mundane etiology, recognized in the Compact's *Threshold Pathology* (classified; 4th edition, 2021):

- **Tide-mark bleed (chronic).** Persistent false-memory intrusion from ambient Tide-marks — the patient "remembers" being places they've never been. Distinguished from psychosis by *consistency*: the false memories are stable, detailed, and occasionally verifiable (the patient remembers a room that exists, three streets over, in 1974). Treatment: Stillwater, stillness, and — counterintuitively — *visiting* the remembered places, which seems to discharge the bleed.
- **Hollow lung.** Granulomatous inflammation of the lungs in prolonged high-pressure environments (Sweeper teams, disposal crews). Responds to steroids *and* to leaving. The radiology is distinctive; three pulmonologists outside the hidden world have published it as "idiopathic" — they're not wrong, just uninformed.
- **Rule echo syndrome.** After surviving a predatory Rule (the thirteenth-person stairwell and you were twelfth), patients develop compulsive counting, avoidance rituals, and hypervigilance *tuned to the specific Rule*. It looks like OCD. It responds to OCD treatment *plus* Lantern-verified confirmation that the Rule can't reach them anymore. The confirmation matters more than the medication.
- **Quiet grief.** Not a disease — the Majelis Fenomena's ([REL-003](10_RELIGIONS.md)) term for what happens to Loud people who watch Quiet loved ones forget shared trauma. The patient remembers; the family doesn't; the family thinks the patient is ill. The most common psychiatric presentation in Loud patients, and the least treatable by pharmacology.

## 16.5 Psychiatric effects of the Veil and Loudness

### The Quiet majority

The Veil Reflex ([03_SUPERNATURAL_SYSTEM.md](03_SUPERNATURAL_SYSTEM.md)) edits memory, not physics — which leaves a specific psychiatric residue:

- **Unexplained trauma.** The body remembers what the mind deleted: startle responses, phobias with no origin story, nightmares about stairwells. Standard trauma therapy *works* — the therapist doesn't need to know what happened, only that something did. The Order of the Veil's ([REL-001](10_RELIGIONS.md)) counseling network treats thousands of these cases annually, blind.
- **The residue dream.** 60%+ of exposure victims report one recurring dream: *being somewhere that counts them*. Sleep researchers outside the hidden world have published on "the counting dream" as a cultural phenomenon. It isn't cultural.

### The Loud minority

> CANON: Loudness is protective and corrosive at once. The Loud retain Seep events with unusual vividness and stability — including the things no one else can corroborate, which makes them look unreliable to exactly the people whose help they need. It is not general eidetic memory: mundane recall is ordinary. Arthur Reed's ([CHAR-001](29_PROTAGONIST_ANOMALY.md)) exact-detail recall — serial numbers, vector strings, the wording of a form filed once, years ago — is exceptional *even among the Loud*, and unpriced by everyone who has met him.

- **Remembrance disorders.** The Loud-specific cluster: hypervigilance, social isolation ("why don't you remember? *you were there*"), and the quiet grief above. Misdiagnosed as schizophrenia or delusional disorder at alarming rates — the standard psychiatric toolkit has no category for "accurate memories of impossible events."
- **The Rememberers** ([IND-002](12_FACTIONS.md)) — the Loud support group — is the de facto frontline mental-health provider for this population. Peer support works where clinical psychology misfires, because the first therapeutic act is *believing the patient*.

## 16.6 Emergency medicine protocols

**Sweeper-medic doctrine** (Compact standard, adopted by most threshold agencies):

1. **Scene Rule assessment first** — the Lantern clears or characterizes before medics enter. Medics who skip this step become patients.
2. **Mundane stabilization** — ABCs, hemorrhage control, the normal algorithm. The supernatural doesn't change triage categories.
3. **Tide-noise perimeter** — [CORP-005](11_CORPORATIONS.md) Obsidian-pattern jammers suppress ambient pressure so the wounded can be moved without the Seep *noticing*.
4. **Stillwater on indication** — for channeling wounded (suppress involuntary channeling during transport) and for exposed Quiet civilians (blunt acute panic — controversial, consent-waived under emergency doctrine, litigated twice).
5. **Memory-care handoff** — Quiet Insurance ([08_ECONOMY.md](08_ECONOMY.md) 8.2.1) caseworkers meet the ambulance. Exposure victims get "Quiet leave" paperwork before discharge. The system works because it's boring.

**What paramedics actually see:** most Seep-adjacent calls present as gas leaks, mass hysteria, or vandalism. The paramedics treat them as such. The paperwork goes to insurers. The insurers' analytics flag the pattern. This is the detection pipeline ([27_TECHNOLOGY.md](27_TECHNOLOGY.md)) — and it starts with an EMT writing "patient anxious, cause unknown" at 3 a.m.

## 16.8 Referral ladders and misdiagnosis

### The ladder (how a case climbs)

> CANON: GP / ER → "unexplained" flag → threshold-agency medical liaison → regional Quiet-care ward ([MED-001](15_EDUCATION.md)-type) → national center ([RES-005](15_EDUCATION.md)) → Garden house or Choir hospice (end of life). Each rung requires the previous rung's paperwork — which is why the night-shift records clerk's indexing matters more than it looks.

### Misdiagnosis patterns (the honest ones)

- **Tide-mark bleed → schizophrenia.** The most common and most harmful. Distinguishing test: the false memories are *stable and occasionally verifiable* (16.4). Most psychiatrists never learn this; the ones who do usually learned it from a patient.
- **Rule echo syndrome → OCD.** Partially correct — treat the OCD *and* confirm the Rule's absence.
- **Erosion (40–60) → early-onset dementia / personality disorder.** The cognitive battery overlaps. The differential is the Hollow Pattern imaging — which requires knowing to order it.
- **Quiet grief → delusional disorder.** The cruelest misdiagnosis: the patient is *right*, and the treatment plan is built on them being wrong.

> CANON: The referral ladder works *because* misdiagnosis is the default. The system assumes every clinician will miss it, and routes around them — the liaison layer exists to catch what the ER can't see. It's inefficient, it's paternalistic, and it saves lives weekly.
