# 28 — ANOMALY CATALOG

**Foundation:** [00_INDEX.md](00_INDEX.md) · [01_CORE_PREMISE.md](01_CORE_PREMISE.md) · [02_WORLD_RULES.md](02_WORLD_RULES.md) · [03_SUPERNATURAL_SYSTEM.md](03_SUPERNATURAL_SYSTEM.md) · [04_POWER_SYSTEM.md](04_POWER_SYSTEM.md) · [05_ANOMALY_CLASSIFICATION.md](05_ANOMALY_CLASSIFICATION.md) · [36_TERMINOLOGY.md](36_TERMINOLOGY.md)

> CANON: "Anomaly" is the Meridian Compact's official term for a Seep (see `03_SUPERNATURAL_SYSTEM.md` §3.3). This catalog uses the Compact Classification Office's **Meridian Vector** — the five-axis standard specified in full in `05_ANOMALY_CLASSIFICATION.md`:
>
> `MV-T3/C-C/I+R/Urban/Reactive`
> - **Axis 1 — THREAT (T0–T7):** maximum demonstrated output equivalent — the Attuned tier that would be needed to match what the anomaly just did. Retrospective, revisable upward.
> - **Axis 2 — CONTAINMENT (A–E):** **A** = contained, stable, passive measures suffice; **B** = containable with known active procedures; **C** = contained *at cost* (continuous active intervention); **D** = uncontained but tracked; **E** = uncontainable — mitigation and evacuation only.
> - **Axis 3 — HAZARD FLAGS** (compose freely, joined with `+`; `—` = none): **I** = infohazard (knowledge is dangerous); **B** = biohazard; **P** = psychohazard (attacks cognition/identity/sanity); **R** = reality distortion (locally rewrites physical law); **M** = memetic (propagates through culture/media); **V** = vector/propagating (actively spreads itself).
> - **Axis 4 — RANGE:** Local → Urban → Regional → Continental → Global → Memetic (geography irrelevant; moves through information networks).
> - **Axis 5 — INTEL:** None (no detectable response) · Reactive (responds in fixed, non-novel ways — schedules and triggers count) · Sapient (novel problem-solving; triggers Sapient protocols) · Unknown (insufficient data; treated as Sapient for rules of engagement until disproven).
>
> Filing note: the vector is the *filing*, not the field truth. Operational traits the six flags don't capture — mobility, public visibility (masquerade strain), Erosion-vector exposure, wide-area footprints — are documented in each entry's prose below, which is what a Sweeper team actually reads. Vectors are living documents; re-audit triggers in `05_ANOMALY_CLASSIFICATION.md` §5.4 apply.

## ANOMALY-002 — "The Thirteenth Chair" — MV-T2/C-B/—/Urban/Reactive

**Substrate:** Object (a mahogany dining chair, circa 1920s, Batavia-made).

> CANON: The Rule — When exactly twelve people sit for a meal in a room containing the chair, the chair produces a thirteenth diner: a polite, silent, empty-suited presence that occupies it for the duration of the meal. The thirteenth diner eats nothing but *remembers everything said*, and anyone who speaks to it directly forgets one true thing about themselves within the hour. If fewer or more than twelve sit, the chair is inert furniture.

**Discovery:** Surfaced 2009 from the estate of a Surabaya shipping magnate; three dinner-party guests filed Quiet Insurance claims (memory editing) before a Lantern auditor traced the common object. The magnate's family had used it for decades as a "parlor trick."

**Current status:** Vaulted at SITE-004 (Compact regional vault, Singapore). Loaned twice to the Tribunal as a witness-examination aid — the Compact no longer does this, after the chair "remembered" a classified testimony and a junior archivist asked it a question.

**Narrative use:** The perfect low-stakes horror prop that becomes a plot device — a dinner scene where someone counts chairs; an interrogation where the only honest witness is the furniture. Demonstrates the genre rule: *learn the Rule, exploit the Rule*.

## ANOMALY-003 — "The Unsent Letterbox" — MV-T1/C-B/—/Urban/Reactive

**Substrate:** Object (a British pillar box, relocated to a Brighton side street).

> CANON: The Rule — Any letter posted in it that is addressed to someone *dead* is delivered: the writer receives a reply within one lunar month, in handwriting that matches authenticated samples of the deceased, containing only true statements the deceased knew while alive. Letters to the living are returned stamped "NOT YET." It cannot be used for the recently dead (under 40 days); those letters rot unopened.

**Discovery:** Noted by Royal Mail staff in 1996 ("the box that argues with the sorting office"). A Cartographers' surveyor flagged it in 2002; the UK Threshold Directorate (GOV-007) negotiated quiet custody.

**Current status:** In-situ, warded, under GOV-007 observation. Posting is a licensed activity: grief counselors may authorize one letter per bereaved family. The waiting list is four years long.

**Narrative use:** A mercy and a trap — the replies contain only truths the dead knew, which makes them devastating in inheritance disputes, murder cases, and unfinished apologies. Someone will eventually post a letter to a person whose death is *disputed*.

## ANOMALY-004 — "The Counting Stair" — MV-T1/C-B/—/Local/Reactive

**Substrate:** Place (the third-floor service stair of a 1960s Jakarta walk-up, Kebayoran).

> CANON: The Rule — The staircase has thirteen steps going up and fourteen going down. Anyone who counts aloud while climbing arrives one floor higher than the building possesses; the extra landing contains a single door, locked, behind which something breathes in a rhythm matching the counter's heartbeat. Descending while counting backwards returns the climber safely. Running the stairs without counting does nothing.

**Discovery:** Tenants reported it for years as "the building's joke." A Night Clerk (IND-004) logged it in 2017; BPF (GOV-004) sealed the top landing as a precaution.

**Current status:** In-situ, BPF-monitored. The door has never been opened; three Lantern remote-viewing attempts produced three different descriptions and one nosebleed.

**Narrative use:** A Jakarta-side mystery that could sit in a file for twenty chapters before it matters. The locked door is a Chekhov's gun the whole bible refuses to fire — until it must.

## ANOMALY-005 — "The Borrowed Dog" — MV-T2/C-D/—/Urban/Reactive

**Substrate:** Organism (a medium brown dog of no consistent breed; microchip scans return different IDs).

> CANON: The Rule — The dog attaches itself to a grieving person and stays exactly forty days, during which the person's grief is *absent* — not processed, absent, as if the dog is carrying it. On the forty-first day the dog leaves, and the full forty days of deferred grief arrive at once. If the dog is prevented from leaving, it begins to starve regardless of feeding, and the grief returns doubled. It cannot be photographed clearly; every image shows a different dog.

**Discovery:** Compiled from Quiet Insurance grief-counseling files across Java, 2007–2014, by an actuary at Nusantara Quiet Assurance (CORP-011) who noticed the same forty-day pattern in 31 claims. (The actuary was Loud. She was transferred to archives.)

**Current status:** D-class, tracked; believed to be a single mobile instance circulating Java. The Quiet Garden (SUP-003) considers sheltering it an act of mercy; the Red Ledger (SUP-004) has a standing buy order from an anonymous client.

**Narrative use:** A mercy that is also a loan shark. Forces the question the Quiet Garden exists to ask: is it kinder to feel pain now or later? A sharp clerk's eyes would spot the pattern in the claims data first — which is why NQA's Ravenhurst branch archives, not just Jakarta's, now get audited for forty-day grief gaps.

## ANOMALY-006 — "The Thursday Rain" — MV-T3/C-C/R/Regional/Reactive

**Substrate:** Event (rainfall over the Lagos Island district, every third Thursday, 03:12–03:47 local time).

> CANON: The Rule — The rain falls *upward* from the gutters and storm drains, but only water that has touched human skin in the previous 24 hours. It rises approximately forty meters, hangs for ninety seconds in a shimmering sheet, then falls normally. During the hanging, anyone beneath it hears — clearly, in their own language — one sentence they once said and have forgotten. The rain never floods; drainage records show zero net gain.

**Discovery:** Lagos traffic cameras caught it in 2015; static rot ate the footage within hours, but 200+ Loud witnesses filed reports. The Nigerian agency's file labels it "meteorological sentimentality."

**Current status:** Monitored. The ratified vector is `C-C` (05.5); the JCC's 2002 posture revision (Regional Containment Doctrine) lowered the *posture*, not the classification — containment means regional weather-service coordination and road closures nine days a year, forever, which *is* regional-scope effort. Crowd management and footage noise-farming sit on top of that posture. (Classification Office dispute note, 05.3: field catalogs sometimes log the posture as the class; the ratified vector governs.) A Lantern Bearers cell (SUP-005) treats it as a pilgrimage site. A Red Ledger crew sells "rain seats" — rooftop access — at extortionate prices.

**Narrative use:** The High Tide made visible: a beautiful, public, uncontainable event that the masquerade can only *manage*. Shows how civilians, cults, and mercenaries all monetize or worship the same phenomenon.

## ANOMALY-007 — "The Ward Six Lullaby" — MV-T4/C-D/I+M+V/Memetic/Reactive

**Substrate:** Information (a four-bar melody; humming it correctly is the vector).

> CANON: The Rule — Anyone who hears the complete melody *and understands it is being sung to them* falls asleep within one minute and dreams the same dream: a white ward with six beds, five occupied by sleeping figures, the sixth empty and waiting. The sleeper wakes with one new true memory that is not theirs — a fragment of someone else's life — and one missing hour they can never account for. Repeated exposure (three or more hearings) causes the sleeper to seek out the nearest hospital and occupy any empty sixth bed they can find; two documented cases ended in Drowning-adjacent Erosion spikes in the *singers*, not the sleepers.
>
> > STATUS: THEORY (Compact medical directorate): the lullaby is a predatory information-pattern that uses human nervous systems as relay stations; the "singers" are not the anomaly, they are its current hosts.

**Discovery:** Traced 2019 to a night-shift nurse in a Manila hospital who had been humming it for months; eleven patients and four staff affected before a Lantern quarantine team identified the melody as the common factor. The nurse had learned it from a patient who died in Ward Six in 2017 — whose chart has no admission record.

**Current status:** Active containment: all known hummers under Stillwater sedation and observation; the melody itself is a **Class-2 infohazard** — written notation is permitted, *performance is prohibited* under Compact law. One notated copy is held at SITE-002; three unauthorized performances have occurred since 2020, all at livestreamed events (see ROT/FEED, IND-003).

**Narrative use:** The infohazard done right — the danger is not knowing *about* it, it's *completing the pattern*. A livestreamer humming four bars to 40,000 viewers is a mass-casualty scenario the Compact genuinely fears. Built for the internet age.

> CANON (Tribunal ruling *Quiet Desk v. Ward Six Propagation*, 2021 — a separate ruling, jurisprudentially adjacent to CASE-006's liability doctrine, not part of it): the Lullaby is the CANON's boundary case, and it falls on the lawful side. It is **suggestion seeding at memetic scale, not direct mind control**: the compulsion admits resistance (documented refusers exist — singers who heard the complete melody, refused to sing, and reported; the V flag exists precisely because propagation is resisted, not automatic), and the memory degradation in singers is the **backlash mechanism** of [02's no-mind-control rule](02_WORLD_RULES.md) — the relay-station pays the distorted echo, exactly as seeding doctrine predicts. A melody no one can resist would be mind control; a melody *most* people fail to resist is the oldest kind of suggestion. The ruling is citable, and the Compact's examiners cite it constantly.

## ANOMALY-008 — "The Coin of the Drowned Year" — MV-T2/C-B/—/Local/Reactive

**Substrate:** Object (a VOC duit, 1792, found in a Jakarta shipwreck salvage).

> CANON: The Rule — The coin always lands on the year of the *holder's* death — rendered as the portrait side showing a date that shifts per holder. It cannot be spent: every till it enters is robbed within a week (the Rule seems to dislike circulation). If flipped three times in a row by the same person, the third flip shows the holder's *birth* year instead, and the coin goes inert for one year. The Compact's actuaries have verified 41 predictions; all 41 holders died in the shown year. No holder has yet tried to change it. The Compact's official guidance is: *do not try*. **The coin only reveals — it has never edited:** no verified case exists of a holder changing their death-year after seeing it. It is a window, not a lever.

**Discovery:** Salvage diver's find, 2006; entered the Gray Market in Trieste; purchased by a Pale Court collector (SUP-009) who flipped it twice, saw 2041, and donated it to the Compact "as a courtesy."

**Current status:** Vaulted at SITE-004. Access requires Tribunal (INTL-002) warrant; two requests pending, both from terminally ill Attuned.

**Narrative use:** Chekhov's prophecy. The anomaly is honest and useless — knowing the year changes nothing, which is the point. Tests characters' philosophy, not their power.

## ANOMALY-009 — "The Mirror Commute" — MV-T3/C-C/—/Urban/Reactive

**Substrate:** Place (the 07:40–08:10 northbound service on a specific London Underground line; the Compact's file redacts the line name, but every London Night Clerk knows it).

> CANON: The Rule — On mornings when the train is delayed by more than nine minutes, the carriages briefly contain *two* sets of passengers: the living commuters, and the commuters of the same service exactly ten years earlier, visible in the windows' reflections and audible as a second layer of announcements. The two sets cannot interact, but the living can *hear* the past service's delays — which predict the current day's signal failures with 100% accuracy. Anyone who photographs the reflections develops the photos to find themselves, ten years older, looking back.

**Discovery:** A transport union rep (Loud) compiled driver reports, 2013–2018; GOV-007 confirmed the predictive accuracy and quietly began buying the drivers' silence with early-retirement packages.

**Current status:** Monitored in-situ; GOV-007 runs a classified "signal intelligence" desk that listens to the 2014 service for advance warning of infrastructure faults. The Quiet Garden has petitioned — twice — for the service to be recognized as a place of mourning.

**Narrative use:** Infrastructure as oracle. A government secretly running its rail maintenance off ghost announcements is the exact flavor of tired-competent bureaucracy this world runs on.

## ANOMALY-010 — "The Taxidermist's Choir" — MV-T3/C-C/—/Urban/Reactive

**Substrate:** Organism (eleven taxidermied songbirds in a Brighton antique shop; they were alive when purchased in 1987).

> CANON: The Rule — Each bird sings, once per night, the last song its *taxidermist* heard before dying — the same taxidermist, one Hendrik Voss, drowned 1992. The songs are always accurate to recordings Voss owned. If all eleven sing in the same night (it happens when a living person sleeps in the shop), a twelfth voice joins: Voss himself, humming, and the sleeper wakes with Voss's hands — skilled, scarred, wrong — for one hour. During that hour the sleeper can stuff and mount anything, including things that object. The hands revert. The skill does not entirely leave.

**Discovery:** The shop's owner died in 2017; the estate sale catalog listed "eleven birds, one voice extra." A Cartographers' buyer flagged it; the sale was intercepted.

**Current status:** Vaulted at SITE-003 (the Moon Vault, Ravenhurst) under Ledger escrow. Two authorized research sessions; one unauthorized (a Pale Court collector bribed a Blackwater Syndicate guard in 2021 — the collector now mounts insects professionally and will not discuss the hour). **Ledger title is pending — a stalled three-way dispute:** the UK threshold bureau (recovery jurisdiction), the Compact Claims Office (vaulting and examination costs), and the Ferrymen (SUP-010, who filed a salvage claim on the interception). None will concede; the escrow holds until someone does. The birds sing on regardless.

**Narrative use:** Body-horror with a craftsman's soul. The Rule's cruelty is precise: it doesn't hurt you, it *gives you something* — and what it gives is someone else's life's work, which is a kind of haunting.

## ANOMALY-011 — "The Harbormaster's Ledger" — MV-T2/C-B/—/Local/Reactive

**Substrate:** Object (a water-stained cargo manifest book, Tanjung Priok harbor, 1931–1937).

> CANON: The Rule — Any debt *written* in the ledger — in any hand, any ink — is settled within one year: the creditor is paid, the debtor ruined, or both, by means that are always technically legal and always morally appalling. Debts written in pencil are settled gently. Debts written in blood are settled *exactly*. **The settlement always runs through the debt writer's own agency: the Rule settles the debt as though *only the debt writer could have settled it while alive* — the means are traceable to the writer's life, assets, and relationships.** The ledger has 40 blank pages left. It accepts entries in any language, including ones the writer doesn't speak (the handwriting changes).

**Discovery:** Found in a flooded customs archive during the 2011 Jakarta floods; a BPF archivist noticed that every "settled" stamp in the book corresponded to a real bankruptcy or windfall in harbor records.

**Current status:** Vaulted at SITE-001 (Jakarta black site). BPF's internal auditors are forbidden to touch it; the Red Ledger (SUP-004) has offered — repeatedly, through cutouts — to buy blank pages at 2 billion rupiah per page.

**Narrative use:** The monkey's paw as accounting. Every faction wants the blank pages; every faction is terrified of what happens when someone writes in it. A blank page is the most dangerous currency in Jakarta.

## ANOMALY-012 — "The Static Saint" — MV-T5/C-D/I+M/Memetic/Sapient

**Substrate:** Information (a face that appears in corrupted video — the "saint" of static rot itself).

> CANON: The Rule — In any recording undergoing severe static rot (T4+ events, Drowndust detonations, Drowning sites), frame-by-frame analysis sometimes resolves a single human face in the noise, always the same face, always looking at the camera. Anyone who *recognizes* the face — not sees, recognizes, as in “I know that person” — begins to dream of drowning within a week, accrues **resonance damage** at ~1 Fathom per month without channeling, and eventually seeks out deep water. There is no record of what happens after they find it; the Seismograph logs a pressure spike and the person's file is closed. The face has never been matched to any living or dead person. Composite sketches drawn from memory by three separate witnesses are identical.
>
> > STATUS: UNKNOWN (author-level): what the Static Saint is. The Compact's working theory (Membrane Theory, STATUS: THEORY) is that it is a stable Undertow pattern wearing the *idea* of a face. The Drowned Choir (SUP-001) venerates it as the Mother Below's first missionary. Both may be wrong.

> CANON: **Terminology (Classification Office correction, 2022):** the Saint does not inflict *Erosion* — Erosion is the wear-cost of channeling ([04](04_POWER_SYSTEM.md)), and the victims are not channeling. What the victims accrue is **resonance damage** — externally imposed Veil-wear, measured on the Fathom scale for convenience but ontologically distinct from channeling Erosion. (The Compact's field shorthand "Fathom-debt" for such damage is an accounting metaphor, not a physical claim — see the Gilded Cradle, ANOMALY-015.) The distinction matters legally: Erosion is self-incurred and non-compensable; resonance damage is *inflicted*, which is why the Saint's file is I-flagged and why its victims' estates are eligible for Meridian Re coverage that true Erosion never qualifies for.

**Discovery:** Compiled 2016 by a Seismograph Authority (INTL-004) analyst correlating "unexplained Erosion cases" with corrupted footage archives. The analyst recognized the face. The analyst's file is closed.

**Current status:** Ratified `C-D`. The Seismograph Authority proposed **E** (uncontained, uncontainable) in 2022; the JCC refused to file it — "you don't rate what you can't bound." The proposal stands as an unfiled dispute note with the Classification Office (05.3), not a status. Countermeasures only: footage from T4+ events is now auto-quarantined and machine-screened before human review; analysts work in pairs and are rotated off "saint-positive" material. The knowledge that the face *exists* is itself restricted — this paragraph is the most the Compact permits in a bible-class document.

**Narrative use:** The setting's apex predator that isn't a predator — it's a pattern. It weaponizes the world's own documentation against it: the harder you investigate the supernatural, the more likely you are to meet the Saint. Every archivist, every analyst, every Arthur-like clerk is one corrupted video away.

## ANOMALY-013 — "The Salt Garden" — MV-T4/C-B/R/Urban/Reactive

**Substrate:** Place (a walled courtyard in the old quarter of Istanbul, approximately 40×60 meters, unmapped since 1923).

> CANON: The Rule — The garden grows salt: crystalline formations that bud from the soil like plants, in shapes that mirror the *regrets* of whoever last walked the paths alone. Tasting a crystal shows the walker the moment of the regret, perfectly, from the outside — as a witness, not a participant. Walking the garden with another person grows nothing. The garden has been walked alone ~2,000 times; the oldest crystals are dated (by Tide-mark reading) to the 1400s. Once a year, on the night of the first frost, the garden *prunes itself*: all crystals shatter, and the sound is heard across the old city as a single chord. Seismograph stations log it as a T4 pressure event.

**Discovery:** Known to locals for centuries as "the crying courtyard"; formally surveyed by the Cartographers (SUP-002) in 1961 and sold — the maps, not the garden — to four governments.

**Current status:** Ratified `C-B`. The courtyard cannot be *vaulted* (it "rejects" warding — three attempts collapsed the scaffolding), so the Turkish agency maintains a perimeter and a waiting list — standing regional-scope containment effort, which is what the C axis measures. Threat and containment are orthogonal (05.6): "cannot be sealed" is the *reason* for the B threat reading, not an argument against the C containment one. (Classification Office dispute note, 05.3.) Pilgrims, spies, and the heartbroken queue together. The Ninth Bell (SUP-007) attempted to salt the earth with Drowndust in 2017; the garden grew a crystal shaped like the saboteur's own regret, and he walked in and never walked out.

**Narrative use:** A place the whole hidden world agrees is *useful* — intelligence services debrief assets by walking them through it; therapists prescribe it; cults pilgrimage to it. Its neutrality is its power: the one place enemies will stand side by side, alone.

## ANOMALY-014 — "The Punctual Fog" — MV-T1/C-B/—/Urban/Reactive

**Substrate:** Event (a dense, warm fog that fills the Triestine waterfront, 04:44–05:11, on the first Tuesday of each month).

> CANON: The Rule — The fog arrives exactly on schedule and departs exactly on schedule, regardless of weather. Within it, all sound is delayed by eleven seconds — you hear your own footsteps after you've taken them. Smugglers (see the Ferrymen, SUP-010) use the eleven-second window the way others use darkness: deals are spoken, then unheard. The fog has never caused a single accident; harbor records show zero collisions in fog hours since 1952, which the port authority cites in its safety brochures without explanation.

**Discovery:** Never "discovered" — Trieste simply *has* the fog, the way it has the bora wind. The Compact file was opened in 1974 when a Seismograph station logged the pressure signature and an analyst wrote, in the margin: "it's polite."

**Current status:** Monitored; effectively a public utility of the Gray Market. The Memorandum Group (INTL-007) holds its monthly dead-drop exchanges inside the fog window.

**Narrative use:** World texture: not every anomaly is a crisis. Some are civic infrastructure. The fog is also a clock — when a deal must happen "in the fog," the deadline is absolute, which is a thriller engine.

## ANOMALY-015 — "The Gilded Cradle" — MV-T4/C-C/—/Urban/Reactive

**Substrate:** Object (a gilded cradle, Venetian, 17th century; currently in a Pale Court (SUP-009) private collection, on "indefinite loan" to the Compact — neither side will say whose idea the loan was).

> CANON: The Rule — Any infant placed in the cradle sleeps without waking for exactly one year, and wakes *one year older* — healthy, unaged in mind, having dreamed (so the cradle's keepers claim) "the year they skipped." Any adult who rocks the cradle falls asleep for one hour and wakes having *lost* one year of Erosion — no, not lost: *transferred*. The cradle does not heal Erosion; it moves it. The infant wakes carrying the rocker's *Erosion*, measured in Fathoms, distributed as developmental anomalies the Compact's doctors refuse to describe in writing. The cradle has been used eleven times. There are eleven children.

> CANON: **Ontology (Classification Office correction, 2022):** the cradle transfers *Erosion* — the physical Veil-wear — not "Fathoms." Fathoms are a measurement scale (0–100), not a substance; the Compact's field shorthand "Fathom-debts" for the eleven transferred burdens is an accounting metaphor, and the file's own Rule description has been corrected to match ("the infant wakes carrying the rocker's *Erosion*, measured in Fathoms"). No Fathom-debt ledger exists anywhere; there is only damaged Veil, moved from one bearer to another.

**Discovery:** Venetian Inquisition records mention it in 1688 ("the cradle that eats time"); it resurfaced in a 2013 Geneva estate inventory and triggered a three-faction bidding war the Compact won by threatening everyone.

**Current status:** C-class containment at SITE-002. Use is prohibited by Tribunal order; the eleven children are wards of the Compact, identities sealed. The Quiet Garden (SUP-003) has offered to take them. The Drowned Choir (SUP-001) calls them "the first congregation" and has attempted contact four times.

**Narrative use:** The trolley problem as furniture. Every faction's position on the cradle reveals its ethics: who deserves to have their Erosion taken away, and what may be done to a child who never consented? This is the anomaly that starts wars in committee rooms.

## ANOMALY-030 — "The Second Silence Archive" — MV-T5/C-C/I/Memetic/Sapient

**Substrate:** Information (a distributed archive: no central copy exists; it is the *set* of all surviving pre-1949 hidden-history documents held by the Archivists of the Second Silence, SUP-008).

> CANON: The Rule — The Archive is self-protecting. Any attempt to *destroy* a document in the Archive causes two copies to surface elsewhere (M-flag: memetic propagation). Any attempt to *publish* a document causes all readers to misremember its contents within 72 hours (the Veil Reflex, weaponized — the Archive *rides* it). Any attempt to *comprehensively index* it causes the indexer's notes to develop gaps corresponding exactly to the most important documents. The Archive can be *read* — slowly, in person, one document at a time, by someone who isn't trying to do anything with what they learn. The Archivists call this "the courtesy of the dead." The Compact's information theorists call it "an immune system."
>
> > STATUS: THEORY (Archivists' doctrine): the Archive is not one anomaly but a *colony* — hundreds of small informational Seeps that learned, over centuries, to protect each other. Whether "learned" is metaphor or mechanism is the Archivists' oldest internal argument.

**Discovery:** Not discovered — *inherited*. The Archivists have curated it since before the Meridian Accords; the Compact's file on it is itself incomplete (the file notes this, which the auditors find funny and the directors do not).

**Current status:** Ratified `C-C`. The 1996 Archivists' Charter argument — "a Sapient anomaly that cooperates is not a containment problem, it is a *custody* problem" — was a custodian's *filing proposal*, rejected by the Classification Office: the Charter is a compact document and cannot override a Compact ratification. (Filing the D would also have removed the Compact funding the rotation protocol needs.) Containment = the Archivists themselves, on a Compact-funded rotation the C axis pays for. The Compact's arrangement with SUP-008: the Archivists keep the Archive, the Compact gets *reading privileges* (slow, supervised, no copies), and both sides pretend this is stable. The Ninth Bell wants to burn it. The Drowned Choir wants to *baptize* it. The Pale Court wants to buy it. The Archive wants, as far as anyone can tell, to be left alone.

**Narrative use:** The past as an active player. Every historical revelation in the series must pass through the Archive's courtesy — which means research is a *negotiation*, not a lookup. And the memetic-propagation Rule means the harder anyone tries to suppress history, the more copies exist: a built-in engine for "the truth gets out" plots.
