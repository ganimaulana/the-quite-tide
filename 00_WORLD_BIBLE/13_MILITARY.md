# 13 — MILITARY

> CANON: Modern militaries did not become irrelevant when the supernatural went institutional — they became *employers*. Every great power's armed forces now include Attuned specialists, anomaly-response engineers, and containment logisticians alongside infantry and pilots. The rifle was not replaced. It was supplemented — because the economics of Erosion mean the rifle usually wins.

## 13.1 Why conventional weapons remain relevant

Five interlocking reasons, all downstream of [World Rules](02_WORLD_RULES.md) and the [Power System](04_POWER_SYSTEM.md). Every general staff college in the Compact teaches some version of this list:

1. **Erosion economics.** Channeling is priced in Fathoms, and the price curve is exponential past 40. A T4 can win a firefight; a T4 cannot win a *campaign* — there is no sustained supernatural bombardment, because the bombardier Drowns. Conventional forces are the only fires that don't bill the shooter in selfhood.
2. **Endurance beats peak output: the ninety-second rule.** Staff-college shorthand: *any engagement longer than ninety seconds favors the side with logistics.* An Attuned's peak output is extraordinary and brief; an infantry company's output is ordinary and indefinite. Militaries are in the indefinite business. The rule governs **sustained engagements** — firefights, sieges, campaigns. It does **not** govern **raids**: a raid is decided *pre-entry*, by intelligence, ward-survey, and the plan; once the breach starts, the ninety seconds are a budget the planners already spent on paper. (This is why raid doctrine obsesses over the threshold survey and treats the entry itself as execution, not decision.) It also does **not** govern **passive detection**: listening posts, Seismograph watch, and long-duration Lantern overwatch are *observation*, not expenditure — the rule prices output, not attention. The staff-college mnemonic for the gap: *"ninety seconds is how long you can fight; forever is how long you can be watched."*
3. **Most Attuned are T1–T2.** ~890,000 of the ~900,000 Attuned worldwide are T1 or T2. A rifle kills a T2. Body armor stops a T1's party trick. The median supernatural combatant is a specialist with a useful edge, not a weapons platform — and is employed accordingly.
4. **Seismograph visibility.** Any channeling above ~T3 is detected globally within minutes ([World Rules](02_WORLD_RULES.md)). Large-scale Attuned combat is therefore *witnessed* combat — strategically visible, diplomatically expensive, and legally a treaty incident. Conventional forces, paradoxically, are the *deniable* option.
5. **Law and logistics.** Armies are legal; Attuned strikes are incidents. And armies feed themselves: the hidden world's scarcest military commodity is not firepower but **Stillwater** — the dampener every Attuned unit needs in its supply chain. Cut the Stillwater and the Attuned company becomes a liability within days.

> CANON: The result is a stable division of labor: Attuned serve as specialists (forward observers, breachers, Lantern reconnaissance, emergency medicine), and conventional forces do the sustained work. No military on earth fields "super-squadrons" as line units — the three known attempts all ended in Drownings (see [Power System](04_POWER_SYSTEM.md): no artificial Awakening has ever worked, and concentration of high-tier Attuned in combat roles burns them out in months).

## 13.2 Force structure: the adapted military

A modern signatory military now contains, alongside its conventional order of battle:

- **Attuned units.** Embedded specialists, not independent formations. Typical pattern: one or two Attuned per company in reconnaissance/engineering roles (usually Lantern or Bone T2s — cheap to run, low Fathoms, high utility). Tide-combat Attuned are held at battalion level and above, deployed like close air support: brief, decisive, withdrawn before the Fathom bill comes due.
- **Anomaly response units.** Combat engineers cross-trained in ward construction, Lantern survey, and rapid classification. The unglamorous backbone — they build the warded perimeter while everyone else argues about the vector.
- **Containment units.** Heavy logistics: Stillwater supply chains, Anchor-restraint stocks, transport for seized anomalies, evacuation management. The US military's containment command is larger than most countries' entire threshold agencies.
- **Intelligence divisions.** Lantern intelligence (Tide-mark analysis of denied areas), Hollow-pattern screening of recruits, and — everywhere — the **seeding desk**: counter-suggestion-seeding for command staff, the most paranoid and least discussed office in any headquarters.
- **Liaison cells.** Officers seconded to the national threshold agency and to the [Joint Containment Command](07_INTERNATIONAL_RELATIONS.md). Their real job is making sure the military is never surprised by what the civilians contain — and vice versa.

## 13.3 Anti-Attuned weapons

> CANON: Every great power fields weapons designed to kill, suppress, or neutralize Attuned. The programs are public inside the hidden world and denied outside it.

- **Drowndust munitions.** Erosion-inducing particulates: inhaled or absorbed, they *force* Fathom accumulation — the victim's own pattern does the damage. **Banned by the 1949 Meridian Accords** (made mandatory by the 1977 Quiet Night revision; re-ratified by the 1979 Second Protocols); stockpiled anyway by at least four states ([Power System](04_POWER_SYSTEM.md)). Compact assessment ([STATUS: THEORY](00_INDEX.md), high confidence): the US, Russia, China, and North Korea. Drowndust is the hidden world's chemical weapon: everyone denies it, everyone plans around it, and its use would be the fastest route to a JCC intervention in history.

> CANON: **Drowndust mechanics and strategic doctrine.** Drowndust is **processed Drowning-site residue** — particulate sifted from the sites where Attuned Drowned ([CRIM-003](12_FACTIONS.md): Smelters seek to *manufacture* supply through deliberate Drownings). Supply is therefore **capped by Drownings**: no Drowning, no dust; the four stockpiles are finite and shrinking with every use. **Quiet people are unaffected** — the dust forces *Fathom accumulation*, and the Quiet have no Hollow Pattern to accumulate in; it is a weapon that works only on the Attuned. **Forensic verification is weak**: residue degrades, signatures are deniable, and the Seismograph logs *an event*, not a munition — which is why the ban has no verification regime, only deterrence. The Compact's own counter-stockpile sits at [SITE-006](23_SECRET_FACILITIES.md) (the JCC's deniable reserve). **The target set** (stated in the JCC's sealed annex, not in any national doctrine): Drowndust is not aimed at states, factions, or populations — it is aimed at *events*: E-class contingencies (uncontainable anomalies rated for evacuation-only), pre-Drowned T5+ subjects past recovery, and Drowning-site denial (salting a site so nothing Attuned can work it). The dust is useless against the Quiet and counterproductive against the contained — it is a weapon with exactly one legitimate target class, *the thing nothing else can stop*, which is why every stockpile's existence is also an admission. The red line the Smelters know: **industrial Drowning — manufacturing Drownings to make dust — is a Compact casus belli**, the one crime the JCC's charter names explicitly. The 2022 Kyiv deployment ([EVENT-083](25_TIMEmessaging app.md)) remains **forensically disputed**: logged by the Seismograph Authority, denied by Department 12, no civilian mass-signature ever confirmed — the sealed Tribunal case sits in a Geneva drawer because no one can prove what the dust was, or that it was dust at all.
- **Tide-noise generators.** Area-denial systems that flood the spectrum with artificial Tide pressure — jamming both channeling and Seismograph detection. The double edge: you can't see in, but neither can anyone see *you*, which is why noise generators are standard cover for covert Attuned operations. **The line the doctrine draws:** *tactical* engagements are concealable — noise-farming covers them, and the Seismograph's T3-floor does the rest. *Strategic* Threshold events are not concealable: the Seismograph sees them, and the cover story is "earthquake/explosion" — the **Batavia Static** ([EVENT-033](25_TIMEmessaging app.md), 1948: Dutch and Indonesian Lanterns clashing over Kota Tua's thin places, laundered as independence-war artillery) is the standing precedent, and every noise-farming plan since has been graded against it. A recent engagement *seen* past its cover story is a plot in progress, not a doctrine failure.
- **Anchor systems.** Vehicle-mounted and emplaced Hollow-pattern disruptors — the area version of Anchor restraints. Standard at checkpoints in high-threat zones; Singapore's are built into the border infrastructure.
- **Stillwater aerosol.** Non-lethal area denial: dampens channeling across a zone. Standard for riot control where Attuned may be present; controversial because the side effects (emotional blunting at scale) read as a Choir operation to anyone watching.
- **Ward-breaching charges.** Shaped charges tuned to shatter architectural Seep-suppression. Russia fields the most; their doctrine assumes warded targets.

## 13.4 Anomalous weapons programs

The temptation is permanent; the record is instructive.

- **Artificial Awakening.** Three known state programs (China's Project Vermilion, North Korea's Project Deep Root, and a corporate program — see [Governments](06_GOVERNMENTS.md), GOV-002/GOV-012). All ended in Drownings. **This is CANON and load-bearing** ([Power System](04_POWER_SYSTEM.md)): there are no super-soldier programs because the biology refuses. Every general who proposes a fourth is shown the three files.
- **Borrowed-matter munitions.** Conjured weapons that *revert within 72 hours* ([World Rules](02_WORLD_RULES.md)) — useless for stockpiling, perfect for deniable one-off strikes: a weapon that literally disappears, along with the evidence. At least two states are assessed to maintain "reversion teams" — Tide Attuned whose entire job is one operation, once, at ruinous Fathom cost. The Compact's position: use is a war crime under the Drowndust-adjacent provisions; proving use is nearly impossible, because the evidence reverts. The perfect crime the law cannot touch.
- **Bound-seep arsenals.** Containing high-threat anomalies as break-glass assets. Russia's **Saratov sleeper** (a comatose T5 — see [Governments](06_GOVERNMENTS.md), GOV-003) is the open secret; the sleeper carries *suppressed* Fathoms — unmeasurable while comatose, fully accrued the moment it wakes (that is the sleeper's definition, and why waking one is a launch decision, not a medical one); the BTA's "custody program" (T4 objects held under a legal theory the BTA's own lawyers call "aggressive") is the legal gray zone. The Compact's 2017 Sapient protocols were written partly to make this harder — with limited success.

## 13.5 Emergency protocols: the BLACK TIDE ladder

Signatory militaries use interoperable alert tiers (JCC standard):

| Tier | Trigger | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| **WATCH** | T3 event, C-C+ | National agency lead; military liaison notified |
| **SURGE** | T4 event, Regional+ | Civilian leadership + military support; JCC on standby |
| **BLACK TIDE** | T5+, E-class, or Continental+ | Head of state/government + Compact notification; JCC activation mandatory |

**The Drowndust release asymmetry** — the system's most dangerous known fact: in the US, release requires the President *and* the BTA Director concurring; in China, the Central Military Commission; in Russia, **General Volkov (CHAR-004) can authorize on his own authority** under Department 12's charter. The Compact knows. The Memorandum Group war-games it annually. Nobody has a solution that doesn't start with "don't let it happen."

**Evacuation doctrine** is the honest part of every plan: at BLACK TIDE, containment is aspirational and evacuation is the operation. The JCC's standing plans assume 72-hour civilian clearance for a Continental-range event — a number every planner privately calls fictional, because the Veil means evacuees won't remember *why* they're running, and the rot means the footage won't convince anyone who wasn't there.

## 13.6 Doctrines: five (six) ways of war

**Albion — "Quiet Disaster Relief" (静かな災害派遣).** Constitutional constraint as doctrine. the constitutional prohibition on offensive war means the Albion Defence Force has no war-fighting mandate for the hidden world; anomaly response is instead legally framed as **disaster relief** — the same authority as an earthquake deployment. The SMD (GOV-006), a civilian Albion Cabinet Office organ, holds command; the Albion Defence Force provides engineering, lift, and perimeter **only on SMD request**, under SMD tasking. Attuned serve in the Albion Defence Force as technical specialists (Lantern survey, ward engineering, Stillwater logistics) — never as combat formations; the "no super-squadrons" rule is, in Albion, also constitutional law. Force structure follows the three services: the **Albion Army** contributes ward-construction engineers and disaster-relief companies (the unglamorous backbone — they build the warded perimeter while the SMD argues about the vector); the **Royal Navy** holds maritime seep perimeters (the Kuroshio coast is its training ground); the **Royal Air Force** provides airlift for evacuation — the honest part of every British plan, rehearsed like a typhoon drill. **SMD–Albion Defence Force liaison** runs through a standing cell in the Albion Cabinet Office's crisis-management center, with Albion Defence Force officers seconded to SMD field offices (including Ravenhurst) as "disaster-preparedness advisors" — everyone knows what they advise on. The arrangement survives because it lets the Albion Defence Force do the job while calling the job earthquakes, and lets the SMD command military assets without admitting it commands them. Strength: the best civil-military disaster integration on earth — a T3 response looks like a well-run typhoon response, because legally it *is* one. Weakness: anything that can't be framed as a disaster can't be done — a BLACK TIDE the government won't declare is a response the Albion Defence Force legally cannot join, and the SMD knows it.

**USA — "Threshold Dominance."** Standoff, contractors, and airpower. Attuned serve as JTAC-equivalents — forward observers who designate, never the strike itself. The BTA's franchise Sweepers handle the quiet work; the military handles the loud work; the doctrine assumes both stay in their lanes. Strength: overwhelming resources and the best Lantern-targeting integration on earth. Weakness: the contractor seam — in a real BLACK TIDE, the franchises' contracts have liability caps, and no one has tested what happens when the lawyers say stop.

**China — "People's Quiet War."** Civil-military fusion. Attuned embedded at company level across the PLA; mass Stillwater logistics; doctrine built around **endurance** — win by outlasting the enemy's Fathoms. The Jade Office's work-unit system means China's Attuned are the best-supported and least free soldiers in the hidden world. Strength: depth — no one can attrit China's threshold capacity. Weakness: rigidity; the system is optimized for the war it expects, and the Tide keeps inventing new ones.

**Russia — "Dead Hand Quiet."** Escalation-first. Department 12's doctrine assumes the next war *will* be supernatural and plans to win it by being the side willing to go furthest fastest: Drowndust artillery as the poor man's equalizer, the Saratov sleeper as the rich man's threat, ward-breaching as standard. Strength: credible deterrence — everyone believes Volkov would authorize, which is the whole point. Weakness: the deterrent is a Drowning waiting to happen, and the conventional force behind it is hollowed by the same budget that feeds Department 12.

**Indonesia — "Seribu Pulau" (Thousand Islands).** Archipelago denial. No power projection — the doctrine doesn't pretend. The military's threshold role is **disaster relief with wards**: rapid local triage, evacuation, and perimeter-holding while the BPF and the village auxiliaries do the actual containment. Folk practitioners are formally listed as "community defense auxiliaries" — the only military on earth with dukun on the auxiliary rolls. Strength: honesty about capacity; the best civil-military-folk coordination in the world. Weakness: anything above T4/Regional exceeds the doctrine entirely — the plan for a T5 is, verbatim, *"request JCC, hold the ports."*

**North Korea — "Songun Tide" (assessed, not published).** Human wave plus Drowndust. Attuned treated as expendable munitions — deployed at ruinous Fathom cost for single decisive effects, then written off. The Memorandum Group's assessment: tactically terrifying, strategically brittle — you can only spend people once, and the Bureau is running out of trained Attuned faster than conscription replaces them.

## 13.7 The anomaly-war doctrine (fighting, not containing)

> CANON: The 1949 Accords' core bargain is that anomalies are *contained*, never fought as enemies — the Compact forbids anomaly-war doctrine as a category. What follows is the standing doctrine for the moment containment has already failed and the anomaly must be *defeated*, because every military on earth maintains one and lying about it in the bible would be the actual continuity error. **(1) Containment-first:** every engagement plan opens with the containment option, and the JCC is notified before the first shot — the paper trail is the doctrine. **(2) The 90-second rule governs:** no Attuned formation sustains peak output past ninety seconds, so Fathoms are budgeted like ammunition and no plan survives the expenditure it can't afford. **(3) Drowndust is the last resort:** release requires head-of-state-level authority (the release asymmetry, 13.5), and any use is Compact-reviewable — the dust is a weapon you inherit the audit for. **(4) The escalation ladder is the doctrine:** WATCH → SURGE → BLACK TIDE, and at BLACK TIDE evacuation *is* the operation. The **Saratov precedent** is taught in every staff college as what happens when step 1 is skipped: a containment failure fought as a war, at the cost the war always charges.
