## 9.1 Normal currencies

> CANON: The world's money is the real world's money. USD, EUR, CNY, JPY, GBP, IDR, and every other fiat currency work exactly as they do in reality, and 99%+ of supernatural-adjacent commerce settles in them.

The hidden economy does not need its own coins for daily life. A Lantern contractor in Jakarta gets paid in rupiah by bank transfer. [CORP-002](11_CORPORATIONS.md) Vesper books revenue in Swiss francs. Supernatural salaries ([08_ECONOMY.md](08_ECONOMY.md) 8.3) are ordinary payroll with extraordinary NDAs.

This is deliberate and load-bearing: **using normal money is camouflage.** A parallel circulating currency would be detectable, traceable, and â€” worst of all â€” *interesting*. The hidden world's financial doctrine, set by the Ledger ([INTL-003](07_INTERNATIONAL_RELATIONS.md)) in the 1960s, is: *be boring on the books*.

## 9.2 The secret settlement layer: soundings

> CANON: High-value anomaly transactions â€” insurance reinsurance settlement, auction clearing, inter-faction transfers, Tribunal fines â€” settle in a unit of account called the **sounding** (plural: **soundings**; code **SND**). Soundings are not a circulating currency. They are ledger entries, existing only inside the Ledger's settlement system and its member banks ([CORP-008](11_CORPORATIONS.md) Argent Vault chief among them).

### Why soundings exist

Anomalies are non-fungible ([02_WORLD_RULES.md](02_WORLD_RULES.md), II). You cannot price "one anomaly" the way you price one barrel of oil â€” every Seep is unique, every deal bespoke. But reinsurers, courts, and auction houses still need a *common denominator* to net obligations against each other. The sounding is that denominator: an abstract unit for settling *claims about unique things*, not a price tag on the things themselves.

### What a sounding is

> CANON: 1 sounding (SND) is defined by the Ledger as the assessed value of **one standard containment-week for a T2 object Seep** â€” i.e., seven days of vaulting, monitoring, and insuring a mid-grade anomalous object. All other anomaly valuations are expressed relative to this benchmark by accredited assessors.

In practice:

- A T1 object Seep at auction might clear at 0.2â€“2 SND.
- A T3 place-Seep containment contract might be denominated at 400â€“2,000 SND.
- Tribunal fines are levied in soundings; so are Meridian Re's reinsurance treaties.

### The reference rate

> STATUS: PROVISIONAL (the Ledger publishes a quarterly reference band, not a peg): **1 SND â‰ˆ $12,000â€“16,000** (2024 band). The band moves with Tide pressure â€” High Tide has pushed it up ~35% since 2018, because containment costs more when pressure rises.

The sounding is not convertible at a window. Conversion happens through member banks (Argent Vault and six others) as negotiated foreign-exchange-style deals, reported to the Ledger. Large conversions move the band. Small ones don't.

### Who can hold soundings

Membership tiers, set by the Ledger:

- **Settlement members** (~120 entities): reinsurers, major banks' anomalous-asset desks, states' threshold agencies, the Compact itself. Full clearing access.
- **Assessed clients**: auction buyers/sellers, disposal firms, large collectors (including [SUP-009](12_FACTIONS.md) Pale Court families). They transact *through* a settlement member.
- **Everyone else**: cannot touch soundings. If you're settling a Seep deal in dollars, you're retail. If you're settling in soundings, you're in the room.

> CANON: Soundings are the reason anomaly auctions don't destabilize normal finance. By denominating the weird economy in its own unit and settling it inside a closed system, the Ledger quarantines supernatural price volatility away from fiat markets â€” a firewall built of accounting.

## 9.3 Black-market currencies and Tide-broker networks

Below the Ledger's white-glove settlement layer runs the informal plumbing.

### What actually changes hands

In order of volume:

1. **Cash** â€” USD, EUR, CNY. Still king. A duffel bag of euros buys a T1 object Seep in Trieste as well as it buys anything else.
2. **Cryptocurrency** â€” Monero first, then Bitcoin via mixers. Used for *coordination* (bounties, retainers) more than for the goods themselves, because the goods are physical and someone has to carry them.
3. **Mundane gold and gems** â€” the Gray Markets' (see [36_TERMINOLOGY.md](36_TERMINOLOGY.md)) preferred store of value. Boring, portable, and â€” critically â€” *not* anomalous, so it doesn't rot, revert, or show up on a Seismograph.
4. **Kind** â€” Tide-brokers routinely settle in favors, introductions, and *information*. A broker who knows which warehouse the Sweepers will hit Thursday is richer than one holding cash.

### The Tally ([NET-001](09_CURRENCIES.md))

> CANON: **The Tally** is the hidden world's hawala: a trust-based clearing network of Tide-brokers who net obligations against each other without moving money â€” or moving it only in balancing transfers, disguised as ordinary remittances, trade invoices, and (increasingly) mobile-money hops.

How it works:

- A buyer in Lagos pays a broker in naira. A seller in Jakarta is paid by a different broker in rupiah. No money crosses borders; the two brokers settle against each other later, in whatever the network owes â€” cash, kind, or soundings via a settlement member's back door.
- Settlement is enforced by **reputation and exposure**, not law. A broker who defaults is *named* â€” in a world where anonymity is survival, naming is a death sentence by other means. Default is therefore rare and usually fatal.
- The Tally charges 3â€“8% per clear, more for urgency or deniability. Its total throughput is unknowable; Compact analysts guess $2â€“5B/year (STATUS: THEORY, wide error bars).

### Kestrel Drops ([NET-002](09_CURRENCIES.md))

The physical layer: a decentralized courier network for moving anomalous objects â€” warded cases, mislabeled cargo, diplomatic-pouch abuse, and the occasional fishing boat. Kestrels don't ask what's in the case; they charge by weight, discretion, and how much the case *hums*. Styx Logistics ([CORP-007](11_CORPORATIONS.md)) is the legal industry; Kestrels are what you use when Styx would file paperwork.

> CANON: **Wards suppress emissions, not just Tide.** A properly maintained ward suppresses **acoustic, thermal, and RF emissions** as well as Tide pressure â€” the case doesn't hum, doesn't warm, doesn't ping a scanner. *Properly maintained* is doing heavy work in that sentence: wards **degrade as Tide pressure rises** ([08_ECONOMY.md](08_ECONOMY.md) 8.2.2), and the High Tide is a rising failure mode. **Why couriers still fly commercial.** Slip-courier chains â€” relaying cargo Slip-to-Slip â€” are legal and, for emergencies, used. For commerce they are economically dead ([02_WORLD_RULES.md](02_WORLD_RULES.md), I): each link pays full Erosion per leg, and the chain's price compounds past any cargo's value within three hops. The Kestrels' fishing boats beat teleportation on price. Every time.

## 9.4 Supernatural assets as stores of value

## 9.5 Anti-loophole rules: why powers don't break finance

> CANON: Every obvious scheme has been tried. Every one fails on at least one hard rule. The following are settled doctrine, taught to every threshold-agency recruit and every Argent Vault compliance officer:

**Conjured gold / conjured anything of value.**
Fails on [02_WORLD_RULES.md](02_WORLD_RULES.md) I & II: *borrowed matter reverts within 72 hours*. Financial settlement clears on T+2 at the fastest; by the time the receiving bank's assay finishes, the gold is gone â€” sometimes *during* the assay, which is how the 2009 "Zurich reversion" was discovered (three vault officers, early retirement, generous pensions, no public record). Attempting this at scale is also Seismograph-visible (02, II). Verdict: *works once, badly, then you're famous and poor.*

**Precognition trading.**
Fails on economics and Erosion. Financial-precognition Resonances exist at small scales (02, II) â€” and every major exchange runs Lantern-screened compliance: listed-company news flows are monitored for Tide-pressure signatures, and "impossibly lucky" traders are investigated by people who know what Erosion looks like.

> CANON: **What a precog actually perceives.** The Undertow has no time (02, I) â€” so precognition does not "see the future." It reads **present informational traces and probability branches**: order-book pressure, the informational wake of decisions already made but not yet announced, the shape of the adjacent possible. **Observer reflexivity** is the binding constraint: the more observers price in a branch, the faster the branch decays â€” a precog trading *against* a market they can see is sawing the branch they're standing on. Edge is therefore self-limiting: it works small, briefly, and alone.
>
> **The per-trade price (CANON):** a market-relevant precognitive read costs **~0.1â€“0.5 Fathoms per trade** at small scale, rising steeply with position size and holding period (the deeper the read, the more of the pattern it rents). A desk running twenty reads a day burns ~2â€“10 Fathoms a *week* â€” a career measured in months. The expected lifetime P&L of a precog trader is negative once you price the selfhood. *"Lucky traders keep Drowning"* is not a proverb. It's a mortality table.
>
> **Crypto and offshore venues (CANON):** the compliance perimeter followed the money. Unregulated crypto venues are *not* outside the system: the Ledger's banking leverage (9.2 â€” soundings settle through member banks) means any venue that touches fiat on-ramps touches Lantern-screened compliance, and pure-crypto P&L still has to be *spent* somewhere. The "impossible luck" pattern-analysis runs on-chain too â€” Meridian Re's models don't care what the ledger is called. Two crypto-precog desks have Drowned since 2019; their wallets are case studies.

**Energy generation (free power).**
Fails on scale and visibility. Tide-tapped generation ([CORP-009](11_CORPORATIONS.md) Helios) works in pilot plants and nowhere else: output above trivial levels is Seismograph-visible, the Erosion cost of sustained channeling bankrupts the operator faster than the plant pays back, and Undertow-sourced energy has a documented habit of *doing something else* at the worst moment (Helios's 2020 "surplus event" in Nevada is the industry's cautionary tale). Verdict: a research subsidy, not a power grid.

**Suggestion-seeding markets (Choir manipulation).**
Fails on law and backlash. Direct mind control is impossible; suggestion seeding is resistible, produces backlash in the seeder (02, I), and Choir market manipulation carries the heaviest penalties in Accords law â€” the Tribunal treats it as an attack on the settlement system itself. Two convictions, 2002 and 2017; both defendants Drowned within eighteen months of sentencing, which the compliance manuals note without comment.

**Tide-mark viewing (seeing the past to trade on it).**
Fails on actionability. Tide-marks are viewable, never editable (02, I) â€” and viewing the past at *market-relevant* resolution requires Lantern work at T3+, which is Seismograph-visible, licensable, and auditable. Knowing what happened is not the same as being able to *prove* it to a counterparty, and the deepfake dividend (02, III) means nobody believes your Tide-mark readouts anyway. The information is real; the edge isn't.

**Information Seeps as insider trading.**
Fails on containment. Information Seeps (03, 3.3) are the rarest and hardest to contain â€” *you cannot put a fact in a vault*. Anyone who has traded on Seep-derived information has also *become* a vector for it. The Compact's information-hygiene doctrine treats this as a containment breach first and a crime second. There are no rich survivors of this trade. There are case files.

> CANON: The financial system survives contact with the supernatural for the same reason buildings survive earthquakes in Tokyo: it was designed by people who assumed the ground moves. Every control above exists because someone tried the exploit first.

## 9.7 The fiat firewall: the conversion protocol

> CANON: Soundings are quarantined inside the Ledger's closed system (9.2) â€” but Quiet *money* crosses into ordinary money every day: salaries, claim advances, materials sales. The crossing is legal, licensed, and the masquerade's load-bearing financial control.

**The protocol:**

1. **Licensed converters only.** The Ledger ([INTL-003](07_INTERNATIONAL_RELATIONS.md)) licenses seven member banks â€” [CORP-008](11_CORPORATIONS.md) Argent Vault chief among them â€” as the only legal Quietâ†”ordinary converters. 3. **Mandatory reporting.** Every conversion is reported to the Ledger. Conversions above threshold are additionally reported to the Seismograph Authority: a large conversion is a large operation's exhaust, and pattern-break analysis starts with the money.
4. **Licensed retail-edge crossings.** Ordinary payroll (salaries are ordinary money by design), [CORP-011](11_CORPORATIONS.md)'s rupiah bridge advances (9.1), and [CORP-003](11_CORPORATIONS.md) Blackforge's resonant-materials sales into ordinary supply chains (8.2.5) are *licensed* crossings, not violations â€” this is how Quiet money re-enters the ordinary economy without breaking the masquerade. **The seasoning rule (CANON).** Quiet-adjacent settlement of *physical* deliveries â€” bullion, materials, collateral â€” is value-dated **T+5**, outside the 72-hour borrowed-matter reversion window ([02_WORLD_RULES.md](02_WORLD_RULES.md) I).
