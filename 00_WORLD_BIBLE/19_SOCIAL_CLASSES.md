# 19 — SOCIAL CLASSES

Who stands where in the Quiet Tide — and why ability doesn't buy a permanent aristocracy.

> CANON: Social class in this world works like the real world's, with one extra axis: **hidden-world position** (Attuned tier, institutional role, Loudness, knowledge). The two axes interact constantly and neither determines the other.

Related files: [08_ECONOMY.md](08_ECONOMY.md) (incomes), [18_CIVILIAN_LIFE.md](18_CIVILIAN_LIFE.md) (daily texture), [04_POWER_SYSTEM.md](04_POWER_SYSTEM.md) (why power doesn't compound).

## 19.1 The classes

### Ordinary civilians (~8 billion, minus the rest)

The Quiet majority. They farm, code, nurse, drive, teach, and scroll. Their hidden-world role is *ballast*: they fund the masquerade (through insurance premiums and taxes), staff its camouflage (every mundane job inside a supernatural company), and forget its evidence. They are not stupid, not sheep, not background — they are *people*, and the world is built to keep them that way. The bible's moral center lives here.

### The Loud (~2–4% of humanity)

Not a class in the economic sense — Loudness cuts across all incomes — but a *social* one. The Loud remember. This makes them:

- **Valuable** (witnesses worth more than footage — [03_SUPERNATURAL_SYSTEM.md](03_SUPERNATURAL_SYSTEM.md) 3.4; [CORP-011](11_CORPORATIONS.md) hires them deliberately),
- **Vulnerable** (quiet grief, misdiagnosis — [16_MEDICINE.md](16_MEDICINE.md) 16.5; recruitment targets for cults and agencies alike),
- **Connected** — [IND-002](12_FACTIONS.md) the Rememberers is the Loud's mutual-aid society, and the [IND-004](12_FACTIONS.md) Night Clerks (service-worker info network) is disproportionately Loud. Arthur's ([CHAR-001](01_CORE_PREMISE.md)) natural community, if he ever finds it.

> CANON: Loudness is the hidden world's most *democratic* trait — it ignores wealth, and the institutions that exploit it (insurers, agencies, cults) all recruit downward, not upward.

### The Attuned (~900,000; T1–T4 working population)

The hidden world's working class *and* its professional class, depending on tier:

- **T1–T2** are *labor*: technicians, contractors, gig detectors. Respected the way electricians are respected — skilled, necessary, not rich. Most live ordinary middle-class lives with extraordinary NDAs.
- **T3** are *professionals*: the surgeons and senior engineers of the hidden world. Comfortable, watched, licensed to the hilt.
- **T4** are *assets*: maintained by states, named in classified briefings, unable to live normally. The gilded cage class.
- **T5+** are not a class; they're *weather systems with pensions*.

> CANON: The Attuned are *not* an aristocracy. They're a **workforce with a terminal condition** — and they know it. Attuned labor politics (the quiet unionization of Sweeper teams, the freelance Lanterns' rate-setting networks) is one of the hidden world's most under-reported stories.

### The threshold-adjacent

The hidden world's service class — the stratum this taxonomy and daily life both kept gesturing at without naming. Drivers who know which warehouses not to ask about. Cleaners with Quiet-clearance mops. Fixers, dispatchers, Kestrel ground crews, the Night Clerks' day-job selves. Mostly ordinary civilians by the taxonomy above, mostly Loud-*adjacent* by exposure: you cannot mop a containment cell for five years and remember nothing. Their class marker is *proximity without clearance* — they see the edges of everything and the center of nothing, which makes them the hidden world's best-informed and worst-paid witnesses. Arthur starts here (the 1K class is its Ravenhurst barracks — [18_CIVILIAN_LIFE.md](18_CIVILIAN_LIFE.md) 18.5). The masquerade's labor shortage lives *here*, not among the Attuned: you can train a Lantern, but you cannot train discretion at scale.

### Researchers and experts

The Meridian Institute's ([RES-001](15_EDUCATION.md)) fellows, PKF-UI's ([RES-002](15_EDUCATION.md)) field teams, Vesper's ([RES-004](15_EDUCATION.md)) pharmacologists, the actuaries at Meridian Re ([CORP-004](11_CORPORATIONS.md)). Mostly unpowered, mostly Loud-adjacent, *indispensable* — because information is the scarce resource ([02_WORLD_RULES.md](02_WORLD_RULES.md), II), the people who produce it outrank the people who channel. A T2 Lantern takes orders from a civilian analyst with a better model. This happens daily. Nobody finds it strange except newcomers.

### Officials

Threshold-agency staff, Compact bureaucrats, Tribunal ([INTL-002](07_INTERNATIONAL_RELATIONS.md)) jurists. The hidden world's *nomenklatura*: moderate pay, extraordinary access, total discretion. Their class marker isn't money — it's *clearance*. A BTA section chief ([GOV-001](06_GOVERNMENTS.md)) earns less than a Halcyon VP and can end the VP's career with a memo. Everyone in the room knows the exchange rate.

### Corporate

The executives and specialists of CORP-001–011. Well-paid, well-insured, mostly unpowered. Their class power is *infrastructural*: they own the companies the masquerade runs on. The Halcyon VP, the Argent Vault ([CORP-008](11_CORPORATIONS.md)) partner, the Meridian Re actuary — these are the hidden world's bourgeoisie, and like all bourgeoisies, they believe their position is earned, which is half true.

### Criminal elites

[CRIM-001](12_FACTIONS.md)–[CRIM-005](12_FACTIONS.md): the Koi Network's brokers, Naga Hitam's Jakarta leadership, the Ash Exchange's auctioneers. Wealthy, paranoid, *useful* — the hidden world's states tolerate criminal elites the way states have always tolerated useful criminals: as deniable capacity. Their class marker is *exposure without protection*: rich, but one raid from ruin, which makes them the most risk-literate people in the room.

### The Pale Court ([SUP-009](12_FACTIONS.md))

Old money that collects anomalies the way other old money collects art — except the art *does things*. Five to eight families (the number is disputed; the families prefer it that way), wealth measured in centuries, holdings including an estimated $8–15B in anomalous assets ([09_CURRENCIES.md](09_CURRENCIES.md) 9.4, STATUS: THEORY).

- **What they are:** the closest thing the hidden world has to an aristocracy — and the proof that aristocracy here *isn't built on ability*. The Pale Court's power is *capital and continuity*: they were rich before the Accords, stayed rich through them, and buy what the Attuned can't keep (Erosion takes the channeler; it doesn't take the vault).
- **What they do:** collect, endow ([ACAD-002](15_EDUCATION.md) White Reed's oldest donors), fund ([08_ECONOMY.md](08_ECONOMY.md) 8.6's gray money), and *wait*. The Court's time horizon is generational; it has outlasted every rival by simply continuing to exist.
- **What they don't do:** channel (vulgar, and the Erosion would be *visible*), govern (they prefer owning the governors' mortgages), or explain themselves (to anyone, ever).
- **Internal life:** dynastic marriages, inheritance law as blood sport, and the *succession problem* — the Hollow Pattern doesn't breed true ([04_POWER_SYSTEM.md](04_POWER_SYSTEM.md) 4.1), so every generation's Attuned heirs are a lottery. The families that handle this gracefully survive; the ones that don't become case studies in [CORP-010](11_CORPORATIONS.md) Cradle's pitch deck.

> CANON: The Pale Court is the hidden world's *ruling class without ruling anything directly* — influence through capital, not command. They are not evil-by-default ([02_WORLD_RULES.md](02_WORLD_RULES.md), IV); they are *patient*, which is more durable.

## 19.2 How ability affects social mobility

> CANON: Ability helps — then the Erosion clock and the Census cap it. **No superhuman aristocracy stabilizes.** The mechanism:

### The boost (Awakening to ~T3, 0–35 Fathoms)

An Awakened T2 from a poor background gets: academy scholarship ([15_EDUCATION.md](15_EDUCATION.md)), a professional salary ([08_ECONOMY.md](08_ECONOMY.md) 8.3), institutional protection, and — for the first time — *options*. This is the hidden world's great meritocratic engine, and it's real: a plurality of working Attuned are first-generation, and the agencies recruit talent wherever it Awakens. Social mobility *up* is the norm at the entry level.

### The ceiling (T3+, 35+ Fathoms)

Then the caps engage:

1. **The Erosion clock** ([04_POWER_SYSTEM.md](04_POWER_SYSTEM.md) 4.4). Wealth compounds over decades; Erosion compounds faster. You can't build a dynasty on a depreciating self. The most powerful die youngest — T5s rarely see 60.
2. **The Census** ([36_TERMINOLOGY.md](36_TERMINOLOGY.md)). Registration means visibility: to the state, the Compact, the Seismograph network. Visibility means *constraints* — licensing, monitoring, service obligations. The registered powerful are *managed*, not free.
3. **The inheritance lottery** ([04_POWER_SYSTEM.md](04_POWER_SYSTEM.md) 4.1). Children of Attuned carry the pattern ~40% of the time and Awaken ~30× more often — but Awakening still needs a trigger, and dynasties shatter on the First Tide. The Pale Court's genealogists have the data: *no Attuned lineage has held T4+ for three consecutive generations*. Power doesn't inherit; money does.
4. **The Veil economics of prominence** ([08_ECONOMY.md](08_ECONOMY.md) 8.7). Real power requires obscurity. Anyone who converts ability into visible status becomes a target — for recruiters, for rivals' Rule-interference, for the Census's full attention. The optimal strategy is comfortable invisibility, which is *not* how aristocracies are built.

### The result

> CANON: The hidden world's class structure is **fluid at the bottom, capped at the top, and dominated in the middle by the unpowered**. The people with the most *durable* power are the actuaries, the bankers, the archivists, and the old-money collectors — people whose assets don't Erode. Ability is a *career*, not a *caste*.

## 19.3 Cross-class encounters (where the novel lives)

- **The clerk and the asset.** Arthur ([CHAR-001](01_CORE_PREMISE.md)) files claims generated by operations he'll never be cleared to know about. The T3 whose mission created the paperwork earns 20× his salary and will Drown a decade before Arthur retires. Neither envies the other. Both are right.
- **The nurse and the envelope.** Mariana (Mexico City — [18_CIVILIAN_LIFE.md](18_CIVILIAN_LIFE.md)) holds a protocol written by people she'll never meet, protecting a child she'll never see again. The system's design *depends* on her never being promoted into it.
- **The collector and the courier.** A Pale Court buyer and a [NET-002](09_CURRENCIES.md) Kestrel meet in a Trieste warehouse. One has centuries of money; the other has a warded case and a cousin with a boat. The transaction is perfectly equal and perfectly unequal at once.
- **The actuary and the god.** Priya Nair ([CORP-004](11_CORPORATIONS.md)) prices the risk of a T5 event the way she prices hurricanes. The T5 in question is a 58-year-old woman in Oslo who gardens and is afraid of her own hands. The model doesn't know about the garden. The model doesn't need to.
