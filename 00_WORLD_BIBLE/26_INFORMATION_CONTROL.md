# 26 — INFORMATION CONTROL

> CANON: The masquerade is not a secret. It is **seven nested information regimes**, each of which knows the previous one is lying, and each of which is right. The question is never "who knows?" — it is "who knows *what*, and what does it cost them to know it?"

## 26.1 The seven levels

### Level 1 — The public

Knows: urban legends, a strange night nobody can quite remember, conspiracy subcultures, the "Quiet" meme, ten thousand fake paranormal videos for every real one. The public's model of the supernatural is **entertainment-shaped**: ghosts, aliens, creepypasta. This is not an accident (see 26.2, noise farming).

What the public *doesn't* know it doesn't know: that the Veil edited its memory this morning. The Quiet majority cannot retain Seep exposure ([Supernatural System](03_SUPERNATURAL_SYSTEM.md)) — the public is not ignorant; it is *immunized*.

### Level 2 — Loud civilians

Knows: everything they have personally witnessed — permanently. The Loud (2–4%) remember every Seep event in detail. What they mostly *don't* know: the system. A Loud witness knows what happened on their street; they don't know about the Classification Office, the Ledger, or the Census. They are witnesses without a courtroom.

Infrastructure: the Rememberers (IND-002) — support groups, part therapy, part samizdat archive. The Night Clerks (IND-004) — service workers who trade incident information as professional currency. Arthur belongs to this level's edge: employed by the system, but not *of* it.

The Loud's structural position: **valuable and unprotected.** Witnesses are worth more than footage ([Supernatural System](03_SUPERNATURAL_SYSTEM.md)) — which makes every Loud civilian a potential asset and a potential target, with no agency formally responsible for them. The Compact's position, never written down: *the Loud are a self-maintaining sensor network we don't have to pay for.*

### Level 3 — Corporate

Knows: whatever the contract requires, compartmentalized by NDA and need-to-know. Halcyon engineers know containment hardware specs but not what the hardware contains. Quiet Insurance actuaries — Arthur's world — know **vectors, claim frequencies, and payout tables**: the statistical shadow of the supernatural, without the substance. Meridian Re (CORP-004) sees the global reinsurance book: the closest any corporation gets to the whole picture, and still only the *financial* picture.

What corporate doesn't know: the politics. Corporations see incidents as line items; they are systematically blind to *why* the BTA seized something or *who* the Jade Office is watching. This blindness is structural — and it's why corporate actors keep blundering into faction conflicts they can't see.

### Level 4 — Religious

Knows: the pastoral ground truth. Parish priests, imams, and monks file more first-reports than any intelligence agency — the Vatican's network alone (see [Governments](06_GOVERNMENTS.md), GOV-010) out-reports most states. Majelis Fenomena scholars (REL-003) and the Parisada working group (REL-004) hold centuries of folk-heuristic archives the Compact's scientists cite without fully understanding.

What religious doesn't know (officially): the system. The OEP knows; the parish priest doesn't. The result is the hidden world's strangest information flow: the people closest to the phenomena understand them through theology, and their reports are translated into vectors by people who've never met them. Something is always lost in translation — usually the part that mattered.

### Level 5 — Government

Knows: its own national picture, in full — vectors, Census rolls, budgets, operations. What it doesn't know: everyone else's. The Memorandum Group (INTL-007) shares Lantern intelligence among nine states; China and Russia share bilaterally; everyone evades the 2017 information-sharing floors to the exact extent they can afford to. Every government in [06_GOVERNMENTS.md](06_GOVERNMENTS.md) is certain its rivals are hiding something — and each is correct about the others and discreet about itself.

### Level 6 — The Compact inner circle

Knows: the closest thing to the whole picture that exists — the Seismograph raw feed, the Medical Directorate's Erosion data, the Classification Office's disputed files, the Arbitration Court's sealed awards, the two missing Trieste lots ([EVENT-062](07_INTERNATIONAL_RELATIONS.md)). Secretary-General Diallo (CHAR-008) reads the morning brief that no head of state gets.

What even the inner circle doesn't know: **the cause of the Tide** ([Supernatural System](03_SUPERNATURAL_SYSTEM.md) — every theory is wrong or incomplete), the full contents of the Vatican's pontifical-secret archive, what the three Worldtides perceive (see below), and — the inner circle's own recurring nightmare — how many vectors are wrong. The Classification Office's internal estimate: *8–12% of active vectors are materially wrong, and we don't know which.* The system runs on a map it knows is misdrawn.

### Level 7 — The three Worldtides

> CANON: Three confirmed T6 Attuned are alive ([Power System](04_POWER_SYSTEM.md)). All are under permanent Compact observation. All are past 70 Fathoms. All are dying by inches.

- **CHAR-009 — Adaeze Okonkwo** (Tide discipline, ~81 Fathoms; observation residence, Geneva). A former civil engineer. Perceives Tide pressure the way others perceive weather — continuously, involuntarily. Has not channeled deliberately in eleven years; the Medical Directorate's position is that one more major channeling would Drown her. What she knows: *the shape of the Tide itself* — patterns in the pressure that no instrument resolves. What she shares: almost nothing. Her debriefings are famously courteous and famously empty. Whether this is Erosion, choice, or the thinning is the subject of a sealed medical file.
- **CHAR-010 — Tomás Aquino Reyes** (Choir discipline, ~74 Fathoms; observation residence, Manila). A former radio priest. The only Choir T6 in history — which is why his observation protocol was written by lawyers, not doctors. What he knows: the *emotional* texture of crowds under Tide stress — he claims he can feel a city's Veil weakening before the Seismograph sees it. The Compact treats his warnings as data and his company as a controlled substance. He asks after his former parishioners by name. The staff are not allowed to answer.
- **CHAR-011 — Hana Shirakawa** (Hollow discipline, ~88 Fathoms; observation residence, Hokkaido). A former surveyor. The deepest of the three and the least communicative — Hollow T6 perception doesn't translate well into language, and at 88 Fathoms, neither does she. Her file contains the Compact's most unsettling standing assessment: *"Subject's Tide-mark bleed includes impressions that do not correspond to any known event. Flag for Layer-5 review."* ([Mysteries](33_MYSTERIES.md).)

> STATUS: UNKNOWN (author-level): what the Worldtides know that they aren't saying — and whether any of them still *can* say it. Level 7 is the top of the hierarchy and the least useful: perfect perception, zero transmission. The hierarchy's final irony.

## 26.2 The masquerade's mechanics

Seven pillars, interlocking. Remove any one and the system strains; remove two and it fails. The Compact's internal planning assumes the High Tide will eventually remove two.

1. **The Veil Reflex.** The foundation: 96–98% of humans cannot retain Seep exposure ([World Rules](02_WORLD_RULES.md)). The masquerade's cheapest and most reliable asset — it works while everyone sleeps, costs nothing, and cannot be subpoenaed.
2. **Static rot.** Recordings of active Seeps corrupt proportional to Tide pressure. The stronger the event, the less recordable — the universe's own evidence policy. **Transitivity (CANON):** rot applies to *records of events*, not to *records of records* — a photograph of a Seep rots; a written report *about* the photograph does not. The catalog is safe — but its fidelity to rotted primaries is bounded: where the primary recording died, the catalog preserves the report, not the event, and every serious archive carries the gap as stated provenance.
3. **Noise farming.** Since the 1990s, intelligence-adjacent media operations (Pale Lantern Media, CORP-006, chief among them) have flooded platforms with fake paranormal content. Real leaks drown in ten thousand fakes — and the farmers are *good at their jobs*: the fakes are more entertaining than the truth, so the truth never trends.
4. **The deepfake dividend.** Since ~2020, public trust in video evidence has collapsed — the best secrecy ally ever invented, and it was free. It cuts both ways (factions can't prove things to each other either — [World Rules](02_WORLD_RULES.md)), but for the masquerade's purposes it is pure profit: *even a perfect leak reads as synthetic.*
5. **Sweepers.** Field teams who do exposure cleanup and memory care — the human layer. They arrive, they document, they dose, they file. The system's hands.
6. **Quiet Insurance.** The financial layer: post-exposure memory care and incident cover as a *consumer product*. The genius of the system — people who experience the impossible are given a claims process, a counselor, and a payout. A victim with an open claim is a victim who isn't posting about it. Arthur's employer is not peripheral to the masquerade; it is one of its load-bearing walls.
7. **Compartmentalization + boredom.** The seventh pillar, unofficial: the hidden world is mostly *paperwork*. Leaks about filing procedures, budget meetings, and vector re-audits don't go viral — there is no footage of a committee. And no one person holds "the secret": the secret is eighty years of distributed infrastructure, ten thousand clearances, and a million boring files. You cannot leak a system.

### How the Compact remembers (institutional memory)

> CANON: The Veil edits the Quiet — so the Compact's memory is built out of the people the Veil can't touch. The **archives are Loud-staffed** by policy: classification offices, Seismograph watch floors, and the Tribunal's registry recruit Loud civilians the way intelligence agencies recruit linguists — quietly, and for the same reason. The doctrine is **paper-over-video**: write the report, file the paper, distrust the footage — static rot eats video, the Veil eats witnesses, but a written report filed by a Loud archivist *stays filed*. **I-flag handling** is the sharp end: information-hazard-adjacent material is never stored as recordings or verbatim transcripts — only as examiner summaries, compartmentalized, with the original destroyed after summarization. The **Archivists** ([SUP-008](12_FACTIONS.md)) are the doctrine's oldest practitioners and its auditors: they certify the Compact's archives the way accountants certify books. When an archivist Drowns, the protocol is brutal and practiced: immediate compartmentalization of everything they touched, a Lantern sweep for residue, and a junior promoted into the empty chair within the week. The archive does not grieve. The archive *continues*.

### The capacity arithmetic (classified)

> CANON: The following is Compact-internal (Classification Office / JCC joint planning figure, 2024-01, classified — the numbers the minority report in §26.4 is built on). Seep frequency has roughly tripled since 2000 ([01_CORE_PREMISE.md](01_CORE_PREMISE.md)); the January 2024 peak ran 900+ Seep events in a single day ([EVENT-090](25_TIMEmessaging app.md)).

| | 2000 | 2024 |
|---|---|---|
| Seep events/year (global, all tiers) | ~110,000 | ~330,000 |
| of which T3+ | ~4,500 | ~13,500 |
| Sweeper field personnel (Compact-registered) | ~31,000 | ~48,000 |
| Events per Sweeper per year | ~3.5 | ~6.9 |

- **Regional headcount (2024):** East Asia/Western Europe ~14,000; Europe ~9,000; North America ~8,000; South Asia ~6,000; Middle East/Africa ~5,000; Latin America ~4,000; reserve/rotational ~2,000. T0–T2 events are mostly insurance-led (no Sweeper dispatched); Sweeper teams cover T2+ exposures and all T3+.
- **Cost-per-containment curve (2024 USD, all-in):** T0–T2: $2–8k (Quiet Insurance absorbs); T3: ~$40k; T4: ~$400k; T5: ~$4M (JCC command); T6+: unpriceable — evacuation and mitigation, not containment. A C-class anomaly costs roughly 40× a B-class one ([05_ANOMALY_CLASSIFICATION.md](05_ANOMALY_CLASSIFICATION.md) 5.5).
- **The funding gap:** the JCC's 2022 request — a 60% corps expansion to hold the 2024 event rate — was voted down by the Council (the vote the minority report cites). The corps is sized for the *2000* event rate at 2024 wages. Premium growth (+40% since 2018, [11_CORPORATIONS.md](11_CORPORATIONS.md)) is still lagging loss growth. The arithmetic the masquerade runs on: **~330,000 events, ~48,000 Sweepers, and a Council that keeps voting down the difference.**

> CANON: The Compact's internal planning assumption (2024) is that the corps can cover ~60% of T3+ events within the 72-hour protocol window at current headcount. The rest get insurance-led cleanup and a file. This is the number behind the minority report's conclusion: *outpaced, not exposed*.

## 26.3 What Arthur knows at story start

> CANON: [LEGACY / PRE-ACADEMY] CHAR-001 Arthur Reed, 24, night-shift records clerk — a regular employee — at the Ravenhurst branch of Nusantara Quiet Assurance (CORP-011). Loud, not Attuned. For the current Academy branch, this workplace status is superseded; use the Academy locks for Arthur's active role.

**He knows:**
- The *paperwork* of the hidden world, intimately: Meridian Vectors (redacted tiers — he sees the vector strings and the claim narratives, not the I-flag annexes), incident codes, payout tables, Sweeper invoices, BPF referral forms. He can read a vector the way a musician reads a score.
- That the Census exists, that classifications exist, that the BPF is underfunded (he sees the denied claims), which Jakarta neighborhoods generate incidents, what a D-class file looks like when it's being slow-walked.
- That his memory is unusual — but **he assumes it's normal-for-the-job**. His defining early misunderstanding: he thinks everyone in Quiet Insurance remembers like he does, the way a fish doesn't know about water. He has never met another civilian Loud adult to compare with. (The Rememberers, IND-002, would change his life. He doesn't know they exist.)
- His anomaly: the Laggard follows him, drinks light, is filed `MV-T1/C-A/—/Local/Unknown`. He thinks it's a curiosity. Everyone who has scanned him agrees. Everyone is reading the same wrong file.

**He doesn't know:**
- Anything about Compact inner politics, the Memorandum Group, Drowndust, the Asylum Protocol, or the Worldtides. These are above his clearance the way the stratosphere is above a basement.
- That his perfect recall is rare *even among the Loud* — most Loud remember events; Arthur remembers *detail*: serial numbers, vector strings, the exact wording of a form he filed once, three years ago. This is the asset no one has priced yet.
- What the Laggard is. (See [29_PROTAGONIST_ANOMALY.md](29_PROTAGONIST_ANOMALY.md). He will learn this the slow way: by noticing what his files don't say.)

**His structural position:** Arthur sits at the exact junction where Level 2 (Loud civilian) meets Level 3 (corporate): he has a witness's memory and a clerk's access. He is the cheapest sensor in the system and the least protected — which is why, when the story starts moving, every faction's first instinct will be to *acquire* him, and his only defense will be knowing the paperwork better than the people holding the guns.

## 26.4 Stress-test: why hasn't a Snowden ended the secrecy?

> CANON: There have been leakers. Three are documented below. All failed — not because they were incompetent, but because **the system is structured to digest leaks**. A Snowden needs three things: an audience that can retain the leak, evidence that survives, and a single secret worth stealing. The hidden world denies all three, structurally.

**1. The audience forgets.** The 1992 **Geneva clerk** — a Classification Office archivist, Loud, meticulous — exfiltrated the classification manuals and mailed them to six newspapers. Three editors read them; two wrote memos; all six forgot the details within weeks (Veil), and the manuals' I-flag-adjacent sections rotted in every photocopier that touched them. The clerk spent the next decade writing conspiracy books — which the noise farmers amplified, gently, into discredit. He died in 2009, cited by exactly the subcultures he'd tried to warn. The system didn't silence him; it *outlived* his evidence.

**2. The evidence doesn't survive — and the boring kind doesn't travel.** The 2006 **Jakarta actuary** — a Meridian Re analyst — tried to leak Quiet Insurance payout data proving Seep frequency was tripling. The data was real, the analysis was correct, and the leak was *spreadsheets*. Spreadsheets don't go viral. Two journalists confirmed the numbers, couldn't confirm what the numbers *meant* (every vector was redacted), and killed the story. The analyst was bought out with a pension and a non-disclosure agreement she now describes, accurately, as "the best-paid irrelevance of my life." The system's genius: the most damning evidence is actuarial, and actuarial evidence is boring.

**3. The perfect leak reads as content.** The 2014 **livestreamer** — an Attuned T2 who streamed her channeling to 40,000 live viewers. Static rot ate the footage in real time; what the audience saw was artifacting, dropout, and — at the end — her Drowning, which the Veil edited into *a weird performance-art piece*. Clips circulated for a week as "insane ARGs," the noise farmers seeded seventeen superior fakes, and the deepfake dividend did the rest. She is the hidden world's cautionary tale, taught in every academy: *the audience will applaud your death and call it content.*

> CANON: **The doctrine is currently working on Arthur Reed — and he is the hard case.** The regime has not failed to notice Use 10; it has *responded* the way the doctrine prescribes: not with a strike (martyrs leak louder than the living) but with the Quiet Desk's human-intelligence interest — recruitment, surveillance, the escrow's cold arithmetic. The instruments never saw the recordings (Use 10's signature is below the Seismograph's threshold — [the drafter's ruler](29_PROTAGONIST_ANOMALY.md)); the *people* saw the clerk. And the escrow inverts the doctrine's usual math: seizing him triggers the dead-man's switch, so the system that digests leakers by outliving them cannot outlive *him* by seizing him. The anti-Snowden regime's answer to the walking Snowden is the oldest one in the book — *hire him before he publishes.* Whether the Desk succeeds is the series.

**4. There is no single secret.** Snowden stole *a system of files*. The hidden world's equivalent — the whole apparatus — is eighty years of distributed infrastructure across 191 states, ten thousand clearances, and a million boring files. No one person holds it. The Geneva clerk stole the *manual*; the manual without the Seismograph feed, the Census rolls, and the Ledger is a dictionary without a language.

**5. Golden handcuffs.** Defection means losing Stillwater access, Erosion care, Quiet Insurance, and the pension — for the Attuned, it means losing the medical infrastructure that keeps them alive. The system doesn't need to threaten leakers; it needs only to *stop treating* them. Every Attuned past 40 Fathoms knows exactly what their care costs and exactly who provides it.

**6. The partial successes were absorbed.** Conspiracy subcultures — the Static Hour podcast (IND-001), the ROT/FEED livestream collective (IND-003) — function as **containment layers**: the truth is out there, labeled entertainment, monetized, and algorithmically fenced. The system's final defense is not suppression but *genre*: once the truth is filed under "content," it can never be filed under "news" again.

> CANON: The Compact's internal minority report (2022, leaked within the Meridian House and suppressed there) concludes the masquerade's real vulnerability was never a leaker. It is **arithmetic**: the High Tide is producing more events than the Sweeper corps can cover, and smartphones are producing more Loud-adjacent capture than the rot can eat. The system isn't going to be *exposed*. It's going to be **outpaced** — and no one in the building has a plan for that except "more funding," which the Council keeps voting down.

### Mutually assured disclosure

> CANON: The doctrine has a name: **mutually assured disclosure**. No great power exposes a rival's phenomena program because exposure is *symmetric*: the Seismograph network, the Ledger's escrow records, and eighty years of shared cover operations mean any disclosure dossier implicates the discloser. The deepfake dividend (02, III) cuts both ways — a state that releases "proof" of a rival's program cannot prove its own hands are clean, and the Tribunal's discovery rules would open *both* files. The **near-miss**: in 1983, a Department 12 faction prepared a disclosure package on the American program; the package was killed when the Memorandum Group ([INTL-007](07_INTERNATIONAL_RELATIONS.md)) demonstrated — in a closed briefing — that the Americans' retaliation file was thicker. The Group's standing role is this demonstration: it keeps, updated, the disclosure dossiers of every signatory, and its only weapon is showing each of them to the others' enemies. The masquerade's final lock is not secrecy. It is *hostage exchange*.

### The seizure doctrine and the quiet visit

> CANON: **Unregistered Seep materials are seized, not debated.** The seizure doctrine: any anomaly, residue, or Seep-derived object held without a license or Ledger title may be seized on sight by a threshold agency — no warrant where the vector is T3+, warrant-backed below that. The 1992-manuals lesson ([26.4](#264-stress-test-why-hasnt-a-snowden-ended-the-secrecy), the Geneva clerk) applied to *objects*: the system learned that seizing the *manual* is useless if the *copies* survive — so the doctrine targets the material first and the knowledge second.
>
> **The quiet-visit escalation ladder (CANON):** (1) **The visit** — two officers, plain clothes, a conversation: *we know what you have; here's the licensing form.* Most seizures end here. (2) **The audit** — the quiet desk opens a file; the holder's life becomes paperwork. (3) **The seizure** — warded van, Lantern examiner, chain-of-custody forms. (4) **The Tribunal referral** — for the ones who fought step three. The ladder is deliberately *boring* at every rung: a raid makes martyrs; a filing cabinet makes compliance. ROT/FEED ([IND-003](12_FACTIONS.md)) has survived three step-threes by being too decentralized to visit effectively — the agencies consider this an acceptable stalemate.
