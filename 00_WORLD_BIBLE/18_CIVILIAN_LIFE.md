## 18.2 The supernatural adjacent: how civilians actually encounter it

Most civilians never encounter a Seep directly. They encounter the *edges*:

- **"Road works."** Containment perimeters are always road works, gas leaks, or film shoots. Jakarta has had seventeen "film shoots" this year with no film crews.
- **Quiet policy mail.** The annual insurance renewal with the rider nobody reads ([08_ECONOMY.md](08_ECONOMY.md) 8.2.1). Some civilians *do* read it, call the number, and get a very patient customer-service representative who explains that "post-exposure counseling" is "like trauma cover." It is. Sort of.
- **The weird night.** The dream about the stairs. The evening nobody can quite remember. Civilians trade these stories the way they trade ghost stories â€” because that's what they are, mostly. The Veil Reflex ensures the real ones feel exactly like the fake ones.
- **The job ad.** "Night-shift records clerk. Attention to detail required. Discretion essential." ([CORP-011](11_CORPORATIONS.md) hires Loud the way other companies hire bilingual â€” [11_CORPORATIONS.md](11_CORPORATIONS.md) CORP-011's secret #2.)
- **The price signal.** Premiums rise. Rents near "the weird intersection" stay mysteriously low. Nobody connects the dots, because the dots dissolve when you look at them.

> CANON: Civilian encounters with the supernatural are *statistically common and epistemically weightless*. Millions of people have brushed against Seeps; almost none of them know it; none of them can prove it. This is not a conspiracy â€” it's cognitive immunology plus economics.
