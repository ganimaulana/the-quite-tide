# 03 — SUPERNATURAL SYSTEM

## 3.1 OBJECTIVE TRUTH (what is actually happening)

> CANON-LOCKED: The following is true whether or not any character ever learns it. In-world, **no living person knows all of it**. The closest human model (the Compact's Membrane Theory) gets roughly 70% right and is wrong about the cause of the Tide.

### The Membrane and the Undertow

> CANON: Reality as humans experience it is a thin, semi-permeable **Membrane** — the layer where causality, time, and identity hold — stretched over a deeper substrate: **the Undertow**, a lightless ocean of raw, unstructured information in which causality, time, and identity do not hold.

The Membrane is not perfectly sealed. At points of stress — geological, emotional, historical, informational — pressure from below intrudes. A **Seep** is any place, object, organism, event, or piece of information where Undertow pressure has intruded and *set* into Membrane-consistent behavior.

> CANON: Seeps are not "magic." They are intrusions of a deeper rule-set behaving as consistently as they can inside ours. Every Seep follows a **Rule** — alien, but fixed and discoverable. A Seep that "always counts the people in a room and removes the thirteenth" is not being cruel; it is executing Undertow grammar the way a crystal executes geometry.

### The Veil Reflex ("the Quiet")

> CANON: Human minds evolved a subconscious cognitive immune response — the **Veil Reflex** — that rationalizes, degrades, or deletes memories of Seep exposure. It is involuntary, universal in degree-varying strength, and the single largest reason civilization functions.

- ~96–98% of humans ("the Quiet majority") lose most Seep memories within hours, retaining only a dreamlike residue.
- ~2–4% are **Loud**: the Veil barely touches them. They remember **every Seep event** — unusually vivid, unusually stable, resistant to editing — but *not* everything: Loudness is resistance to Veil loss, not general eidetic memory. Mundane recall is ordinary, sometimes worse (the vivid signal drowns the quiet one). Loudness is inborn or acquired through Seep exposure; it cannot be trained into the Quiet majority.
- The Veil is *not* perfect: it edits memory, not physics. Insurance claims, broken windows, and corpses remain — which is why Quiet Insurance exists.

### The Hollow Pattern and the Attuned

> CANON: ~1 in 5,000 humans carries the **Hollow Pattern**, a distinctive cortical folding structure (detectable by MRI, which is why several countries quietly screen for it). In the Loud subset of carriers, the pattern allows **deliberate channeling** of Undertow pressure. These people are **the Attuned** — what the public would call "superpowered."

- Carrying the pattern is not enough: it must be **Awakened**, almost always by trauma, near-death, or direct Seep exposure ("First Tide").
- Inheritance raises the odds (children of Attuned awaken ~30× more often) but guarantees nothing.
- Channeling is *willed intrusion*: the Attuned opens a controlled Seep through their own nervous system.

### Erosion and Drowning

> CANON: Every deliberate channeling wears the user's Veil — measurable as **Erosion**, colloquially counted in **Fathoms** (0–100). Erosion is irreversible. At 100 Fathoms the pattern dissolves: **Drowning**. The Drowned usually leave a new Seep where they stood.

This is the system's built-in ceiling: the more you use power, the less of *you* remains to use it. The strongest burn out fastest. (Full mechanics in `04_POWER_SYSTEM.md`.)

### The Tide cycle

> CANON: Undertow pressure oscillates on a centuries-long cycle. We are entering a **High Tide** — the first since the 14th century. Global Seep frequency has roughly tripled since 2000 and is still rising. Tide pressure is *why* the masquerade is straining: more events, stronger events, more Loud witnesses.

> STATUS: UNKNOWN (author-level intentional mystery): **what drives the cycle.** Every in-world theory is wrong or incomplete. See Layer 5 in `33_MYSTERIES.md`.

### Why Seeps look absurd

> CANON: Undertow grammar has no concept of human relevance. A Seep's Rule optimizes for informational symmetry, not utility — hence stairs that count, elevators that remember, coins that always land on the year of your death. Absurdity is not a joke the universe tells; it is what alien consistency looks like from inside the Membrane.

## 3.3 Seep taxonomy (by substrate)

> CANON: Every anomaly is one (rarely two) of:

- **Object Seeps** — items with Rules (the Laggard's class, loosely). Most common; most containable.
- **Place Seeps** — locations with Rules (the stairwell that counts; the intersection that replays Tuesdays).
- **Organism Seeps** — living things with Rules (the cat that is always found, the tree that fruits other people's lost keys).
- **Event Seeps** — phenomena with Rules (the rain that falls upward on the third Thursday; the blackout that whispers).
- **Information Seeps** — data with Rules: cognitohazards, self-propagating patterns, images that cannot be correctly remembered. Rarest, most feared, hardest to contain — you cannot put a fact in a vault.

## 3.4 Static rot

> CANON: Undertow-structured information does not persist cleanly in Membrane media. Recordings of active Seeps corrupt at a rate proportional to Tide pressure — "static rot." Consequences:
- A T1's trick films fine; a T5 event melts sensors.
- Written notes survive better than video; human Loud memory survives best of all — which is why **witnesses are worth more than footage** and why Arthur (perfect Loud recall) is valuable.
- Rot is *why* the internet hasn't ended the masquerade: the most convincing events are the least recordable.
