# RED-TEAM AUDIT 1 — SUPERNATURAL & POWER SYSTEMS

> STATUS: AUDIT REPORT · Report-only red team · No files edited · 2026-09-19
> Attacks attempted: 58 · Findings: 0 CRITICAL / 2 HIGH / 10 MEDIUM / 8 LOW · RQs: 30 (22 answered from canon, 8 flagged)

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The bible's hard-rule architecture is genuinely strong: the economy exploits (conjured wealth, precog trading, Tide-energy, Choir market manipulation) are *already closed* by an explicit anti-loophole section (09_CURRENCIES.md §9.5), the Erosion ceiling is arithmetically coherent at the macro level, and the five Layer-5 mysteries are genuine unknowns with resolution conditions — not masked contradictions.

The real damage is concentrated in three places: **(1)** the Laggard's transfer mechanic is farmable and undefined at the edges (the protagonist's entire threat model hinges on two sentences in 29 B.3/B.8); **(2)** the Erosion micro-economics are asserted, not modeled (the "T2 works daily for years" claim has no cost curve beneath "serious use"); **(3)** internal vector inconsistencies between Drafter A's 05 and Drafter C's 28 (eight anomalies disagree; ANOMALY-001 disagrees with *itself* in three places).

## FINDINGS

### HIGH

**F-H1 · 29 (B.3, B.8) — Laggard transfer is farmable; no-witness case undefined.**
*Problem:* "On the holder's death or Drowning, the tether passes to the **nearest Loud witness**" (B.3); "The transfer mechanism is proximity + Loudness, not inheritance, not worthiness, not fate" (B.8). Nothing constrains *staging*. A faction at partial/full truth can: isolate Arthur from friendly Loud witnesses, position its own Loud operative as nearest, and kill him — harvesting the tether on demand. Conversely, a Quiet cutout killing him with no Loud witness in radius hits a rule the bible never defines: what happens with zero Loud witnesses? Dissipation? Dormancy? Nearest mind regardless of Loudness? Every assassination plan at knowledge level (d) hinges on this undefined case.
*Why it matters:* This is the load-bearing sentence of the protagonist's threat model. As written, "the universe does not choose holders" is false the moment a faction stages the choice — the protection the bible promises Arthur (transfer poisons theft) evaporates.
*Fixes:* (1) Require *genuine witnessing* (perception of the death), not mere proximity. (2) Staged/engineered transfers fail: the tether dissipates if the death was arranged to direct it ("the Undertow doesn't transact") — thematically consistent with "not worthiness, not fate." (3) Define the no-witness case explicitly (dissipate vs. dormancy vs. nearest-mind fallback).
*Recommended:* (2) + (3). Preserves genuine-transfer cases (Ilsa→Yusuf→Arthur all stand), kills farming in one clause, makes assassination-for-denial a meaningful (if costly) option rather than an exploit. Discoverable in-fiction via Ilsa's journals.

**F-H2 · 29 (B.5, B.11 use 5) — Out-of-phase movement vs. solid matter undefined.**
*Problem:* Stepping into the lag makes Arthur "unseen, intangible, unable to act" — "three seconds of ghosthood" (B.11). "Cannot touch... or be touched" (B.5). Can an intangible Arthur walk *through* a wall? If yes, 3 seconds ≈ one wall; at deepened 3-minute lag he strolls out of any facility — containment of Arthur becomes nearly impossible and every capture scene needs re-examination. If no, the text's "intangible" is doing unexplained work.
*Why it matters:* This single ambiguity decides whether any competent faction can hold him. The bible cannot leave its protagonist's escape hatch to reader inference.
*Fixes:* (1) Out-of-phase Arthur moves normally; walls still block him — "intangible" means only cannot-touch/cannot-be-touched. (2) He can pass through matter but only along positions his body occupied within the lag window (a "rewind walk"). (3) Full phasing, priced by ratchet.
*Recommended:* (1). All 12 uses keep working (dodge, eavesdrop, unseen observation need no wall-phasing); containment stays credible. One clarifying sentence in B.5.

### MEDIUM

**F-M1 · 02-I vs 28 (ANOMALY-020) — The Bell breaks the CANON-LOCKED causal-bubble caps.**
*Problem:* 02.I (CANON-LOCKED): bubbles "cannot extend beyond line-of-sight scale, cannot last more than minutes." ANOMALY-020: 1 km radius, *one hour* unhappened. The compliance note (28) concedes the breach and justifies it with "the bell's Rule *is* the bubble's geometry" and the false claim that "duration is minutes-scale in effect (one hour, one kilometer)." One hour is not minutes-scale. A CANON-LOCKED absolute is being violated by a filed anomaly with hand-waving.
*Why it matters:* CANON-LOCKED rules are the bible's load-bearing promises; if one has an undocumented exception, readers can't trust the others' absolutes.
*Fixes:* (1) Scope 02.I to *Attuned channeling*; add CANON-LOCKED line that anomaly Rules may exceed Attuned caps but inherit proportional costs. (2) Nerf the bell (damages a beloved artifact; don't). (3) Formalize a general "anomaly grandfathering" principle with per-entry compliance notes.
*Recommended:* (1). The bell's costs (8 Fathoms to striker, 1×/lunar month, can't unhappen death, striker remembers everything) already satisfy "small, brief, expensive" in spirit.

**F-M2 · 04 vs 30 — Erosion micro-costs missing; "T2 works daily for years" is unmodeled.**
*Problem:* 30.2 prices only *serious uses* (T2: 0.5–2 Fathoms). 04.4 asserts "A T2 can work daily for years at low Fathoms." Arithmetic: at 0.5 Fathoms/use, one serious use per workday = 125 Fathoms/year — Drowning in months. Sustained daily work therefore requires routine costs ≪0.1 Fathoms/day (100 Fathoms ÷ 20 yrs ÷ 365 ≈ 0.014/day), but no routine-cost figure exists anywhere. The same gap covers T1s ("decades; most never notice" at "<0.1 Fathom" per serious use — even 0.01 × 10 uses/day × 20 yrs = 730 Fathoms). "Discipline hygiene" and "efficiency training" are cited as the bridge but never quantified.
*Why it matters:* The entire professional class of the hidden world (90,000 T2s) rests on an asserted-but-unpriced cost curve. Any skeptical reader doing the multiplication finds the gap in seconds.
*Fixes:* (1) Publish routine-work costs: e.g., discipline-hygiene professional work ≈ 0.005–0.02 Fathoms/day; career budget ≈ 5 Fathoms/yr routine + rationed serious uses. (2) Redefine "serious use" thresholds per tier so the table and the prose share units. (3) Weaken the prose claim ("can work *most days* for years *with rest cycles*").
*Recommended:* (1). Converts an assertion into a model the rest of the bible (insurance pricing, handler arithmetic, the "cruel arithmetic") can reference.

**F-M3 · 02:15; 04:65 — Power-theft Erosion mechanics unquantified; "net loss, always" asserted.**
*Problem:* Theft "accelerates catastrophically" — no number, no curve, no duration. Attack: a 0-Fathom T1 thief steals a weak T1 party trick via the Drowning-adjacent ritual. Is the thief dead? If acceleration is a *rate multiplier*, they survive and retire with a free trick — "net loss, always" is false. If it's a flat jump, how large? Also unaddressed: can you consume the pattern of an *already-Drowned* Seep (no murder needed)?
*Why it matters:* "Three corpses in Compact vaults" is evidence, not a mechanism. Without one, the no-power-sponge guarantee is a promise, not a rule.
*Fixes:* (1) The thief **inherits the victim's accumulated Fathoms** at consumption, plus their own accumulation rate doubles — elegant: useful victims are high-Fathom, so theft is *structurally* net-negative. (2) Flat +40 Fathom jump on theft. (3) Stolen Resonances cost 10× normal Erosion to operate.
*Recommended:* (1). Explains the corpses (they stole from strong victims), keeps weak-victim theft survivable-but-pointless, derives from existing canon.

**F-M4 · 05 §5.5 vs 28 (catalog table) — Filed vectors contradict each other.**
*Problem:* Eight anomalies carry different ratified vectors in 05 vs the 28 catalog table: 006, 007, 012, 013, 020, 025, 029, 030. Worse, ANOMALY-001 is filed three ways: 05 §5.5 `T1/C-A/—/Local/Unknown`; 29 `T1/D/Local/Reactive` (claiming a "reconciliation" that 05's text falsifies); 29 `T1/C-A/Local/None`; 01 `T1/C-A`. Containment class and INTEL axis drive legal triggers (05 §5.2: T4+/C-D+ → 72-hour Compact reporting; C-class determines who pays), so these aren't cosmetic.
*Why it matters:* The vector is "the hidden world's common language" and a legal instrument. The bible disagreeing with itself on nine filings (including the protagonist's) breaks in-world legal reasoning and reader trust.
*Fixes:* (1) Single reconciliation pass: pick one vector per anomaly, update both files, correct 29's false reconciliation claim. (2) Declare one file the master (05) and mark 28's table as provisional. (3) In-world explanation (re-audits in progress) — weakest; don't.
*Recommended:* (1). Note the 29 INTEL discrepancy is *partially* intentional (examiners wrote "Reactive" to dodge the Sapient-presumption protocol — 29 says so explicitly, which is good tradecraft), but the filed vector still needs one canonical value with the dodge documented as in-world behavior, not authorial inconsistency.

**F-M5 · (gap) — No rule on licensed Choir use in elections/politics.**
*Problem:* Unlicensed Choir use = prison in 140+ countries (04.2, 14). But *licensed* Choir T4+ crowd-scale emotional steering exists, and nothing — no Accords clause, no election law — addresses its political use. The cast includes CHAR-010 Tomás Aquino Reyes, a **T6 Choir** under Compact observation. *Why it matters:* The single most obvious real-world attack on the system; the bible's densest regulatory regime (Choir) has a hole exactly where democracy lives.
*Fixes:* (1) Accords-level ban: Choir channeling in elections/referenda is a Tribunal offense; campaigns Lantern-monitored. (2) Disclosure regime: licensed political Choir use legal but registered and attributed. (3) Leave to social constraints (Reyes is watched; handlers intervene) — weakest.
*Recommended:* (1). Matches "most regulated discipline on earth" and 09.9.5's precedent (Choir market manipulation is already a Tribunal offense — extend the principle from markets to votes).

**F-M6 · 14:29; 29 B.9 — Person-attached anomalies fall through property law.**

*Fixes:* (1) Canon line: person-attached anomalies cannot be Ledger-titled; the holder retains full personhood; sale/transfer of the holder is trafficking under Accords law (enforcement varies — the Red Ledger exploits the variance). (2) The reverse: explicitly legal in non-signatory gray markets (deliberate dystopia). (3) Leave ambiguous as a plot question — not recommended; 02.V forbids relabeling gaps as mysteries.
*Recommended:* (1). Keeps the threat (variance in enforcement is the exploit) while giving the Tribunal and Arthur's allies legal footing.

**F-M7 · 02:7 — Pattern-capture mechanism undefined (echo-exploit surface).**

*Fixes:* (1) Pattern-capture is only possible as the pattern collapses (death/Drowning-adjacent) — no pre-mortem backups, ever. (2) Capture requires a dedicated ritual with ruinous cost (specify: T3+ Lantern work, Seismograph-visible, consent or Tribunal warrant). *Recommended:* (1) + (2). Closes the backup exploit completely; keeps post-mortem echoes (31:209 Quiet-claim scenario) viable.

**F-M8 · 29 B.2/B.5 (gap) — Wards vs. the Laggard tether undefined; flare vs. wards undefined.**

*Why it matters:* Every containment/kidnapping scenario at knowledge levels (b)–(d) needs this answered; it's also the answer to "why don't they just lock him in a vault."
*Fixes:* (1) Wards dampen Membrane-side effects (dimming, flare visibility) but cannot sever an Undertow-side tether; deep use inside wards is possible but *louder* to the entity (attention compounds). (2) Military wards fully suppress all Laggard function. (3) Wards do nothing to it at all.
*Recommended:* (1). Containment remains *physically* viable (he's T0; a locked door works) but *strategically* imperfect (he can still flare at a cost; deep use still possible) — the best story surface, and it explains why factions prefer custody-with-leverage over vaults.

(2) Add a seeding-range constraint (seeder must hold the target in mind; no broadcast). (3) Leave as-is.
*Recommended:* (1). Note 14:47 already concedes the honest version ("Choir crime is under-punished by an order of magnitude") — the bible is strongest where it admits limits.

*Fixes:* (1) One explicit rule: Lanterns can read *residue* (Tide-marks on places/objects) and *surface impressions* from the living, but extracting structured secrets from a living mind is low-fidelity, degrades the source impression, and is detectable — making human moles the rational choice. ## Method

Each issue was tested three ways: (1) **arithmetic** — do the stated numbers reconcile with each other; (2) **incentive** — does any actor have a reason to behave as described, and does the money bind them to it; (3) **mechanism** — is the transaction described completely enough that a reader could follow who pays whom, when, and what happens on default. An issue is listed when it fails at least one test *as written*. PROVISIONAL in-world estimates are granted wider tolerance than CANON-tagged claims; CANON-tagged numbers that do not reconcile are treated as the most serious economic findings.

## Severity definitions

- **Critical:** breaks a CANON-tagged economic rule, or makes a load-bearing plot/faction engine (RC-026, the Laggard market, the berth) financially incoherent. Blocks downstream coherence until resolved.
- **Major:** direct contradiction between files, or a load-bearing number/mechanism that does not reconcile as written. Needs a coordinator decision.
- **Minor:** sync artifact, relocation leftover, unsupported detail, or fuzzy-but-tolerable figure fixable with a line or two.

## ECON-001 — Meridian Re's supernatural book is as large as the entire anomaly-insurance sector

- **Severity:** Major
- **Location:** `08_ECONOMY.md` §8.1 (sector table); `11_CORPORATIONS.md` (CORP-004)
- **Conflicting statements:**
  > `08`: "| Anomaly insurance (Quiet policies) | $28–35B | [CORP-004](11_CORPORATIONS.md) Meridian Re, [CORP-011](11_CORPORATIONS.md) Nusantara Quiet Assurance |"
  > `11`: "**Revenue:** ~$41B gross written premium (~70% supernatural-attributable — the highest ratio of any major firm)." (CORP-004 Meridian Re)
  > `08`: "Reinsurance concentrates at [CORP-004](11_CORPORATIONS.md) Meridian Re, which backstops ~60% of the world's Quiet risk."
- **Why it is a problem:** 70% × $41B ≈ **$28.7B** of supernatural-attributable gross written premium — roughly the *entire* global anomaly-insurance sector estimate ($28–35B). Two readings, both broken: (a) if the sector figure is primary premium, a reinsurer's supernatural GWP cannot equal total primary revenue — ceded premium is a fraction of primary, so Meridian Re's book alone would imply a sector several times larger; (b) if the sector figure already includes reinsurance, Meridian Re alone fills it, leaving no room for NQA's ~$1.2B supernatural revenue (`11`: "~$2.4B (~50% supernatural-attributable)") and every other primary writer. The "backstops ~60% of the world's Quiet risk" line compounds it: 60% of *risk* against ~100% of *premium* implies a rate-on-line no reinsurer would write. A sophisticated reader (the target audience) will do this multiplication.
- **Proposed solutions:** (1) Raise the sector estimate to $55–70B and keep Meridian Re's GWP; (2) cut Meridian Re's supernatural ratio (e.g., ~35–40% supernatural, the rest conventional specialty reinsurance booked through the same entity — which also improves its camouflage story); (3) clarify that the $28–35B is *primary net written premium* and restate Meridian Re's figure as *gross assumed reinsurance premium* with an explicit cession ratio, so the two bases are visibly different. Any one of the three closes the gap; doing nothing leaves a CANON-adjacent number that fails arithmetic.

  > `08`: "This keeps the market fragmented, relationship-driven, and permanently resistant to financialization."
- **Why it is a problem:** If Kurogane can *grow* anomalous materials repeatably, the output is by definition fungible at the batch level: repeatable supply breaks "no commodity market," undermines the no-futures logic, and collapses the information scarcity (`08` §8.1.2) that justifies the entire provenance economy. "The technique works" is doing enormous load-bearing work for a two-word claim — it is the single most financially destabilizing fact in the bible, and it is asserted, not bounded.
- **Proposed solutions:** (1) Bound it hard: the technique yields *unique, non-repeatable* specimens (each growth is a distinct Rule — non-fungibility preserved, output is bespoke, Kurogane becomes a premier provenance house rather than a manufacturer); or cap throughput (a handful of specimens per year at ruinous cost, so it never reaches commodity scale). (2) Or make the destabilization the story: the Compact knows, the price is suppression/containment of the technique itself — but then the secrecy economics (who pays for suppression, what Kurogane charges for silence) must be stated. As written it reads as an unbounded miracle.

## ECON-009 — The Red Ledger's turnover cannot support its stated force

- **Severity:** Major
- **Location:** `12_FACTIONS.md` (SUP-004); `08_ECONOMY.md` §8.3
- **Conflicting statements:**

That is *below* the bible's own fully-loaded cost of a single T3 contractor ($400k–1M), for a force that is 70% Attuned (1,680 Attuned, including T3s and T4s) with the best field medicine outside the Compact. Either the Ledger loses money on every operator, or "turnover" means something narrower than total revenue (e.g., net contract margin after pass-throughs) — which is not stated. This is the force-economics version of the handful-rule violation (see FAC-003): the bible prices Attuned labor honestly in `08` and then staffs a private army it cannot afford in `12`.
- **Proposed solutions:** (1) Raise turnover to ~$2.5–3B (consistent with ~$1M fully-loaded per Attuned operator plus overhead); (2) cut the force to ~600–800 operators (a "handful"-adjacent elite cadre, which also eases FAC-003); (3) define "turnover" as net retained margin with pass-through costs billed to clients separately — but then state the gross. Note: resolving FAC-001 (the Ledger's identity) may change which of these fits.

## ECON-013 — Borrowed gold vs. T+2 settlement: the loophole is not fully closed

- **Severity:** Major
- **Location:** `09_CURRENCIES.md` §9.5; `02_WORLD_RULES.md` I & II
- **Conflicting statements:**
  > `09`: "Fails on [02_WORLD_RULES.md](02_WORLD_RULES.md) I & II: *borrowed matter reverts within 72 hours*. Financial settlement clears on T+2 at the fastest; by the time the receiving bank's assay finishes, the gold is gone — sometimes *during* the assay, which is how the 2009 'Zurich reversion' was discovered."
  > `02` (hard rule): borrowed matter reverts within 72 hours.
- **Why it is a problem:** The bible's own numbers leave the window open: borrowed matter may persist up to **72 hours**, while T+2 settlement can clear in as little as **~48 hours** — a fast counterparty that assays on receipt and releases funds same-day keeps a ~24-hour window in which the gold is real, the money has moved, and the reversion happens on someone else's balance sheet. "Sometimes *during* the assay" is anecdote, not control: the hard rule guarantees reversion *within* 72 hours, not *before* settlement. A laundering scheme that needs only one clean 48-hour cycle is viable as written, and the "Verdict: *works once, badly*" is asserted rather than derived.
- **Proposed solutions:** (1) Add the missing controls the banks would actually use: assay holds (funds released only after 72h+ seasoning), title clawback on reversion, collateral seasoning requirements for anomalous-materials desks. (2) Or shorten the real-world window in-world: member banks clear anomalous-materials settlement on T+5 by convention *because of* reversion risk — which also explains why the Ledger's settlement system exists. Either closes the 24-hour gap with one sentence.

## ECON-014 — RC-029's bank run contradicts the sounding's closed-system design

- **Severity:** Minor
- **Location:** `31_CONFLICT_ENGINE.md` (RC-029); `09_CURRENCIES.md` §9.2
- **Conflicting statements:**
  > `31` (RC-029): "Argent Vault Bank's Liwanag branch holds sounding-denominated accounts for Gray Market (IND-006) clients — and a rumor of a KNF audit starts a bank run. The run isn't for cash; it's for silence — everyone withdrawing at once to close the paper trail."
  > `09`: "Soundings are not a circulating currency. They are ledger entries, existing only inside the Ledger's settlement system and its member banks."
- **Why it is a problem:** In a closed ledger system, "withdrawing" soundings means converting to fiat through a member bank — which *creates* conversion records at that bank. A run "for silence" conducted by generating FX paper trails at the system's most surveilled choke point achieves the opposite of silence; the rational silent move is transferring balances to another member or the Tally, not withdrawing. The setup is dramatic but mechanistically backwards. (Secondary: `11` profiles Argent Vault as Geneva-centered with no Liwanag branch listed — see FAC-027.)
- **Proposed solutions:** Rewrite the run as a *transfer* stampede (clients moving balances out of Argent Liwanag to cut the audit trail — which works, because the receiving ledger is a different member's problem) or as a *conversion* stampede whose irony is the point (the run for silence generates the evidence — a feature, if the novel wants it, but then say so).

## ECON-015 — Argent's sounding-denominated credit vs. the 1987 fractional-reserve ban

## HOLDS — what the audit confirms as sound

The following were stress-tested and hold as written. They are the load-bearing beams; the issues above are the joints that need work.

1. **NQA's rider economics are plausible.** ~$2.4B revenue × ~50% supernatural ≈ $1.2B, spread over ~4M Indonesian + ~900k British riders (`11` CORP-011) ≈ **~$245 per rider-year** in supernatural-attributable premium. That is a believable price for a Quiet-policy rider bundle in 2024. The unit economics of the novel's home institution work.
2. **The 55-Fathom T3 $500k example is arithmetically reachable.** $140–230k base × 1.55–2.1 depth multiplier ≈ $217–483k, plus stacked hazard pay → ~$500k (`08` §8.3). The bible prices Attuned labor consistently; the perverse incentive around it is ECON-010, not the number.
3. **$2B/year severe-Erosion care is a credible order of magnitude.** 10,000 severe cases × ~$200k/year, buried in defense/health budgets (`08` §8.4). The overlap with T5 maintenance ($3–15M/year × ~40 T5s ≈ $120–600M) should be clarified as subset-vs-total, but the scale is right.
4. **Non-fungibility + information scarcity genuinely explain the missing arbitrage.** No two anomalies alike, no shorting, the Ledger's 1987 ban on securitization/derivatives/fractional reserve, reversion risk, and ruinous transaction costs are jointly sufficient to keep the market fragmented and relationship-driven (`08` §8.1, `09` §9.4). The *mechanism* needs detail (ECON-007); the *logic* is sound.
5. **Blackwater Syndicate and Alon can both profit in Ravenscroft.** Kuroshio owns berth/warehouse/toll economics through licensed fronts; Alon owns diaspora labor, ferry routes, and placement niches and pays access "rent" (`12` CRIM-006/007). Toll-taker vs. tenant is a stable, legible split — the waterfall just needs writing down (ECON-012).
6. **Ordinary-money camouflage is the bible's best economic writing.** "Auditors audit *numbers*, not *causes*" (`08`: "How $140–200B hides from auditors," CANON) — boring accounting, aggregate loss triangles, strategically tedious investor presentations — is a genuinely sufficient explanation for how a $140–200B economy hides. Nothing here needs fixing.
7. **Borrowed matter prevents durable wealth — once settlement controls exist.** The 72-hour reversion rule is a hard, elegant cap on supernatural money-printing (`02`, `09`). ECON-013 is about the settlement window, not the rule.
8. **The Tally's $2–5B throughput as THEORY with wide error bars is honest.** Unknowable-by-design flows should be presented exactly this way (`09`); more false precision would be worse.
9. **Seep bounties price below auction clearing, as they should.** T1 bounty $500–5k vs. T1 auction clearing 0.2–2 SND ($2.4k–32k): the bounty is a finder's fee, not a purchase price. The incentive gradient points the right way.
10. **Vesper's graywater liability is rationally structured.** An 11% diversion the board chose to contain rather than disclose (`11` CORP-002) is exactly how a real pharma board would behave given Stillwater's patent-expiry war and the Compact's procurement weight. The numbers need denominators (ECON-011); the behavior needs nothing.
