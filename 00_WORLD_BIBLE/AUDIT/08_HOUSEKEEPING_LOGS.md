## 2. Background summary

Arthur grew up in Willowmere, Ravenscroft's 1970s new-town bedtown. His first remembered Seep came at fifteen: a *high street* that sold yesterday through a flood week. In 2016–2017, aged seventeen, he ran errands for and sat with the dying Yusuf Hidayat (CHAR-018), who had been lodged in a spare room on the Reed family's street; when Yusuf died of a stroke in March 2017 with Arthur beside him, the tether passed to the nearest Loud witness. Arthur told no one for five years, finished his *vocational college*, and in 2022 was hired onto the night shift of NQA Ravenscroft's records archive — his hiring file flagged *"Loud, unregistered, anomaly-adjacent"* by Lantern-screened HR and approved anyway (EVENT-087), by an unknown approver.

His camouflage is smallness: a 1K, a convenience store, a night shift, his parents' house ten minutes away. His precarity is thinner and stranger than papers — he is a citizen; he cannot afford a police file, a hospital file, or a Census file, because every file is read by someone.
