# 04 — POWER SYSTEM (The Attuned)

## 4.1 Emergence

> CANON: An Attuned is made, not born — but some are born far more likely to be made.

- **The Hollow Pattern** (cortical folding variant) occurs in ~1/5,000 people. Carriers who are also Loud can be **Awakened**.
- **Awakening** ("First Tide") requires a trigger: trauma, near-death, or direct Seep exposure. ~60% of carriers never Awaken; they live ordinary lives, sometimes with lifelong déjà vu and a talent for noticing wrong things.
- **Inheritance:** children of Attuned carry the pattern ~40% of the time and Awaken ~30× more often than baseline — but Awakening still needs a trigger, and many Attuned parents deliberately raise children *away* from triggers. Dynasties exist (the Pale Court) but are fragile: every generation must survive its own First Tide.
- **No artificial Awakening** has ever worked reliably. Every attempt to mass-produce Attuned (three known state programs, one corporate) ended in Drownings. This is CANON and load-bearing: armies of super-soldiers are impossible.

## 4.2 The Five Disciplines

> CANON: Every Resonance falls into one discipline — the "shape" of the channel. Discipline determines what *kind* of Rule the Attuned can impose, never the scale.

| Discipline | Domain | Example (weak → strong) | Regulation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LANTERN** | Perception, information, memory | Sense Tide pressure → read Tide-marks of a room → track a person by their "wake" | Lightest; Lanterns staff detection, forensics, journalism |
| **TIDE** | Matter and energy | Warm a cup → shatter concrete → (T5+) weather-scale events | Heavy; Tide combat licenses are the most restricted |
| **BONE** | Body and self | Accelerated healing → physical augmentation → (T5+) regeneration | Medical licensing; sports bans |
| **CHOIR** | Social influence, suggestion | Calm a room → seed a suggestion → (T4+) crowd-scale emotional steering | **Most regulated discipline on earth.** Direct mind control is impossible (02.I), but Choir is close enough to terrify legislators. Unlicensed Choir use = prison in 140+ countries. **Choir use in elections and referenda is an Accords/Tribunal offense** ([14_LAW_ENFORCEMENT.md](14_LAW_ENFORCEMENT.md) 14.3); campaigns in signatory states are Lantern-monitored |
| **HOLLOW** | Direct Undertow interfacing | Sense thin places → briefly "wade" (partial phase) → Slipping | Rarest (~3% of Attuned); monitored by every intelligence agency; most Hollow work is state-monopolized |

> CANON: Discipline is fixed at Awakening. No one changes discipline. (What looks like "versatility" is always clever application within one discipline.)

## 4.3 Tiers T0–T7

> CANON: Tier measures *maximum sustainable output*, not skill, not danger, not Erosion rate.

| Tier | Name | Capability ceiling | Approx. global population |
|---|---|---|---|
| T0 | **Quiet** | No channeling (ordinary humans, unawakened carriers) | ~8 billion |
| T1 | **Spark** | Party tricks; inconvenient or minor utility | ~800,000 |
| T2 | **Lantern** | Professional-grade; the working class of the hidden world | ~90,000 |
| T3 | **Surge** | Can decide a firefight or a boardroom; registered, watched | ~8,000 |
| T4 | **Tempest** | National asset / national threat; Seismograph-visible | ~700 |
| T5 | **Cataclysm** | City-scale consequences; every use is a geopolitical event | ~40 |
| T6 | **Worldtide** | Continental consequences; three confirmed alive | 3 confirmed |
| T7 | **Unknown** | Reserved for "we don't know what that was" | 0 confirmed; the designation exists because it has been needed |

> CANON: Tier is *not* destiny in a fight. A prepared T2 with knowledge, terrain, and the right countermeasure routinely survives — sometimes defeats — a T4. (Mechanics in `30_POWER_HIERARCHY.md`.) Raw tier wins *unprepared* encounters; preparation is the great equalizer, which is why intelligence is the true currency.

## 4.4 Erosion (the cost)

> CANON: Channeling is priced in **Fathoms** (0–100), a clinical scale maintained by the Compact's medical directorate. Colloquially: *"How deep are you?"*

- **Cost curve:** output scales linearly; Erosion scales *exponentially* past ~40 Fathoms. A T2 can work daily for years at low Fathoms. A T5 pushing maximum output can go from 60 to Drowning in a single afternoon.
- **Routine rates (CANON):** ordinary low-output channeling — a working day's worth of small uses — accrues roughly **0.005–0.02 Fathoms/day**, depending on discipline, output, and rest. A career Attuned budgets on the order of **~5 Fathoms/year** for routine work; *serious* uses (combat output, deep Lantern reads, long Slips) are rationed individually against the lifetime budget, because one bad afternoon can cost more than a decade of careful days. This is the arithmetic behind every pension, every retention bonus, and every "past 45 Fathoms, everyone buys continuity" ([08_ECONOMY.md](08_ECONOMY.md) 8.4).
- **Side effects by depth:** <20: nosebleeds, migraines. 20–40: memory gaps, emotional flattening, sleep disorders. 40–60: personality erosion ("the thinning"), Veil weakening (the Attuned starts *remembering too much*, including things that never happened to them — Tide-mark bleed). 60–80: identity instability, involuntary channeling, physical wrongness (reflections lag, shadows misbehave). 80–100: pre-Drowning. There is no coming back from 100.
- **Erosion is irreversible** (02.I). Stillness practices (the Quiet Garden's disciplines), Stillwater-class drugs, and rest *slow* further accumulation. Nothing reverses it.
- **The cruel arithmetic:** the most powerful Attuned are on the shortest clocks. T5s rarely live past 50. This is why there is no superhuman ruling class — power is a terminal condition.
- **The career budget (CANON):** ~5 Fathoms/year of routine work × 20 years = the Drowning line — so the Compact's agencies do not let careers run that math. The **relief-rotation system** caps career accumulation at ~60 Fathoms: operators approaching the cap are rotated to non-channeling postings (training, archives, liaison — the "garden leave" track), and mandatory stand-down years are priced into every staffing model. Depth pay (08_ECONOMY.md) hedges the residual risk; the pension system ([08_ECONOMY.md](08_ECONOMY.md) 8.4: "past 45 Fathoms, everyone buys continuity") is the actuarial expression of the same budget. A T2 who works clean and rotates on schedule retires; a T2 who doesn't is a case study.

## 4.5 Training, detection, suppression

- **Training:** academies (see `15_EDUCATION.md`) teach *efficiency* — achieving effects at minimum Fathoms — and *discipline hygiene* (never channel angry, tired, or grieving). The best-trained T2 outperforms an untrained T3 at lower cost.
- **Detection:** the **Seismograph network** (Compact-operated, ~2,000 stations) detects Tide-pressure events ≥T3 globally within minutes. Lantern-sensitives and Hollow-pattern MRI screening handle the small scale. Several countries screen newborns; several others ban it as a human-rights violation — a live political fault line.
- **Suppression:** **Stillwater** drugs (dampen channeling; side effects: emotional blunting), **Anchor restraints** (Hollow-pattern-disrupting cuffs), warded containment cells (architectural Seep-suppression; expensive; the reason secret facilities are built like temples).
- **Countermeasures (why militaries still matter):** most Attuned are T1–T2; a rifle still kills a T2. Erosion limits sustained combat — a T4 cannot fight an army, only *survive* it briefly and expensively. **Drowndust** munitions (Erosion-inducing particulates — processed Drowning-site residue; mechanics and strategic doctrine in [13_MILITARY.md](13_MILITARY.md)) exist, are banned by the Meridian Accords, and are stockpiled anyway by at least four states. Conventional forces remain relevant because *endurance beats peak output* in every engagement longer than ninety seconds.

## 4.6 Hard limits per power type (CANON-LOCKED restatements with mechanics)

- **Resurrection:** impossible. Echoes are not people (02.I).
- **Teleportation (Slipping):** Hollow discipline only; arrival requires recognition (another living person's memory — not your own, not an echo); recognition decays with time; cost scales with distance/unfamiliarity; warded zones shred arrivals, hardened wards shred all non-whitelisted arrivals. No combat-blinking across continents.
- **Time:** causal bubbles only — line-of-sight scale, minutes at most, ruinous Erosion. No past alteration, ever.
- **Mind control:** impossible directly; suggestion seeding only, resistible, with backlash (02.I). Choir T5s can steer crowds emotionally — which is why Choir licensing is the strictest law in the hidden world.
- **Power copying:** requires consuming the source's pattern (murder by Drowning-adjacent ritual); the thief's Erosion accelerates catastrophically. Net loss, always. There are no power-sponges walking around with forty abilities. (There are three corpses in Compact vaults that prove why.)

> CANON: **The arithmetic of the theft.** The thief **inherits the victim's accumulated Fathoms** (the stolen pattern arrives pre-eroded) **and** the thief's own future accumulation rate **doubles** (the pattern was never theirs; the Membrane keeps trying to reject it). Stealing a 40-Fathom pattern means starting at 40 with a 2× burn rate.
- **Matter creation:** borrowed matter reverts ≤72h (02.I). Conjured weapons work for a fight, not a war.
- **Healing:** Bone-discipline healing *accelerates* the body's own repair — it cannot regrow what the pattern no longer "remembers" (old amputations fail), cannot cure Erosion, and aggressive healing *transfers* Erosion-risk to the healer. There is no free hospital of miracles.
- **Reality warping:** there is no general-purpose "rewrite reality" discipline. The closest is Hollow T6 — and the three confirmed Worldtides are all under permanent Compact observation, all past 70 Fathoms, all dying by inches.

## 4.7 Ability interactions

> CANON: Resonances interact through **Rule interference**, not power levels. A Lantern T2 who knows a Tide T4's Rule can *spoil* the channeling conditions (break line-of-sight, flood the area with Tide-noise, trigger the Rule's exception clause). This is the mechanical basis of "the weak defeating the strong": **powers have conditions, and conditions can be attacked.** The protagonist's entire survival strategy is built on this fact.

## 4.8 Pre-Drowning (T6) doctrine

> CANON: The Compact's T6 doctrine is short because T6 is. A pre-Drowned Attuned (81–100 Fathoms) is placed on **mandatory relief rotation** (no channeling, no stressful postings — the doctrine's euphemism is "garden leave"), under **Drowning watch**: a two-person Lantern detail, continuous Seismograph flagging, and a standing Sweeper standby for the Drowning itself (the watch is not for the person; it is for everyone near them when the person goes). At 95+ Fathoms the detail becomes a containment team and the subject is moved to a warded facility — SITE-009's Liwanag wing handles the Pacific caseload. **There is no doctrine for 100**, because there is no one left to apply it to: the Drowning *is* the doctrine. Three confirmed Worldtides live past 70 Fathoms under permanent observation; none has ever come back down. (Arthur's ratchet climbs *toward* this band — the endgame's physics is the Drowning watch with his name on it. The parallel is institutional, not physical: Arthur accrues no Fathoms — the ratchet is measured in lag-seconds, a property of the tether, not Erosion — but at high lag the Compact will watch him the way it watches the pre-Drowned, because a man holding a lengthening window onto the Undertow presents the same attention economics as a man dissolving into it.)

## 4.2A — INTERNAL / EXTERNAL RESONANCE APPLICATION — AUTHOR LOCK 2026-09-26

All Awakened Esper cadets may learn **baseline Resonance application through the body**, subject to individual output, control, discipline, physiology, and training. This is not a sixth discipline and does not replace the Five Disciplines.

- **Internal Resonance:** channeling through the body to reinforce movement, reaction, strength, durability, coordination, balance, or weapon handling.
- **External Resonance:** expressing the individual's discipline-specific Resonance as an outward effect. Fire projection, pressure effects, barriers, constructs, and similar expressions are examples only where the character's established Ability permits them.

Internal reinforcement is not equally effective for every Esper. A high-Rank Esper may have overwhelming external output but poor internal control; another may be physically formidable through Bone or disciplined internal channeling while having limited external range.

**Arthur Reed:** early Academy output is too weak for meaningful external projection. He can develop limited internal enhancement, especially when combined with physical conditioning and dagger training. This does not make him conventionally high-Rank. His later combat capability may exceed what his Esper Rank predicts through training, technique, Motion Sense, weapon competence, tactical reasoning, Contracts, and situational advantages.

**Hard separation:** Discipline ≠ Rank ≠ APR ≠ combat outcome. Internal Resonance is a combat foundation, not an automatic power multiplier.
