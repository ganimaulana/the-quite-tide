# THE QUIET TIDE — WESTERN WORLD MIGRATION CANON

**Status:** AUTHOR-APPROVED — ACTIVE 2026-09-25  
**Purpose:** Record the full migration from the previous East-Asian/Japanese presentation to the British/Western setting now governing active canon.

## 1. Locked World Identity

| Element | Active Canon |
|---|---|
| Country | **Kingdom of Albion** |
| Primary City | **Ravenhurst City** |
| County / Region | **Ravenshire** |
| National Specialist Institution | **The Albion Institute of Esper Studies** |
| Academy | **Ravenscroft Academy** |
| Protagonist | **Arthur Reed** |
| Primary Cultural Register | **Modern British / Western European** |

## 2. Migration Rule

This is a **total setting migration**, not a cosmetic rename.

Active canon now uses British/Western:

- personal names;
- place names;
- everyday vocabulary;
- architecture and urban texture;
- education and institutional presentation;
- government and professional context;
- family and social texture;
- Academy identity and branding.

The supernatural system, anomaly taxonomy, power rules, Contract System, Motion Sense, mystery architecture, chapter events, and earned progression remain structurally intact unless separately amended.

## 3. Academy Structure

**The Albion Institute of Esper Studies** is the national-level specialist institution and accreditation framework.

**Ravenscroft Academy** is a state-recognized specialist academy operating within that wider system. It is not primarily a military officer school.

The seven professional Divisions, Irregular Classification, Rank F / ERROR / UNDEFINED, Records & Archives assignment, reform program, and international admissions architecture remain active.

## 4. Protagonist

**Arthur Reed** replaces the former protagonist identity in active canon.

Arthur retains the established narrative functions:

- physically capable but inexperienced;
- observational and analytical;
- natural ability: **Motion Sense**;
- conventional Esper output: weak / ERROR–UNDEFINED;
- Irregular classification;
- Records & Archives assignment;
- private Contract System;
- maximum six active Contract slots;
- **THE POLITE KNOCK** remains Contract #1;
- Contract #2 remains undecided.

## 5. Historical Material

Old Japanese names, locations, and cultural references are retired from **active canon**. QA reports, historical audit records, and migration evidence may retain them where necessary to preserve an accurate development trail.

They must not be used as authority for future chapter generation.

## 6. Internationalization

Albion is Western in its primary cultural identity but remains internationally connected. Existing international characters may retain their real cultural/national identities where they are explicitly international students or foreign institutions.

In particular, **Nadia Falisha remains Indonesian**. International identity is not to be flattened into a British naming convention when the character's nationality is narratively relevant.

## 7. Generation Rule

From CH043 onward, all new prose and planning must use the active Western canon by default. If an older file conflicts with this migration, the migration canon supersedes it unless that file is explicitly marked historical, deprecated, or audit-only.

**END — WESTERN WORLD MIGRATION CANON**
