## 1. Concept

### 1.1 The Pressed Map (working concept) — [PROPOSED]

The Uncarved Seal and Arthur's body share **one hidden pre-existing figure** — the **Under-pattern**: already there, extremely faint, indecipherable where unrevealed — a watermark, a tide-line on stone.

**Revelation ≠ creation.** Binding makes one small region of the always-present figure *legible*; deeper understanding *resolves* geometry that was always there but unreadable. The visual counterpart of the progression architecture's load-bearing sentence: *"The Contract doesn't get stronger. Arthur's map of it gets truer — and a truer map is leverage."* The body is where the truer map becomes visible.

### 1.2 Why a seal produces a pattern — [PROPOSED]

A seal's ontology is **impression, custody, authority** (`ARTIFACT_DESIGN.md` §1). Extended one step: the phenomenon **presses the Seal** → a face carves **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**; the binding **presses Arthur** → the body answers with a seed-motif **[PROPOSED]**. Not a tattoo or reward — **what an impression looks like when the pressed thing is a person**. The Seal holds the *terms*; the body holds the *echo of having been pressed*.

### 1.3 What the pattern is not — [PROPOSED, fenced]

Not a status window (no numbers — author's veto). Not "Slot 1 = one line, Slot 2 = two lines" (author's veto). Not a gauge (no quantities; deferred visibility stays deferred — §9.4). Not a second power system (§8.1). Not Toll (§9.1).

## 2. Visual Grammar

All definitions **[PROPOSED]** unless marked.

### 2.1 Core terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Under-pattern** | The complete hidden figure, shared by Seal and body. Pre-existing, extremely faint, indecipherable where unrevealed; the whole stays a budgeted mystery (§7). |
| **Seed-motif** | First legible trace of one Contract's region, revealed at binding. Small, faint, incomplete by design. |
| **Stroke clarification** | "Progress": a verified discovery (falsifiable, recorded-before-test, world-confirmed) makes one stroke/segment of the seed-motif *distinguishable*. Geometry pre-existed; clarity is new. |
| **Bridge-line** | Connection between two Contracts' motifs, revealed by a verified *interaction* discovery. Mirrors the Seal's composite-face mechanic. |
| **Gap-stroke** | A deliberately *missing* stroke — the visible shape of an incomplete binding; proposed for Contract #1's PARTIAL outcome (§5.2). |
| **Weathering** | Crackle/stain/dulling *over* clarified motifs — visible trace of cumulative interference load, mirroring the Seal's micro-fractures (don't heal). Obscures; never adds content. |
| **Scorch** | Burnt-out motif, never clarifiable again — mirrors a kill-scarred face. Kill-forfeit only. |
| **Dormancy fade** | Whole field dimming toward baseline when the Seal's continuous-recognition condition lapses; reversible by genuine solving. |

### 2.2 Clarity stages (qualitative only — no numbers)

1. **UNREGISTERED** — indecipherable; not "empty" but *unread*. 2. **SENSED** — felt more than seen (ridge, peripheral line); the Contract #1 starting state. 3. **TRACED** — visible in good light: hairline, scar-like line, faint glyph. 4. **LEGIBLE** — readable as structure, comparable across sessions. 5. **INTEGRATED** — bridge-lined to neighbors; part of a larger figure still unknown. A motif need not reach INTEGRATED. Stage = *record of verified understanding*, not a rank.

### 2.3 Revelation triggers

| Trigger | Result | Never triggered by |
|---|---|---|
| Binding (FULL/PARTIAL/FRAGMENT) | Seed-motif at SENSED (completeness reflects outcome — §5.2) | — |
| Verified discovery (Mastery loop) | One stroke clarification | Reading about it; being told; time; Depth totals |
| Verified interaction discovery | Bridge-line between motifs | Assuming two Contracts "should" interact |
| Interference load rising | Weathering over motifs | — |
| Kill-forfeit | Scorch of the motif | — |
| Tide-season without genuine solve | Dormancy fade (reversible) | — |

Explicitly **not** triggers: kill count, invocation count, Depth totals, Lifetime Depth, rank, favor, popularity, narrative need — allergic to every XP-shaped input. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**

### 2.4 What "clearer" means

Higher contrast, finer edges, slight tactile ridge. *Never* invented lines, tier color-coding, or glowing runes. Aesthetic: **archaeological**, not arcane-neon.

## 3. Artifact Pattern

### 3.1 Canon base — [CANON-COMPATIBLE]

Faces carve one by one as impressions bind — visible, countable. Micro-fractures accumulate as the Seal bears the pattern-price — visible under magnification, don't heal. Kill-scarred faces permanently dead — legible. Stone visibly cools/goes dormant when compatibility lapses. Residue ledger: uncarved faces hold faint residue of genuine solves in the Seal's presence. Thirteenth face: unreadable mark, budgeted mystery. (All `ARTIFACT_DESIGN.md` §6–§8, §12–§13.)

### 3.2 Proposed addition — [PROPOSED]

The carved faces are the Seal's **institutional side**: countable, examiner-visible (why the custody doctrine forbids handling). The proposal adds the **personal side**: the stone *between and around* the faces carries the artifact-side half of the Under-pattern — the same faint figure, in stone instead of skin. Face carving = Seal-side revelation, mirrored by the body's seed-motif; micro-fractures = Seal-side weathering, mirrored by motif crackle; kill-scar = scorch; cooling = dormancy fade; the thirteenth face's unreadable mark anchors the body's **unreadable center** (§6.1). Structural correspondence — unresolved by design (§7).

The 12-face structure is **not redefined**: faces remain the slots; the Under-pattern is not a thirteenth slot or capacity backdoor. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**

## 4. Body Pattern

### 4.1 Location — [PROPOSED, AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED]

Recommended field: the **pressing arm** — back of the right hand, wrist, forearm; the hand that presses the Seal. Seal ontology (the impression's echo lives where the pressing happens); concealability (sleeves — secrecy is work); adjacency-with-distinction to Contract #1's toll-mark (*"his knuckles ache before it rains,"* LOCK 8 §1): toll aches in the knuckles (felt, Toll channel), pattern traces the wrist/forearm (seen, record channel) — almost confusable, productively, but different channels (§9.1). Later motifs accrete toward the shoulder; the implied whole suggests continuation that never quite arrives.

### 4.2 Contract #1 starting state — [PROPOSED]

One faint hairline on the wrist — *"like a scar that hasn't decided what it is"* — SENSED: raking light, a ridge under the fingertip, unnoticed by anyone not looking. It carries the **gap-stroke** (§5.2): *one stroke missing* — the PARTIAL outcome made visible, its meaning unknown to Arthur and reader alike.

### 4.3 Visibility behavior — evaluated and recommended — [PROPOSED]

| Behavior | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Always vs conditional | **Baseline faint, conditionally clearer.** TRACED and below hide under sleeves; LEGIBLE+ sharpens after rain, in cold, during that Contract's invocation — tide-adjacent triggers. |
| Darker with understanding | **Yes — clarity, never power-darkness.** Stroke clarification is the only "more visible" path, gated by verified discovery. Never time or use-count. |
| Visible during invocation | **Yes, as epiphenomenon.** The active motif *warms and darkens slightly* — informational only, no boost/shield/aim. Creates **witness risk** (bodily cousin of the knock-witness mechanic, LOCK 8 §13): every invocation a small secrecy negotiation. A feature. |
| Permanent after revelation | **Yes, with two exceptions.** Strokes stay revealed (understanding can't be unlearned); weathering can *obscure* a stroke (record damaged, discovery intact); dormancy fade *dims* reversibly. |
| Physical symptoms | **Transient only, at revelation.** Brief "pressing" sensation — pressure, warmth — then gone. Chronic symptoms belong **exclusively to Toll** (locked). Channels never blur (§9.1). |
| Record vs active function | **Record, strictly.** Grants nothing, reduces no cost, aims nothing. Warming is epiphenomenal. |

### 4.4 What the pattern never depicts — [PROPOSED, fenced]

Motifs encode **ruleset terms**, never media: no weapon-glyphs — medium validity is discovered by test (progression proposal §5.7), never read off skin, or the pattern becomes a strategy guide. Never people, places, futures, or faction secrets: it records *the relationship*, not intelligence.

## 5. Progression Relationship

### 5.1 Recommended: (E), layered — [PROPOSED, not locked]

**(E) — a combination with strict layering.** Each stratum has its own register, trigger, and visual language:

| Stratum | Answers | Trigger | Visual register |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Acquisition** | "A binding exists" | Binding event | Seed-motif at SENSED; *completeness reflects outcome:* FULL → whole seed; PARTIAL → seed with **gap-stroke**; FRAGMENT → partial seed; FAILED → no seed (closed dot at most). |
| **2. Understanding / Mastery** | "How true the map is" | Verified discovery | Stroke clarification (SENSED→TRACED→LEGIBLE); bridge-lines for interaction discoveries. **The dominant stratum.** |
| **3. Consequence / cost** | "What the portfolio costs" | Interference load; kill-forfeit; dormancy lapse | **Degradation only**: weathering; scorch; dormancy fade. Cost never *adds* motif content — it weathers, burns, or dims what understanding built. |

(A) alone = "one Contract = one tattoo" (vetoed). (B) as a number resurrects XP (vetoed). (C) alone leaves cost invisible. (D) alone makes a damage meter. (E)-layered gives each question its own grammar inside *one* pattern. **(C) — genuine understanding — is the dominant register in practice**: acquisition happens at most 12 times ever, consequence accrues slowly, but Mastery discoveries (rule, restriction, application, failure-mode, compatibility) are the chapter-to-chapter engine — the pattern is the visible trail of the understanding loop.

### 5.2 Contract #1 as worked example — [PROPOSED]

- **Binding (ch12, PARTIAL):** seed-motif at SENSED — one hairline with the **gap-stroke**: visibly missing a stroke. The AD-1 hazard ruling made *legible on his body* without explaining it.
- **ch13 (application discovery — "the file that answers back"):** one stroke clarifies to TRACED.
- **ch15 (failure-mode discovery — felt-certainty):** no new clarity; the first **weathering** — faint crackle across the motif. The misreading's cost, visible, no numeric penalty.
- **Later Mastery:** strokes clarify toward LEGIBLE; the gap-stroke stays missing unless the PARTIAL's unresolved region is ever genuinely understood (whether PARTIAL can complete toward FULL is **[DEFERRED]** — the pattern pre-answers nothing).

### 5.3 Correspondence table (artifact ↔ body) — [PROPOSED]

| Artifact side (interface) | Body side (record) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Face carves at binding | Seed-motif at SENSED | The impression landed |
| (Faces are binary — no equivalent) | Stroke clarification per verified discovery | The map got truer |
| Two faces pressed (composite) | Bridge-line between motifs | Rulesets interacted |
| Micro-fractures accumulate | Weathering over motifs | The portfolio has weight |
| Kill-scarred face | Scorched motif | The forfeit is permanent |
| Stone cools / dormancy | Dormancy fade | Recognition lapsed |
| 13th face: unreadable mark | Field's unreadable center | The whole is unknown |

## 6. Possible Forms — the 10 Candidates

Criteria: (a) larger-structure reveal; (b) motif + whole composition; (c) seal-ontology fit (12+1); (d) anti-LitRPG / anti-cultivation feel; (e) originality (no copyrighted design copied). **No form locked.**

| # | Candidate | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Totem / glyph | Ancient register; glyph-scale suits per-Contract motifs — but "totem" drifts to tribal cliché, and one glyph can't imply a larger structure. **Motif vocabulary only.** |
| 2 | Constellation | Larger-structure reveal is native and asymmetry natural — but connect-the-dots reads gamey, night-sky is overused, and it promises the whole *wants* connecting. **Rejected as primary.** |
| 3 | Geometric seal | **Hanko ontology**: square seal-script in a round field is the Seal's own language; impression/custody/authority read geometrically. Alone too complete — needs the broken treatment. **Leading element — motif DNA.** |
| 4 | Spiral | Growth metaphor, simple — but overused in manga/anime, inward-focused, "Slot 1 = one ring" collapse risk. **Rejected.** |
| 5 | Mandala radial | 12-fold radial maps to 12 faces with zero forcing — but religious baggage, invites slot-counting ("twelve spokes"), cultivation feel. **Rejected as primary.** |
| 6 | Broken circle | **The gap is structural**: unfillable break = 13th-face echo, PARTIAL gap-stroke, incompleteness as design. Too simple alone. **Leading element — field structure.** |
| 7 | Six-point seal | Geometric and compact — but six is arbitrary against 12 faces; star-polygon occult baggage. **Rejected.** |
| 8 | Ancient diagram | Larger-structure reveal native — but "diagram" promises legibility (information-dump risk, §8.4); diagrams are *made*, contradicting pre-existence. **Rejected.** |
| 9 | Unknown symbolic map | Mystery-native; "map of what?" is series-grade — but "map" sets expectation of eventual full legibility. **Adopt the *question*, not the form.** |
| 10 | **Hybrid original (recommended, not locked)** | Combines 3+6+9, avoids their weaknesses; needs careful original execution. **Recommended for development.** |

### 6.1 Recommended hybrid (not locked): "seal-script tide-field" — [PROPOSED]

- **Field (from 6):** a broken ring of twelve *uneven*, irregular arc-segments that don't meet (not uniform, numbered, or in slot order). The unfillable break = the 13th-face echo.
- **Motif DNA (from 3):** compact, square-ish, invented seal-script-like glyphs — angular, stamp-like — seated on or between arcs; each a complete micro-figure rendered 90% invisible until clarified.
- **Connective tissue (from 9):** tide-line hairlines — faint, meandering, asymmetrical — that may or may not become bridge-lines. Most never do.
- **Center (budgeted mystery):** the ring implies a center bearing no motif — an unreadable middle mirroring the thirteenth face. Deliberately unresolved (§7).

## 7. Mystery Potential — [PROPOSED]

A mystery engine, not a solution:

1. **The whole-figure question.** Seal-script sentence? Diagram of the contract system? Map of something? The three origin theories (`ARTIFACT_DESIGN.md` §3) each imply an answer — instrument → *index*; bequest → *signature*; accretion → *weather* — and the story confirms none.
2. **Hidden forward, legible backward.** The pattern never tells Arthur what to do — only what he has verified. The seed-motif "was always that shape": fair-play (LOCK 8 L8-2).
3. **The gap-stroke as dramatic irony.** Contract #1's motif is visibly broken from ch12 — the reader knows *something* is missing long before Arthur maps it. Dread without exposition.
4. **The unreadable center.** Both halves imply an unreadable middle. Whether it can *ever* clarify is series-grade unknown — the visual form of the fenced "complete Contract system" (LOCK 8).
5. **Institutional illegibility.** Unfiled, unmeasured, absent from every manual. If the Quiet Desk or a Lantern examiner ever sees it, they have no vocabulary — scars, or nothing. Protected by the world's *lack of concepts*, not a concealment mechanic.
