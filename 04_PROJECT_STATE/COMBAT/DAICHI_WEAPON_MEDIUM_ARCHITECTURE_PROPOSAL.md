## 1. Design Statement

**The Contract is the grammar. The medium is the voice. The weapon is the vocabulary of harm. A grammar does not need a voice to be true — but without a voice, it cannot be heard.** **[PROPOSED]**

Three roles, kept exact:

| Concept | Answers | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Contract** | *What is permitted?* — the licensed ruleset | "One confirmed sentence of demonstrated terms, presence-only, one-tap" |
| **Medium** | *Through what physical thing is it applied?* — the channel | A hand, a blade, a vessel, a length of cord |
| **Weapon** | *How does it land as harm or control?* — a medium specialized for harm/control | Sword, spear, bow, gun — illustrative classes only |

Four load-bearing theses **[PROPOSED]**:

1. **Application requires a channel; the channel is not the license.** A Contract's effect must touch the world through something physical — chosen, found, lost, replaced — and the Contract does not care, because the medium is not part of the Contract's identity.
2. **The medium stores nothing.** No enchanted objects, no magic swords: a blade that carried a Contract's effect on Tuesday is, on Wednesday, a blade. Validity is knowledge about the *ruleset's* requirements, not a property the object acquires.
3. **One Contract may admit many media; a medium may serve many Contracts.** Many-to-many, governed per-Contract by discovered validity. (Two Contracts sharing one *active* medium simultaneously: **[DEFERRED — §9.1]**.)
4. **The knock is the zero-medium boundary.** THE POLITE KNOCK admits no medium — no force, no protection, no action; its channel is presence itself. Canonical proof that medium is optional and CONTRACT ≠ WEAPON. Any design assigning the knock a weapon is **[CONFLICT]**.

## 2. What Makes a Medium "Valid" — The Binding Logic

### 2.1 Validity is rule-determined **[PROPOSED]**

A medium is valid for a Contract when the Contract's **settled terms name or imply the physical channel** the effect requires. The ruleset is the specification; the world of objects is the inventory. Validity questions are therefore *reading* questions first ("what does the ruleset's channel-clause actually say?") and *testing* questions second.

Three validity shapes a future Contract's terms might take **[PROPOSED — capability class, not assigned to any Contract]**:

- **Named channel** — the terms name the class ("through a bound vessel"). Narrow, legible, easy to test.
- **Property channel** — the terms name a property ("through iron"). Wider; each candidate class tested separately.
- **Silent channel** — the terms say nothing. **[DESIGN QUESTION — §9]:** does silence mean *any* contact suffices, or *no* medium is admitted? Recommendation: silence defaults to **zero-medium until proven otherwise** — the knock boundary is the safe default. **[PROPOSED; AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED]**

### 2.2 Validity is discovered, never assumed **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**

Compatibility discovery (mastery proposal §3.2.8) is the mechanism: a falsifiable hypothesis ("a spear of this class is a valid channel for this Contract's terms"), recorded before the test, confirmed or denied by the phenomenon's behavior under test. **[PROPOSED application of the locked loop]**

Each validity test:
- **Spends Current Depth** — it is an invocation test, priced like one. **[PROPOSED]**
- **Risks witnesses** — presence mechanics apply; testing a spear in the archive is a secrecy event. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (knock-witness mechanic generalized)
- **Can be wrong** — a medium that "felt right" and failed is the compatibility analogue of felt-certainty. The map includes the mirages. **[PROPOSED]**

### 2.3 What an invalid-medium attempt does **[PROPOSED; AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED]**

Recommendation: **the effect does not fire, and the Depth is still spent.** The attempt was an invocation — the phenomenon was addressed through a channel the ruleset does not admit, and the world answered with silence. This is consistent with the anti-creep rule that the price is upstream of the benefit (master framework §8.1).

Anti-farming guardrail **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**: a failed attempt produces **no Mastery** — no world-confirmation, no discovery; accidental/luck results bank nothing. You cannot grind Depth into validity by throwing spears at a ruleset. The phenomenon is the grader and it does not curve.

### 2.4 Granularity: per-class vs per-item **[DEFERRED — §9.2; AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED]**

Recommendation **[PROPOSED]**: validity is discovered **per class** (a spear of this make and material), but institutional caution means each *item* is verified before trusted under pressure — a one-line Repository note, not a full test. This keeps discovery counts bounded (classes, not inventory) while honoring the world's paranoia.

### 2.5 Loss, theft, breakage **[PROPOSED]**

Losing the medium **loses nothing of the Contract**. Validity knowledge persists; the Contract persists; only the physical object is gone. A replacement of the same validated class must be found — and, per §2.4's recommendation, verified. This is the structural reason the medium can never become a power system: **there is nothing to loot**. Stealing Arthur's spear steals a spear.

## 3. Switching Mechanics — A Future-Contract Capability Class

**Scope fence:** everything in this section describes a *class of mechanics* for hypothetical future Contracts. It is evaluated for coherence only. **No durations, multipliers, reset rules, or numbers are locked** — all numeric specifics are **[DEFERRED]**. Nothing here is assigned to Contract #1 (zero-medium boundary) or to Contract #2 (undesigned). **[CANON-COMPATIBLE fencing]**

### 3.1 What "switching" means **[PROPOSED]**

**Switching = changing a Contract's active medium from one validated medium to another** — one active channel at a time (simultaneity: §9.1). The switch is a physical act (put down the spear, take up the bow) **and** an invocation act (the effect re-addressed through the new channel).

### 3.2 The class rule: switch = close-and-reopen **[PROPOSED; AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED]**

Recommendation: a switch **closes** the prior medium's invocation and **opens** a new invocation through the new medium. Consequences:

- The reopen **reprices**: a new invocation spends Current Depth at the applicable tier. There is no free stance-dance. **[PROPOSED]**
- An **ongoing effect** through the old medium ends when the channel closes — unless the ruleset's duration class says otherwise (which is itself a discovery). The mastery proposal's illustration — *"changing the valid medium resets the duration"* — is kept exactly as what it was: a **hypothetical future-Contract illustration**, never a general rule and never the knock's. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE fencing]**
- Switching **under pressure** costs what pressure always costs: time, attention, a free hand, exposure. The world's budget prices the switch before the artifact does. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (the world prices the laboratory)

### 3.3 Switching conditions **[PROPOSED]**

A switch is permitted only when **all three** hold:

1. The target medium's validity has been **discovered** (not assumed). An undiscovered medium is an invalid-medium attempt (§2.3).
2. The Contract's terms do not **forbid** it. A ruleset may name exactly one channel (single-medium Contracts are a legitimate design — "Contracts must feel different").
3. The physical act is **possible** in the situation — hands, time, access. The artifact does not teleport the spear into his grip.

### 3.4 Switching limits and risks **[PROPOSED]**

- **No stacking:** one active channel per Contract — a switch replaces, never adds. (*Combination leverage* between Contracts is a different, separately fenced mechanic.)
- **No switching as cost-dodge:** switching evades no restriction, resets no toll, sheds no interference. The invoice follows the Contract, not the channel. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**
- **Switch-frequency is Depth-bounded:** every switch reprices as a new invocation, so the Depth economy is the natural governor. No numeric cooldown invented. **[DEFERRED numerics]**
- **The characteristic switching failure mode:** switching to a medium whose validity was *assumed under pressure* rather than discovered — the compatibility analogue of the ch15 over-invocation. **[PROPOSED]**

### 3.5 What switching is NOT

- Not a combo system. Not a stance with multipliers. Not "weapon skills" unlocking. The switch changes the *channel*; it changes nothing about the *ruleset*, the *Mastery*, or the *skill* in his hands. **[PROPOSED]**

## 4. The Training Architecture — Uncertainty as Curriculum

### 4.1 Why broad: he cannot know what he will need **[PROPOSED]**

Arthur does not choose his Contracts (master framework §1.1: he chooses what to *do* with the terms offered; the Grantor *settles*). A specialist bets his future on rulesets that admit his specialty. For someone who cannot see the next ruleset, **the rational curriculum is breadth** — narrowness is a gamble he cannot afford.

> **He trains broadly because the next Contract's channel-clause is unknown. Uncertainty is the curriculum; specialization is the risk.** **[PROPOSED]**

Contract #1 teaches it from the other side: the knock required **zero** physical skill — presence, observation, nerve. The rational response to "the channel might be anything, including nothing" is: **be adequate at everything reachable, master of the decision.** **[PROPOSED]**

### 4.2 How proficiency is earned: the world's budget **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**

**KNOWLEDGE ≠ SKILL — strictly.** Discovering "the spear is valid" grants zero spear skill. Combat proficiency is earned through:

- **Hours** — the same hours the archive wants; the training/investigation time-budget conflict is a designed anti-creep pressure (§7.6). **[PROPOSED]**
- **Instruction** — Records & Archives does not teach the spear. He must seek teachers across Divisions, costing **favors, access, and exposure** — the knock-witness economy generalized to the training hall. Every instructor is a relationship and a secrecy negotiation. **[PROPOSED]**
- **Injury** — training injures; injury costs recovery time, Medical attention (which *sees* things — the ch15 mark precedent), and missed investigation. **[PROPOSED]**
- **Sparring outcomes** — skill is certified by behavior under test, not declaration. No self-certification of skill either. **[PROPOSED extension of the loop ethic]**

### 4.3 Two tracks, never merged **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**

| Track | What advances | Certified by | Never grants |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mastery** | Map of a Contract's ruleset | The phenomenon under test | Skill with any medium |
| **Skill** | Embodied proficiency with a medium class | Sparring, instruction, outcomes | Understanding of any Contract |

They **interact** — a trained hand delivers a discovered application more reliably; a deep map tells the hand *where* to be — but neither grants the other. A SEASONED map with a novice hand fails physically; a master hand with a BOUND map has nothing to apply. **[PROPOSED]** (LOCK 12 competence layer; EAR firewall.)

### 4.4 The ceiling: a competent human, never more **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**

Arthur is conventionally Esper-weak — permanently. Training makes him a **well-trained ordinary human**, and that ceiling is structural, not temporary. His leverage is never the weapon; it is **the ruleset, applied through a hand trained enough not to fumble it**. The spear never hits harder because of a Contract — the Contract was never about hitting harder. **[PROPOSED]** (LOCK 8 power-curve rule; LOCK 12 Layer 1/2 firewalls.)

This is also the anti-power-fantasy guarantee for the training arc: the reader watches him get *competent*, not *superhuman* — and competence, priced in injury and hours, reads as earned rather than granted. **[PROPOSED]**

## 5. "Master of Weapon" — Redefined

**Status:** the phrase is **not canon** and must never appear as an in-world title, rank, or system label — design shorthand for a *trajectory of judgment*, usable by the author, invisible to the world. **[PROPOSED]**

### 5.1 The rejected meaning **[CONFLICT if adopted]**

"Master of Weapon" must **never** mean: perfect or automatic proficiency with every weapon; instant skill upon discovering a medium's validity; a status that grants combat output. All three contradict KNOWLEDGE ≠ SKILL and would be chosen-one omnipotence through the back door.

### 5.2 The proposed meaning: four capacities **[PROPOSED]**

1. **Selection** — choosing the *right* medium: reach vs concealment, lethality vs control, presence vs distance. The decision is the skill.
2. **Adaptation** — performing *adequately* with an unfamiliar medium under pressure. Not mastery of the object — mastery of the *adjustment*.
3. **Rule exploitation** — applying the ruleset *through* the medium's mundane properties: reach satisfying a presence condition at safer distance, a vessel's containment fulfilling a channel-clause literally, a cord's binding rhyming "what is bound." The medium's properties become the ruleset's vocabulary.
4. **Restraint** — knowing **when not to use a weapon at all**. Some Contracts admit no medium; some situations punish the armed hand. The rarest of the four capacities.

### 5.3 Why it coexists with Rank F **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**

All four capacities are **judgment**, not output. None of them moves Esper Rank, grants superhuman force, or contradicts conventional weakness — because none of them *is* force. A master of decisions about weapons is exactly what Arthur's engine already is (observation → hypothesis → decision); the weapon trajectory is the engine with a spear in its hand. **[PROPOSED]**

### 5.4 Trajectory, not status **[PROPOSED]**

"Master of Weapon" is an **aspiration the reader tracks through decisions**, never a threshold he crosses. No ceremony, no announcement, no system message. The reader knows he's getting there when his medium decisions start being right *before* they're tested — and the story must keep punishing the wrong ones, or the trajectory becomes omnipotence.

## 6. Medium × Mastery × Body Pattern

### 6.1 Medium validity is a Mastery discovery **[PROPOSED]**

Discovering that a medium class is valid is a **compatibility discovery** (mastery proposal §3.2.8) — a verified proposition about the Contract's application surface, held in the Repository like any other, under the same certification grammar.

### 6.2 Does the pattern record it? **[PROPOSED recommendation; AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED]**

Per the Body-Pattern proposal, the pattern is the **record of Mastery** — it grants nothing, meters nothing. Recommendation: medium-validity discoveries appear as **minor connective strokes** — the pattern's connective tissue between motifs — because validity *is* understanding of the Contract, and the pattern records the whole map.

The conservative alternative (Repository method-log only, pattern silent on media) is equally coherent. The deciding principle: **the pattern never stores the medium** — the object is replaceable, the pattern is permanent; recording the *object* would be ontological contamination. What is recorded, if anything, is the *proposition* ("this class is a valid channel"), never the spear. **[PROPOSED]**

### 6.3 The pattern grants no channel **[CANON-COMPATIBLE with the proposed design]**

The pattern does not make a medium valid, improve skill, or substitute for the object. If Arthur loses every spear he owns, the pattern's strokes remain — a map of channels with nothing to sail. Record, not instrument. **[PROPOSED]**
