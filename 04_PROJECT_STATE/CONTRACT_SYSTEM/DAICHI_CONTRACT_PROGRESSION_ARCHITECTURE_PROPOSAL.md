## 1. Design Statement

Arthur's progression is governed by two independent axes:

- **A. BREADTH** — new Slots → new Contracts → new rulesets.
- **B. DEPTH (of understanding)** — an already-bound Contract → deeper understanding → more sophisticated, flexible, or efficient application.

Neither axis is XP. Neither axis is kill-count, monster-count, or generic leveling. The currency of both axes is **understanding**, certified the way the world already certifies it: falsifiable prediction, recorded before the test, confirmed or denied by the phenomenon.

Two load-bearing principles:

1. **Arthur does not choose the power he receives. He chooses what to do with the power/ruleset he receives.** The acquisition chain (ANOMALY → SOLVED → RESONANCE → CONTRACT) is world-driven; the application chain is Arthur-driven. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-6/L8-7: he chooses the *terms offered*; the Grantor *settles*.)
2. **A bad Contract applied intelligently can outperform a stronger Contract used poorly.** Because Contracts are rulesets with conditions and restrictions — and conditions can be attacked — the differentiator is never raw output. It is the map. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-4: "conditions can be attacked"; Contract #1 power-curve rule.)

## 2. The Six Concepts — Exact Relationships

The separation is ontological, not decorative. Each concept answers a different question.

### 2.1 ARTIFACT — "Where does the Contract live?"

- **The Uncarved Seal** is the vault, the notary, and the medium. It witnesses, binds, and stores Contracts. It does not judge, advise, or intervene. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (`CONTRACT_SYSTEM_MASTERS.md` §1; LOCK 8 §1).
- It does not grant power. It grants **a place for a binding to exist and a procedure for invoking it**.
- The 13th face is NOT a slot — it is a separate, budgeted mystery. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 author decision 2).

### 2.2 SLOT — "Which anchoring position holds it?"

- A **Slot** is one of the artifact's up to **12 binding faces** — a mooring position that anchors at most **one** Contract binding. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8: 12 faces → 6 active Contract slots maximum).
- **SLOT ≠ CONTRACT.** The Slot is the *hook*; the Contract is what hangs on it. An empty unlocked Slot is capacity, not a power. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (AD-12-2: maximum capacity ≠ currently unlocked slots; slot #2 opens end-Nov 2024 and remains EMPTY in Arc I).
- Slot lifecycle states attested in canon: FREE → HELD → RESTING → FREE (mooring lifecycle, LOCK 12 Layer 3). **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**
- **Slot interference:** the more Slots are filled, the less reliable each Contract becomes — breadth is priced in reliability, not rewarded with it. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-8: "forty contracts is not forty abilities; it is forty mouths and a shaking hand"; tending-load compounding).

### 2.3 CONTRACT — "What supernatural ruleset is licensed?"

- A **Contract** is a license to invoke a specific **supernatural ruleset** — a bounded set of conditions, restrictions, costs, and a duration class, settled with a phenomenon's Rule and witnessed by the artifact. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-6: OFFER → SETTLING → BINDING).
- **CONTRACT ≠ POWER.** The Contract is the *keyring entry*, not the hand. It grants no force, no protection, no action — only the licensed terms. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (Contract #1: "it grants no force, no protection, no action — only a sentence of terms").
- **CONTRACT ≠ WEAPON.** A Contract has no inherent weapon form. It may admit zero, one, or many valid physical media (see §2.5). **[PROPOSED]** (candidate author direction; not contradicted by canon).
- One Contract per Slot. A second Slot holds a **different Contract**, never an "upgrade" of Slot 1. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (author direction point 5; slot interference makes stacking-in-one-Slot incoherent).

### 2.4 CONTRACT MASTERY — "How well is the ruleset understood?"

**Terminology guardrail:** the locked term **DEPTH** means the anomaly-derived *resource* (Current/Lifetime). The understanding axis must not be called "Depth." This proposal uses **MASTERY** (contract understanding) and treats it as strictly distinct from DEPTH (resource). **[PROPOSED terminology; CANON-COMPATIBLE distinction]**

- **Mastery** = the completeness and accuracy of Arthur's *map* of a bound Contract's ruleset: which rules he knows, which restrictions he has found, which interactions he can predict, which applications he has verified. **[PROPOSED]**
- Mastery is **epistemic, not numeric**. There is no "Mastery Level 5." The state of Mastery is expressed as *discoveries held*, each one a falsifiable, recorded, world-confirmed proposition about the Contract. **[PROPOSED]**
- **Mastery can be wrong.** A FULL binding on false premises *snaps* when reality contradicts the recorded prediction; the knock's felt-certainty failure mode (confirming a misreading with full conviction) is the micro-scale template. Mastery is therefore a *risk-bearing* asset, not a score. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-7, L8-11).
- **Mastery is per-Contract.** Knowing Contract #1 deeply teaches method, not the next Contract's rules. Each Contract is a new map to draw. **[PROPOSED]** (prevents "one grind fits all" collapse).

### 2.5 WEAPON / MEDIUM — "Through what physical thing is the Contract applied?"

- A **Medium** is a physical object through which a Contract's effect can be legitimately applied. A **Weapon** is a medium specialized for harm or control. **[PROPOSED]**
- The medium is **not part of the Contract's identity**. Losing the medium does not unbind the Contract; gaining a new medium does not create one. **[PROPOSED]**
- One Contract may admit **multiple valid media** (the candidate direction: one Contract → sword, spear, bow, gun, other). Validity is determined by the Contract's *rules*, discovered — never assumed. **[PROPOSED]**
- **KNOWLEDGE ≠ SKILL.** Knowing that a medium is valid does not grant proficiency with it. Combat skill is earned through training and experience, priced in hours, injury, and instruction — the world's budget, not the artifact's. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 12 Layer 1/competence separation; EAR firewall: competence is documented, not destiny).
- Medium-switching mechanics (duration windows, reset conditions, penalties) are **generic future-contract mechanics, NOT canon** — evaluated for coherence only, never assigned to Contract #1. **[PROPOSED / DEFERRED specifics]**

### 2.6 POWER — "What is Arthur actually capable of, in a situation?"

- For Arthur, **POWER** is not Esper output (he has none, permanently). Effective power is a *situational product*:
  > **Effective capability = (Contract ruleset) × (Mastery) × (medium suitability) × (preparation) − (costs + interference + risk)**
  **[PROPOSED formulation; CANON-COMPATIBLE components]**
- No term in this product is a level. Ruleset is fixed at binding; Mastery grows by discovery; medium suitability is situational; preparation is the world's budget; costs/interference are the anti-creep brakes.
- This is why a "weak" Contract can beat a "strong" one: the product has five multipliers, and intelligence operates on four of them. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (principle 2, §1).

## 3. The Two Axes in Detail

### Axis A — BREADTH: new Slots → new Contracts → new rulesets

- **Gated by:** the locked acquisition chain (genuine firsthand solve → RESONANCE → Contract formation → FULL/PARTIAL/FRAGMENT/FAILED) and the slot-unlock schedule. Capacity ≠ acquisition. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-5/L8-6; LOCK 12 Layer 3).
- **Priced by:** one toll-mark per FULL/PARTIAL (permanent, visible, irreversible); slot interference (each additional Contract makes all Contracts less reliable); tending-load compounding (upkeep rises across all active Contracts). **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-8).
- **Ceiling:** 12 slots maximum, hard. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 author decision 2; "hard ceiling of seven" SUPERSEDED).
- **Breadth is not automatically good.** The interference curve means the optimal number of Contracts is a *judgment*, not a maximum — and sometimes the correct move is to refuse a binding. **[PROPOSED]** (extends the locked interference logic).

### Axis B — MASTERY: existing Contract → deeper understanding → sophisticated application

- **Gated by:** the investigation loop (§4). No understanding without observation, hypothesis, test, and world-confirmation.
- **Priced by:** Depth spend per invocation (tier-scaled — curiosity about dangerous things is expensive); investigation priced by the world (hours, exposure, favors, secrecy); failure priced in opportunity, blowback, and sunk Depth — never in the lifetime ledger. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-9; L8-5: "failures cost opportunity and blowback, never the lifetime ledger").
- **Ceiling:** per-Contract and uneven. Some Contracts have severe ceilings; some are inherently dangerous to deepen; some reward flexibility over force. **[PROPOSED]** (author direction: Contracts must feel different from each other).
- **Mastery never changes the ruleset.** The Contract's rules are what they are. Mastery changes the *map* — which is the only thing that was ever going to save him. **[PROPOSED]**

### Why the two axes stay separate

- A new Contract does not deepen an old one. Deepening an old Contract does not open a new Slot. Slot-unlock timing is independent of Mastery; Mastery is independent of slot count. **[PROPOSED]** (prevents the classic collapse where "progression" becomes one number).
- This separation is what keeps Contract #1 dramatically alive after Contract #2 arrives: the knock is still being *mapped* long after it is bound. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (ch13–30 dramatize cost, limits, fallibility — LOCK 8 §15).

## 4. The Understanding Loop (the only "grind" allowed)

```
OBSERVE → HYPOTHESIS → TEST → RESULT
    → UPDATED HYPOTHESIS → SECOND TEST
    → RESOLUTION / FAILURE → DEEPER UNDERSTANDING
    → CONTRACT DEVELOPMENT (a new verified discovery)
```

Rules of the loop **[CANON-COMPATIBLE unless marked]**:

1. **Observation must be firsthand.** Reading about a Contract's rules does not count as understanding them — the firsthand rule that gates acquisition (LOCK 8 L8-6) also gates Mastery. **[PROPOSED extension of a locked rule]**
2. **Hypotheses must be falsifiable and recorded before the test.** The Repository timestamps the prediction; the world confirms or denies. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-2: "the world is the certifier; the Repository is only the notary").
3. **Accidental results do not count.** A correct outcome reached by luck banks no Mastery. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-7).
4. **Tests cost.** Every invocation spends Current Depth (tier-scaled); every field test risks exposure, witnesses (presence-only mechanics), and the felt-certainty trap. There is no free laboratory. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-9, L8-11).
5. **Failure is productive but priced.** A failed test that is *understood* is a restriction discovery (see §5). A failed test that is *misread* is the felt-certainty failure mode — and it can kill a solve. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-7, ch15 beat).
6. **No self-certification.** Arthur cannot declare a discovery verified. The phenomenon's behavior under test is the only signature. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-7, D-08).

## 5. Development Categories

### 5.1 Rule discovery — [CANON-COMPATIBLE]

Learning a true proposition about what the Contract *does*. Template: the knock's one confirmed sentence — *"it cannot cross what is bound."* Each discovered rule is citable precedent for future solves. This is the primary, safest, most canonical category.

### 5.2 Restriction discovery — [CANON-COMPATIBLE]

Learning a true proposition about what the Contract *cannot do, or may only do under conditions*. Template: demonstrated-only, one-knock-per-phenomenon, presence-only, non-sapient targets. Restrictions are **assets**: every restriction is a boundary the enemy also cannot cross, a condition that can be attacked, a price that can be planned around. The design treats restrictions as *the* leverage surface.

### 5.3 Interaction discovery — [PROPOSED]

Learning how the Contract's ruleset interacts with *other* rulesets: another phenomenon's Rule, another Contract's terms, a ward, a filed protocol, an Esper discipline's conditions. Template: the knock as "exception-clause finder" — "the exception clause is the key to Rule interference" (LOCK 8 L8-4). This is where cross-Contract sophistication lives — and it is the category most likely to be fenced by the mystery architecture (interactions that reveal too much, too early, stay undiscovered).

### 5.4 Application discovery — [CANON-COMPATIBLE]

Learning a *new use* for an already-known rule. Template: ch13 — the first invocation solves a records-level problem ("the file that answers back"). The rule didn't change; the problem class did. Application discovery is the category that converts investigation utility into operational utility without any power increase at all.

### 5.5 Efficiency improvement — [PROPOSED, with guardrails]

Not "spend 20% less Depth." Efficiency here means: **achieving the same verified outcome with a smaller invocation footprint** — narrower scope, shorter duration class, fewer phenomena touched, less exposure. Because Depth pricing is tier-scaled and qualitative (no numeric values invented — **[DEFERRED]**), efficiency is expressed as *scope discipline*, not as a discount percentage. **Guardrail:** efficiency must never become a second XP bar; it is demonstrated per-situation, not banked as a stat.

### 5.6 Control improvement — [PROPOSED]

Reducing the *blast radius of being wrong*: tighter invocation framing, better abort conditions, pre-committed falsification checks that catch a felt-certainty misreading before it compounds. Control is the category that makes deeper Mastery *survivable* — and its absence is what ch15 dramatizes.

### 5.7 Compatibility discovery — [PROPOSED]

Two subtypes, kept distinct:
- **Holder↔Seal compatibility** — attested in canon as continuous recognition (`ARTIFACT_DESIGN.md` §6.2). **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** as a phenomenon; its *development* is **[PROPOSED]**.
- **Contract↔medium compatibility** — discovering which physical media a Contract's rules admit. Never assumed; always discovered by test (which spends Depth and risks witnesses). **[PROPOSED]**

### 5.8 Failure-mode discovery — [CANON-COMPATIBLE as a category]

Learning *how this Contract lies to you* — its characteristic misreading shape. The knock's is felt-certainty. Every future Contract should have its own characteristic failure mode, discovered the hard way, priced accordingly. This category is what keeps Mastery honest: the map includes the known mirages.

## 6. "Contract Upgrade" Without RPG Leveling

An "upgrade" in this system is **a new verified proposition about the Contract**, never a stat increase. The canonical demonstration:

- Arthur initially believes: *"the Contract lasts for a fixed duration."* (hypothesis, recorded)
- After testing: *"the duration is actually continuous use."* (rule discovery — the map corrected)
- Later: *"changing the valid medium resets the duration."* (interaction + compatibility discovery)

The power did not increase. **The territory was always that shape; the map got better.** This is the core progression philosophy, and it should be adopted as the system's load-bearing sentence:

> **"The Contract doesn't get stronger. Arthur's map of it gets truer — and a truer map is leverage."** **[PROPOSED]**

What "upgrade" therefore means, category by category:

| Instead of… | The system uses… |
|---|---|
| Power +20% | A new application the old map didn't permit |
| Mana cost −30% | Scope discipline: same outcome, smaller footprint |
| New skill unlocked | A restriction discovered, converted to leverage |
| Level-up | A failure mode mapped, so the next test is survivable |
| Rarity tier up | An interaction discovered with another ruleset |

**What an upgrade NEVER is:** a numeric multiplier on output, a new effect invented by the artifact, a reward for kill-count, a threshold crossed by accumulated Lifetime Depth (Lifetime Depth is a historical measure, never a currency — **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**, LOCK 8 §1).

## 7. Stronger Without Raw Power — The Five Multipliers

A Contract becomes *more dangerous to Arthur's enemies* (never more "powerful" in the Esper sense) through:

1. **Rule precision** — narrower, truer propositions → fewer wasted invocations → less Depth burned per solved problem. **[PROPOSED]**
2. **Condition coverage** — more phenomena mapped → more situations where the Contract applies. The knock's one-tap-per-phenomenon restriction makes each mapped phenomenon a permanent asset. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (restriction as asset, §5.2).
3. **Cost efficiency** — scope discipline (§5.5) stretches the same Current Depth across more invocations. **[PROPOSED]**
4. **Application breadth** — new problem classes solved with old rules (§5.4). **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**
5. **Combination leverage** — interactions between Contracts (or between a Contract and a phenomenon's Rule) that neither ruleset permits alone. This is the late-game multiplier and the most mystery-fenced. **[PROPOSED]**

Note what is absent: output magnitude. Nothing in this system makes an invocation *hit harder*. The knock will never deal damage. That absence is structural — it is what keeps Arthur Rank F forever without contradiction. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 power-curve rule; LOCK 12 Layer 1/2 firewalls).

## 8. Anti-Power-Creep Rules

1. **The price is upstream of the benefit.** Depth is spent *before* the test confirms; tolls are banked *at signing*, before any invocation. You pay to learn, not after winning. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-7/L8-9).
2. **Breadth taxes reliability.** Every new Contract makes every Contract less reliable (interference) and raises all upkeep (tending load). There is no free portfolio. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-8).
3. **Understanding can be wrong, and the system doesn't insure it.** Felt-certainty, FULL-on-false-premises, invocation-through-unresolved-gap hazard — the epistemic risk *scales with* Mastery, because a truer-feeling map invites bigger bets. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-7, L8-11; AD-1 hazard ruling).
4. **One system changed, adjacent systems price it.** No discovery solves two unrelated problems at once. A rule discovery about Contract #1 does not transfer to Contract #2. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 12 Track A finding).
5. **Some Contracts have hard ceilings.** A Contract may be fully mapped at three discoveries and never grow again — or be too dangerous to deepen past a point. Ceilings are *designed*, not failed. **[PROPOSED]** (author direction).
6. **The world prices the laboratory.** Investigation costs hours, exposure, favors, witnesses, institutional attention. There is no safe training room for Contracts. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (ch11 solve "priced: hours, a favor owed"; knock-witness mechanic).
7. **Refusal is a valid progression move.** Declining a binding, declining to deepen, declining an invocation — the system must keep these *mechanically* viable (interference and tolls make them rational), not just morally praised. **[PROPOSED]** (extends locked cost logic).
8. **No numeric escalation without narrative meaning.** No invented Depth amounts, no thresholds, no percentages — qualitative pricing only, until the deferred systems pass. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 deferred list).

## 9. Compatibility Audit

### LOCK 8 (Contract #1 / Resonance / Depth / outcomes) — COMPATIBLE

- The architecture uses Contract #1 purely as a *boundary example*: presence-only, demonstrated-only, one-tap, tier-scaled Depth, PARTIAL outcome, felt-certainty failure mode. Nothing redefines it. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**
- Terminology preserved: RESONANCE = event/state; DEPTH = resource (Current/Lifetime, Lifetime never decreases); FULL/PARTIAL/FRAGMENT/FAILED; SOLVED ≠ FULL CONTRACT. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**
- The PARTIAL outcome is load-bearing for the philosophy: Contract #1 itself demonstrates that *binding is not the end of understanding* — a PARTIAL binding is a map known to be incomplete, which is exactly what Axis B exists to complete. **[PROPOSED reading of locked facts]**

### LOCK 12 (Power / Contract / Depth separation) — COMPATIBLE

- The five-layer iron rule (POWER ≠ CONTRACT ≠ DEPTH ≠ INSTITUTIONAL ≠ CHARACTER) is preserved and extended: Mastery is placed explicitly *outside* all five — it is an epistemic layer, not a sixth currency. **[PROPOSED placement; CANON-COMPATIBLE with the separation]**
- The effective-capability product (§2.6) never lets Contracts move Esper Rank, never lets Mastery become channeling, never lets Depth become XP. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**

### LOCK 13 (Arc architecture) — COMPATIBLE, with one pacing note

- The architecture is *pacing-native*: discoveries are chapter beats (ch13 application discovery, ch15 failure-mode discovery are already staged). Axis B gives Arcs II+ their progression engine without requiring new Slots on a schedule. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**
- **Pacing note [PROPOSED]:** Mastery discoveries should be budgeted like revelations, not like loot — roughly one load-bearing discovery per arc-segment, each priced visibly. Over-discovery in a single arc would read as leveling.

### LOCK 14 (Final consistency) — NO NEW CONTRADICTIONS INTRODUCED

- No existing file is redefined. The only terminology action is *additive*: "Mastery" as a new term, fenced off from "DEPTH." **[PROPOSED]**
- Stale-term risk flagged: any future file must not write "Contract Depth" to mean the resource or the understanding interchangeably. **[AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED]** on final term ratification.

### Writing Engine v1.0 — COMPATIBLE

- The understanding loop (§4) *is* a retention engine: hypothesis → test → result is the mystery-reader's native beat structure (question, experiment, payoff). Discovery beats double as retention beats. **[PROPOSED alignment]**
- The felt-certainty failure mode is a retention asset: the reader shares the false confidence, then shares the correction — the deepest form of "fair play." **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-2 fair-play note).

### Writing Production Protocol — COMPATIBLE

- This proposal creates no new stage obligations. It *constrains* Stage 10+ planning: any chapter plan that "upgrades" a Contract must name the discovery category (§5), the test that verified it, and the price paid — a checklist item, not a new stage. **[PROPOSED]**

## 10. Contradictions and Unresolved Design Questions

1. **[CONFLICT — resolved by terminology]:** "Contract Depth" (task vocabulary) collides with DEPTH (locked resource). Resolved in this proposal by using **Mastery** for the understanding axis. Final term ratification: **[AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED]**.
2. **[DEFERRED]:** Can a PARTIAL binding ever be *completed* toward FULL by later Mastery? LOCK 8 locks the outcome vocabulary but not re-binding mechanics. This proposal does not assume it. **[AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED]** in the future contract systems pass.
3. **[DEFERRED]:** Slot-relock mechanics (FREE → HELD → RESTING → FREE) — can Arthur voluntarily release a Contract and reuse the Slot? Attested as lifecycle states; voluntary release undesigned. **[AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED]**.
4. **[UNKNOWN]:** Can Mastery *decrease*? (Forgotten rules under interference? Corrupted maps?) No canon. The proposal assumes monotonic-with-risk (maps improve or are revealed wrong), not decay. **[AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED]** if decay is ever wanted.
5. **[PROPOSED tension]:** The firsthand rule for Mastery (§4.1) extends a locked acquisition rule to a new domain. It is consistent but not canon. If the author wants book-learnable Mastery for some Contracts, this needs a carve-out. **[AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED]**.
6. **[DEFERRED]:** Numeric Depth values, gauge visibility, toll-kind taxonomy — all deferred per LOCK 8/AD-12-3. This architecture is designed to work *without* them (qualitative pricing).
7. **[CONFLICT — avoided]:** Any design in which Lifetime Depth gates slot unlocks or Mastery tiers. Explicitly forbidden: Lifetime Depth is a historical measure, never a currency. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 §1).

## 11. What This Proposal Asks the Author to Decide

1. Ratify or replace the term **Mastery** for the understanding axis (vs. the colliding "Contract Depth").
2. Whether every Contract receives all eight development categories or a designed subset (recommendation: subsets — Contracts must feel different).
3. Whether the firsthand rule extends from acquisition to Mastery, or allows book-learnable discoveries for some Contracts.
4. Whether PARTIAL bindings can ever be completed toward FULL.
5. Whether voluntary Slot release is possible.
6. The per-arc discovery budget (pacing): how many load-bearing discoveries per arc before it reads as leveling.
