## 1. Design Statement

**The Contract never evolves. The understanding of the Contract evolves — and a Contract understood deeply is a different instrument than the same Contract understood shallowly.** **[PROPOSED]**

1. A Contract is a **fixed ruleset** — the terms settled at binding are the terms, forever. The artifact does not patch them. **[PROPOSED]** (what the Grantor *settles* is settled — LOCK 8 L8-6.)
2. What changes is **Arthur's map**: which rules he holds as verified propositions, which restrictions he has converted into leverage, which applications he has proven under pressure, which failure modes he has mapped before they map him. **[PROPOSED]**

If Contracts *themselves* evolved, progression would be a property of the artifact and Arthur's engine (observation, hypothesis, testing) would be decorative. The novel's identity rule — *"Everyone else becomes stronger by awakening powers. Arthur becomes stronger by understanding things that should not exist"* — requires that **the understanding is the power, and the ruleset is the constant**. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (identity rule; LOCK 8 L8-6/L8-7).

Load-bearing sentence, adopted from the master framework:

> **"The Contract doesn't get stronger. Arthur's map of it gets truer — and a truer map is leverage."** **[PROPOSED]**

## 2. What Mastery Is — and Is Not

### 2.1 Definition (master framework, restated for operational use)

**Mastery** = the completeness and accuracy of Arthur's map of a bound Contract's ruleset — which rules he knows, which restrictions he has found, which interactions he can predict, which applications he has verified. **[PROPOSED]**

### 2.2 Hard distinctions

| Mastery IS | Mastery IS NOT | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Epistemic: verified propositions held | Numeric: no "Mastery Level 5" | **[PROPOSED]** |
| Per-Contract: each Contract is a new map | Transferable: the knock teaches *method*, never another Contract's rules | **[PROPOSED]** |
| Risk-bearing: it can be wrong (felt-certainty template) | A safe score that only grows | **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-7, L8-11) |
| World-certified: phenomenon behavior under test | Self-declared: no self-certification | **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-7, D-08) |
| Distinct from DEPTH (the resource) | A second currency; Lifetime Depth never gates Mastery | **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 §1: Lifetime Depth is a historical measure, never currency) |
| Distinct from the Body Pattern | The pattern *records* Mastery; it is not Mastery | **[PROPOSED]** (Body-Pattern proposal §5: pattern = record, grants no power) |
| Distinct from skill (KNOWLEDGE ≠ SKILL) | Combat proficiency with a medium | **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 12 competence layer; EAR firewall) |

### 2.3 The certification grammar (shared with the understanding loop)

A "discovery" counts toward Mastery only under loop certification: **falsifiable prediction → recorded before the test → phenomenon behaves as predicted under test → the world is the certifier, the Repository is only the notary.** **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-2, L8-7.)

This is what keeps Mastery from becoming grinding: no action "pays Mastery" except *a genuine, falsifiable, firsthand encounter with the unknown that resolves true*. You cannot farm it — the phenomenon is the grader and it does not curve. **[PROPOSED reading of locked rules]**

## 3. The Eight Development Categories — Universal vs Subsets

The master framework established eight categories with their canon statuses. This proposal resolves the **structural question the master framework deferred**: which are universal, which are per-Contract subsets. **[PROPOSED]**

### 3.1 The four universal categories (every Contract participates)

These are the categories that follow from the nature of "a ruleset bound by a human investigator" and therefore apply to every Contract, whatever its rules:

1. **Rule discovery** — learning a true proposition about what the Contract *does*. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-10/L8-11; boundary template: the knock's one confirmed sentence — *"it cannot cross what is bound."*)
2. **Restriction discovery** — learning what the Contract *cannot do, or may only do under conditions*. Restrictions are **assets**: each is a boundary the enemy also cannot cross, a condition that can be attacked, a price that can be planned around. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-11; the knock's demonstrated-only / one-tap / presence-only / non-sapient restrictions are the LOCKED boundary inventory.)
3. **Application discovery** — learning a *new use* for an already-known rule. Converts investigation utility into operational utility with **zero power increase**. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (ch13 staged, LOCK 8 §15: "the file that answers back.")
4. **Failure-mode discovery** — learning *how this Contract lies to you*: its characteristic misreading shape. The map includes the known mirages. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE as a category]** (felt-certainty is the knock's LOCKED instance; "each Contract has one" is the proposed extension.)

These four are universal because they are not properties of any particular Contract's content — they are properties of *binding a ruleset you don't fully understand*: it will have unknown rules, unfound restrictions, untried uses, and ways to mislead you. **[PROPOSED]**

### 3.2 The four subset categories (assigned per Contract, by design)

These depend on the Contract's *content* and may be absent, capped, or dangerous for a given Contract. **This is how Contracts feel different from each other.** **[PROPOSED]**

5. **Interaction discovery** — how the Contract's ruleset interacts with *other* rulesets (another phenomenon's Rule, another Contract's terms, a ward, a filed protocol, an Esper discipline's conditions). **[PROPOSED]** (extends LOCK 8 L8-4.)
   - *Archetype fit:* highest for Contracts whose ruleset names *conditions* or *exceptions* (interference-native); near-zero for self-contained rulesets. The category most likely **fenced by the mystery architecture** — interactions that reveal too much, too early, stay undiscovered by design.
6. **Efficiency improvement** — **scope discipline**, never a discount percentage: the same verified outcome with a smaller invocation footprint (narrower scope, shorter duration class, fewer phenomena touched, less exposure). **[PROPOSED, with guardrails]** (master framework §5.5.)
   - *Archetype fit:* high for ongoing-duration or area effects; meaningless for instantaneous single-event invocations (the knock's one-tap is the boundary example of a Contract with **no meaningful efficiency category**).
   - *Guardrail:* demonstrated per-situation, never banked as a stat. A "cost-reduction percentage" is **[CONFLICT]** — numeric grinding through the back door.
7. **Control improvement** — reducing the blast radius of being wrong: tighter invocation framing, better abort conditions, pre-committed falsification checks. The category that makes deep Mastery *survivable*. **[PROPOSED]** (master framework §5.6; ch15 dramatizes its absence.)
   - *Archetype fit:* essential where invocations have **irreversible or wide effects**; marginal where invocations are self-limiting.
8. **Compatibility discovery** — two subtypes, kept distinct **[PROPOSED]** (master framework §5.7):
   - **Contract↔medium compatibility** — which physical media a Contract's rules admit. Never assumed; always discovered by test (Depth spend, witness risk). *Fit:* only for Contracts whose rules admit physical application — others have this category **closed**.
   - **Holder↔Seal compatibility** — attested as continuous recognition (`ARTIFACT_DESIGN.md` §6.2) **[CANON-COMPATIBLE as phenomenon]**; its *development* is **[PROPOSED]**, tracked once for the holder — not per-Contract.

### 3.3 The subset-assignment principle

At design time (future Contracts — **not this proposal's scope**), each new Contract receives **the four universals plus a designed subset of the four optional categories**, the remainder marked **closed** or **dangerous**. Some Contracts cap at three meaningful categories; some are dangerous to deepen past a point. Ceilings are *designed*, not failed. **[PROPOSED]** (author direction.)

This is the structural answer to "Contracts must feel different": the difference is not power level but **which kinds of understanding a Contract rewards and which it punishes**. **[PROPOSED]**

## 4. How a Contract Gets "Stronger" Without Raw Power

### 4.1 The five multipliers (adopted from the master framework, §7)

A Contract becomes *more dangerous to Arthur's enemies* — never more "powerful" in the Esper sense — through five multipliers:

1. **Rule precision** — narrower, truer propositions → fewer wasted invocations → less Depth burned per solved problem. **[PROPOSED]**
2. **Condition coverage** — more situations mapped where the Contract applies. The knock's one-tap-per-phenomenon restriction (LOCKED) makes each mapped phenomenon a permanent asset — the restriction is the amplifier. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**
3. **Cost efficiency** — scope discipline (§3.2.6) stretches the same Current Depth across more invocations. **[PROPOSED]**
4. **Application breadth** — new problem classes solved with old rules. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (ch13 staged.)
5. **Combination leverage** — interactions between Contracts (or between a Contract and a phenomenon's Rule) that neither ruleset permits alone. The late-game multiplier and the most mystery-fenced. **[PROPOSED]**

### 4.2 What is absent — structurally

**Output magnitude.** Nothing in this system makes an invocation *hit harder*. The knock will never deal damage. That absence is what keeps Arthur Rank F forever without contradiction. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 power-curve rule; LOCK 12 Layer 1/2 firewalls.)

### 4.3 The canonical demonstration (generic future-Contract illustration — NOT Contract #1)

*Hypothetical future Contract with duration class and medium-dependent invocation. Illustration only — assigns nothing to THE POLITE KNOCK (duration DEFERRED, mechanics locked).* **[PROPOSED; CANON-COMPATIBLE fencing]**

- Arthur initially believes: *"the Contract lasts for a fixed duration."* (hypothesis, recorded)
- After testing: *"the duration is actually continuous use."* (rule discovery — the map corrected)
- Later: *"changing the valid medium resets the duration."* (interaction + compatibility discovery)

The power did not increase. The territory was always that shape. **The map got better.** This is the progression philosophy in miniature — and the chapter-planning checklist: name the category (§3), the test, and the price. **[PROPOSED]**

| Instead of… | The system uses… |
|---|---|
| Power +20% | A new application the old map didn't permit |
| Mana cost −30% | Scope discipline: same outcome, smaller footprint |
| New skill unlocked | A restriction discovered, converted to leverage |
| Level-up | A failure mode mapped, so the next test is survivable |
| Rarity tier up | An interaction discovered with another ruleset |

**What an upgrade NEVER is:** a numeric multiplier on output; a new effect invented by the artifact; a reward for kill-count; a threshold crossed by accumulated Lifetime Depth. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 §1: Lifetime Depth is a historical measure, never a currency.)

## 5. Evolution Philosophy

### 5.1 The ruleset is fixed; the map changes — [PROPOSED]

"Evolution" means **evolution of understanding**, never of the thing understood. The settled terms are immutable because they were *settled* — with a phenomenon's Rule, witnessed by the artifact — not issued as a starter kit. A Grantor that "settles rather than negotiates" (LOCK 8 L8-1) does not issue firmware updates. **[PROPOSED extension of locked acquisition logic]**

Narrative consequence: **no "Contract awakens its true form" beat.** The awe-beat is inverted — the true form was always there; the reader thought it small because the map was small. Fair-play payoff (LOCK 8 L8-2): the full shape was deducible from the demonstrated terms all along. **[PROPOSED]**

### 5.2 PARTIAL → FULL: the deferred question — [DEFERRED]

Can a PARTIAL binding ever complete toward FULL? LOCK 8 locks the outcome vocabulary, not re-binding mechanics. This proposal **does not assume it** — and stays consistent with the Body-Pattern proposal: the gap-stroke stays missing unless the unresolved region is ever genuinely understood, and the question is pre-answered as **nothing**. **[DEFERRED — AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED in the future contract systems pass]**

Design note: the unresolved-gap hazard (AD-1, LOCKED) is more valuable if completion is never guaranteed — the gap becomes a permanent structural feature, and Mastery means working *around* a hole, not filling it. **[PROPOSED design reading; the canon question stays DEFERRED]**

### 5.3 Ceilings — [PROPOSED]

Some Contracts are **fully mapped at a handful of discoveries and never grow again** — or are too dangerous to deepen past a point. The ceiling is a designed property of the ruleset's richness, not a failure of the investigator. A shallow Contract is not a bad Contract: three discoveries and one narrow, decisive application can outweigh a deep Contract with diffuse uses. **[PROPOSED]** (author direction.)

Ceilings are the anti-creep guarantee: **no Contract is an infinite progression well**, and "refuse to deepen" becomes a mechanically rational move (§7).

### 5.4 Can Mastery decrease? — [DEFERRED]

No canon. This proposal assumes **monotonic-with-risk**: maps improve or are revealed wrong; they do not decay. (Slot interference degrades *invocation reliability* — the portfolio shakes his hand — not understanding itself. The distinction matters: Arthur can know the map perfectly and still fail the invocation.) **[PROPOSED assumption; CANON-COMPATIBLE distinction]**

Whether interference can *corrupt* a map (forgotten rules, corrupted records) is **[AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED]** — left for the cost/consequence pass.

## 6. Qualitative Mastery States (States, Not Levels)

Mastery needs describable states for chapter planning and reader tracking — but **states must never harden into levels**. The following are *narrative shorthand* for Arthur's current relationship with a Contract, aligned with the Body-Pattern proposal's qualitative stages (UNREGISTERED → SENSED → TRACED → LEGIBLE → INTEGRATED), which record Mastery without metering it. **[PROPOSED]**

| Mastery state | Meaning | Pattern correspondence |
|---|---|---|
| **BOUND** | The ruleset is licensed; the map is blank. Terms known only as offered. | Seed-motif at SENSED |
| **PROBING** | Hypotheses in flight; first verified discoveries; first misreading survived. | Strokes beginning to clarify |
| **MAPPED** | Core rules and key restrictions verified; the Contract is *usable under pressure* — the first state where Arthur can plan around it. | Strokes at TRACED |
| **INSTRUMENTAL** | Applications and interactions deployed; the Contract is *load-bearing* in his method, not just understood. | Approaching LEGIBLE; bridge-lines for interaction discoveries |
| **SEASONED** | Failure modes mapped; control matured; ceiling known — including the decision *not to deepen further* | LEGIBLE; weathering where costs accrued |

**Rules for using these states:**

1. They are **per-Contract and asynchronous** — the knock can be SEASONED while a future Contract is still PROBING. **[PROPOSED]**
2. They are **descriptive, never prescriptive** — no chapter grants a state; states are *recognized after* discoveries are certified. **[PROPOSED]**
3. They **do not unlock anything**. The world never gates on Mastery states — only on verified propositions and their tests. **[PROPOSED]** (the load-bearing anti-LitRPG fence — §7.1.)
4. Some Contracts **never leave MAPPED** — the ceiling is the design (§5.3). **[PROPOSED]**
5. The pattern's qualitative stages stay *uneven by design* (some motifs stall at TRACED forever — Body-Pattern proposal §5) — the record can lag or lead the lived relationship; both are fine, because the pattern is a record, not a meter. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE with the body-pattern design]**

## 7. Risk Analysis

| # | Risk | Mitigation — [PROPOSED] |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **LitRPG feel** (states read as levels) | States grant nothing (§6.3); no numbers; discoveries budgeted like revelations — ~one load-bearing discovery per arc-segment, each priced visibly (master framework §9) |
| 2 | **XP grinding** | Only falsifiable, firsthand, world-confirmed discoveries count; accidental results bank nothing; farming worthless by math — kind-diminishing, novelty-weighted yield (LOCK 8 L8-8) **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** |
| 3 | **Power creep** | No output-magnitude term exists (§4.2); ceilings designed (§5.3); breadth taxes reliability (LOCK 8 L8-8) **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** |
| 4 | **Chosen-one omnipotence** | Mastery can be wrong and the system doesn't insure it (felt-certainty, LOCKED for the knock); epistemic risk *scales with* Mastery — a truer-feeling map invites bigger bets (master framework §8.3) **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** |
| 5 | **Automatic skill gain** | KNOWLEDGE ≠ SKILL: valid-medium knowledge grants zero proficiency; combat skill priced in hours, injury, instruction — the world's budget (LOCK 12; EAR firewall) **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** |
| 6 | **Convenient Contract generation** | Mastery is per-Contract, non-transferable (§2.2); a new Contract starts at BOUND; acquisition gated by the locked chain (firsthand solve → RESONANCE → formation) **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** |
| 7 | **Useful-power lottery** | Arthur never chooses Contract content (master framework §1.1: he chooses what to *do* with the terms offered; the Grantor *settles*) — "getting a useful Contract" is not a progression mechanic **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** |
| 8 | **Consequence immunity** | Price upstream of benefit (Depth before the test; Toll at signing); failure priced in opportunity, blowback, sunk Depth — never insured (LOCK 8 L8-7/L8-9) **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** |
| 9 | **Empty numeric escalation** | Qualitative pricing only until the deferred systems pass (LOCK 8 deferred list) **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** |
| 10 | **"Mastery" as a sixth currency** | Mastery sits *outside* the five-layer iron rule — an epistemic layer, not a spendable resource; it cannot be transferred, spent, or banked (LOCK 8: "Depth is banked understanding; understanding cannot be transferred") **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** |
