## 1. Design Statement

Every Contract in this system has five different things that can hurt, and they are not the same thing. The design's central claim:

> **The price is upstream of the benefit. You pay to learn, not after winning. And paying more never buys more.**

The five tiers — **RULE**, **COST**, **TOLL**, **TEMPORARY PENALTY**, **PERMANENT CONSEQUENCE** — are five different questions, not five names for one price.

The load-bearing fences:

1. **TOLL is a LOCK 8 term** — preserved verbatim, never redefined or diluted: one toll-mark per FULL/PARTIAL signing; permanent, visible, irreversible; the anti-sponge engine. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**
2. **Cost is qualitative** — no numeric Depth amounts, no percentages, no HP/mana deductions, ever. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 numeric veto)
3. **Paying more never buys more power** — Depth spend never increases output magnitude; Toll never unlocks capability; consequence never converts to leverage. No power-for-price trade loop exists. **[PROPOSED doctrine; CANON-COMPATIBLE mechanism]**

## 2. The Five Tiers — Exact Definitions

### 2.1 TIER 1 — CONTRACT RULE ("what shape does this Contract have?")

- **Definition:** the ruleset itself — conditions, restrictions, duration class, the settled terms. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-6: OFFER → SETTLING → BINDING).
- **Not paid.** A rule is the *shape* of what is possible, not an invoice. Presence-only is not a "cost" — it is why every invocation is a secrecy negotiation; one-knock-per-phenomenon is not a "price" — it is why each mapping is a permanent asset.
- **Structuring role:** rules *create* the cost surface — tier-scaled pricing exists because the rule prices curiosity; the knock-witness mechanic because the rule mandates presence. **[PROPOSED framing of locked facts]**

### 2.2 TIER 2 — CONTRACT COST ("what does firing it cost?")

- **Definition:** the Depth price of invocation and signing — per-invocation drain scaled to filed tier; grade-banded signing Depth; renewal = full-price re-signing; buyout; quiet-use premium; per-query pricing. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-8/L8-9).
- **Paid:** by the holder, in Current Depth — the *resource*, never the body — **at each firing**, before the test confirms; sunk, never refunded on misread (ch15). **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**
- **No debt, ever.** The Repository never extends credit. Desperation uses Ballast-Vows (volunteered self-restrictions), the Queue, or buyout. **Cost-shifting is forbidden** — "Depth is banked understanding; understanding cannot be transferred." **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-8)

### 2.3 TIER 3 — TOLL ("what did signing it cost forever?")

- **Definition, locked:** the permanent signing cost — each FULL or PARTIAL leaves **one** visible, irreversible toll-mark (a sensory or behavioral alteration, legible to Lantern examination). Tolls never leave. Contract #1's mark: *"his knuckles ache before it rains."* **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-7, verbatim)
- **Paid:** once, at signing — banked before any invocation. Register: sensory / behavioral / physiological / cognitive only; broader kinds have no canonical home. **[DEFERRED — toll-kind taxonomy, FUTURE SYSTEMS PASS]**
- **Mechanical, never flavor-only.** No cosmetic toll exists anywhere in the tree: tolls drive the anti-sponge — **slot interference** (more tolls = less reliable Contracts, qualitative, no formula) and **tending-load compounding** (each active Contract raises all upkeep). "Forty contracts is not forty abilities; it is forty mouths and a shaking hand." A toll-mark is an invoice, not a currency — it cannot be spent, traded, or sacrificed for a benefit. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**

### 2.4 TIER 4 — TEMPORARY PENALTY ("what does a bad day cost?")

- **Definition:** prices that heal, fade, or recover — but whose *opportunity* is gone forever. **[PROPOSED]**
- **Attested inventory [CANON-COMPATIBLE]:** Current Depth drained to near-zero (ch15 — recoverable via new certified solves; the lost hours are not); the Quiet Desk's Unknown watchlist noting "signal flicker" while instruments log nothing anomalous (Laggard masking) (LOCK 8 §13); the knock-witness mechanic — every invocation a secrecy negotiation with whoever stands there; a Medical cadet seeing mark #1 facing the files-it-or-pockets-it fork; condition-breach voiding the Contract instantly and **locking the Mooring for a stated cooldown** — the spent Depth sunk (LOCK 8 L8-6); dormancy fade — the body pattern dimming when recognition lapses, reversible by genuine solving **[PROPOSED]**.
- **Guardrail:** temporary ≠ trivial. A cooldown that misses the one window that mattered is a temporary penalty with a permanent opportunity cost. **[PROPOSED]**

### 2.5 TIER 5 — PERMANENT CONSEQUENCE ("what can never be undone?")

- **Definition:** prices that never reverse — *earned by use and events*, not banked at signing like Toll. **[PROPOSED]**
- **Attested inventory [CANON-COMPATIBLE]:** toll-marks (Tier 3, but the register's anchor — they never leave); the **kill-scarred face** — kill *after* signing degrades the Contract to FRAGMENT and scars the face permanently, never to hold another impression (LOCK 8 L8-5); **scorch** — a burnt-out body motif, never clarifiable again, kill-forfeit only **[PROPOSED]**; **weathering** — crackle/stain/dulling over clarified motifs, cumulative interference load made visible; *obscures* the record, adds nothing **[PROPOSED]**.
- **Evaluated classes [PROPOSED]:** pain, fatigue, nerve/sensory/cognitive damage, loss of function, bodily changes, memory effects, dependency, anomaly exposure. Design vocabulary for future profiles (§4) — **not automatic**; no Contract receives all of them; each class's canon appearance needs an author decision.

## 3. Visibility — Who Sees What

The cost system is a *detection economy*: different tiers are legible to different watchers, and that asymmetry is the relationship engine. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE unless marked]**

| Tier | Arthur | Witnesses (human) | Institution (instruments) | The reader |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RULE (settled terms) | Knows what he offered and what settled | Only if shown | Repository timestamps; method log "accurately boring" by design (cultivated undervaluation) | Full |
| COST (Depth spend) | **Gauge visibility: [DEFERRED]** — infer-only vs visible unresolved | Cannot see | Instruments log **nothing anomalous** (Laggard masking) | ch13: "Current visibly drains (the reader tracks the meter)" |
| TOLL | Feels it (knuckles ache before rain) | **Sees it** — Medical cadet sees mark #1 (relationship fork) | Legible to Lantern examination | Counts the marks |
| TEMPORARY | Feels it | Witnesses exposure directly | Watchlist notes "signal flicker"; second traces | Full |
| PERMANENT | Lives with it | Sees toll-marks, scorches | Face-scars visible under custody handling | Full |

**Design consequence [PROPOSED]:** the asymmetry means Arthur's cost story is always *negotiated with witnesses* — presence makes cost public. Cost can never be a private XP ledger; the world is standing there watching the invoice arrive.

## 4. Per-Contract Cost Profiles — Designed, Not Uniform

### 4.1 The doctrine

**Each Contract gets its own designed cost profile** — its own classes of price, escalation behavior, and danger shape. **[PROPOSED]** A profile specifies **(a) cost classes** (which Tier 2/4/5 prices this Contract's invocation and deepening pay, from §2.5's register), **(b) escalation behavior** (flat, capped, escalating, cliff-shaped — §5), and **(c) danger profile** (what *failure* looks like for this Contract specifically — its characteristic blowback shape, learned the hard way as failure-mode discovery).

### 4.2 Why uniform pricing would break the system

1. **It would imply interchangeable Contracts.** Same cost for every Contract turns them into SKUs — collapsing CONTRACT ≠ POWER and inviting price-list optimization instead of ruleset reading. **[PROPOSED; CANON-COMPATIBLE]**
2. **It would make the anti-sponge arithmetic, not judgment.** The interference curve should force a *judgment* (is this binding worth its reliability tax?), not a calculation. **[PROPOSED]**
3. **It would flatten the mystery.** "Knuckles that ache before rain" vs "a memory that goes quiet for an hour after each invocation" — different instruments of dread. **[PROPOSED]**

### 4.3 Contract #1's profile — the boundary example, not a redesign

- **Tier 2:** per-invocation Depth drain scaled to filed tier — *curiosity priced by danger*. **Tier 3:** one toll-mark at signing — *"his knuckles ache before it rains."* **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**
- **Tier 4:** felt-certainty misreading risk (ch15); knock-witness exposure per invocation; PARTIAL unresolved-gap hazard (AD-1). **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**
- **Tier 5:** none attested beyond the toll anchor — the knock's consequence register is deliberately *thin*: it is the calibration Contract. **[PROPOSED]**
- **Danger profile:** epistemic, not bodily — the knock hurts by *being believed*. Template for all future profiles: **the danger rhymes with the ruleset.** **[PROPOSED]**

## 5. Escalation Philosophy — Designed, Never Automatic

### 5.1 The rule

**Deeper use may deepen severity or permanence — never by automatic law.** No formula, no ticking price tiers, no invocation-count multiplier. **[PROPOSED doctrine; CANON-COMPATIBLE with the numeric veto]**

### 5.2 Two distinct escalation axes

| Axis | What escalates | Mechanism | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Bet escalation** | The *stakes* of being wrong | Deeper Mastery → truer-feeling map → bigger bets → harder-compounding failures; the *holder* wagers more while the *ruleset* charges the same. | **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (progression §8.3; ch27 fallibility thesis) |
| **B. Profile escalation** | The *severity/permanence* of designed consequences | Per-Contract designed — flat, capped, escalating, or cliff-shaped; some too dangerous to deepen past a point. | **[PROPOSED]** |

The crucial distinction: **Axis A is the holder's confidence; Axis B is the Contract's design.** The world does not punish understanding — it punishes *unexamined confidence*, which is what felt-certainty is. **[PROPOSED]**

### 5.3 When escalation is designed vs forbidden

- **Designed:** cumulative load — the anti-sponge's tending-load compounding is canonical: breadth escalates *reliability cost* across the portfolio. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**
- **Designed:** cliff-shaped Tier 5 classes — safe within a designed bound, permanent past it, the cliff *discoverable by test*, never a surprise punishment. **[PROPOSED]**
- **Forbidden:** the leveling treadmill — per-invocation price creep, prices indexed to Lifetime Depth or discovery count, severity tracking Mastery: numeric escalation without narrative meaning, all vetoed. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (progression §8.8)
- **Forbidden:** "power up = price up" — there is no power-up, so nothing exists for price to be proportional *to*. Paying more never buys more. **[PROPOSED fence]**

## 6. The Price-Upstream Doctrine

The coherent cost doctrine, stated once:

1. **Signing pays before any invocation.** Toll banked, signing Depth spent, Mooring assigned — before the Contract ever fires. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-6/L8-7)
2. **Every invocation pays before confirmation.** The price is for the *attempt*, not the *result*. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-9, ch15)
3. **Investigation pays before the artifact is involved.** Hours, favors, exposure, secrecy — spent before any Depth moves (ch11: "hours, a favor owed"). **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (progression §8.6)
4. **Failures pay in opportunity and blowback — never the lifetime ledger.** Sunk Depth, locked moorings, Oslo-pattern blowback; Lifetime Depth never decreases. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-5/L8-6)
5. **No debt, no credit, no retroactive pricing.** Per §2.2: desperation uses Ballast-Vows, the Queue, or buyout; cost-shifting is forbidden. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-8)

**Why it matters [PROPOSED]:** price-upstream is the honesty engine — every invocation a *decision*, and decisions are what the reader tracks.

## 7. Failure Costs and Refusal

### 7.1 What failure costs

- **Failed tests:** sunk Depth + lost hours + Oslo-pattern blowback (the anomaly intel-upgrades, attracts attention, retaliates). A *misread* failure corrupts the map — felt-certainty prices *future* tests wrong, the most expensive failure there is. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**
- **Condition-breach:** voids the Contract instantly, locks the Mooring for a stated cooldown, sinks the spent Depth. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-6)
- **FAILED outcome:** sunk signing Depth, locked mooring, blowback risk; toll-mark at FAILED signing **[UNRESOLVED]** — §11.1.
- **Kill-after-signing:** degrades the Contract to FRAGMENT and scars the face permanently — never to hold another impression; *moral-structural*, not numeric. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-5)

### 7.2 Refusal is a mechanically valid move

Declining a binding, declining to deepen, declining an invocation — the system must keep these *rational*, not just praised. It does, three ways:

1. **Slot #2 unlocks end-Nov 2024 and remains EMPTY in Arc I** — capacity refused is canon; an open slot is not a shopping trip. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 §15)
2. **Interference + tolls price every acquisition.** Each new binding taxes the reliability of *all* bindings and banks a permanent mark — refusal is the portfolio manager's primary tool. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]** (LOCK 8 L8-7/L8-8)
3. **Some Contracts are designed with cliffs** — too dangerous to deepen past a point, making "stop" the correct move. Designed, not cowardice. **[PROPOSED]** (§5.3)

## 8. Relationship With the Body Pattern

Two channels, fenced: **Toll = FELT; the pattern = SEEN.** This section keeps that fence load-bearing. **[PROPOSED, consistent with body-pattern proposal §9.1]**

1. **The pattern records consequence as degradation only** — weathering, scorch, dormancy fade. Cost never *adds* motif content: it weathers, burns, or dims what understanding built. The pattern is the *trace* of cost, not the *ledger* of cost. **[PROPOSED]**
2. **The pattern never meters cost:** no stroke-count encodes Depth spent, no shading encodes severity. Weathering *obscures* rather than informs. A meter would resurrect the XP bar through the back door. **[PROPOSED fence]**
3. **The ledgers, properly placed:** the *body* carries Toll (felt; chronic symptoms belong exclusively to Toll); the *Repository* timestamps predictions and outcomes; the *reader* counts the marks. Three ledgers, none a status window. **[PROPOSED]**

| 2 | **Martyr fantasy** | Costs are bureaucratic and embarrassing — an aching knuckle, a favor owed, a mark a Medical cadet must decide about. Nobody salutes the invoice. |

| 4 | **Generic penalty soup** | Per-Contract cost profiles (§4): the danger rhymes with the ruleset. |
| 5 | **Torture-porn** | Cost is procedural and documented — timestamps, filed logs, watched marks. The dread is in *permanence*, not intensity. |
| 6 | **Escalation-as-leveling** | Escalation is per-Contract designed (flat/capped/escalating/cliff) or bet-escalation — never numeric, never automatic. |
| 7 | **Toll dilution** | LOCK 8 term kept strict: one mark per FULL/PARTIAL, specific register. Other prices have their own names. |
| 8 | **Omnipotent suffering** | Tolls make him *less* reliable (interference), not more special — a portfolio tax, never a badge. |
| 9 | **Failure without teeth** | Failures sink Depth, lock moorings, invite Oslo blowback, corrupt the map. A *misread* failure can kill a solve. |

5. **[PROPOSED tension]:** Tier 4's "temporary" label vs permanent opportunity cost: temporary in mechanism, permanent in consequence. The taxonomy holds the mechanism; chapters hold the irony. **[AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED]** whether any Tier 4 class should be reclassified per Contract.
