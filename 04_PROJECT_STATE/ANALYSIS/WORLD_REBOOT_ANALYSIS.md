## 3. Options evaluation

### Option A — Minor adaptation (preserve the old world, graft contracts minimally)

- **What survives:** ~90% of the bible untouched. The Laggard stays Arthur's primary anomaly; Arthur stays the ordinary non-Attuned clerk; the 1949 Accords, the secrecy stack, T0–T7, and Erosion all stay canonical; the 500-question audit becomes mostly advisory.
- **What dies:** The directive's core. Public F–S ranks cannot exist under the Accords (Q346, Q350); 'Ability: ERROR / UNDEFINED' as a *public* filing cannot survive the legibility rule (Q090); fast payoff cadence cannot survive the five-budget architecture (Q494); the undervalue→reversal engine cannot survive a world that keeps Arthur genuinely ordinary; athletic-Arthur cannot survive 29's "physically untrained" (Q382). Contracts would have to be shrunk to rare, investigative-only curiosities — essentially formalized Laggard Uses — which is not the commissioned system.
- **Fit verdict:** `[INFERENCE]` Fails the commission. The brief explicitly says this is *not* a preservation decision by default. Option A preserves a world the user did not ask to re-commission, and delivers a story the user did not ask for. It is honest work applied to the wrong target.

### Option B — Major reconstruction (keep the world, rebuild its power and publicity layers)

- **What survives:** `[INFERENCE]` The load-bearing canon: 02's seven hard rules and CANON-LOCKED corollaries (no resurrection, no mind control, no copying, no past alteration, 72-hour reversion, irreversible Erosion, anchor doctrine); 03's taxonomy and Awakening mechanics; 28's anomaly catalog (the solve-target inventory); 30's T0–T7 doctrine and the seven weak-vs-strong mechanisms; 33's mystery architecture (guarded); Ravenhurst and its districts; the faction web (12, 34); the secrecy stack's *machinery* (26); the tone rules (legibility, distributed competence, the indifferent world); the memory of the Laggard as a solved precedent.
- **What dies (honest casualty list):**
  - 01's secrecy premise `[REWRITE]` — replaced by a two-tier Disclosure amendment: *persons* may be public (Esper ranks), *phenomena* stay secret (Q346/Q350 solution).
  - 04.1's Awakening mechanics `[REWRITE]` — the F–S rank layer is unified with T0–T7 by one mapping table (Q396 solution 1); the rank measurand is defined once (certified deployable output × control — Q027 solution 1).
  - 02.I's apparent ban on contracts `[ADAPT]` — cleared by the license-ontology: contracts are mediated permissions, not copied patterns; living Esper Resonances are categorically non-contractable (Q010/Q433/Q435 solutions).
  - The cost void `[REWRITE]` — a designed cost currency: contract-specific activation tolls + irreversible Recognition/Attention + slot interference + hard slot cap (Q127/Q497/Q432 solutions).
  - 29's Arthur definition `[REWRITE]` — athletic and intelligent Arthur (trained-human ceiling, verification protocol) replaces the untrained clerk's body; the Laggard's status is *declared* (coexistence with designed interaction — Q051 solution 1 — or explicit sunset; `[RECOMMENDATION]` sunset or integration must be chosen, not left silent).
  - The "almost never new capability" rule `[REWRITE]` — replaced by the contract-era restraint: new capabilities arrive as contracts, which are priced, visible, and narrow (Q391 solution 1).
  - The Approach band's fuel `[REWRITE]` — re-fueled after Disclosure spends it (Q360 solutions: new masquerade decision, escalation cost, or retire the band).
  - "Not chosen, present" as thesis `[ADAPT]` — kept for the tether; retired or reframed as mechanical fit for the artifact (Q462 solutions 1 or 3).
  - 31's conflict generators `[REWRITE]` — re-tuned from weak-clerk generators to contracted-F-rank generators (Q221/Q281).
- **Fit verdict:** `[INFERENCE]` The only option that honors *both* the commission (the new direction is the point) and the old world's genuine assets (the hard rules already do ~80% of what the contract system needs: legibility, priced power, anti-sponge precedents, information scarcity). It is major surgery, not a touch-up — roughly 40% of the bible is rewritten or amended — but the patient keeps its skeleton.

### Option C — Total reboot (new continuity, same IP names)

- **What survives:** Names (Arthur, Ravenhurst, factions), the tone rules (legibility, distributed competence, the indifferent world), the identity sentence.
- **What dies:** Everything structural. The audit's 500 questions become moot; the bible is rewritten from scratch; all 37 root files are new canon.
- **Fit verdict:** `[INFERENCE]` Wastes the reusable assets. 02's hard rules, 30's mechanisms, the anomaly catalog, and the faction web are *premise-independent* — they work under any publicity regime. Discarding them gains nothing the reconstruction doesn't already achieve, and forfeits years of design labor for a blank page that still has to re-solve the same 500 questions.

### Option D — New world preserving character DNA (fresh setting, same people)

- **What survives:** Arthur, the Reeds, Saitō, Nadia, the clerk's-eye method, the identity sentence.
- **What dies:** The entire cosmology — Tide, Membrane, Erosion, Fathoms, the Pale Court, the Accords.
- **Fit verdict:** `[INFERENCE]` The fallback, not the plan. If the ontology work (02.I clearance, cost currency, rank measurand) proves undoable, a clean room is better than a self-contradicting patchwork. But it abandons the user's investment in this world, and the audit shows none of the 20 NEEDS REDESIGN findings is metaphysically unfixable — they are all "design this room," not "the building is condemned."

## 4. Final recommendation: Option B — major reconstruction, conditional on doing the ontology work

**Recommended option: B.** `[RECOMMENDATION]`

**Honest reasoning:**

1. **The audit's hard findings cluster in solvable places.** The premise clash (public Espers vs. the Veil) has a single high-leverage fix — the two-tier Disclosure amendment (Q346/Q350). The rank duplication has a one-table fix (Q396). The 02.I collision has a designed ontology (license-not-copy + exclusions, Q010/Q433). The cost gap has three candidate currencies (Q497/Q432). Every one of the 20 `[NEEDS REDESIGN]` findings names a missing room, not a condemned building.
2. **The old world's best assets are premise-independent.** 02's hard rules, 30's seven mechanisms, the anomaly catalog, and the faction web survive any publicity regime — C and D discard them for no gain.
3. **A fails the explicit commission** ("NOT preserving it by default"), and a half-executed B is worse than D — a world that contradicts itself on page one would be dishonest work. So the recommendation is conditional: **B only if the team actually does the ontology work** (02.I clearance, cost currency, rank measurand, contract blacklist, Laggard-status declaration). If the team will not do that work, the fallback is D, not a patched B.
4. **The "world without Arthur" test (Q475/Q298) is the binding constraint on B.** `[CANON]` The old world deletes 0 nodes and 0 edges when Arthur is removed. The reconstruction must keep that property: the contract system must be *his* affordance, not the world's sole key — otherwise the reboot fails the one test the old world passed perfectly.

**The three decisions that must be made first** (everything else is downstream):
1. **The metaphysics of contracts** — `[UNKNOWN]` until decided: license-ontology (recommended) vs. pattern-ontology vs. bargaining-ontology. This single decision resolves Q010, Q433, Q435, Q461, Q463, and Q467.
2. **The cost currency** — `[UNKNOWN]` until decided: what irreversibly prices a contract (resonance-drain, marks, obligations, attention). This resolves Q127, Q432, Q497, and the entire stress test.
3. **The Laggard's status** — `[UNKNOWN]` until decided: coexistence with designed interaction, integration, or sunset. The brief's silence (Q051) is the single largest undeclared variable.
