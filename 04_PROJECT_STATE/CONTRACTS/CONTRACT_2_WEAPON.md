It is deliberately lateral to THE POLITE KNOCK: Contract #1 supplies narrow investigative information; Contract #2 converts Arthur's accumulated weapon study into temporary combat capability.

## 2. Core effect

When Arthur selects a weapon, the Contract provides temporary high-level operational familiarity/mastery with that weapon and weapon-performance amplification up to approximately **2Ã—** under valid conditions.

The active window is approximately **10 minutes for the current weapon selection**.

### Weapon terminology firewall

The story distinguishes four layers:

- **Weapon Knowledge:** knowing what a weapon is, its terminology, classification, limits, and records.
- **Weapon Familiarization:** being able to identify, handle, and understand the ordinary operating requirements of a weapon.
- **Weapon Mastery:** demonstrated ability to use a specific weapon effectively under practical conditions, including control, handling, timing, movement, and adaptation under pressure.
- **Weapon Contract:** the later supernatural mechanism that temporarily elevates Arthur into high-level operational mastery of the selected weapon and amplifies its effective performance.

Arthur's early Academy training develops the first three through ordinary study and practice. Contract #2 does not invent the concept of mastery or prove that Arthur was secretly a supernatural weapon genius; it temporarily elevates trained capability beyond his ordinary level.

## 3. Ten-minute limit and body-medium consequence

The normal active window is approximately ten minutes for the current weapon selection.

If Arthur forces the same active weapon state beyond that window, the consequence is paid by the **body part acting as the Contract's weapon medium**.

## 5. Setup/payoff

Arthur learns Weapons in Operations Support because he needs to understand Weapons records: classification, identification, authorization, deployment, maintenance, damage, chain of custody, and terminology. He is not initially trying to become a weapons expert.

## 6. Academy reputation engine

Different cadets may interpret Arthur's weapon breadth literally and differently:

- **Show-off:** he studies/carries many weapons to attract attention.
- **Weapons master:** he must be an exceptional weapons specialist.
- **Still weak:** weapon knowledge does not change Rank F, conventional Esper weakness, or physical vulnerability.

All three opinions may coexist.

## 7. Power firewall

Contract #2 does not raise Rank, awaken an Esper Ability, raise conventional output, grant superhuman durability, make Arthur automatically superior to trained specialists, remove the ten-minute consequence, permit unlimited simultaneous weapon states, or guarantee equal effectiveness for every weapon.

Arthur's advantage remains **knowledge + preparation + Contract interaction + weapon choice + timing + adaptation**.
