## 1. The Load-Bearing Requirements

Every candidate must: (1) **explain cost tolerance** — Toll, Depth, and the world's budget (hours, exposure, favors) must be rational to him: worth paying, not painless; (2) **explain refusal** — the refusal instinct (declining a binding, declining to deepen, declining an invocation) must live *inside* the motivation; (3) **drive investigation, not revenge** — revenge ends; the series needs a question he cannot leave unanswered (author direction: "deeper than revenge"); (4) **fit the ordinary-man constraint** — no pre-existing training, secret power, hidden identity, or loop-short-circuiting knowledge; (5) **survive Rank F** — unsolvable by promotion or Esper output, which he will never have; (6) **be fence-safe** — must not resolve 9/18 (UNDETERMINED), Saitō's glance (UNRESOLVED), Pram (DEFERRED), the artifact origin, or ERROR/UNDEFINED; (7) **never explain the Artifact** — may touch it (custody, cost, family relationship) but never explain its origin. (1–3, 7: **[PROPOSED]**; 4–6: **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**)

## 2. The Option Set

Schema per option: **Premise / Knows / Withheld / Why investigation, not revenge / Cost & refusal / Fence safety.** No ranking. No winner.

### Option A — The Erased Incident

**Premise [PROPOSED]:** An anomaly event struck Ravenhurst; the official record was erased — files redacted, witnesses re-interviewed into doubt. Someone Arthur cared about was lost or broken in it. The wound is the erasure, not the event: *the world agreed to forget, and he refuses to.*
**Knows:** that something happened; the loss's rough shape; the official denial's exact wording (memorized — the records instinct, present from childhood).
**Withheld:** the event's true nature; who ordered the erasure; whether the loss was necessary, malicious, or accidental.
**Why investigation:** no clear perpetrator — only a bureaucracy and a Rule. You cannot stab an erasure. The only response is documentation: build the file the world deleted ("if it's not in the file it didn't happen").
**Cost & refusal:** accepts investigation costs (hours, favors, exposure — cheap relative to forgetting); refuses costs that would make him *like the erasers* — burning witnesses, trading others' safety. Instinct: *never delete a person.*
**Fence safety [CANON-COMPATIBLE]:** safe — mid-tier anomaly, mid-tier institution; no cosmology required.

### Option B — The Forbidden Discovery

**Premise [PROPOSED]:** A family member (a grandfather Arthur barely remembers, or an unspoken-of uncle) investigated something forbidden and disappeared — not died; *disappeared*, leaving a desk with papers mid-sentence. The family sealed the room. Arthur found the papers as a child.
**Knows:** the papers existed; the family's silence is deliberate; the forbidden thing had a shape — a place, a name, a question.
**Withheld:** what the papers said (lost, hidden, or destroyed — **[AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED]**); whether the family member is dead, lost, or transformed; what the forbidden thing *is*.
**Why investigation:** nobody killed anyone — at least, nobody he can name. The engine is a *question*: what was worth disappearing for? His Mastery habit becomes biographical: finishing someone else's sentence.
**Cost & refusal:** accepts dangerous curiosity's cost; refuses the *repetition* cost — he will not disappear the way they did. Instinct: *I am not allowed to vanish; I am the only one still asking.*
**Fence safety [CANON-COMPATIBLE with care]:** safe if the forbidden thing stays generic (a phenomenon, a place, a file) — never the Artifact's origin, Pram, or 9/18. Fence its identity at adoption.

### Option C — The Sacrifice

**Premise [PROPOSED]:** A family member gave something up — years of life, health, presence — to keep a door closed: maintaining the Artifact's dormancy, paying a folk price the family half-understood. Arthur watched it happen and understood it only later.
**Knows:** the cost was paid; the payment worked; the payer never complained and never explained.
**Withheld:** whether the sacrifice was necessary or merely customary; whether it is *over* or merely *paused* now that the Artifact has activated; whether Arthur must take up the payment.
**Why investigation:** the enemy is arithmetic — was the price fair? Is it still being charged? The engine is *audit*: was the suffering a real payment or an old habit nobody questioned? His cost-consciousness becomes biographical: he prices everything because he watched someone pay without a receipt.
**Cost & refusal:** accepts costs that *protect*; refuses costs that *merely continue* — he will not pay a price just because the family always paid it. Instinct: *show me the invoice.* Strongest support for "refusal is a valid progression move."
**Fence safety [CANON-COMPATIBLE with care]:** touches the Artifact (custody/dormancy) — safe only as *custody*, never *origin*. Fence: "custody, not use; maintained, not explained." **[AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED]** on what was sacrificed.

### Option D — The Question Worth the Killing

**Premise [PROPOSED]:** An organization killed (or broke, or took) someone in Arthur's orbit. The engine is deliberately NOT the killing — it is the *question the killing implies*: whatever they took was important enough to kill for, and nobody will tell him what it was. His motivation is not the corpse; it is *the file the corpse was standing in front of.*
**Knows:** the organization exists dimly (a name overheard, a car that shouldn't be there); the official story is wrong; the dead person knew something.
**Withheld:** the organization's identity, scope, motive; what the dead person knew; whether the killing was ordered or local.
**Why investigation:** revenge is *structurally impossible* early (he cannot find, reach, or fight them — Rank F, permanently) and *intellectually insufficient* later (even dead, they wouldn't answer the question). The engine is the *motive*: what was worth killing for? That question outlives any revenge.
**Cost & refusal:** accepts costs of approach (exposure, favors, danger); refuses costs that would make him *their instrument*. Instinct: *I will not be recruited by my enemy.* Strongest support for "calculating, hesitant."
**Fence safety [CANON-COMPATIBLE with care]:** the organization must be new and mid-tier — NOT the Pale Court (canonically a misread of custody, bible §2.5), NOT connected to 9/18, Saitō, or Pram. Fence its identity at adoption or this option eats the mystery architecture.

### Option E — The Failed Rescue

**Premise [PROPOSED]:** Someone died (or was lost to an anomaly) because the response was too slow, too ignorant, or too weak — and Arthur was *present*. Not a hero who failed heroically; a bystander who didn't know enough, didn't prepare, didn't see the Rule in time. The death was, in retrospect, *solvable* — and he knows it.
**Knows:** exactly what happened, in what order; the moment the solve became impossible; his own precise inadequacy.
**Withheld:** whether knowing more would actually have saved them (the story must keep this genuinely uncertain — otherwise the motivation is guilt with an answer); what the phenomenon's Rule was.
**Why investigation:** the phenomenon is gone or filed; no one to kill. The engine is *preparation*: "never again unprepared." This most directly explains "uncertainty is the curriculum" — his broad training, checklists, and refusal to improvise are *the rescue, rehearsed forever.*
**Cost & refusal:** accepts costs of readiness (training hours, investigation, Depth spend); refuses costs of *haste* — he will not rush a solve to feel less guilty, because rushing is what kills. Instinct: *not yet; not until the map is true.* (Rhymes with the Mastery doctrine: a FULL binding on false premises snaps.)
**Fence safety [CANON-COMPATIBLE]:** safe — a small, local anomaly; no institutional or cosmological weight.

### Option F — The Custodial Debt

**Premise [PROPOSED]:** The Reed family has held the Artifact for generations — custody, not use, never activated (bible §3.1: "inherited for generations" meant *custody*). Arthur grew up inside the customs and only at activation understands: his family was *guarding something* without knowing what it was — and the guard failed on his watch. Dormancy broke in his generation.
**Knows:** the customs (from childhood); that the Artifact skipped his father's generation (bible §2.5); that the family kept faith with something they didn't understand.
**Withheld:** everything about the Artifact's origin (fenced — never explained); whether the custody *mattered* or was theater; whether activation is a failure or a schedule.
**Why investigation:** no enemy — only a duty inherited without instructions. The engine is *stewardship*: what was he guarding? What does a custodian owe the thing in his care? Closest to the bible's existing frame; the Artifact relationship becomes *relational* rather than *explanatory*: he owes it care, not comprehension.
**Cost & refusal:** accepts costs of stewardship (tending load is literal — the Contracts are mouths to feed); refuses costs that would make him *the Artifact's* rather than its keeper. Instinct: *custody is not surrender.* Makes slot interference thematic: every binding is another mouth at a table he didn't choose to set.
**Fence safety [CANON-COMPATIBLE]:** the safest option — built from bible materials. Strict fence: custody, never origin.

### Option G — The Compound: Loss + Cover-Up + Anomaly

**Premise [PROPOSED]:** The author's combined direction as one option: a personal loss (someone taken by an anomaly) AND an institutional cover-up (the event erased or refiled) AND the anomaly itself unsolved and possibly active. Three strands: grief (the person), anger at the lie (the cover-up), the open file (the anomaly).
**Knows:** the loss; the cover-up's existence (he can prove the file was altered); the anomaly's filed classification (redacted — but he has the redaction's shape).
**Withheld:** the anomaly's Rule; the cover-up's authorizing level; whether the loss was preventable.
**Why investigation:** the compound is deliberately *too big* for revenge — three strands pull in different directions (grief wants rest, anger wants a target, the file wants a solution). The engine is the *file*: the only strand he can pull.
**Cost & refusal:** accepts almost any investigation cost (three motives agree); refusal comes from the *strands disagreeing* — grief would trade safety for answers; the file would not. Instinct: *the person I lost would not want me to become the cover-up.* Must be dramatized as conflict, not given as a trait.
**Fence safety [CANON-COMPATIBLE with care]:** the most fence-risky — a cover-up invites institutional depth, which invites the protected mysteries. Fence at adoption: the covering institution is mid-tier and local; the anomaly is unrelated to 9/18, Saitō, Pram, and the Artifact's origin.

### Option H — The Unwritten Fear (original)

**Premise [PROPOSED]:** As a child, Arthur witnessed — or nearly witnessed — a person being *unwritten*: not killed, but removed from records, memories, consequence. A neighbor's name gone from the school roster; a face his mother almost remembers. Nothing proven; everything *almost*. He has lived with the terror that existence is fragile and unrecorded things cease. His records instinct, his notebook, "if it's not in the file it didn't happen" — not personality. *Prophylaxis.*
**Knows:** the shape of an absence (he can point to where the person should be); nothing provable — that is the point.
**Withheld:** whether it happened at all (the story should keep this genuinely ambiguous — his motivation may rest on a true memory or a child's misreading, and *he knows that*, which is why he documents everything); what unwrites.
**Why investigation:** no perpetrator — possibly no event. The engine is *epistemic dread*: every solved Rule proves the world has structure, and structure keeps people written. His Mastery habit is existential: a truer map is a world that holds.
**Cost & refusal:** accepts costs of documentation and understanding (Depth is *cheap* to someone buying proof the world is real); refuses costs that would *unwrite him* — memory effects, identity erosion. Instinct: *I will not pay in myself.* (Gives the body pattern personal meaning: the one record nobody can redact.)
**Fence safety [CANON-COMPATIBLE]:** safe — the unwriting is never explained as 9/18, Pram, or the Artifact; ideally stays ambiguous whether it was anomalous at all. **[AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED]** on whether the unwriting was real.

### Option I — The Sister He Must Not Save (original)

**Premise [PROPOSED]:** Arthur knows — or has worked out — that his younger sister Hannah was *eligible* for the Artifact and was passed over (bible §2.5: she was also eligible; the story never tells her). He carries this alone. His motivation is protective and negative: keep the supernatural world from ever noticing her. The unspoken purpose of every Contract: *draw the fire toward himself.*
**Knows:** Hannah was eligible; the Artifact chose him instead; institutions (Pale Court interest, EVENT-098 — bible §2.5) misread custody as bloodline, putting the *family* in the file.
**Withheld:** from Hannah, everything (the story must never tell her — bible §2.5); from Arthur, whether his protection is working or performative.
**Why investigation:** protection is forward-looking. The engine is the gap between what he knows and what threatens: understand the world that might come for his sister before it does. His broad preparation becomes *loving*, not merely prudent.
**Cost & refusal:** accepts costs that *distance* danger from her (he will be the visible one, the filed one); refuses absolutely any development that would make *her* interesting — any Contract, alliance, or revelation touching Hannah is refused, whatever its power. Instinct: *not her.* Sharpest, most legible refusal — but the story must never let "protect Hannah" become a plot coupon or cheap enemy leverage.
**Fence safety [CANON-COMPATIBLE with care]:** built from bible materials. Fence: Hannah's eligibility must never become a *mechanism* (no "she can take up the Artifact" escape hatch); the Pale Court's misread stays a misread. **[AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED]** on how much Arthur knows vs suspects.

## 3. Cross-Cutting Analysis

**What every option must provide:** a question that outlives any answer (each engine is a *question* — what was erased? / what was found? / was the price fair? / what was worth killing for? / could I have saved them? / what did I guard? / what is the file? / did it happen? / is she safe? — never a target); a reason to pay (the question is priced: hours, Depth, Toll, exposure — worth paying, not painless); a reason to refuse (each option's internal instinct: *never delete a person / not allowed to vanish / show me the invoice / not recruited / not yet / custody is not surrender / don't become the cover-up / not in myself / not her*); rank-proofness; ordinary-man compatibility. Arthur starts as he starts: 17, Rank F, a notebook, a question.

**What differs:** *Locus* — family-history (B, C, F: longest fuse, least heat); personal-experience (A, E, H: immediate heat, early-resolution risk); relational (D, I: a permanent live wire); compound (G: richest, most sprawling). *Heat* — D, G run hot; B, F, H run cold; the "deeper than revenge" direction favors cold engines, but a cold engine must be *dramatized* as costing him or it reads as detachment. *Artifact proximity* — F, C direct (custody frame); H oblique (the record); the rest independent. Closer = more thematic unity, more fence risk. *Revenge-adjacency* — D is most revenge-adjacent (deliberately disarmed); G revenge-capable (must be fenced). Clean set: B, C, E, F, H, I.

## 4. Interaction With the Five Prior Systems

**Progression architecture — why he chases understanding.** Motivation is the crank-turner: every option's question is answerable only by genuine firsthand understanding — the currency of both axes. **[PROPOSED]** He does not chase Contracts; he chases *answers*, and Contracts are where answers live. Acquisition stays world-driven, application Arthur-driven: the world offers phenomena; his question decides which ones he cannot walk past. **[CANON-COMPATIBLE]**

**Mastery — why he tolerates failed tests.** Motivation makes a failed test *data* instead of *defeat*. **[PROPOSED]** E makes it literal (the rescue, rehearsed); H makes it existential (a failed test still proves the world has structure worth mapping). Felt-certainty is a *moral* hazard too: the map feeling true when it isn't is the shape of every option's original wound (the erasure felt true; the sacrifice felt necessary; the rescue felt impossible).

**Weapon training — why breadth.** "Uncertainty is the curriculum": his question never tells him what shape the answer takes, so he must be ready for any shape. **[PROPOSED]** E gives the most legible form (breadth is the vow); I the most emotional (breadth is the shield). KNOWLEDGE ≠ SKILL holds everywhere: wanting readiness does not grant it — training stays priced in hours, injury, instruction.

**Cost system — which costs are "worth it."** The five-tier doctrine is *priced*; motivation is the *wallet*. Each option draws the worth-it line differently (§2), but all share the load-bearing rule: **paying more never buys more.** **[PROPOSED; CANON-COMPATIBLE mechanism]** Grief does not strengthen the knock; love does not discount Depth. Refusal stays valid because every option's question can survive an unanswered season — the question is patient, even when he is not.

**Body pattern — what the record means to him.** The pattern is a record only — grants nothing, meters nothing (SEEN channel; Toll is FELT). Motivation gives it *personal* meaning, never mechanical: A/H — the file nobody can redact; C/F — the invoice; E — the rehearsal; I — the decoy ledger (*look at me, not her*); B/D/G — the margin note, on the only page the institutions can't file. **[PROPOSED; CANON-COMPATIBLE with the pattern fence]**
