## THE PICK: Candidate 7 — THE POLITE KNOCK

**What it is:** a rule-fragment / exception-clause finder. Arthur knocks — once per phenomenon, in person, after demonstrated observation — and the phenomenon's Rule answers with one confirmed sentence of its own terms. Not the solve; the *shape* of the solve.

**Authority:** the phenomenon's Membrane-side Rule expression (non-sapient; settling, not bargaining — G's counterparty ontology).
**Condition:** demonstrated-only — he must have stood in front of the phenomenon and observed it firsthand; one knock per phenomenon; presence-only (no remote knocking).
**Cost:** Depth per invocation, scaled to the phenomenon's filed tier (spendable Current Depth; visible drain).
**Limitation:** yields terms, never identity, never the future, never other people; cannot reach what auto-solve needs.
**Failure mode (disclosed, load-bearing):** felt certainty — the knock can *confirm a misreading* with full conviction. The failure must read as *his* error, legible in hindsight, never as the system lying.

## WHY THE KNOCK WINS

**1. It is the calibration contract (Q196).** The First Mooring must teach Arthur — and the reader — the system's grammar in the fewest scenes. The Knock teaches the single most load-bearing sentence in the canon: **powers have conditions, and conditions can be attacked** (04 §4.7 [KEEP] — the protagonist's entire survival strategy). Every future survival scene runs on knocked terms; every future contract cites them as precedent. No other candidate puts the bible's core mechanic in the reader's hands this early.

**2. It passes all seven R6 acceptance constraints** (`04_PROJECT_STATE/FINAL_CANON_PROPOSAL/RULESETS.md` R6):
1. Weak in combat, foundational in method ✓ — investigative, never martial.
2. Named boundary + named counter ✓ — demonstrated-only / one-knock-per-phenomenon / presence-only; the felt-certainty counter is disclosed.
3. Non-I-flag ✓ — no infohazard content.
4. Phenomenon-specific ✓ — one phenomenon per knock; range-limited by presence.
5. Spendable cost ✓ — per-invocation Depth, tier-scaled, visible.
6. Local witness, not answer ✓ — yields the shape of the solve, subject to corroboration.
7. Non-sapient source ✓ — bilateral contracts stay late-series (sapient exclusion).

**3. It is the best chapter engine in the set.** The knock's answer as a closing sentence (*"it cannot cross what is bound"*) is a repeatable scene-ender that never repeats, because each answer is different. The opening arc's satisfaction requirement (directive §14: frequent explicit satisfaction) is carried by visible wins the Knock produces *now*, unlike the Ledger's early INSUFFICIENT RECORDs.

**4. Its weakness is dramatically productive.** The felt-certainty failure mode is not a bug to patch — it is the series' fallibility thesis (29 §B.5 [KEEP]) made mechanical. When the Knock confirms a misreading, the reader learns the intended lesson: distrust *unearned* certainty. The Ledger's misreading-laundering mode needs sustained prose discipline to stay honest; the Knock's failure mode *is* the drama.

**5. Its artifact hook is the right kind of quiet.** The knocked terms speak the Seal's own vocabulary ("bound") — the small thing speaking the big language, tying the artifact mystery to a T1 phenomenon. It is subtler than the Ledger's INSUFFICIENT DEPTH (which cannot be missed) — appropriate for Contract #1, whose mystery beats should accumulate, not detonate.

**6. It fits the March→September structure.** The First Invoice (March activation; yield consumed by waking the stone) means Contract #1 must feel *earned, minor, and invoiced* — J's pacing already stages it as "dramatically minor" at ch7 **[SUPERSEDED — LOCK 2 timeline: binding at ch12 per LOCK 8 F-07; C-14-07 repair 2026-09-23]** with meaning peaking at ch15 (the first true cost). The Knock's first binding is a receipt, not a coronation: exactly the anti-chosen-one grammar the reboot needs.
