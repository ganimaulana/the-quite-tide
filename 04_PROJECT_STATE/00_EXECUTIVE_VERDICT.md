## §2 — WHY (standalone reasoning)

**2.1 The audit's numbers.** The 500-question audit produced 94 [CONTRADICTION], 20 [NEEDS REDESIGN], 67 [LOOPHOLE], and 305 [UNRESOLVED]. The contradictions are not evenly distributed: they cluster in *solvable* places. The premise clash (public Esper society vs. Meridian secrecy) resolves to a two-tier Disclosure amendment. The rank duplication (F–S vs. T0–T7) resolves to a single mapping table. The 02.I collision (no power copying) resolves to a license-ontology. The cost vacuum resolves to a designed currency (Resonance/Depth + Toll). A contradiction that resolves to one amendment, one table, one ontology, and one currency is not a world that must die; it is a world that must be rebuilt in specific, named places.

**2.2 The old world's best assets are premise-independent.** The eight design workers, working in parallel from the audit, independently converged on the same load-bearing assets: 02's hard rules, 30's seven weak-vs-strong mechanisms, the anomaly catalog, the faction web, the Laggard, the Erosion arithmetic. None of these depends on the secrecy premise or on Arthur's old spec. Total reboot (Option C) or a new world (Option D) would discard them for no gain; minor adaptation (Option A) fails the explicit commission, which mandates the Esper-rank society, the family artifact, and the contract system.

**2.3 The Arthur-free test still passes.** The "IF ARTHUR NEVER EXISTED" simulation (10 timelines) shows the world surviving completely and indifferently without him. The contract system is his *affordance*, not the world's keystone. The reboot therefore does not make the old world a stage built for one actor — it gives one actor a new instrument inside an intact world. This is the strongest single piece of evidence that B, not C/D, is the honest verdict.

**2.4 The eight workers independently converged.** Artifact, anomaly, mission, story-engine, contract, pacing, arc, roadmap, risk, and both canon-proposal workers — designed in isolation — produced mutually reconcilable designs on the Option B hypothesis. The six friction points found in the coordinator's consistency pass (see §5) all resolve to single-line amendments, not redesigns. A design direction whose parallel workers converge is a direction the world supports.

**2.5 The heaviest objection is answered, not dismissed.** Worker K's R-01 — "the protagonist rewrite kills the reader contract" — is the risk most likely to kill the project, and it is real: the directive's letter (genius athlete from page one) collides with the 20 chapters' ordinary clerk and with the user's standing arc principle ("he must not suddenly become a completely different person"). The design answers it by *deviating from the directive's letter in service of its identity rule*: Arthur's genius/athleticism is the destination, not the ch1 state. He starts as the ordinary clerk and earns every capability on-page, inside a permanent trained-human ceiling. His arc is an **exposure curve** (what the world is forced to see about him), not a power climb. The undervaluers are *right on their own terms* — Rank F is a true reading of an Esper ability he genuinely lacks — so every reversal changes the terms of judgment instead of revealing hidden stats. This is documented as an explicit, deliberate deviation (see §5.6). If the user rejects the deviation, the verdict must be revisited — the deviation is load-bearing.

### 5.5 Currency: RESONANCE (gauge) / DEPTH (unit) / TOLL (per-signing mark) — [RECOMMENDATION]

> **[LOCK 8 — 2026-09-23] This section's terminology ruling is [SUPERSEDED] on terminology only.** The story logic (one currency, two tiers, Toll-mark distinct from First Invoice, novelty-gated income, per-use pricing) is [KEEP]. The locked vocabulary (LOCK 8, author decisions 3–4): **RESONANCE** = the event/state in which genuine understanding of an anomaly reaches the condition necessary for contract formation — NOT a currency. **DEPTH** = the anomaly-derived resource: **CURRENT DEPTH** (currently available) and **LIFETIME DEPTH** (cumulative historically obtained — NOT XP, Rank, Level, Power Tier, Slot count, or conventional Esper energy; does not decrease). Do NOT use "Current Resonance" / "Lifetime Resonance" as resource terminology. Outcome vocabulary: FULL / PARTIAL / FRAGMENT / FAILED; "SOLVED" = investigative state (SOLVED ≠ FULL). Wherever this file says "Current Resonance"/"Lifetime Resonance," read Current Depth/Lifetime Depth.

**Apparent conflict:** H denominates the cost currency DEPTH; G denominates it resonance; J tracks Current vs. Lifetime Resonance.

**Ruling:** One currency, three terms, no contradiction:
- **Resonance** = the currency (canonical diegetic name; the directive's "Hidden Resonance Gauge").
- **Depth** = the unit of account ("spend 40 Depth"; the Ledger's refusal beat *INSUFFICIENT DEPTH* stays diegetic).
- **Current Depth** (spendable; invocations, renewals, buyouts drain it) vs. **Lifetime Depth** (cumulative historically obtained; does NOT decrease; NOT a current progression resource, NOT an XP-like pool) = the two tiers. **Slot access is separately governed (access-gated capacity); no numeric DEPTH threshold is canon; Lifetime Depth never defines slot unlocks.** [C-14-06 repair 2026-09-23 — the "slot-unlock thresholds; woundable by priced failures" reading is SUPERSEDED per LOCK 8 F-11; AD-T5 remains OPEN.]
- **Toll-mark** (H) = the permanent, visible alteration per signing — distinct from G's **First Invoice** (the one-time activation debt, paid March→September). Both stand; they are different invoices.
- Income: certified novel solves only, difficulty-scaled by filed tier AND novelty-gated (re-solving pays nothing; scheduled phenomena cannot become a salary). Slots relock if Current falls below threshold (G + L1; J's ch30 "lifetime threshold" amended to "gauge threshold").

### 5.6 The protagonist deviation: DOCUMENTED (J + L2 compatible) — [PROPOSED]

**Apparent conflict:** The directive's letter (genius, athletic, combat-capable from the start) vs. the earlier L2/J reconciliation (trained self-erasure, athletic base treated as modest/ordinary, capability earned on-page).

**Ruling — REVISED AUTHOR CANON 2026-09-24:** Arthur is genuinely athletic from the start, with good conditioning and fast natural reactions, but has no Esper-powered physical enhancement and no combat-specialist training. The directive's athletic starting adjective is therefore retained; the old "ordinary/modest fitness" reconciliation is superseded. What is earned on-page is not basic physical capability but **application under Academy pressure**: reaction, endurance, positioning, coordination, and the ability to combine a strong human body with his observation method. "Combat-capable" remains rescoped to evasion/endurance/positioning with a permanent trained-human ceiling. The identity rule — "Everyone else becomes stronger by awakening powers. Arthur becomes stronger by understanding things that should not exist" — remains intact. "NEW CAPABILITY" remains "NEW PRICED UNDERSTANDING."
