## 0. Engine discipline (applies to all thirteen)

1. **Independence:** every engine must be *runnable* without the others — each has its own fuel, its own cast, its own stakes. Arthur can spend a whole arc inside one engine while the others idle (idle ≠ paused — see rule 3).
2. **The IF-ARTHUR-NEVER-EXISTED test** (MEMORY.md's standing rule [CANON-directive]): every engine must have a **Arthur-less state** — the world has its own causal structure; the engine *runs* without him, and his entry *perturbs* it rather than *creating* it. Each engine below states its Arthur-less state explicitly.
3. **Idle engines advance:** an engine Arthur isn't touching still moves (the Tide doesn't wait). Off-screen advancement is delivered as *consequences*, never as homework — he returns to find the board changed.
4. **The fence** (Q100 [KEEP]): no engine may resolve the 9/18 UNDETERMINED or Saitō's glance UNRESOLVED. Engines may *approach* the fence; crossing it requires coordinator CHANGELOG approval (33's rules [CANON]).
5. **Collision over parallelism** (MISSION_TASK_SYSTEM.md §6.6): engines don't run side-by-side — they *interfere*. Each engine below names its primary collision partners.

## Engine 1 — The Underbrush Investigations

*The procedural engine: one phenomenon, one Rule, one settlement at a time.*

- **Short-term payoff:** a complete mystery per arc — the threefold test (ANOMALY_DESIGN.md §2) gives every investigation a *shape*: falsifiable statement → prediction → settlement. The reader gets closure; the closure is *procedural*, never total (residues and relations remain).
- **Medium-term conflict:** the backlog (MISSION_TASK_SYSTEM.md §5) — 3–5 open threads collide; the 72-hour clock ticks per mission; the Academy grades what the street needs hidden. Investigations *interfere* (the hunter's target is the researcher's subject).
- **Long-term mystery:** the *pattern* across investigations — the underbrush is getting *denser* (High Tide — 01 [CANON]). Are the phenomena increasing, or is he just *seeing* more? (Both. The answer being "both" is the long game.)
- **Sustainability:** the underbrush is *inexhaustible by design* — T1/T2 phenomena below reporting thresholds (Q048 solution 2), generated by the Tide itself (01's master clock [CANON]). Novelty-gating (Q057) forces *new* phenomena, never repeats. Hundreds of chapters: the city is 8.9 million people on a thinning Veil — the case supply is cosmological.
- **Arthur-less state:** the phenomena occur without him; the Classification Office ignores them (attention economics — 05.5); hunters occasionally decommission one; the city endures. His entry *settles* what was previously *endured*.
- **Collides with:** 2 (contracts from solves), 8 (the market prices solved phenomena), 9 (disasters are investigations at scale).

- **Short-term payoff:** the loosened face — a new impression, a new capability, a new *price*. Every acquisition is a heist-in-reverse: understand the un-understandable, then *carry* it.
- **Medium-term conflict:** the gauge economy (ARTIFACT_DESIGN.md §9) — slower growth is *structural* (invocation drains; costs rise with fill — no numeric cost curve is canon **[AD-12-2: DEFERRED / UNSAFE — NOT CANON; no replacement number exists]**; slot relock is **[DEFERRED — AD-T4, not canon]**). Breakthroughs are bigger (composites — ANOMALY_DESIGN.md §8) but composites are hazards. Curation vs. accumulation: twelve faces, and discharge means *letting go*. [C-14-03 repair 2026-09-23.]
- **Long-term mystery:** the thirteenth face (ARTIFACT_DESIGN.md §12.5 [UNKNOWN]) — what happens when the twelve are filled. The inversion (the Seal as lure) — at high fill, is he still the holder, or the *bait*?
- **Sustainability:** the cost curve *is* the pacing — later faces are priced harder than earlier ones (qualitative: the progression *decelerates by structure*, not by formula). **[AD-12-2: no numeric slot-unlock threshold is canon — the "~3× cost per face / face 6 costs ~243× face 1" reading is DEFERRED / UNSAFE, NOT CANON.]** Fragments, partials, and conditional impressions (ANOMALY_DESIGN.md §1) provide *mid-tier* progression beats between full acquisitions. Hundreds of chapters: the Seal fills *slowly*, and every face is an arc. [C-14-03 repair 2026-09-23.]
- **Arthur-less state:** the Seal sleeps in the drawer (dormancy — ARTIFACT_DESIGN.md §5). No engine without the holder — which is *correct*: this is the one engine that *is* him (and the design says so openly).
- **Collides with:** 1 (solves yield contracts), 10 (the second solver), 3 (each face is evidence for/against an origin theory).

## Engine 3 — The Artifact Mystery

*The myth engine: what is the Seal, who made it, what does it want?*

- **Short-term payoff:** the custody paper trail — the 1987 appraisal, the grandmother's papers, the salt-customs (ARTIFACT_DESIGN.md §4). Every document is a *fact* about the stone that raises two questions.
- **Medium-term conflict:** the three theories (ARTIFACT_DESIGN.md §3) — Surveyor's Instrument vs. Drowned Bequest vs. Accretion — each with *partisans* (the Archivists back Theory A; the Pale Court's bloodline theater leans B; the Cartographers' measurements suggest C). The theories are *fought over*, not just pondered.
- **Long-term mystery:** the origin decision itself [UNKNOWN] — with stated decision procedures (each theory carries its proof/kill conditions). The ultimate purpose (§14) is downstream. The maker's face is the physical hinge.
- **Sustainability:** the decision procedures are *evidence-gated* — the mystery advances only when the story *earns* evidence (the Locker Bank's seam-work → Theory A; a resonance-date → Theory B; a new face forming → Theory C). Hundreds of chapters: the evidence arrives *one finding per long arc*, and each finding re-weights all three theories rather than killing two. The design *forbids* early resolution (the mystery capital is budgeted — 33's rules).
- **Arthur-less state:** the theories exist without him — the Archivists argue about Second Silence instruments; the Pale Court collects bloodlines; the Cartographers measure folds. His Seal is *evidence* in their argument — which is why they all want it.
- **Collides with:** 4 (the custody history is the evidence), 7 (researchers are the partisans), 13 (the Classification Office's files *are* the archive the theories fight over).

## Engine 4 — Family History

*The intimate engine: the drawer, the grandmother, the women who kept the stone.*

- **Short-term payoff:** the customs — "we don't sell grandmother's thing," salt by the door, the *protective charm* drawer (ARTIFACT_DESIGN.md §4). Every custom is a *folk Rule-condition* (03.2 [CANON]) — the family's ordinariness is *data*.
- **Medium-term conflict:** the silence (Q096 [ADAPT]) — his secrecy protects them; the drawer makes secrecy harder. Eleanor's questions; Hannah's eligibility (the criteria are behavioral — she *could*); Thomas's obliviousness as camouflage. The family-dinner beat now carries the Seal.
- **Long-term mystery:** the grandmother — who was she, what did she *know* (customs, not mechanics — ARTIFACT_DESIGN.md §4), and why did the Seal come to *her*? The custody chain backward: who gave it to *her*? (The chain leads into the Second Silence — Engine 3's territory.)
- **Sustainability:** the family is *not* the supernatural world — they're the *control group*. Hundreds of chapters: ordinary life (meals, shifts, festivals, the sister's exams) is the *ballast* that makes the supernatural legible. The engine's fuel is *intimacy*, not mystery — it doesn't deplete; it *deepens*. The long game: what happens when the family can no longer be kept outside (the Pale Court's interest in *bloodlines* points at them — EVENT-098).
- **Arthur-less state:** the family endures — the drawer stays closed, the customs persist, the grandmother's papers yellow. His entry *opens* what they kept closed — the engine's tragedy is that *he* is the disturbance.
- **Collides with:** 3 (custody history), 5 (the Court's bloodline theater targets the family), 10 (Hannah as the potential second solver).

Every dismissal is a setup; every reversal is *earned* (never secretly-OP — the identity rule [CANON-directive]).

His existence *offends* the classification (the composite-vector space — 05.7 [CANON] — made flesh). The long question: is the rank system *wrong about power itself*? (04's ported cost curves vs. his gauge — two economies of strength, mutually illegible.)
- **Sustainability:** the rank system is *society* — every new Esper, every faction, every institution re-poses the question. Hundreds of chapters: the political board *refills* (new students, new Court moves, new registry fights). His eventual capability "outside conventional classification" (directive [CANON-directive]) is the *horizon*, never the arrival — the engine runs on *approach*.
- **Arthur-less state:** Esper politics runs without him — ranks, Courts, registries, the whole status economy. His entry is a *category error* walking around — the engine's comedy and menace.

## Engine 6 — Academy Progression

*The institutional engine: practicum, grading, being seen to be ordinary.*

- **Short-term payoff:** the assignment — a curriculum-safe mission (MISSION_TASK_SYSTEM.md §2.1) with a *grade*. The practicum's structure (brief → field → report) is the mystery formula *done straight* — the comfort-food engine.
- **Medium-term conflict:** the grading *is* a filing — every report is an intelligence product (MISSION_TASK_SYSTEM.md §2.1's failure mode). He must solve *while being seen to be ordinary* — the undervalue engine's home ground. Classmates (the social cast), instructors (the evaluators), the practicum board (the misfiling machine that keeps sorting him into opportunity).
- **Long-term mystery:** what the Academy *is* — its Second Silence roots, its Pale Court entanglements, its *real* curriculum (the practicum sorts students *for* someone — who?). The Academy as an institution has a *file* — and his clerk's hands can find it.
- **Sustainability:** the Academy *refills* — new terms, new students, new practicum tiers. His progression through it is *slow* (the gauge economy gates his field capability; the Academy gates his *standing*). Hundreds of chapters: school stories are inexhaustible because *people* are inexhaustible — the engine's fuel is the cast, not the curriculum.
- **Arthur-less state:** the Academy teaches without him — students graduate, get ranked, get hired. His entry *perturbs* the sorting (the T1 who solves T3 problems — the board's anomaly).
- **Collides with:** 5 (rank politics), 7 (researchers recruit from the Academy), 1 (practicum assignments *are* investigations).

## Engine 7 — The Research–Hunter Axis

*The ideological engine: study vs. decommission — the Compact's conscience, split in two.*

- **Short-term payoff:** the subcontract — hunters hire him to *settle* what they'd otherwise *kill* (MISSION_TASK_SYSTEM.md §2.3); researchers hire him to *witness* what they can't *keep* (MISSION_TASK_SYSTEM.md §2.4). Every job is a *doctrinal* conflict with a paycheck.
- **Medium-term conflict:** the misalignment — researchers want phenomena *prolonged* (a settled anomaly is a closed paper); hunters want them *ended* (a settled anomaly is a lost commission). He is the third option *neither* license names (Q336 [ADAPT]) — and both sides increasingly need him *while* resenting him. The kill-pressure (hunters) vs. the publish-pressure (researchers) — he's the rope in their tug-of-war.
- **Long-term mystery:** the Compact's *doctrine* — why containment-or-decommission, why no settlement doctrine? (The answer is historical — 24/25's suppressed vessel program [PROPOSED, details UNKNOWN] — the Compact *tried* his third option once, and it produced the three corpses. The doctrine is a *scar*.) The long game: does he *heal* the doctrine, or *prove* it right?
- **Sustainability:** the axis is *structural* — study vs. kill is a permanent tension in any containment regime (05 [CANON]). New phenomena re-pose it; new researchers and hunters *personify* it. Hundreds of chapters: the ideological war doesn't end — it *evolves* (the Asylum Protocol — ANOMALY_DESIGN.md §10 — is the axis's endgame legislation).
- **Arthur-less state:** researchers publish, hunters decommission, the Compact endures — the axis runs without him. His entry *adds the third option* — the engine's disruption is doctrinal, not personal.
- **Collides with:** 11 (the Asylum Protocol is this axis's legislation), 8 (the market buys from *both* sides), 9 (disasters force the axis to *choose* — study or kill, with a city at stake).

## Engine 8 — The Black Market

*The economic engine: the Factor's buyers, the appraisal, the price of the priceless.*

- **Short-term payoff:** the job — real money (MISSION_TASK_SYSTEM.md §2.7: the only source that pays what the work is worth — which is the tell). The market's missions are *clean*: no brief-lies (the market can't afford lies — prices are information, 02.II), no jurisdiction, no clock.
- **Medium-term conflict:** the appraisal — every job *examines* him (the Factor's buyers appraise the solver). The market wants the *Seal*, not the solving — and the void (ARTIFACT_DESIGN.md §11: theft gets a dormant stone) means they must *rent* him willing. The conflict: the money is real, the *interest* is realer. The counterfeit-copy gray market (Q014 solution 2 — fragment-only pressed copies at ruinous gauge cost) is the market's *workaround* — and his *temptation*.
- **Long-term mystery:** *what the market knows* — the Red Ledger's holdings (Q086's footprint logic: the 1987 appraisal is their cold file). The market is the *memory* of every suppressed program (the vessel program's paper trail — 24/25 [ADAPT] — surfaces *here*, in a lot catalog, not in an archive). The long question: who is the Factor *buying for*?
- **Sustainability:** markets don't close — new lots, new buyers, new appraisals. The price-discovery on *him* (what's a solver worth? what's a pressed copy worth? what's the *stone* worth dormant vs. awake?) is an *ongoing* auction. Hundreds of chapters: the market *reprices* him continuously — every capability he reveals moves his price, and his price moves the market's *interest*.
- **Arthur-less state:** the market trades without him — anomalies, records, appraisals. His entry *lists* a new asset class (solvers) — the engine's disruption is economic.
- **Collides with:** 2 (the market wants his contracts — can't have them), 7 (the market buys from researchers and hunters), 10 (the second solver is *also* priced — the auction has two lots).

## Engine 9 — Disasters

*The catastrophe engine: High Tide events, Drowning seasons, the Veil at its thinnest.*

- **Short-term payoff:** the event — a High Tide spike (01 [CANON]: detectable, scheduled-ish), a Drowning cluster, a Quiet that won't lift. Disasters are *investigations at scale* — the threefold test applied to a *city*.
- **Medium-term conflict:** the triage — the Compact's doctrine (contain or decommission) *fails* at disaster scale (attention economics — 05.5: the auditors can't be everywhere). His settlement method (Q336) becomes *policy-relevant* — the disaster is where the third option gets *tested* in public. The research–hunter axis (Engine 7) is forced to *choose*.
- **Long-term mystery:** the Tide's *pattern* — 01's master clock [CANON]: is the High Tide *rising* (a trend, not a cycle)? The disasters are *data* — and his records (29.B.5: records, not knowledge) are the dataset the Cartographers need. The long question: what is the Tide *tending toward*? (The series' cosmological horizon — never answered early.)
- **Sustainability:** the Tide is *cosmological* — 01 [CANON] guarantees the supply. Disasters *escalate by construction* (High Tide dynamics) — the engine's stakes rise without authorial inflation. Hundreds of chapters: the disaster scale *is* the series' clock — each Tide-season bigger than the last, each demanding more of the settlement doctrine.
- **Arthur-less state:** disasters happen without him — the Compact contains, hunters decommission, the city endures and mourns. His entry *adds settlement* — the engine's hope is doctrinal.
- **Collides with:** 1 (disasters are investigations), 7 (the axis must choose), 12 (world anomalies *become* disasters at High Tide).

## Engine 10 — Competing Contractors

*The rivalry engine: the second solver — the loaded gun (Q476 solution 1).*

- **Short-term payoff:** the rumor — someone *else* is solving phenomena. A filed anomaly settles *wrong* (terms he didn't negotiate). A face-shaped *absence* in the market (the Factor's buyers appraising *someone else*).
- **Medium-term conflict:** the criteria are *reproducible* (ARTIFACT_DESIGN.md §6) — a second Loud solver *could* wake the Seal (it's re-wakeable — ARTIFACT_DESIGN.md §11). The conflict isn't "evil counterpart" — it's *standing*: who gets to be the solver? The Pale Court's bloodline theater (EVENT-098) wants a *Court* solver; the Red Ledger wants a *Ledger* solver; the Academy wants a *ranked* solver. He is *none* of these — he's the *drawer*'s solver. The rivalry is *institutional*, not personal — until it becomes personal.
- **Long-term mystery:** *whether* the second solver appears — and *what they do differently*. (The design holds this as a *possibility*, not a schedule — the loaded gun fires when the story needs it, not before.) The long question: is the Seal's *purpose* (Engine 3) better served by *one* holder or *many*? (Theory A says archive — archives have *staff*. Theory B says heir — heirs are *singular*. Theory C says weather — weather doesn't care.)
- **Sustainability:** the *possibility* sustains — the rumor engine runs on *absence* (settled phenomena he didn't settle; market prices moving without him). When the rival *arrives*, the engine becomes the series' central rivalry — sustainable for hundreds of chapters because the rivalry is *methodological* (two solvers, two methods, two doctrines — the story can stage *solve-offs* indefinitely). Hannah as the *potential* second solver (Engine 4) keeps the engine *intimate*.
- **Arthur-less state:** the *possibility* exists without him — the criteria are public-in-principle (ARTIFACT_DESIGN.md §6: stated, behavioral, reproducible). Any Loud solver with a genuine solve near the stone *could*. His entry *occupies* the slot — the engine's tension is *tenure*.
- **Collides with:** 2 (two holders, one stone — the custody crisis), 4 (Hannah), 5 (the Court wants *their* solver), 8 (the market prices *two* lots).

## Engine 12 — World Anomalies

*The world engine: the catalog's long tail — the world's own weirdness, independent of Arthur.*

- **Short-term payoff:** the *file* — a 28-style catalog entry (ANOMALY-001–028's precedent [CANON]): the Bell, the Spoons, the Salt Garden, the Cartographer's Compass — the world is *already* strange, filed, and *ongoing*. - **Medium-term conflict:** the *stewardship* — filed anomalies need *keepers* (the Bell's Tribunal, the Spoons' Admiralty rota). Keepers age, retire, die, *disagree*. The medium game: the world's strangeness needs *maintenance* — and the maintainers are *people* (the Spoons' rota politics; the Compass's surveyors). Arthur's settlements *perturb* filed anomalies (re-audit triggers — 05.4 [ADAPT]) — the world pushes back.
- **Long-term mystery:** the *catalog's* pattern — 28's entries, taken together, *imply* something (the Second Silence's shape; the Tide's history). The long game: the catalog is *itself* a mystery — who filed the first entry, and what were they *not* filing?
- **Sustainability:** the world is *bigger* than him — 8.9 million people, the Western Europe (MEMORY.md's regional directive), the catalog's implied *global* scope. New places, new filings, new keepers — inexhaustible. Hundreds of chapters: the engine is the series' *setting*, and settings don't deplete.
- **Arthur-less state:** the world anomalies run *entirely* without him — this is the engine that *proves* the IF-ARTHUR-NEVER-EXISTED test. The Bell rings; the Spoons are wound; the Garden grows. His entry is *optional* — the engine's dignity is independence.
- **Collides with:** 9 (world anomalies become disasters at High Tide), 13 (the catalog *is* the Classification Office's product), 1 (his investigations *add* entries).
