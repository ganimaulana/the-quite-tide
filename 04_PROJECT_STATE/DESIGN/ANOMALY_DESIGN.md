## 1. The outcome taxonomy

**[PROPOSED]** Every solve attempt resolves into one signing outcome × one duration. The taxonomy is *states*, not just signing moments — sources can move between states (Q403 solution 3).

### Signing outcomes
- **Full:** the impression forms with complete terms. The Rule was understood to falsifiable depth, the prediction test passed, the settlement holds.
- **Partial:** the impression forms **with gaps** — the terms have holes where understanding failed. Invoking *through a gap* is a hazard (the effect executes unpriced or misaimed). Partial impressions are *usable but dangerous* — and gaps can be **closed later** by deeper solving (a partial is a promissory note, not a failure). [INFERENCE] This is the design's answer to Q338: the dangerous remainder is *named, bounded, and closable* — not hand-waved.
- **Fragment:** no impression — only **residue**. A fragment can be *witnessed* (Tide-mark-style: impressions, not video) but never *invoked*. Fragments are the consolation prize of honest failure, and the *only* yield of coerced solving (Q446 solution 2), echo-residue (Q408), and dead sources (Q402/Q403). A fragment can *become* a partial later if the solver returns with deeper understanding — fragments are seeds.
- **Failure:** no impression, no fragment. The phenomenon **notices the attempt** (attention is two-way — ARTIFACT_DESIGN.md §13.2). Failure has a cost: the source's wariness rises (repeat attempts get harder), and in hostile cases the source *acts*.

### Durations
- **Temporary:** the impression expires — after N invocations or one Tide-season, whichever comes first. Most early impressions are temporary: the terms are *rented*.
- **Conditional:** the impression holds **while the terms are kept**. Missing the 03:33 (§7.4) breaks it; violating a Salt Law condition voids it. Conditionality is the default for schedule-contracts and Rule-bindings (premise 8: temporary/conditional/permanent all available [CANON-directive]).
- **Permanent:** the terms are self-sustaining — rare, and "permanent" modifies **the terms**, never the Seal's willingness: discharge (§8, ARTIFACT_DESIGN.md) is always available. Nothing in the system is irrevocable-by-design except kill-scars.

### State transitions
- Counterparty **death or Drowning**: impression → **fragment-only** (Q403 solution 3). The understanding survives; the living terms don't.
- **Gap closure**: partial → full (re-solving the hole).
- **Term breach**: conditional → **suspended** (not destroyed — the terms can be *re-earned*, at a price: the re-earning costs double resonance — the Seal remembers breaches).

## 2. What "solved" means — certification

**[PROPOSED]** "Solve" is hereby given the load-bearing definition the audit demanded (Q336 solution 1, Q441 solution 1):

### The threefold test
1. **The falsifiable statement.** The Rule is written down in a form that can be *wrong* (29.B.11's method: observe → hypothesize → test → pay [CANON]). "The stairwell adds a step for mourners" is a statement; "the stairwell is sad" is not. If it can't be falsified, it can't be solved.
2. **The prediction test.** The stated Rule predicts a *new, unobserved* behavior — which is then observed. The phenomenon is not solved when it is *described*; it is solved when it is *predicted*.
3. **The settlement.** The harmful expression is **addressed** — stopped, bound by terms, or given a harmless substrate (Q056's three options) — *while the Seep persists*. **Solving ≠ decommissioning** (Q336 solution 1 [ADAPT]): the Seep stays; the *harm* is settled; the *terms* are negotiated. The world certifies via **changed or stabilized behavior** — the phenomenon *acts differently afterward*, observably.

### Who certifies (three certifiers, none sufficient alone)
- **The Seal** (mechanical): it forms the impression or it doesn't. The Seal is the *instrument*, not a judge — it cannot be bribed, flattered, or fooled, but it also cannot explain *why* it refused (the refusal is legible — the stone goes cold — but unexplained).
- **The phenomenon** (behavioral): it settles or it doesn't. A phenomenon that keeps harming people after the "solve" was not solved — the world's veto.
- **The Classification Office** (institutional, *later*): filed anomalies that change expression fire **re-audit triggers** (05.4 [ADAPT] — ARTIFACT_DESIGN.md §17 #21). The auditors' eventual arrival — to *thank* him — is scheduled, not hypothetical.

### Evidence standards
- **Firsthand only** (Q344 solution 1): reading Ilsa's notes ≠ understanding. Secondhand material yields fragments at best. No proxy solving: the impression binds to the *solver's* understanding (Q055).
- **Corroboration required** (Q042 solution 1): impression-derived knowledge is **inadmissible** as evidence — to the Compact, to a court, to anyone — without independent corroboration. He can *know* without being able to *prove*. Acting on uncorroborated impression-intel is his characterized failure mode (Q441 solution 2).
- **Veil-edited memory** (Q413 solution 2): memory-impressions degrade in *his* retention within hours unless transcribed — the transcribe-within-hours mini-game. The understanding is his (Loud [CANON]); *sharing* it subjects it to the Veil (Q442 solution 2).
- **Rot-bounded derived evidence** (Q442 solution 1): photos, recordings, and notes of *invoked effects* rot past the depth-pressure product (the T4/≤3h equivalent restated for impressions). Impressions themselves are Membrane-native and do not rot (ARTIFACT_DESIGN.md §7).

### Authority
No authority *grants* contracts. The solver declares; the Seal confirms; the Compact files (later, if reported). The Ledger cannot license him to farm — coercion yields garbage (Q446 solution 2) — and the Compact cannot *order* a solve into existence. **Authority over contracts is mechanical, not bureaucratic.** [INFERENCE] This is why the Seal is ungovernable in the way the Compact fears most: it doesn't disobey orders; orders are *inapplicable*.

## 4. The five-fold anti-auto-solve brake

**[PROPOSED]** (Q439–Q442, Q042, Q440; porting 29.B.5's fallibility [CANON]):

1. **Impressions, not video.** Invoking an information-impression yields *witness-impressions* (Tide-mark limits, 29.B.5–B.11 [KEEP]): fragmentary, perspective-bound, local to the solved Rule. Never identities, motives, or complete histories — **residues and relations** (§9: the Contract #1 counter).
2. **Fallibility.** Misreading is always possible (29.B.5 [CANON]). The Seal confirms *that* an impression formed, never *that the understanding was correct* — a confidently-wrong solve forms a confidently-wrong partial, and the gap is where the story lives.
3. **Scope-lock** (Q440 solution 2): the impression reveals **only what the solved phenomenon encompassed**. Solving the locked room doesn't name the killer; solving the fog doesn't find the ledger. Each impression is a *key to one room*.
4. **Inadmissibility** (Q042 solution 1): uncorroborated impression-intel is a *lead*, not evidence. He must do the mundane work anyway — interview, stakeout, paperwork. The mystery is never auto-solved because *knowing* and *proving* are different currencies.
5. **Budget** (Q042 solution 3 / Q439 solution 2): every investigative invocation **spends resonance**. He must *budget* truth — the question is never "can he know" but "can he afford to know *now*."

## 5. Anti-gaming rules

**[PROPOSED]** — each rule names its exploit and its closure:

1. **Novelty-gating** (Q057 solution 2): the gauge pays for *new understanding only*. Re-solving a filed Rule teaches nothing. Farming the same phenomenon is unprofitable by construction.
2. **The salary ban** (Q066 solution 1): scheduled phenomena pay **once**. The second Punctual Fog teaches nothing — but the *attendance* still costs (schedule-contracts are a leash, not a salary).
3. **Firsthand** (Q344 solution 1): reading ≠ understanding. Rivals who read his files get fragments. No free-riding on solved work.
4. **No proxy solving** (Q055): the impression binds to the *solver's* understanding. A hired solver's solve yields *their* impression (if they had a vessel — they don't; there's one Seal).
5. **The coercion rule** (Q446 solution 2): forced solving yields fragment-only garbage. Farming Arthur is unprofitable. Understanding can't be extracted at gunpoint.
6. **No double-dipping** (Q057): a solved Rule can't be re-solved for yield — by him or anyone. The Classification Office's own records are the anti-farm ledger.
7. **Presence** (Q066 solution 3): impressions require *being there*. No remote contracting, no contracting-by-dossier. The story must move his body.
8. **The Ledger-pipeline ban** (Q446 solution 1): the pipeline escrow covers the *method* (mutually assured disclosure); the coercion rule covers the *man*. Both directions closed.
9. **No filed-farming** (Q048 solution 2): re-solving catalogued anomalies pays ~nothing and fires re-audits — the auditors arrive, which is worse than no payout.
10. **The copy rule** (Q014 solution 2 / Q344 solution 2): voluntary pressed copies are fragment-only, degrading, and ruinously priced. The gray market exists but can't scale.

**[PROPOSED]** (Q454 [NEEDS REDESIGN] answered; Q012; Q015; Q065; Q067; Q340; Q402; Q403; Q408; Q412; Q413):

- The Seal contracts with **Rules**, never with Undertow structures directly (Q454 solution 2): no direct Undertow bargains, ever. The counterparty is the Rule's **Membrane-side expression** — fixed, discoverable (03.1 [CANON]), stable enough to bargain with.
- **"Entities"** [ADAPT] (Q069 solution 1): **sapient anomalies with Membrane-side expression**. The Laggard-thing (L5-2) is explicitly *not* among them — quarantined (Q071 solution 1). Whether the tether-thing is intelligent stays UNKNOWN [KEEP].
- **"Monsters"** = in-world slang (Q011/Q052 solution 3), not an ontological class. Formal class: Organism Seep. Monsters are **Drowned-remnant organisms**: feral, hostile because *unresolved*, not predatory (Q052 solution 1). Every monster is someone who Drowned — "NOT by killing" (premise 6) is load-bearing: you don't kill what's already dead; you *complete* it.
- **Non-sapient sources** yield **Rule-bindings**, not bargains (Q012 solution 1): Arthur binds *himself* to the Rule's conditions — the "contract" is unilateral discipline. Consideration = **Rule-completion** (Q015 solution 1): the Rule "offers" the way a crystal offers geometry; his solving completes an informational symmetry.
- **Phenomena** yield **schedule-contracts** (Q065 solution 2): he binds himself to *attend* — consideration is observance; missing the appointment breaks it (built-in conditionality, premise 8).
- **True bargains** (offer/acceptance) are restricted to **sapient counterparties** — gated late-series (Q340 solution 1: Reactive/None-intel first; sapient contracts require the Asylum Protocol finalized on-page — the personhood frontier as endgame).
- **Hard bans:** V-flag and I-flag sources — the Seal refuses (goes cold; Q067 solution 1). **The dead** cannot be solved (Q402 solution 1) — residue yields fragments only. **Echoes** cannot be stable counterparties (Q408 solution 1). **Memory**: reading permitted, altering forbidden (Q412 solution 1 — the bright line); reads are the counterparty's own memories, Veil-edited (Q413 solution 2). **Time**: mediation throttle (Attuned-scale caps, Q418 solution 1); Tide-mark limits verbatim (Q419 solution 1); derived capabilities only, never the phenomenon itself (Q420 solution 1 — "capability, not control" as a general principle).

## 7. The ten classes

Each class: **05 mapping** · **Rule** · **Behavior** · **Mystery** · **Danger** · **Solution (solve shape)** · **Possible Contract** (grant + price + limits) · **Failure consequence** · **Interesting without a contract** (the class must produce story regardless — directive requirement).

### 7.1 Living entities (05: Organism Seep)

**Exemplar: the Bellwether** — a Drowned-remnant harbor dog (salt-white, wrong number of footfalls) that herds people away from Veil-thin spots along the Ravenhurst waterfront.

- **Rule:** it patrols a *route*, not a territory — the Rule is the route. It interposes itself between pedestrians and thin spots: barking, circling, nipping at heels.
- **Behavior:** at new moon the herding *inverts* — it herds people *toward* the thin spots. The route is the same; the direction reverses.
- **Mystery:** whose dog was it — and what was it herding people away from *before* it Drowned?
- **Danger:** following it on a new moon walks you into a thin spot during High Tide. It is not malicious; it is *unresolved* — the inversion is the unresolved part.
- **Solution:** walk the *full* route with it, at the turning tide, without flinching — completing the patrol the Drowned handler never finished. The settlement: the inversion stops; the route holds year-round.
- **Possible Contract:** schedule-contract — *attendance at the route* (walk it monthly). Grants the **herding-sense**: the dog's agitation as a Veil-thinness detector — phenomenon-specific (Q443: detects *its own kind* — thin spots, nothing else), wards dampen it (Q444), and deep use is louder *at the dog's end* (the dog notices being invoked — the counterparty-echo). Price: the monthly walk (the farm is a leash); per-invocation resonance drain.
- **Failure consequence:** it herds *you* — you become something it moves away from thin spots, i.e., it treats you as a hazard to others. People cross the street when you approach the waterfront. The social cost is the price.
- **Interesting without a contract:** the route *maps* the waterfront's thin spots — a Drowned handler's unfinished patrol is a *chart* of where the Veil is weak, whether or not Arthur ever signs. The mystery (whose dog) is a Ravenhurst history thread independent of the Seal.

### 7.2 Objects (05: Object Seep)

**Exemplar: the Unsent Letters** — a bundle of letters arriving at the night-shift records office, addressed to people who Drowned, postage from decades ago, paper un-aged.

- **Rule:** each letter must be **read aloud once**, by *someone*, before it is filed — or the addressee's name fades from the municipal records (an information-Seep with civic teeth).
- **Behavior:** the letters arrive on no schedule; the bundle is never the same twice. The handwriting is always the *sender's*, never the addressee's.
- **Mystery:** who posts them — and why the records office, why the night shift, why *him*.
- **Danger:** reading aloud **summons the addressee's wake-echo** — a 3-second-stale residue (29.B.5's wake physics) that *notices* being read. It doesn't speak. It *listens*.
- **Solution:** read-and-file: read each letter aloud *once*, in the records room, then file it under the addressee's (fading) name — the Rule wants *witness*, not delivery. The settlement: the names stop fading; the letters stop coming *for those names*.
- **Possible Contract:** impression of the *reading* — grants one **witness-reading** per invocation: the letter-writer's memory of the addressee (Veil-edited — transcribe within hours, Q413; the writer's memories only, Q412). Price: per-reading resonance; the echo notices *every* reading (counterparty-echo — the Drowned listen back). Limits: residues and relations, never identities or motives (§4).
- **Failure consequence:** the echo follows the reader home — a 3-second-stale presence in the apartment that *listens* to everything. It can't hurt him. It can *learn his voice* (cf. §7.8).
- **Interesting without a contract:** the letters are a *Drowned census* — who the city lost, in their own loved ones' handwriting. The records-office frame makes every letter a municipal mystery; the "why the night shift" question is a slow-burn thread.

### 7.3 Locations (05: Place Seep)

**Exemplar: the Stairwell That Counts** — a municipal apartment stairwell in Ravenhurst's harbor district where the step-count between floors changes.

- **Rule:** the stairwell adds **one step per unresolved grief** currently inside the building. The extra step is always the *seventh*.
- **Behavior:** the step is climbable — and climbers report the stairwell *smelling like rain*. No one has ever counted the same number twice on a mourning day.
- **Mystery:** what the building is grieving *for* — the Drowning-year records are sealed (the 72-hour clock's older cousin: a records gap).
- **Danger:** climbing the extra step **descends** you — you arrive at the landing, but the landing is the building *in its Drowning year*. The stairwell files *you* as a mourner. Coming back up requires someone *outside* to call your name.
- **Solution:** map the grief — identify, floor by floor, what each extra step is *for* — and hold the building's first memorial in thirty years. The step is a *memorial*; the Rule wants *acknowledgment*. The settlement: the count stabilizes; the extra step appears only on the memorial day, *as an invitation*.
- **Possible Contract:** place-attendance — grants **safe passage** (the stairwell never descends him) + the **counting-sense** (detects unresolved grief in a building — narrow, building-scoped). Price: attendance at the annual memorial (conditional — miss it and the passage voids); per-use resonance.
- **Failure consequence:** the stairwell files him as a mourner — *his* grief gets a step. Every building he enters afterward counts *him*. The condition spreads by *carrying*.
- **Interesting without a contract:** the sealed Drowning-year records are a municipal conspiracy thread; the floor-by-floor grief map is a *social* mystery (who in the building is mourning what) that needs no Seal at all.

### 7.4 Phenomena (05: Phenomenon Seep)

**Exemplar: the 03:33** — a phantom last-train announcement at Ravenhurst Station, nightly at 03:33, boarding no one. The platform is empty; the announcement is not.

- **Rule:** it is the station's **missed departures** made audible — every train someone *almost* caught, every goodbye *almost* said, folded into one announcement.
- **Behavior:** the announcement names no destination. The departure board shows no train. The station's cats sit facing the empty track.
- **Mystery:** why 03:33 — and why the announcement *pauses*, some nights, as if waiting for an answer.
- **Danger:** answering it — speaking, stepping forward, *boarding* — puts you **on the schedule**. The 03:33 expects you nightly thereafter. It is a time-phenomenon wearing a train's manners.
- **Solution:** *not boarding*, every night, for one Tide-season — the consideration is **observance** (Q065 solution 2). The settlement: the pauses stop; the announcement runs clean; the phenomenon is *witnessed* without being *joined*.
- **Possible Contract:** schedule-contract — *attendance* (be present, do not board). Grants the **missed-departures hearing**: detects unresolved *transit* — people about to miss what matters — station-scoped, phenomenon-specific (Q443). Price: the nightly attendance (the leash); per-hearing resonance. **Viewing only, never triggering** (Q064 solution 1): he can *hear* missed departures; he cannot *summon* the 03:33.
- **Failure consequence:** he's on the roster — the 03:33 expects him nightly, and missing a night *costs*: the announcement names *his* missed departure, aloud, on the platform. The phenomenon collects.
- **Interesting without a contract:** the pause — *what is it waiting for?* — is a pure mystery hook. The station's cats, the night staff who've heard it for years, the *almost*-goodbyes: the phenomenon is a machine for generating human stories.

### 7.5 Rules (05: Structure Seep)

**Exemplar: the Salt Law of Koura Pier** — a local standing Rule: *no one drowns within sight of the salt-storehouse between tides.* It has held for sixty years. It is folk knowledge; the fishermen don't discuss it with outsiders.

- **Rule:** the Law holds **by taking something else** — a boat's catch rots overnight; a marriage fails within the year; a debt comes due early. The price is denominated differently (02.I [CANON]) — never in the currency you'd choose.
- **Behavior:** the Law is *tidal* — it holds between tides and *rests* at slack water. At slack water the pier is just a pier.
- **Mystery:** who set it — and whether the setter *knew* the price, or is still paying it.
- **Danger:** the Law doesn't care *who* pays. A drowning averted for a stranger can bill *you* — proximity to the Law's working is proximity to its ledger.
- **Solution:** understand the price and **pay it knowingly** — the Law wants *acknowledged* payment, not ignorant luck. The settlement: the terms become *legible* (a knowing community can budget the Law — the fishermen's oral ledger, written down for the first time).
- **Possible Contract:** Rule-binding (Q012 solution 1) — Arthur binds *himself* to the Salt Law's conditions: within its bounds and tides, the Law's protection covers him — **and its price can bill him**. Grants: Drowning-immunity, pier-bounded, tide-bounded. Price: the Law's ledger (denominated differently — the story prices each rescue); per-invocation resonance; the binding is *conditional* (violate the Law's customs — disrespect the storehouse, fish at slack water — and the protection voids *retroactively to the last tide*).
- **Failure consequence:** the price falls on someone else — and Arthur *knows* whose catch rotted. The Law's indifference is the horror: it saved a life and billed a stranger, and the solver understands the ledger well enough to read the name.
- **Interesting without a contract:** the sixty-year oral ledger is an economic mystery (who paid, what it cost, who kept the books); the setter's identity is a Second Silence thread; the *ethics* of a protection that bills bystanders needs no Seal to be a story.

### 7.6 Events (05: Event Seep)

**Exemplar: the Lantern Recall** — every Obon, one extra lantern floats in Ravenhurst Bay. No family claims it. The harbor office logs it annually and does nothing.

- **Rule:** it is the **unmourned** — the Drowned with no one left to float a lantern for. The lantern is *theirs*, floated by no one.
- **Behavior:** it drifts against the current, toward the harbor mouth — toward the open water, toward the Drowning sites. It burns out at dawn.
- **Mystery:** *which* unmourned — the Bay has a century of Drowned, and the lantern is only ever one.
- **Danger:** touching it shows you **how they Drowned** — a cognitive hazard (grief as a vector; the Drowning is *witnessed*, 3-seconds-stale, and the witness *keeps* it). The hazard is not death; it's *carrying*.
- **Solution:** float a lantern *for* them — a proxy mourning, with the name spoken aloud (the name must be *found* first — the investigation is the price). The settlement: that year's lantern doesn't come; *another* unmourned takes its place next Obon. The event is not ended — it is *served*.
- **Possible Contract:** event-attendance — grants one **unmourned-sense** per Obon: detects the unmourned dead — *one name*, seasonal, Bay-scoped. Price: the annual proxy mourning (conditional); per-sense resonance. Limits: the sense finds the *unmourned*, never the *murdered* or the *missing* — scope-lock (§4.3).
- **Failure consequence:** the lantern is *for you* — the echo-misidentification hazard: the unmourned *names* the solver, and the Bay starts expecting *his* lantern. He must be mourned-by-proxy to clear it — someone must float *his*.
- **Interesting without a contract:** the one-lantern-at-a-time queue is a *triage* mystery (who decides the order?); each year's unmourned is a self-contained Drowning investigation; the proxy-mourning frame is an engine for Ravenhurst's hidden history.

### 7.7 Memories (05: Memory Seep)

**Exemplar: the School Song** — Ravenhurst North High's pre-merger school song. Alumni who sing it *remember a school that never existed*: a building, a year, classmates with faces.

- **Rule:** the song is a **memory-medium** — an information Seep carried by culture (05.4 [CANON]: information seeps propagate through media). The remembered school is a Drowned school's *unresolved term* — a place that ended mid-semester.
- **Behavior:** one singing = nostalgia. Two singings = *enrollment*: the singer's records gain a year at the drowned school (municipal records, alumni rolls, a yearbook photo with one face too many).
- **Mystery:** which school — the merger records are complete, and no school is missing. The Drowned school was never *built* — so what Drowned?
- **Danger:** the second verse enrolls you. Your diploma is now from nowhere. The Veil edits *around* the enrollment (your employer remembers the drowned school's name).
- **Solution:** sing it **once, in full, with the alumni choir** — the Rule wants *communal* witness (the consideration is shared, not solitary — one singer is a victim; a choir is a memorial). The settlement: the song keeps its nostalgia; the enrollment stops.
- **Possible Contract:** memory-impression — grants the **choir's memory** of the drowned school: one building, one year, scoped (Q440). **Veil-edited** (Q413 solution 2): transcribe within hours or lose it. The writer's — the *choir's* — memories only (Q412). Price: per-recall resonance; each recall risks *enrollment-echo* (the second recall in a week enrolls the *memory* — he starts remembering classmates). Limits: impressions, not video; fallible; inadmissible (§4).
- **Failure consequence:** enrolled — a year at a school that never existed, in records that now say it did. Clearing it requires the choir to sing *him* out (proxy completion — cf. §7.6).
- **Interesting without a contract:** the never-built school is a *planning-records* mystery (who designed a school that was never built — and what Drowned instead?); the alumni choir is a social engine; the enrollment mechanic is body-horror-by-paperwork.

### 7.8 Environmental (05: cross-class — Place/Phenomenon at ambient scope)

**Exemplar: the Low-Tide Quiet** — on certain low tides, Ravenhurst's harbor goes *too quiet*: no gulls, no engines, no water-slap — a sound-shaped hole roughly a kilometer across.

- **Rule:** it is the **Undertow listening** — the Veil at its thinnest (03.1's membrane permeability, tidal [CANON]). The Quiet is not an absence; it is *attention*.
- **Behavior:** the Quiet has *edges* — you can walk into it (the sound of your footsteps stops at the boundary). The old fishermen know: *don't ring the bell at low tide* (folk heuristic — 03.2 [CANON]: folk containment heuristics are data).
- **Mystery:** what it's listening *for* — and whether the Quiet is *one* listener or the *shape* of listening.
- **Danger:** making **new** sound inside the Quiet *teaches* it your voice. Later — days later, elsewhere — your phone rings with **your own voice** giving wrong directions. The Quiet *repeats*, wrong.
- **Solution:** **keep the Quiet** — the settlement is *observance*: no new sound, no bells, no engines, until the tide turns. The town already knows; the solver's job is *enforcement* (stopping the developer's pile-driving barge — the Quiet vs. the harbor expansion is a *political* mystery).
- **Possible Contract:** environmental-attendance — grants the **Quiet-sense**: hears when the Veil thins — ambient, harbor-scoped, phenomenon-specific (Q443). Price: observance (conditional — make new sound in the Quiet and the sense *inverts*: it hears *you*, always); per-use resonance. Limits: the sense detects *thinning*, never *what's on the other side* (scope-lock).
- **Failure consequence:** it learns your voice — the wrong-directions calls. The calls *escalate*: they start naming people you know. Clearing it requires a *tide of silence* — one full Quiet with no sound from him at all, while it calls.
- **Interesting without a contract:** the harbor-expansion fight is a civic/political engine (money vs. the Quiet); the wrong-voice calls are a horror engine independent of the Seal; "what it's listening for" is a series-level thread (the Undertow's attention has an *object*).

### 7.9 Conceptual (05: Concept Seep)

**Exemplar: the Unsaid Thing** — in one Ravenhurst shipping office, a specific *apology* can never be completed. Speakers trail off; meetings adjourn; phones die; the words *reorder themselves*. The concept of *that apology* is Seeped.

- **Rule:** a Drowned manager's **unfinished business** made conceptual (05.4: concept seeps [CANON]). The concept wants *completion* — its grammar is missing its object.
- **Behavior:** the blockage is *specific*: other apologies work fine. Only *that* apology — to *that* bereaved family, for *that* safety failure — cannot be spoken.
- **Mystery:** what the safety failure *was* — the company's records are clean, which is itself the evidence (a clean record around a Drowning is a *filed* record — someone filed it).
- **Danger:** the concept **spreads by understanding** — investigating it *closely enough to understand it* risks colonization: your own unfinished business becomes unsayable. It generalizes.
- **Solution:** **say it for them** — a proxy apology, delivered to the bereaved, in the Drowned manager's name, with the company's acknowledgment. Completing the concept's *grammar* settles it. The settlement: the apology becomes sayable — and *said*.
- **Possible Contract:** **NONE AVAILABLE.** The Seal **refuses** — the concept's spread-by-understanding profile trips the I-flag-adjacent refusal (Q067 solution 1: hard ban, reader-policeable). The stone goes cold. *This is the point.*
- **Failure consequence:** colonization — the solver's own unfinished business becomes unsayable. He can't apologize to his mother, his sister, his dead teacher. The condition is *generalized* and *personal*.
- **Interesting without a contract:** this is the class's thesis — **some Rules you serve, not sign**. The safety-failure cover-up is a corporate mystery; the proxy apology is a human story; the colonization hazard is a horror engine. The Seal's refusal *is* the scene's climax: the one time the artifact that solves everything says *no* — and the reader learns what the refusals are *for*.

### 7.10 Dimensional (05: Structure/Time cross-class)

**Exemplar: the Locker Bank** — Ravenhurst Station's coin lockers include one **unlisted number**. It opens onto *yesterday's* lost-property office: same room, same clerk (younger), same ledger — one day behind.

- **Rule:** it is a **fold**, not a door (03.3: geometry has seams [CANON]). The fold connects *places*, not times — but one of the places is *yesterday's* office, because the office's *informational symmetry* (lost things, kept) is stronger than its clock.
- **Behavior:** the fold is *stable* — it has held for years. The yesterday-clerk does not notice the discrepancy (the Veil edits around the fold — the clerk remembers *two* yesterdays and minds neither).
- **Mystery:** who built the fold — the fold's *seam-work* matches Second Silence surveyor technique (the Theory A thread — ARTIFACT_DESIGN.md §3: the origin mystery surfaces *here*, in a locker bank, not in a lore dump).
- **Danger:** the fold **keeps what you leave** — it files you as lost property. Leave a bag: the bag is *yesterday's* now. Leave *yourself* too long: someone must **claim** you — and the claimant must describe you accurately, or the fold keeps you.
- **Solution:** **claim something** — the fold wants *custody resolved*: lost property claimed or formally abandoned, in the ledger, in triplicate. The settlement: the fold stabilizes (it stops *keeping*); the yesterday-clerk gets a memo he can't quite read.
- **Possible Contract:** dimensional-attendance — grants **fold-sense** (detects folds — narrow, structure-specific, Q443) + one **retrieval** per invocation: reach yesterday's lost property — *attended*, never remote (presence rule, §5.7); the retrieval is *claimed*, never stolen (the fold's Rule-condition: custody resolved). Price: per-retrieval resonance (steep — time-adjacent); the fold notices *every* retrieval (counterparty-echo — and the yesterday-clerk starts leaving *notes*). Limits: yesterday only (Tide-mark depth cap, Q419); impressions, not video; derived capability, never the fold itself (Q420 solution 1).
- **Failure consequence:** filed as lost property — *he* is yesterday's now. Someone must claim him, describing him accurately. The claimant who knows him best is his mother — and she must describe the son she *has*, not the son she *wants* (the claim is a *truth* test).
- **Interesting without a contract:** the Second Silence seam-work is the origin-mystery's on-ramp; the yesterday-clerk's double memories are a character engine; the claim-mechanics are a ready-made rescue plot.

## 8. Composite impressions (cross-class)

**[PROPOSED]** Two faces pressed together invoke **Rule interference** (04.7 [CANON]) — the composite-vector space the Compact refused to build (05.7 [CANON]).

- **The principle:** composites are *discovered*, not granted. Each pairing must be learned through testing; each carries the hazard warning (ARTIFACT_DESIGN.md §8).
- **Design constraints:** (a) composites of *banned* classes (concept/I-flag) are refused; (b) composites are **louder** than their components (tier-mirroring stacks — a T2+T2 composite reads at T3: visibility as the soft cap); (c) composite terms are *renegotiated* — the original contracts' terms don't automatically compose (the interference must be *solved* as its own phenomenon — composites are recursively subject to the threefold test, §2).
- **The payoff** (premise 10: "outside conventional classification"): a solved composite is *literally unclassifiable* under 05 — the Meridian Vector has no axis for it. The Classification Office's eventual encounter with a composite is the institutional sublime: their instrument says *nothing*, and the nothing is *accurate*.
- **Exploit closure:** composites can't be brute-forced (each pairing is its own solve, with its own price); they can't be stockpiled (two faces per composite — the opportunity cost is a face); they can't be hidden (louder than components).

## 9. The Contract #1 counter pattern

**[RECOMMENDATION]** (Contract #1's specific design belongs to the CONTRACT_DESIGN worker; this section specifies what it **must** satisfy — Q042's counter, premises 8–9):

1. **Weak in combat:** the impression's grant must be **non-combative** — detection, witnessing, passage, or attendance. No damage, no restraint, no direct confrontation utility.
2. **Important for investigation:** the grant must open an *investigative* door — a sense, a witness-mode, or a record-access that makes the next mystery *approachable* without making it *solvable*.
3. **Must not auto-solve:** the grant yields **residues and relations, never identities, motives, or complete histories** (§4). It must be **fallible, scope-locked, inadmissible without corroboration, and resonance-priced per use**.
4. **Temporary or conditional** (premise 8): Contract #1 should be **temporary** (expires) or **conditional** (attendance-bound) — never permanent. The first contract *ends*, teaching discharge (§1) early.
5. **Novelty-gated by construction:** its source should be **unfiled underbrush** (Q048 solution 2) — below reporting thresholds, so the solve doesn't fire re-audits prematurely.
6. **The First Invoice** (ARTIFACT_DESIGN.md §6.4): Contract #1's yield **pays the wake-debt** — Arthur's first solve earns him *nothing but the loosened face*. The counter-design must make this dramatically legible: he solves something real, pays everything, and holds an empty hand with a warm stone in it.
