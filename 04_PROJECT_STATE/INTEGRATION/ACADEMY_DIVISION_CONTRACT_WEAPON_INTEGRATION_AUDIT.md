## 3. Core-Question Verdict: YES — with five structural fences

**A low-prestige Division can plausibly serve as the foundation of Arthur's later broad weapon knowledge without becoming a secret power source.** The plausibility is not a single claim; it is the conjunction of five independently audited fences, any one of which would break the integration if it failed:

1. **The Foster guardrail (counterfactual).** Daniel Foster (IR-08) — Division Logistics, Rank F, Ability none, Irregular-classified **[CANON]** — has had the same exposure longer with no weapon-master trajectory. A power source that fails to power its other inhabitants is not a power source. **PASS.**

2. **The skill firewall (causal).** KNOWLEDGE ≠ SKILL holds across all nine earlier proposals and the Academy reconciliation: practicum never grants proficiency; handling familiarity is declarative, fighting is embodied; combat skill is priced in the world's budget (hours, favors, injury). *Logistics teaches what weapons are; nothing in it teaches what to do with one.* A Division that structurally cannot grant the decisive competence cannot be a power source. **PASS.**

3. **Contract independence (ontological).** Contract progression (LOCK 8) and institutional status are separate tracks. Contract #1 (THE POLITE KNOCK) is the zero-medium boundary — the Division gives it nothing, its rules untouched by quartermaster work. Logistics can only ever feed the *hypothesis* layer of future medium-validity discovery; the phenomenon under test is the sole certifier. **PASS.**

4. **Pattern independence (visual).** The body pattern is fed only by verified Mastery (falsifiable, recorded-before-test, world-confirmed discoveries). Depot work and inventory counts produce no strokes and no clarity. The Division cannot inflate the visible record. **PASS.**

5. **The decision guardrail (attributive).** The reader must point to *decisions* — seeking instruction, paying in hours and injury, choosing breadth when specialization was advised — as the cause, with the Division as setting. Setting is not cause. An authorial discipline, not a structural guarantee — the weakest fence, but an explicitly designed one. **COMPATIBLE.**

**The conditionality, stated plainly:** fences 1–4 hold *under the Option B hypothesis*. The final Division is undecided; nothing here adopts Logistics. Under Option A the same question is answered differently (records/information breadth native, equipment breadth only via the mixed-exercise bridge) — the decision comparison's territory, not this audit's.

### Notes on the non-obvious verdicts

**Audits 1 & 2 (exposure; maintenance).** Logistics explains *proximity*, not *knowledge*: the Division supplies the data (which weapons pass through, which parts wear first, which failure shapes recur); the recognition — connecting a failure pattern to a procedural cause — is Arthur's, and it can be wrong. Exposure is the ceiling; observation is his. The function inventory is [PROPOSED] throughout; equipment-side documentation only (archives/incident records/manuals are **not** Logistics functions). **COMPATIBLE**, not PASS — the inventory itself awaits adoption.

**Audit 3 (non-specialist).** The strongest structural verdict in the set: no path inside the Division's documented functions reaches combat instruction, issuance, or certification — and even an extended inventory could not cross the KNOWLEDGE ≠ SKILL firewall (audited across all six progression proposals, now extended to the Academy layer). A Division that cannot grant the competence cannot produce the specialist. **PASS** under the hypothesis; the firewall is canon-compatible regardless of option.

**Audit 4 (misunderstanding).** The four documented misreadings (unfocused, overstudying, compensating, jack of all trades) fit the locked social machinery and the fence discipline: the true reason (Contracts cannot be freely chosen, so broad medium competence is required) is never stated or hinted at through ruleset/slot/channel-clause vocabulary during the Logistics chapters. The misunderstanding must cost him on-page, or the later reveal reads as retcon. **COMPATIBLE** — designed; execution is prose.

**Audit 5 (low status).** Delivered at two independent altitudes: Division-level (middle-low, lowest public prestige of the conventional divisions — [PROPOSED]) and classification-level (L5-2's Grade-3 routing follows the cadet inside any Division — [CANON]). Cosmetic conflict: the three-tier scheme places Logistics at MIDDLE (reconciliation §12.6) — a scale disagreement, not a status collapse. **PASS.**

**Audit 6 (reversal).** The architecture makes the trajectory *possible* without making it *inevitable*: most Logistics cadets pass through the same exposure and become nothing of the sort (Foster). The decisive inputs — cross-Division instruction (priced in favors and exposure), world-certified Mastery, cost experience — are all outside the Division. The career layer adds compatible machinery: Intelligence recognition without transfer, and the stamped-cadet constraint designed to be *surpassed* as a political event. **COMPATIBLE.**

**Audit 7 (vocational subplot).** The governing rule — *Logistics appears in missions, not in the depot* — plus plant→dormancy→payoff and the quantitative guardrails (no depot-centered chapter; one or two one-line plants per early chapter) is the strongest available design answer to the heaviest narrative risk. But discipline is not proof. **PROPOSED** — designed, not verified.

**Audit 14 (trajectory without early inevitability).** The four "Master of Weapon" capacities decompose onto the separation map, and the Division supplies the decisive input for *none*: Selection (Division: property data; Missions: judgment under pressure) · Adaptation (Division: novelty penalty lowered; world's budget: embodied adjustment) · Rule exploitation (Division: mundane vocabulary; Contract: ruleset; testing: validity) · Restraint (Division: the quartermaster's habit; Cost: the wisdom to decline). Every arrow after "familiarity" depends on systems outside the Division — the Logistics candidate's own honest chain. **COMPATIBLE** — fenced by the trajectory-not-status doctrine (no ceremony, no announcement, wrong decisions punished), not structurally guaranteed.

### 5.3 The pattern layer — fence re-audited at the Academy seam

This is the interface where a "secret power source" leak would most plausibly appear (a Division feeding a visible progression display). The audit confirms it cannot:

- The pattern is fed **only** by verified Mastery. Depot work, maintenance rotations, inventory counts, and equipment familiarity produce no strokes and no clarity — certification belongs to the phenomenon, not the duty roster.
- The pattern records **propositions, never media**: the weapon proposal's recommended reading (validity propositions as minor connective strokes) and the pattern proposal's fence (motifs encode ruleset terms, never weapon-glyphs; validity is never read off skin) are compatible on the careful reading the earlier audit established — what may be recorded is the *proposition* ("this class is a valid channel"), never the object, and never as a discovery source. The conservative alternative (Repository-only) remains offered. **AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED** — restated in §6; no conflict as written.
- Toll = FELT / pattern = SEEN preserved; chronic symptoms belong to Toll exclusively. Cost never *adds* motif content — it weathers, burns, or dims what understanding built.
- **Result: PASS.** The Division cannot inflate, accelerate, or meter the record — it cannot be a secret power source at the visual layer either.

### 5.4 Graduation/career proposal × Contract/Weapon architecture

- **The IO ladder / graduation interface:** COMPATIBLE. Career proposal §7.4 — the ladder is curriculum-internal, graduation is Academy-exit; graduation *reads the ladder's documentary output as evidence*, never re-triggering or re-scoring a rung. Holds under either Division option.

- **Stamped-cadet constraint:** COMPATIBLE. The initial recommendation constraint (records/archives/support tracks) is the graduation-scale expression of L5-2 — and the rivalry file's own note anticipates its surpassing as a *political event*: the institutional form of reversal, won in the mission record, not the depot.
