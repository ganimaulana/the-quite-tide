## B) FIVE-LAYER SEPARATION (five core + two satellites)

Each layer: what it measures · what causes change · what it does NOT measure · what can increase it · what CANNOT increase it · reversibility · ceiling · visibility. Then layer-confusion flags.

### LAYER 1 — ESPER POWER (conventional)

- **Measures:** conventional channeling capacity — a person's Awakening-derived output through a fixed discipline. For institutions this is certified as Rank (Layer 2); the layer itself is the ontological fact (Hollow Pattern → Resonance → channeling), not the paperwork.
- **What causes change:** Awakening (one-way, permanent — A1); training in efficiency/discipline hygiene; Erosion accrual degrades usable output. Per `POWER_SCALING_LADDER.md:20`, old-bible 30.1: "Tier measures sustainable output, not skill, not danger, not destiny."
- **Does NOT measure:** skill, intelligence, preparation, danger, destiny, competence, character, contract holdings, information position. (`POWER_SCALING_LADDER.md:21`: "Rank is not combat power, not worth, not danger, not destiny — 30.1's doctrine, kept.")
- **What CAN increase it:** nothing in Arthur's case — `[CARRIED FORWARD — LOCK 8/LOCK 3]`: he never Awakens, never channels, never accrues Fathoms. For others: Awakening is the sole acquisition event; no artificial Awakening (`MASTER_WORLD_BIBLE.md:49`).
- **What CANNOT increase it:** Contracts (mediated execution is a different ontological slot — A3); Rank changes (Rank certifies, doesn't create); institutional promotions; the artifact ("a granted physique violates 'never secretly OP'" — `ARTHUR_CHARACTER_BIBLE.md:23`); understanding alone (per the identity rule, understanding buys contracts and information position, never channeling output).
- **Reversible:** No. Awakening is one-way; Erosion is irreversible (`AUDIT/Q001_050.md:409`: "Erosion is irreversible"; canon's only permanent supernatural facts are scars).
- **Ceiling:** priced by the Erosion arithmetic (T4 ≈ Drowning by 60; T5 ≈ 20 years; T6 ≈ 5–10; T7 ≈ 2–5 — `MASTER_WORLD_BIBLE.md:39`). No numeric output ceiling is stated; the ceiling is temporal/biological. `[PROPOSED]`: do not invent a numeric power scale — none exists in the tree.
- **Visibility:** to institutions via instruments (MRI Hollow Pattern, Lantern examiners T3+ for pattern work — Q007, Seismograph); to readers via Rank letters and filed Tiers; to characters via reputation + instrument readings. Arthur's reading: zero output, correctly reported ERROR.
