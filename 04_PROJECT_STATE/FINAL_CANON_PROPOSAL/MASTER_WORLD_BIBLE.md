## 1. THE PREMISE — `[REWRITE]` of 01's first paragraph

`[CANON/KEEP]` The world is modern, present-day equivalent. The supernatural is real and deadly. The world does not care about the protagonist. Powers are legible and priced. No resurrection. No free lunch. Competence is distributed — every faction has smart people.

`[REMOVE]` The old blanket sentence: *"the supernatural is hidden from ordinary society but known to governments, corporations, militaries, intelligence agencies, religions, and wealthy families."* It is retired as an absolute — not because the masquerade ends, but because the Lagos divergence (EVENT-089) and the Disclosure Protocol (2024) moved it one layer deeper.

`[PROPOSED]` **The two-tier masquerade (the one-line resolution):** *Persons are public; phenomena are secret.* Since August 2024, Esper individuals and F–S ranks are declassified public knowledge under the amended Meridian Accords; Seeps, anomalies, the Undertow, the Tide mechanics, Erosion's true arithmetic, and the Compact's existence remain Veiled and classified. The masquerade did not end — it retreated one layer.

`[PROPOSED]` **The one-line test:** *If the public can put a face on it, it is Tier 1 (public). If the public can only film a smear the Veil eats, it stays Tier 2 (secret).* A T5 Esper doing sponsored content is Tier 1. The thing she contained at 3 a.m. is Tier 2, and always will be.

Rationale `[INFERENCE]`: the audit (Q346/Q350/Q351/Q355) converged on declassifying *personnel* while reaffirming *phenomena* secrecy as the only resolution that keeps the fast webnovel face-slap engine (public F-rank contempt) AND the old bible's deepest machinery (Seeps, Tide, Erosion, Compact) intact. The weightlessness thesis survives for phenomena (Q351 solution 1); Esper encounters are weighty but *unconvincing by design* — evaluations are closed-door, broadcasts post-produced (Q308/Q345 solutions 2).

## 3. THE ESPER PHENOMENON AND THE F–S RANK SYSTEM

### 3.1 What awakening IS — `[KEEP]`, and what it ISN'T

`[CANON/KEEP]` Awakening = the Hollow Pattern: a permanent, one-way neurological event. First Tide exposure before 18 is the classic trigger; no artificial Awakening (`04.1`). Rank/awakening is not heritable, not chosen, not trainable (Q019/Q020/Q330 solutions 1 — carry the canon rejections forward explicitly).

`[PROPOSED/ADAPT]` **Arthur's case (proposal premise 2, corrected):** Arthur Reed does NOT undergo an Awakening. His September 2024 evaluation **[SUPERSEDED — LOCK 2 T-06: assessment on 25 August 2024, age 17; C-14-09 repair 2026-09-23]** returns **ERROR** because the artifact — an Object Seep in sustained physical contact — corrupts the evaluation's pattern-reading. The instrument reports *"signal present, pattern absent,"* which ERC Regulation 7.3's error table maps to **Rank F** ("no certifiable Resonance; classification: known-wrong"). The public paperwork reads `Rank F — Ability: ERROR / UNDEFINED`. Behind it, the Quiet Desk opens an **Unknown watchlist file** (dual filing, Q034 solution 1): the dismissive F is procedural; the Unknown is real. Re-audit in 5 years (the ticking clock).

`[INFERENCE]` Why this is the load-bearing choice (Q006): it preserves `29`'s neurology section 100% (no Hollow Pattern, zero Fathoms — the 2020 Fathom-0 scare becomes foreshadowing, not a retcon), preserves `04.1`'s no-artificial-Awakening rule (the artifact didn't Awaken him; it *broke the test*), and preserves the reboot's own identity rule — "everyone else becomes stronger by awakening powers; Arthur becomes stronger by understanding things that should not exist."

### 3.2 The F–S public rank system — `[PROPOSED]` (new institution, 2024)

`[PROPOSED]` **Institution:** the Esper Rank Certification (ERC), administered in Albion by the SMD's new public arm under a Compact-owned standard created by the August 2024 Disclosure Protocol. The scale is **F, E, D, C, B, A, S** (seven bands). There is no SSS tier in-world — the proposal's "never becomes SSS" promise is re-anchored: *he is never certified S; his capability sits outside the scale entirely* (Q429 solution 2).

`[PROPOSED]` **What ranks measure (Q027 solution 1 — genuinely new information):** ranks measure **certified safe deployable output** = output × control × discipline hygiene × legal compliance. They do NOT measure raw output. This is why the scale doesn't duplicate Tiers and why F-for-ERROR is procedurally honest: an unmeasurable signal cannot be certified deployable.

`[PROPOSED]` **Published Tier mapping (Q031 solution 1):** F ≈ T0–T1 unrated/unmeasurable · E ≈ T2 · D ≈ T3 · C ≈ T4 · B ≈ T5 · A ≈ T6-equivalent certified · S ≈ exceptional national designation (rare, roughly public-facing T5). Mapping is published with caveats; the public scale is coarse by design.

`[PROPOSED]` **Coverage (Q018 solution 2):** Albion folds rank evaluation into employment screening for Threshold-adjacent jobs (extending the existing employer-mediated Census). Arthur, employed at NQA (Threshold-adjacent insurer), is evaluated in September 2024 **[SUPERSEDED — LOCK 2 T-06: assessment on 25 August 2024, age 17; clerk-era phase-3 material; C-14-09 repair 2026-09-23]** — the evaluation is *mandatory*, not sought. F-ranks are **registered but unhandled** — below the T3 handling threshold (Q030 solution 1: a clean port of old Threshold-Registry logic).

`[PROPOSED]` **What Rank F concretely denies** (designed here; Q229 audited but not independently read): licensed Tide-combat deployment; handler assignment (the leash's paperwork without the leash — the camouflage); guild mission-board access above menial tier; rank-indexed hazard-pay multipliers; academy admission; public Erosion-care coverage; evidentiary credibility (his reports file as "F-rank witness statements," administratively dismissible). What F **grants**: registration, mandatory safety education (the remedial track — where instructors become briefing officers), and the right to be ignored.

`[PROPOSED]` **Stigma (Q028 solutions 1+2):** F-shame is subcultural, not civilizational — it lives in Esper institutions and Threshold-adjacent workplaces, where the stigma is *wasted potential* ("evaluated and found empty"). Ordinary civilians mostly don't care. Rank fraud exists and is policed; Arthur's ERROR is non-reproducible (artifact-interference), which is exactly what the Quiet Desk watches for (Q029).

`[PROPOSED]` **Decay and verification (Q311/Q309):** ranks expire and require re-verification by rotated examiners (anti-aristocracy preserved from old canon's anti-power-sponge doctrine). Verification is ritual-physical — in-person witnessed trials; video is souvenir only (Q309 solution 1). S-ranks burn out publicly; Erosion-as-spectacle darkens the tone (Q315 solution 1); the Court captures academies and guilds via funding (Q315 solution 2).

### 3.3 Register split — `[PROPOSED]` (Q352 solution 2)
Two vocabularies, two truths: the **public register** says *Esper, F–S ranks, guilds* (revived 1940s pulp term, chosen by the Disclosure-era style guide to avoid clinical language). The **covert register** keeps *Attuned, T0–T7, Fathoms*. Civilians who say "Attuned" mark themselves as knowing too much.

### 3.4 Loudness — `[KEEP]` + interaction rule `[PROPOSED]`
`[CANON/KEEP]` Loudness (`04.4`, `29`): 2–4% of humans retain Seep-event memory; it is necessary but not sufficient; it is why Arthur is brilliant *and* Loud (Q414 solution 1). `[PROPOSED]` **Interaction rule (Q347):** *his intellect organizes what his Loudness retains; neither replaces the other.* Loudness is the sensor; the clerk's method is the processor.

### 3.5 The second phenomenon: artifact-mediated Rule-licensing — `[PROPOSED]`
Defined here; full mechanics in §6 and RULESETS.md. `[PROPOSED]` Arthur is a **Rule-bearer**, not a channeler: he holds licenses to invoke Seep Rules via the artifact. This is *mediated execution*, sitting alongside channeling as a named ontological slot. Because he channels nothing, `04.2`'s Erosion logic is correctly absent — the old physics is untouched, and the reboot's anti-copy lock (Q433) has a clean foundation: **living persons' Resonances are not contractable; the artifact stores only Rules that exist independent of a living mind** (Q434 solution 1, mind-independence criterion).

`[PROPOSED]` **Exclusivity (Q026 solution 1):** his moat is *method + data + artifact* jointly — the clerk's-eye method, Loud recall, tether-fused Seep data, and artifact conversion. No single element is exclusive (competence stays distributed, `02.VII`); the combination is unreplicable.

### 3.6 The two outsides — `[PROPOSED]` (Q004 solution 1)
The Laggard is outside classification because it has **no output** (a window). The Contracts are outside because they are **composite and portable** (a keyring the Meridian Vector refused to build — the defeated composite proposal, `05.7`). Same filing, different reasons. The artifact's filing is MV-T0/C-A ("a box that does nothing" — the Laggard strategy, Q044 solution 1), misfiled under a colonial-era notation with a 1997 footnote ("Willowmere household, inert curio, no action") — the planted trace (Q335 solution 2).
