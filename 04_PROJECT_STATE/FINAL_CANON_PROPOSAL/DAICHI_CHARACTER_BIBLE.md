## 2. Backstory — reconciling the genius with the clerk

### 2.1 The contradiction, stated plainly

[CANON] Old Arthur: "present, adequate, forgettable" transcript; no club history; lanky from night shifts; loses futsal to Saitō. [PROPOSED] New Arthur: extremely high intelligence, physically gifted base, disciplined, combat-*survivable*. Q382 names this a [CONTRADICTION]; Q231 names the user's constraint (he must not suddenly become a different person); Q093 demands the traits have a source. This bible resolves it with **trained self-erasure + latent base + earned capability** — not secret training, not an Artifact-granted body (both rejected per Q232: secret training violates the ordinary-man premise; a granted physique violates "never secretly OP").

### 2.2 [PROPOSED] The trained self-erasure

Arthur's intellect was real from childhood — [CANON] "Arthur remembers" is the family's earliest fact about him. What the old bible read as apathy, the reboot reads as **discipline aimed downward**:

- After the 2018 *cleansing ritual* episode (mother's terror, the counselor, the crying week — [CANON] 29), seventeen-year-old Arthur learned the household's true curriculum: **being noticed is dangerous**. Every time he stood out, his mother's worry spiked and the family's load-bearing peace wobbled.
- So he aimed for the exact middle of every class, every metric, every room. The transcript's "present, adequate, forgettable" is [PROPOSED] **the most honest document about him because he wrote it that way** — camouflage as a skilled practice, learned before he ever heard the word "Veil."
- [INFERENCE] This is why "unremarkable by design" (29's own phrase) survives the reboot *strengthened*: the old bible used "by design" casually; the reboot makes it literal. He is the best-prepared nobody in Ravenhurst partly because he spent seven years *practicing* being nobody.
- The cost, carried forward: he does not know how to be seen. Praise dysregulates him; attention feels like a threat response. This is a permanent flaw (§11), not a quirk to be cured.

### 2.3 [PROPOSED] The physical base (latent, not secret)

- [CANON] gives him: weekly futsal with the Night Clerks' cell since his teens, port-road walks, a young man's body. The reboot changes **nothing** about his history and re-reads it: futsal weekly for years plus a naturally well-made frame = a genuine athletic *base* (coordination, stamina, durability) that was never *trained*.
- "Physically gifted" therefore means: **good clay, never fired.** He is not secretly a fighter; he has never thrown a real punch. What the directive calls "disciplined, combat-capable" is [PROPOSED] **a trajectory, not a starting stat**: the contract loop's demands (night work, field exposure, near-death tuition) force him to train on-page, starting from the clerk's baseline. The training is visible, costed in time and sleep (§7), and permanently capped at trained-human level (Q230 solution 1; "a bullet still works" re-ratified in §7).
- [RECOMMENDATION] The series bible should forbid any scene that implies pre-existing martial training. His first real fight lesson must be shown, and it must be embarrassing.

### 2.4 What he LOSES in the reboot (the price of the new life)

The task asks directly: how does a genius athlete end up *needing* a rebooted life, and what does he lose? [PROPOSED] answer — **he never wanted the reboot; it happens to him, and every gain is priced:**

1. **He loses the camouflage.** Seven years of practiced smallness, voided in one scan. The ERROR label makes him legible to every institution for the first time in his life. The skill he was best at — being nobody — no longer works.
2. **He loses the option of smallness.** Not the *performance* of it (he keeps trying — §5), but the *choice*. The Artifact's pressure mechanics (§9) and the SMD's existing file (EVENT-092, [CANON]) mean the quiet life is no longer selectable, only mourned. Q252 solution 3 is adopted as theme: ordinariness erodes visibly, and the story mourns it.
3. **He loses his family's performed ordinariness.** The activation breaks the load-bearing peace ([CANON] 29: "the peace is load-bearing"). Per Q095, the reboot stages the activation as a *revelation scene*, not a briefing: the parents' knowledge-states detonate on-page (see §2.5).
4. **He loses the luxury of being uninteresting to himself.** The trained self-erasure was also self-knowledge: he knew exactly who he was (nobody, on purpose). The reboot takes even that: he must now find out what he is when nobody is watching him *not* be someone.

[INFERENCE] This is how the new Arthur satisfies the *spirit* of the user's standing constraint (no WEAK→STRONG power climb; progression compatible with an ordinary background; not suddenly a different person): **his deeper progression is not a power climb but an exposure curve** — he does not become stronger so much as *less hidden*, and every step of visibility costs him something the old Arthur owned. The intelligence was always his; the story is about what it costs him to finally use it.

### 2.5 [PROPOSED] The family knowledge-states (what the activation breaks)

Adopting Q094 solution 2 + Q095 solution 3 + Q097 solution 3 (split knowledge, skip-custom):

- **Thomas** knows the *custom*: the Artifact skips — it passed over his generation, as it passed over his father's. He has performed bewilderment for decades; the performance was protective, not deceitful in his own accounting. His wound-in-waiting: he must tell his son that the family's ordinariness was *maintained*, and watch his son decide what that means.
- **Eleanor** knows the *customs, not the mechanics*: "we don't sell grandmother's thing; salt by the door *because of* the thing." [PROPOSED] Her 2018 *cleansing ritual* did touch the dormant Artifact — folk rites are data ([CANON] 03.2) — and notched its dormancy one step toward activation (Q098 solution 2). She will never know this. The reader may. Her guilt ("the guilt changed everything," [CANON]) gains a causal shadow the story never explains to her — this is deliberate cruelty *by the design*, and it must be handled as tragedy, never as a gotcha.
- **Hannah** knows nothing. [PROPOSED] She was also eligible; the Artifact passed her over (Q097 solution 2) — and the story never tells her. Her ordinariness is the one the reboot *protects*, at Arthur's expense: he carries the knowledge that his sister was a candidate, and it must never become leverage, exposition, or a plot coupon.
- The Pale Court's interest (EVENT-098, [CANON]) is adjudicated per Q092 solution 1: the Court misreads *custody* as *bloodline*. They are right that something is there and wrong about what — the old irony deepens instead of collapsing. "No bloodline" ([CANON]) is **kept**.

## 3. The awakening event and the ERROR/UNDEFINED reading

### 3.1 [PROPOSED] The activation (inciting incident)

- The Artifact activates as the reboot's inciting incident — the first anomaly-solve of the new continuity. Its trigger is **cosmological, not personal** (Q080 solution 1): High Tide pressure crossed the Artifact's dormancy threshold in September 2024. The tether's 2017 arrival was the *sensitizer* (Q080 solution 2, secondary): Undertow-adjacent physics entered the house, and the Artifact spent seven years noticing.
- **Date fence (series-bible rule):** the activation date is fixed in the reboot's canon record and is provably not 9/18 (Q100 solution 1). [PROPOSED] If the reboot verdict continues directly from chapter 020, the activation is the chapter-021 inciting incident, dated in the timeline ≥8 days clear of 9/18 either side. If the verdict declares draft continuity, the date is set in the new timeline's bible under the same fence. The fence is load-bearing: no future drafter may explain 9/18 via the activation without a 02.V CHANGELOG entry.
- [PROPOSED] Why now, for the ancestors (Q080/Q091): null history, stated — no ancestor ever activated it; "inherited for generations" meant *custody*, not use. The family's role was custodial, which reframes "ordinary" as "faithful" rather than ignorant. The Archivists (SUP-008) have a cold misfiled footnote (Q086 solution 2) that Arthur's own research can discover — the Artifact's history is a *mystery he solves*, not backstory he is told.
- [PROPOSED] Dormancy explains the absences (Q077 solution 3, Q089 solution 1): three field examinations correctly found nothing; Yusuf's logbook is correctly silent; the 2018 *cleansing ritual* is admissible as *evidence for* dormancy. The retcon is findable in old canon — the fair-play version.

### 3.2 [PROPOSED] Mechanics of the misfire (why the scan prints ERROR)

The Esper awakening scan measures **channelable output** (Hollow Pattern / Resonance throughput). Arthur's reading fails in three inconsistent ways at once:

1. **Loud-recognition signatures with zero throughput.** He perceives (Loud, [CANON]) but nothing flows *through* him — zero Fathoms, [CANON] kept as a measurement fact. The scan sees a sensor with no actuator.
2. **An unparseable foreign signal.** The Artifact's repository stores contracts *away* from his nervous system — the way the tether drinks ambient information ([CANON] 30.4 Scenario 5; Q051 solution 1's masking parallel is adopted as the working assumption). Instruments misprice what they can't parse ([CANON] 29.B.12). The scan sees *something* where the person is, and the something has no vector the form allows.
3. **The contradiction.** Perception-without-output plus an unfileable signal: the registrar's system has no row for this. It prints **ABILITY: ERROR / UNDEFINED** and the clerk on duty, following procedure, stamps the only rank the error path allows: **F**.

[INFERENCE] The ERROR is therefore **not a mistake and not a secret compliment**. It is the system correctly reporting its own inability — which is exactly why Q388's true-label doctrine is adopted: *the undervaluers are right on their own terms.* Rank F is a true reading of his Esper ability, because he has none. Every reversal must therefore **change the terms** (what should rank measure?) rather than reveal hidden stats. This is the load-bearing design decision of the whole reboot: the face-slap beats are philosophical, not theatrical.

### 3.3 [PROPOSED] What Rank F concretely denies him (Q229 solution 1: rank gates access, not the body)

Rank is a **social fact with teeth**. As Rank F (ERROR), Arthur is denied:

- **Field licensing:** barred as a combatant; eligible only for the consultant license (Q280 solution 3 — the paperwork matches his method exactly, and the paperwork has teeth: every field presence is logged, every log is leverage).
- **Institutional access:** academy library/labs/experts only via sponsor or per-case trade (Q276); sealed zones; mission boards; anomaly-location markets' upper tiers.
- **Legal force powers:** no citizen's-arrest-equivalent for anomaly work; no evidence privileges; his documentation is admissible only as civilian testimony unless a licensed party co-signs.
- **Credit and cover:** no hazard pay, no institutional insurance for anomaly injury, no hazard-rated medical care through official channels (the Vesper/Tide-Medicine interest in §C of the companion file is therefore *economic* pressure, not just scientific).
- **Information:** the rank system rations data by clearance; his genius runs on an information diet (Q238 solution 1) that the rank system *deliberately* starves.

What Rank F does **not** deny him: his body, his memory, his Loudness, his job, his citizenship. The rank's cruelty is precise: it leaves him everything except *permission*.

### 3.4 [PROPOSED] The legal lacuna (Q216 solution 1)

ERROR falls *between* statutes — a genuine legal lacuna he exploits. Esper law covers registered Espers; he is registered F with an undefined ability, which no procedure anticipated. [INFERENCE] Consequences: (a) early-arc fuel — he can operate in the gap; (b) the gap closes the moment a legislature notices — a visible countdown the series bible tracks; (c) Kanda Yōji (the bureaucrat, companion file) exists to embody the gap's absurdity and menace.

## 4. Personality — the continuity manifest (Q247)

### 4.1 [PROPOSED] What survives (the old soul, non-negotiable)

- **Restraint.** The default register. Reversals are performed, not enjoyed (§10.5).
- **Cost-consciousness.** Every expenditure — money, sleep, favors, resonance, other people's safety — is priced before it is spent. This is the clerk's epistemology grown up: *if it's not in the file it didn't happen, and he keeps files* ([CANON]).
- **Ordinary appetites.** Conbini economics, the port road, late-night radio, his mother's texts. Q252's quota is adopted: every movement contains ordinary-life scenes with real content, not breathers.
- **The habit of writing things down.** Externalized cognition (§6.2). His notebook is the series' most honest narrator.
- **Kindness in the night-shift key.** He remembers the cleaning staff's names. This must never become saintliness; it is *attention*, and attention is his weapon and his wound alike.

### 4.2 [PROPOSED] What does not survive (shed honestly, on-page)

- **Passivity as camouflage.** The trained self-erasure (§2.2) stops working; he must learn to act *while visible*. The learning is the arc, not a retcon.
- **Timidity about his own conclusions.** Old Arthur underplayed his reads to stay small. New Arthur must state them — and be wrong in public sometimes (§6.6).
- **The luxury of documentation-as-safety.** [CANON] 29's flaw — "he believes that understanding a thing is the same as surviving it" — is kept as the flaw and *punished* on-page: the nadir (§10.3) is the arc where understanding was not survival.

### 4.3 [PROPOSED] Secrecy posture (Q251 solution 1 + 3: concentric rings, and he is a bad liar)

The series bible keeps a **"who knows what" ledger** from the first reboot chapter:

- **Ring 0 (knows the Artifact):** Arthur. Only Arthur, at activation.
- **Ring 1 (knows the customs):** Thomas, Eleanor — customs without mechanics.
- **Ring 2 (suspects *something*, no Artifact knowledge):** Saitō (frozen at [CANON] — the glance stays sealed; no Artifact knowledge without a 02.V CHANGELOG entry, per Q100 solution 2), Aisha (professional suspicion), Nwosu (clinical suspicion).
- **Ring 3 (the world):** total lies, maintained by **avoidance rather than deception** — [PROPOSED] he is a bad liar and knows it (Q251 solution 3), so his tradecraft is *absence*: he doesn't attend, doesn't answer, isn't there. This constrains his social plotting honestly: he cannot run a double life on charm, only on scheduling.
- Every ring-breach is a plot event with a ledger entry. The ledger is the companion file's cross-reference for the romance design (Q266).

### 4.4 [PROPOSED] The line (Q248 solution 1)

**He will not trade other people's safety for answers.** Named early, pressed in every arc. The line is the character: enemies who learn it (and they will — Q203 solution 2, institutional memory) price their attacks *through* it. The story's hardest scenes are the ones where the line's price is denominated in people he loves.

### 4.5 [PROPOSED] The reversal tone (Q249 solution 1 + Q264 solution 2)

He dislikes the theater but uses it — reversal as tool, not treat. Confidence is an **earned arc**: restrained in movement one, assured by movement three, monitored against the "different person" constraint by the continuity manifest (§4.1). The old restraint governs the new assurance the way a sheath governs a blade: the blade is real, and it stays sheathed until the price is right.

## 5. The FUNCTIONAL intelligence system — how his mind does work on-page

[PRINCIPLE] Genius is never stated; it is **staged as a verifiable chain** (Q235 solution 1). Every deduction scene must contain: the observations (on-page, before the deduction — Q235 solution 3, reader-fair), the alternatives considered and discarded, the smallest falsifiable test, the price paid. A deduction that skips a step is a draft error, not a style.

**Domain rule** ([PROPOSED], Q239 solution 3 / Q205): his genius is **anomaly-native, people-approximate**. He reads Rules fast; he reads people slowly and with explicit error bars. 29 §B.5 ("misreading is always possible") is extended to people-reading by series-bible rule ([CANON] principle, [PROPOSED] scope). His social deductions carry visible uncertainty — and the story must let him be wrong about people at least once per movement.

### The enumerated mechanisms (each with its "how the reader SEES it working" note)

1. **Rule-extraction protocol** ([CANON] kept verbatim, Q237): observe → hypothesize → smallest falsifiable test → pay cost. *Reader sees:* the chain laid out in-scene — the hypothesis stated *before* the test, the test designed to be able to fail, the failure mode priced in advance. Never a leap; leaps are marked in the text as guesses, and guesses are allowed to be wrong.
2. **The working notebook** ([PROPOSED] instrument): perfect retention, **imperfect indexing** (Q236 solution 1 — kept both, bounded). He remembers everything but must *find* the right memory; the notebook is the index. *Reader sees:* the cross-reference moment — two files that don't match, the margin note from three arcs ago, the timestamp. Memory is retrieval drama, not omniscience.
3. **Preparation doctrine** ([CANON] 30 mechanism 2): the strong improvise; the weak prepare. *Reader sees:* the checklist with prices — bought, borrowed, owed. Every prepared scene must show at least one preparation *failing* or proving insufficient per movement, so preparation never reads as plot armor.
4. **Rule interference** ([CANON] 30 mechanism 1): attack the condition, not the channeler. *Reader sees:* the file-reading beat — "the Rule needs line-of-sight / a name / the thirteenth step" — then the condition broken with mundane means. The reader should be able to predict the interference from the stated Rule: fair play.
5. **Staged disclosure** ([PROPOSED], Q279 solution 1): information is non-excludable once spoken, so he reveals the Rule *during* execution, never before. *Reader sees:* the negotiation scene where he withholds the key sentence — and the text marks the withholding as deliberate tradecraft, not authorial coyote. His leverage survives because his mouth stays shut until the moment it can't be unsaid.
6. **Adversarial verification** ([PROPOSED], Q235 solution 2 / Q240 solution 1): his conclusions are checked by designated epistemic peers — Dr. Nwosu (clinical), Fuse Erika (journalistic), and the Artifact's own records (which can contradict his memory, Q240 solution 3). *Reader sees:* the check scene. At least one check per movement must find him wrong or incomplete. A genius nobody can falsify is a narrator with a name.
7. **Attention budget** ([PROPOSED], Q238 solution 3): deep analysis costs time, sleep, and resonance. He cannot genius everything at once. *Reader sees:* the triage — the whiteboard with three problems and only one circled; the problem he *chooses not to think about* becoming the next arc's disaster. His limits are scheduling, and scheduling is visible.
8. **Information diet** ([PROPOSED], Q238 solution 1): his genius runs only on available data; the story rations clues honestly. *Reader sees:* the locked file, the redacted paragraph, the witness who won't talk. When he lacks data, the text must show the lack — no deductions from off-page clues, ever.
9. **Contract formation as falsification** ([PROPOSED], Q083 solution 1): the Artifact does not judge; **reality grades the solve**. A contract forms when the phenomenon's behavior changes legibly (power is legible, [CANON] 02.IV). *Reader sees:* the solve *working or failing* in the world — the fog that doesn't come, the Rule that holds. Misjudged solves (Q083 solution 2's fallible-Artifact variant is *rejected* here; the Artifact is passive storage) still occur — as *his* misreadings, with Oslo-case consequences ([CANON] 05.6 case 3 is the standing warning label).
10. **Fraud immunity protocol** ([PROPOSED], Q292 solution 1): the black market sells counterfeit Rules; his defense is verification procedure, not Artifact magic. *Reader sees:* the quarantine beat — a purchased tip held at arm's length until independently tested. Exactly once, the fraud works devastatingly (Q292 solution 3) — and the arc is the recovery, which rewrites his verification doctrine permanently.

**Rate-limit summary** ([PROPOSED]): data rationing + misreading-with-teeth + attention budget. His errors at genius scale are grander and costlier (Q238 solution 2) — the story must stage at least one grand error per movement, or the limiters are decorative.

## 6. Combat doctrine — how an F-rank with no ability survives fights

### 6.1 The inviolable floor

[CANON] re-ratified verbatim (Q234 solution 1, Q207): **"a bullet still works."** No general defense, no passive durability, no invulnerability at any stage. His body is and remains mortal. The ceiling on "physically capable" is **trained-human, permanently** (Q230 solution 1); his edge is skill, nerve, and preparation — never output. The series bible binds future writers: one "he dodged because he's just that fast" scene is a continuity violation.

### 6.2 [PROPOSED] The doctrine: documentation-shaped victories, extended to the body

[CANON] 30 §15 ("Arthur's action chapters are *evasion and endurance*; victories are documentation-shaped") is adopted and **extended**: his athleticism serves *evasion, endurance, and positioning* — getting to the file room, surviving the collapsing warehouse, outlasting the confrontation — never *winning the fight* (Q398 solution 1). He does not defeat ranked Espers; he makes defeating him *expensive, visible, and legally complicated*.

Every "weak defeats strong" scene must use at least one of 30's seven mechanisms ([CANON] 30.3); his personal loadout, mapped:

1. **Rule interference** — primary weapon. Break the condition (line-of-sight, the name, the threshold, the exception clause).
2. **Preparation** — the real battle. Wards, Tide-noise, blueprints, schedules; preparation converts tier difference into a solved equation.
3. **Terrain** — fight where he chose, or don't fight.
4. **Tide-noise** — the great equalizer; ninety seconds is usually enough.
5. **Information advantage** — *clerk* work. Knowing the enemy's Rule, handler, family, and schedule is worth more than two tiers.
6. **Erosion attrition** — don't win; last. Make them spend. The strong are on clocks; he is not.
7. **Social and legal constraints** — the Tribunal exists; licenses exist; Census files exist. His documentation is a weapon because the law is a leash the weak can pull.

### 6.3 [PROPOSED] Contract effects in combat — the categorical ban (Q204 solution 1)

**No contract may grant direct damage, defense, or stat-boosts.** Contract output in combat is limited to **information, access, and conditional triggers** (e.g., a warning, a door that opens, a Rule's exception invoked at the right second). This is the written ceiling the audit demanded: verifiable, categorical, enforceable. A combat-useful contract is therefore always a *puzzle piece*, never a weapon — and every candidate Contract #1 must be audited against this ban before selection (interface: Worker B).

### 6.4 [PROPOSED] The mid-combat rule (Q202)

**No new contracts mid-combat.** Fights are fought with prepared contracts only — preparation is the real battle (Q202 solution 1). The priced exception: **flash hypotheses** — untested reads the Artifact records as *fragment-grade*, which fail loudly on error (Q202 solution 2; mechanics owned by Worker B). The reader must see the "FRAGMENT-GRADE — UNTESTED" tag in the text's logic: improvisation with a price, never off-book genius.

### 6.5 [PROPOSED] Surprise violence (Q207 solution 2, capped)

Mortality is the point; he survives ambushes the way ordinary people do — sometimes he doesn't, and allies and scars accumulate. The one narrow passive: **the Artifact warns (never shields)** — information, not defense. The warning's reliability is capped by series-bible rule (it is Tide-pressure-sensitive, degrades under Tide-noise, and has false positives the text must show). It gives him a dodge *chance*, not spider-sense.

### 6.6 [PROPOSED] Why the state doesn't just take him (Q215/Q217/Q218)

- **Seizure gets a paperweight.** Custodian-binding (companion ledger, Q084 solution 1 as working assumption): the Artifact goes inert for non-holders — the family's "useless heirloom" reputation *is* the security system. Established in-world before any lab tries (Q217 solution 1).
- **Detention is priced out.** Jurisdictional fog (Q215 solution 1: no agency has clear authority over an ERROR civilian, and agencies block each other) + "more useful free" (Q215 solution 2: his solves are public goods the government harvests without owning him) + mutually assured disclosure (the dead-man's documentation, [CANON] 29.B.11's escrow extended to the Artifact).
- **His refusal of the leash is priced** (Q218): he has read the predecessor file — [PROPOSED] the SMD's "quiet room" record of a prior clever civilian asset who was institutionalized and destroyed by the process (not an Artifact-holder — the Q054 blocker is preserved: no prior *solver* existed). Refusal is evidence-based, and it costs him the comfortable life visibly, on-page.

## 7. Leadership — how he leads without authority

[PRINCIPLE] ([PROPOSED], Q241) Leadership is a **priced economy**, not a charisma aura. The currency is earned per scene and spent per scene.

### 7.1 The three currencies

1. **Competence-credit** (Q241 solution 1): he is right in public, repeatedly, until following him is the rational bet. The early arcs must *stage* the credit-earning — no one follows him in chapter 5 because the reader knows he's the protagonist.
2. **Debt ledger** (Q241 solution 2): he saves people who then owe him. Leadership as ledger — gritty, transactional, and honest about incentives. The black market's favors-currency (Q294 solution 1) is the same ledger at civic scale.
3. **Shared-interest coalitions** (Q241 solution 3): temporary, per-anomaly, disbanded after. No standing party to protect, bloat, or be captured.

### 7.2 [PROPOSED] Style: strategic-informational, transactionally honest (Q246 solution 1 + 3)

His leadership is his intelligence applied to people: plans, roles, contingencies — plus open books. He tells followers what he knows, what he doesn't, and what it costs. Followers are never pieces (the text must show him refusing at least one tactically-useful sacrifice per movement, or the style rots into manipulation).

### 7.3 [PROPOSED] Command responsibility, priced (Q242 solution 1)

Failed leadership costs him followers, reputation, and sleep — **tracked visibly** in the series bible's loss ledger. The story must actually kill or break followers sometimes; a leader who never loses anyone is a mascot. Informed consent is the moral shield (Q242 solution 3): every recruit gets a real consent scene, and the consent is *informed* — he tells them the odds.

### 7.4 [PROPOSED] Structure: solo-capable, team-amplified; the gray network (Q243 solution 1, Q244 solution 1)

- He is designed **solo-capable** — the "world without Arthur" independence principle ([CANON]) is restated for the reboot: no faction, and no solve, structurally requires him.
- His organization, when it exists, is a **deniable contractor network**: no roster, cash and favors, per-incident assembly. It is never formalized (formal = acquirable). The charter-that-must-be-defended plot (Q244 solution 2) is *rejected*: he owns nothing worth seizing, which is itself the defense.
- **Loyalty is never mechanically enforced** (Q245 solution 3 adopted over the comfortable options): followers can take the benefits and leave. Some will — betrayal is a recurring, priced engine. Those who stay, stay to *learn* (Q245 solution 2): he is a teacher-leader, and understanding is the only currency that compounds.

## 8. Motivation — why he solves anomalies instead of living quietly

[PRINCIPLE] Every "why doesn't he just…?" must have an answer grounded in incentives, constraints, cost, law, politics, or Erosion ([CANON] 02.IV, Q054).

### 8.1 Why he doesn't sell the Artifact (Q253)

He **can't, cleanly** — and then he **won't, personally**:
- [PROPOSED] *Can't:* the custodian-binding means sale attempts fail weirdly — they always have ([CANON]-adjacent: Q087 solution 3, the family's "don't sell grandmother's thing" superstition is data; the folkways are the containment). ### 8.2 Why he doesn't live quietly (Q254)

He **can't, mechanically** — and then he **won't, motivationally**:
- [PROPOSED] *Can't:* the Artifact accumulates pressure that must be discharged through solving — unused resonance degrades *him* (the treadmill is mechanical, Q254 solution 1). Quitting is not rest; it is a different kind of dying. [RECOMMENDATION] The series bible should show, not tell, this exactly once early (a bad week of avoidance with visible symptoms), then treat it as established physics.
- *Can't, institutionally:* the SMD's file (EVENT-092, [CANON]) predates the activation. Quitting makes him *more* interesting, not less — the quiet life is surveilled life.
- *Won't:* §8.3.

### 8.3 [PROPOSED] What he wants that only anomaly-solving gives him (Q256)

Three wants, ranked — a want sustains a series; a method does not:

1. **To answer the inheritance.** What is the Artifact, why did it wait, what did the ancestors know, why him and not Hannah. The central mystery is personal. (The answer must be worth it — budgeted mystery, Q085 solution 3.)

Elegant with the identity rule; the map stays incomplete forever by design (the Undertow has no time, [CANON] 02.I — completeness is structurally impossible, which is why the hunger is tragic, not completable).

## 9. Emotional arc — five-phase sketch

[PRINCIPLE] The old arc was UNFILED → ANSWERABLE ([CANON], Q259). The reboot's arc is **[PROPOSED] ALONE → ANSWERABLE-FOR**, with the epistemic spine **UNKNOWN → UNDERSTOOD** (Q259 solutions 1+2 combined): he becomes someone who can bear what he knows, and then someone others depend on to bear it.

- **Phase 1 — UNSEEN (opening movement):** the clerk tries to get his quiet life back. The ERROR label is a humiliation he treats as paperwork. He solves to *discharge pressure*, not to grow. Ends when the first solve is witnessed.
- **Phase 2 — USEFUL:** competence-credit accrues; the gray network forms per-incident; he learns the leadership price. First follower loss. Ends when someone follows him *into* danger rather than *after* his solution.
- **Phase 3 — ANSWERABLE-FOR:** people depend on him structurally. The line (§4.4) is pressed hard. **The nadir** (Q262 solution 1, shape named now, content later): a failed solve with a body count — his understanding was wrong (29 §B.5 given flesh), and someone paid. The recovery arc is the movement's spine.
- **Phase 4 — LEGIBLE:** ERROR becomes a name. Institutions stop underestimating and start *pricing* him (Q203 solution 2: the underworld learns; the undervalue engine's planned obsolescence is itself an arc). His interior label-work (§10.4) completes here.
- **Phase 5 — UNDERSTOOD:** the question of what he is. He becomes someone who can bear what he knows — and the story asks whether bearing it has changed what he is. The answer is [UNKNOWN] by design (budgeted; see §12).

### 9.1 [PROPOSED] The wound (Q260 solution 1)

The inheritance is the wound: **his ordinary life was maintained** — curated by his father's performed bewilderment and his mother's careful silences — and he has to decide whether to be angry or grateful. For a man whose identity was ordinariness-by-discipline, learning the ordinariness was *administered* is the one revelation his epistemology cannot file. The wound never fully heals; it becomes the lens.

### 9.2 [PROPOSED] The fears, ranked (Q261)

1. **Ordinary fears dominate:** poverty, exposure, his mother's worry. The supernatural is the job; Tuesday is the fear. ([CANON]-adjacent: the old cost-consciousness, named.)
2. **Epistemic fear:** that understanding will change him into someone who can't come back (the identity rule's shadow, Q248).
3. **Social fear:** being known — ERROR made legible means nowhere to hide (ties fear to the rank plot).

### 9.3 [PROPOSED] The ERROR label's interior (Q263, blended)

He is a clerk: labels are paperwork, and his private joke is that the system finally filed him correctly — *he is an error*. Underneath, the internalization grows across the arc (Q263 solution 1): by Phase 4, ERROR is his private name for himself — the man the system can't parse. The story must never let this curdle into self-pity; the antidote is the notebook (he files the feeling, the way he files everything).

### 9.4 [PROPOSED] Curiosity's governor (Q248)

The identity rule's drive without a governor is addiction. The governor is **the line** (§4.4) plus **an external pull-back**: Saitō is licensed — quietly, in one early scene — to say "you're doing the thing," and Arthur is bound by series-bible rule to *hear it* at least once per movement (Q248 solution 3). The supporting cast is load-bearing for his humanity (Q224 solution 2): his defense against the cult of understanding is belonging he already has.

## 10. Flaws that do NOT disappear

[PRINCIPLE] (Q250) Designed flaws are load-bearing: they generate subplots, justify the supporting cast, and keep genius from curdling into perfection. These are **permanent** — the story may press them, never cure them:

1. **No channeling, ever.** Zero Fathoms, no passive defense, a bullet still works. The lack of conventional power stays meaningful in every movement — including the last.
2. **Tin-eared, conflict-avoidant, bad at asking for help** (Q250 solution 1). The genius who can't say "I need you." This must never become cute.
3. **A bad liar** (§4.3). His tradecraft is absence. He cannot charm his way through a double life.
4. **Curiosity as attack surface** (Q224 solution 1). His hunger to understand is the cult hook — the smartest-mark failure mode. A cult that offers forbidden answers can move him; his defense is social, not intellectual.
5. **Grief-illiterate** (Q250 solution 3). He understands anomalies better than his own family. The wound (§9.1) and this flaw are the same knot pulled from opposite ends.
6. **Documentation ≠ survival** ([CANON] 29's flaw, kept and punished). He will keep believing, under stress, that understanding a thing is surviving it — and the story will keep collecting the tuition.
