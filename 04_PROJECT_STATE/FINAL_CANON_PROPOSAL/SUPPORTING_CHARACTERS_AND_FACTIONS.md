## PART D — Placement: where does an F-rank ERROR go, and who wants him there?

[PROPOSED] He is never *placed*; he is *admitted per-case*. The placement doctrine (Q272 solution 1 + Q276 + Q280 + Q283):

|---|---|---|---|
| Esper Academy (Ravenhurst) | Archives consultant (staff-adjacent) | The Academy: contain-and-use; file the unfileable | Handler (Hayes); every access logged; gray access via a sympathetic insider for the rest |

[INFERENCE] The placement table is the reboot's answer to Q244's capture problem: he is everywhere *admitted* and nowhere *owned*. The moment any single institution can claim to own his placement, the design has failed.

## PART E — The undervaluation roster (varied actors, varied currencies)

[PRINCIPLE] (Q203) The undervalue→reversal device has a **governor** in the series bible, not just in chapter plans: cap reversals per arc; force some undervaluations to end badly (he stays underestimated and *loses*); let the underworld learn (smart enemies overprepare — the dynamic flips); vary the reversal currency (escape, information, third-party intervention — not always victory). Characters must eventually notice the pattern ("why does everyone keep underestimating the ERROR guy?") — the noticing is itself a plot beat.

| Actor | Undervaluation currency | Reversal currency (when it comes) |
|---|---|---|
| Hayes (instructor) | Professional: paper-pusher auditing a war | A protocol he wrote fails; Arthur's preparation note predicts it |
| Pram (rival) | Curiosity: quaint clever clerk | The files beat the instruments, again, expensively |
| Hattori (teammate) | Protective: support staff who'd die without her | She follows his call into a collapse and lives because of it |
| Nwosu (researcher) | Clinical: subject until peer-reviewed | Peer review confirms him; she says so on the record |
| Aisha (official) | Bureaucratic: correctly filed, kept out of the field | The field comes to the paperwork; her bar breaks usefully |
| Silas (elite) | Ontological: wrong ocean | A shallow-water problem only the chartmaker can read |
| Kanda (bureaucrat) | Administrative: filing anomaly | The form he files becomes the precedent that protects Arthur |
| Fuse (skeptic) | Journalistic: hundred frauds before | The one story she can't debunk — hers to carry |
| Ashworth (Court) | Dynastic: furniture the Artifact sits on | The "furniture" negotiates; the bloodline theory takes another hit |
| The Factor (market) | Commercial: inventory with legs | The inventory prices *him* — blackmail reversed |
