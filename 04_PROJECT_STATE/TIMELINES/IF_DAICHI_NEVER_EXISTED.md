## 11. The verdict: does Arthur pass the test?
**Yes — and the yes is structural, not sentimental.**

Arthur is **IMPORTANT** because: (a) he is the first holder in generations to meet the Artifact's recognition criteria (under the mechanical, reproducible reading — Q476 s1), which makes him the *discoverer of a method* (UNDERSTAND → SOLVE → CONTRACT), not the chosen one; (b) the method is genuinely new knowledge — containment-as-personal-contract has no institutional precedent (Q329 s2's novelty reading, if the reboot takes it) — and new knowledge, once discovered, propagates; (c) his specific cognitive profile (the clerk's method: observe/hypothesize/test at the smallest falsifiable scale — 29 §B.11's generative principle, Q083 s1) is what the Artifact's "entrance exam" selects for, which is why the story is *his* without being *fated* to be his.

Arthur is **NOT the only reason the world exists** because: the faction graph test (0 nodes, 0 edges), the institutional causal chains (§10), the Tide's indifference, and the tether's holder-agnostic transfer rule all hold without him. The world does not stop functioning; it does not even slow down. His absence costs the world *a method and a few local kindnesses*, not its coherence.
