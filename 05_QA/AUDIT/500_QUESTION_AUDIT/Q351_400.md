### Q351 — Public Knowledge
**Question:** Canon states civilian Seep encounters are "statistically common and epistemically weightless." If Espers are public figures, what stops every civilian encounter from becoming epistemically weighty — provable, shareable, belief-forming?
**Status:** [CONTRADICTION]
**Current answer:** `18_CIVILIAN_LIFE.md` §18.2 (canon statement): "Civilian encounters with the supernatural are *statistically common and epistemically weightless*. Millions of people have brushed against Seeps; almost none of them know it; none of them can prove it. This is not a conspiracy — it's cognitive immunology plus economics." The mechanism is the Veil Reflex plus the deepfake dividend: encounters dissolve under rationalization and distrust. Public Espers break the mechanism at its root: a civilian who sees a ranked Esper *use a power on the evening news* has an encounter that is common, weighty, and socially validated — the Veil Reflex cannot rationalize what the state certifies.
**Sufficiency:** Canon's weightlessness thesis is the masquerade's philosophical foundation; public Espers don't just breach it operationally — they refute it.
**Possible solutions:**
1. [RECOMMENDATION] Narrow the weightlessness thesis: *Seep* encounters stay weightless (Veil Reflex, rot, dividend all still apply to phenomena), while *Esper* encounters are weighty — the public knows people have powers but cannot prove, film, or reason about what those powers *touch*. Consequences: `18_CIVILIAN_LIFE.md` §18.2 needs the Esper/Seep split written in; the two-tier premise (Q350) gains its civilian-psychology foundation.
2. [RECOMMENDATION] Keep encounters weightless by making public Esper displays *unconvincing by design*: evaluations are closed-door, broadcasts are edited (Q308/Q345), and the dividend does the rest — the public "knows" Espers the way it "knows" wrestling is real. Consequences: Preserves §18.2 verbatim; but it makes the public-Esper premise a managed spectacle (kayfabe, Q346 option 2) — the proposal must accept that its public is permanently unsure.
**Canon impact:** [ADAPT] — The weightlessness thesis needs the Esper/Seep distinction; its Seep-side logic is untouched.

### Q354 — Public Knowledge

Consequences: The two-tier model becomes psychological rather than legal; canon's civilian-psychology machinery carries the whole load — elegant if the proposal commits to permanent public uncertainty.
**Canon impact:** [ADAPT] — Either engagement protocols or the rationalization model; canon's cover vocabulary (`18_CIVILIAN_LIFE.md`) is reusable as-is.
