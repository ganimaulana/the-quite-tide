### Q051 — Anomalies

**Status:** [UNRESOLVED]

**Sufficiency:** Insufficient — the reboot's silence on the Laggard is itself the problem: 29 Part B is ~8,000 words of canon that the directive neither keeps nor removes.
**Possible solutions:**
1. Keep both, designed interaction: the tether's information-drinking *masks* contract emissions (the Laggard becomes the Artifact's stealth system — canon 30.4 Scenario 5 already establishes the wake-eating). **Status:** [UNRESOLVED]

**Sufficiency:** Insufficient — the contract source list's first entry is undefined.

**Canon impact:** [ADAPT] — 03.3's taxonomy needs the monster entry, whichever solution.
