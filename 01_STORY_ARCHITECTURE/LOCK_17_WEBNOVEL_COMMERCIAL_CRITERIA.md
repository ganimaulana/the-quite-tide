# WEBNOVEL COMMERCIAL CRITERIA — AUTHOR LOCK

> Ratified: 2026-09-24
> Scope: THE QUIET TIDE — commercial-readiness layer, not a replacement for canon or chapter locks.

## 1. Source Basis

WebNovel's published judging material identifies content quality (grammar/language, character design, worldbuilding, story development/creativity), theme compatibility, update stability, reader interactivity, commercial value, and adaptation value as evaluation dimensions. Current WebNovel Q&A also indicates that contract review is editor-reviewed and that word count alone does not guarantee a contract.

These are editorial optimization criteria, not a promise of contract acceptance.

## 2. Story Implementation Locks

### Opening / Retention
- The protagonist appears immediately and the opening establishes concrete anomaly, problem, desire, or pressure.
- CH001–003 communicate the commercial promise without explaining the whole mystery.
- Chapter endings create a concrete reason to continue: question, test, social consequence, discovery, escalation, or imminent payoff.

### Protagonist Commercial Appeal
- Daichi's appeal comes from observation, disciplined reasoning, physical conditioning, preparation, and testing assumptions.
- Competence is demonstrated through action and consequence, not authorial claims.
- Daichi may be wrong; mistakes must have visible consequences and revised reasoning.
- Rank F weakness remains real. Intelligence is not unexplained omniscience.

### Academy-First Promise
- Academy is the main plot engine: classes, training, examinations, missions/trials, status hierarchy, social segregation, contempt, and competence payoffs.
- Operations Support is a supporting layer and must not displace Academy progression.
- Weapon Knowledge → Familiarization → Mastery is ordinary development; Weapon Contract remains a later supernatural payoff.

### Mystery / Power Clarity
- Motion Sense is a low-output innate Esper Ability with a clear movement-only boundary.
- Motion Sense is distinct from Contract formation and does not explain SPs by itself.
- SP is an institutional classification, not a power tier or automatic Contract source.
- Contract #1 remains THE POLITE KNOCK with its locked partial, one-knock-per-phenomenon grammar.
- Mystery answers use observation → hypothesis → test → result → updated hypothesis → second test → resolution/failure.

### Commercial Payoff
- Face-slap moments come from concrete competence, status reversal, or institutional recognition rather than empty humiliation.
- Payoffs are visible enough to create reader satisfaction while consequences remain canon-safe.
- Avoid repeated quiet-mystery endings without a meaningful state change.

### Language / Adaptation
- English remains clear, natural, and accessible to non-native readers.
- Scenes are visually and causally legible for audiobook, comic, short-drama, and related adaptation formats.
- Prefer action, dialogue, tests, and institutional procedure over unnecessary exposition blocks.

## 3. Revision Rule

Commercial optimization may sharpen hooks, compress repetition, improve social/competence payoff, clarify motivation, and strengthen chapter endings. It must not override locked canon, ratified chapter functions, mystery fences, chronology, or the Academy-first / Operations-Support-subplot hierarchy.

## 5. Differentiation Authority

For the specific question of **what makes THE QUIET TIDE commercially distinct**, `LOCK_18_UNIQUE_STORY_IDENTITY.md` is now the companion authority for CH001–015.

Its required identity is not "Rank F becomes strong." The primary promise is **Academy progression through rule-solving, records-based competence, and costly information**.

Commercial revisions must therefore strengthen:

- phenomena as observable/testable rule problems;
- records as an institutional source of consequence and access;
- Daichi's competence through method rather than unexplained power;
- visible correction when his interpretation is wrong;
- concrete competence/status payoffs;
- chapter-specific hooks that advance this identity.
