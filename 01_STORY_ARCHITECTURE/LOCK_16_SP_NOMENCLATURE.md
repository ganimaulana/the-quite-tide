# LOCK 16 — SP NOMENCLATURE

> **STATUS: AUTHOR-LOCKED — 2026-09-24**

## 1. Official term

**SP = Special Phenomenon**

SP is an **institutional classification**, not the name of a creature, organization, or power system. It identifies a phenomenon that cannot be handled adequately through ordinary Esper/Academy doctrine and therefore requires special-case investigation, documentation, and handling.

### Formal usage

- **Special Phenomenon (SP)** — full formal term, used in handbooks, examinations, official records, and first explanations.
- **SP** — standard institutional shorthand after first definition.
- **SP case** — acceptable when referring to an individual investigation/case file.
- **SP-###** — optional case identifier when a numbered case is needed later.

## 2. What SP means in-world

SP does **not** mean a specific monster type, automatically hostile phenomenon, automatically high Threat Tier, automatic Contract source, Esper Ability, or the Uncarved Seal/Echo.

An SP may be an event, location, object, organism, information pattern, environmental condition, or other phenomenon whose behavior requires special-case treatment.

## 3. Relationship to anomaly

**Anomaly** is the broad descriptive/institutional concept. **SP** is a more operational classification used when an anomalous phenomenon requires special-case handling or investigation.

Therefore:

**Anomaly → may be classified as SP → investigated/handled under SP procedures.**

Not every unusual event needs the SP label.

## 4. Relationship to Threat Tier

SP classification and Threat Tier answer different questions:

- **SP:** What kind of institutional handling does this phenomenon require?
- **Threat Tier:** How much sustainable output/consequence can the phenomenon produce?

An SP can be low-threat but difficult to understand. A high-tier phenomenon does not become SP merely because it is powerful.

## 5. Relationship to Contract formation

An SP is **not a Contract vending machine**. Only a genuine solve can produce the conditions for RESONANCE and possible Contract formation under the locked Contract system.

The correct conceptual chain is:

**SP/anomalous phenomenon → investigation → observation/hypothesis/test → genuine solve → RESONANCE → possible Contract formation.**

Failure, partial understanding, or fragmentary information does not guarantee a Contract.

## 6. Relationship to Motion Sense

Motion Sense is Daichi's innate Esper Ability and is independent of SP classification. It detects movement but does not explain an SP, reveal intent, or guarantee a solve. Contract formation remains a separate consequence of genuine SP resolution under the locked Contract system.

## 7. Prose rule

When SP is first explained to the reader, use the full term:

> **Special Phenomenon. SP.**

Then explain its institutional purpose through context rather than a long exposition dump. After the term is established, ordinary dialogue may use **SP** naturally.

## 8. Canon guardrails

- Do not use the term as a reference to any external fictional organization.
- Do not copy external SP-style containment terminology or document conventions.
- Do not make every SP a monster.
- Do not make SP status itself a measure of power.
- Do not reveal that SP status guarantees a Contract.
- Do not use SP as a replacement for **anomaly**, **phenomenon**, **Seep**, **Esper Ability**, or **Contract** where those terms are more precise.

## 9. First-reader explanation target

The reader should eventually understand:

> **SP is what the Academy calls a phenomenon that ordinary doctrine cannot safely or completely explain.**

The deeper question — why some phenomena behave this way and what the classification ultimately misses — remains open.
