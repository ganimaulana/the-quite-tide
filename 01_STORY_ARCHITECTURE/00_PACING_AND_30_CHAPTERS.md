### §2A. CH001–CH015 implementation layer — Academy-first, canon-safe

These are AUTHOR-LOCKED supporting implementation rules, not replacement plot beats.

1. **Motion Sense boundary discovery — CH002–CH005:** Daichi observes, tests, fails, updates the hypothesis, and documents exactly what movement information the ability provides. Motion Sense must not function as precognition or intent reading.
2. **Motion Sense practical payoff — CH002–CH008:** readers see the ability improve positioning, reaction, and field observation without becoming a complete solution engine.
3. **Motion Sense misread — CH005–CH008:** at least one movement observation must be interpreted incorrectly, reinforcing that the ability supplies information rather than objective narration.
4. **Weapons competence — CH003–CH012:** one or two non-combat competence beats demonstrate weapon knowledge/familiarization and practical learning without making Daichi an exceptional fighter.
5. **Operations Support cross-functionality — CH004–CH010:** show that Daichi must understand enough of multiple divisions to maintain useful records and operational context. The job remains a subplot.
6. **Academy segregation — CH004–CH015:** Rank, division, status, and conventional Esper strength should produce concrete social friction. Daichi remains relevant through observation and method rather than raw power.
7. **Intelligence-based face-slaps — CH005–CH015:** reversals come from concrete results, corrected records, successful tests, or useful decisions rather than hidden power.
8. **Artifact ≠ Esper Ability — CH002–CH008:** institutional language establishes that anomalous artifact behavior and Esper manifestation are classified separately.
9. **Weapon knowledge is initially undervalued — CH007–CH012:** a cadet may dismiss Daichi's weapon knowledge as "Records" knowledge; the later Weapon Contract is earned by accumulated preparation, not retroactive genius.
10. **Solving → possible Contract — CH008–CH015:** readers may learn that some SP phenomena can yield a Contract after genuine solving, while the exact binding mechanism, eligibility, and future Contract contents remain fenced.

**Negative constraints for CH001–CH015:** no formal Weapon Contract name, no exact future Contract acquisition sequence, no numeric amplification/damage, no excessive weapon combat, no complete Seal/Echo origin explanation, no Operations Support takeover of the Academy main plot, and no implication that Daichi was secretly overpowered from the beginning.

## §5. Deviations from the directive (noted per task instructions)

- **D1 — Daichi's starting capability** [REVISED AUTHOR CANON 2026-09-24]: Daichi begins the story with genuinely good physical conditioning.
