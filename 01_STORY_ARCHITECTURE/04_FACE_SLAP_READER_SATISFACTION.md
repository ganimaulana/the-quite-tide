## §1. The philosophical foundation: the true-label doctrine [PROPOSED] (Q388 solution 2)

Rank F ("Ability: ERROR / UNDEFINED") is **correct about his Esper ability** — he has none; his exceptional traits (method, nerve, fitness) are *irrelevant* to what ranks measure (sustainable output; Q396: F–S is T0–T7 made public). The undervaluers are **right on their own terms**. Therefore every reversal must **change the terms**, not reveal hidden stats:

- The face-slap is never "I was strong all along" — it is "your *measure* was the wrong instrument."
- The reader's doubt is preserved (Q388's loophole closed): the reader, like the world, must *infer* from observed behavior under fair-play rules — exceptionalism is demonstrated, never declared (PACING file §5-D1).
- Canon's generators are [KEEP] and used irregularly (Q390): the overlooked clerk whose memory holds the exception clause (CG-002), the dismissed witness (CG-027), the camouflage thesis ("He is not important. That's his camouflage. That's also, eventually, his weapon" — 18_CIVILIAN_LIFE §18.1 [CANON]). The proposal's cycle adds *regularity* to these generators — which is exactly what this engine disciplines.

**Matrix rules** [RECOMMENDATION] (auditable at batch planning, §22 step 9):
1. **No cell repeats within 40 chapters.** A used (actor × structure) cell is burned for 40 chapters.

## §3. The 6-stage cycle: chapter-level mechanics [PROPOSED]

UNDERVALUE → EXPECTATION → DEMONSTRATION → REVERSAL → RECOGNITION → HIGHER EXPECTATION. Stages may compress into one chapter or span 2–4; the mapping to SOP beats is mandatory (every stage must move story state — SOP §2):

1. **UNDERVALUE** (setup): the mispricing **stated plainly** — the reader must see the *terms* of the contempt (true-label doctrine, §1). Chapter function: QUESTION posed ("what will the system do with him?"). Must name what the contempt *costs* him (time, access, dignity — §6 rule 4).
2. **EXPECTATION** (priming, 1–3 ch): the reader is given the **pieces** — fair-play (Q374; SOP §30 reader-knowledge rule: reader-lead ≤1 phase). The "what will he do?" beat. Micro-hooks (SOP §4), never artificial stakes. *This is the stage most adaptations skip — skipping it turns reversal into fiat.*
3. **DEMONSTRATION**: the understanding/capability **shown** — need not be combat (catalog §4). **Always priced**: every demonstration spends Current Depth, exposure, a mark, or a relationship (Q497; §24 CONSEQUENCE — creditor named). An unpriced demonstration is a FAIL at the §24 diagnostic.
4. **REVERSAL**: the mispricing **corrected** — must reprice **≥1 relationship and ≥1 faction position** (the old "every major reveal reprices ≥2 factions" rule [ADAPT]ed to reversals). Never applause-without-invoice: the reversal's *witnesses* and its *costs* are both on-page.
5. **RECOGNITION**: the world **updates its file** on him — but recognition is ***access*, not rank** (Q384 solution 1): a door opens, a category is created (ch115), a method enters the manual (ch88), a debt is acknowledged (ch26). He never climbs the ladder; the system bends *around* him. Recognition that arrives as *rank* is a design error — flag and rewrite.
6. **HIGHER EXPECTATION** (the cycle's output): the **new harder problem** — the next undervaluer is higher-status, or the stakes are higher-cost, or the *same* actor returns with a varied structure (escalation *with* variation, §6 rule 2). The cycle feeds the Contract loop: HIGHER EXPECTATION *is* the next phenomenon's QUESTION (chaining — LONG_TERM_ROADMAP §6).

**Irregularity schedule** [RECOMMENDATION] (Q386): reversals fire **irregularly** — ≤3 per arc, never at fixed intervals, never as the arc's *purpose*. Arcs I–III demonstrate the rhythm: 3, 3(+1 conversion), 3(+1 requisition). A fourth reversal in any arc requires naming, in the batch record, why this arc earns it (§26 motif discipline).

## §5. The "why is this F-rank different?" → "what exactly is Daichi?" curiosity ladder, ch1–100 [PROPOSED]

Each rung's question must be **posed by phenomena, never asserted** (Q486's law). Each rung gives a *partial* answer (SOP §6) that opens the next rung:

- **Rung 1 (ch1–10): "Why is this F-rank *different*?"** The ERROR is *interesting*, not pitiable (Q495's machine: bureaucracies file ERROR as broken — but the *reader* sees the artifact react). Partial answer: the artifact reacts to *him* (F-01/F-03 paid ch7). New question: what does it want?
- **Rung 2 (ch10–30): "What does the artifact want?"** It chose him (choosing paid ch7); it has twelve hollows (F-02 paid ch17); it marks him (ch15). Partial answer: it *binds* — it is a repository (the directive's premise, *shown*). New question: why him? why this family?
- **Rung 3 (ch30–70): "Why him? Why this family?"** The appraisal history (F-04); the previous holder (F-05 paid ch70); the transfer rule (it cannot be taken). Partial answer: the choosing has *precedents* — and precedents have costs (M-04). New question: what does the choosing *cost*, and what happens when the system tries to use him?
- **Rung 4 (ch70–100): "What exactly is Daichi?"** The deployment (Arc III); the L4 refusal (the fence *demonstrated*); the filing category (classification-escape #1 — the system's first bend); the marks' pattern becoming noticeable. Partial answer: he is **filed but unpriced** — UNFILED→ANSWERABLE in motion. The rung's question is *never* answered — it becomes the series' standing curiosity (M-11's weak reading operates; the strong reading stays budgeted).

**Ladder rule**: no rung may be climbed by *assertion* (a character explaining him); every rung is climbed by *phenomena* (the artifact doing something the taxonomy can't file). The ladder is the satisfaction engine's long game — the face-slap beats are its short game.

## §6. Anti-bullying guardrails [RECOMMENDATION]

When undervaluation must STOP escalating and convert — the rules that keep the engine from becoming a humiliation farm:

1. **Per-actor contempt cap**: ≤2 contempt beats per recurring actor, then **mandatory conversion** — to respect (rival, ch44), fear (S-rank senior, ch110), political interest (researcher, ch26; institute), or watchful neutrality (skeptic, ch29). One-shot actors: exactly 1 beat.
2. **No escalation without variation**: if an actor's contempt escalates, the *structure column* must change (researcher: C4 → C2, ch4 → ch16). Same-structure escalation is bullying — cut or rewrite.
3. **Daichi's response must evolve** (Q387): hurt → tactical → weary → strategic. The same reaction twice to the same actor is a §26 mechanic — vary or cut.
4. **Name the cost**: every cycle states what the contempt cost him (ch13's safe job costs field access; ch29's audit costs exposure-risk; ch76–88's deployment costs the operation's failure). Contempt without cost is sadism; cost without acknowledgment is numbness.
5. **Conversions pay**: the converted actor must *pay* for the earlier contempt — a debt acknowledged (ch26: citation + access owed; ch44: professional deference; ch88: his method enters the manual). Unpaid conversion is cheap grace — the reader feels it.
6. **Hard stop — the predatory line**: any actor whose contempt becomes *physical endangerment for sport* is reclassified from cycle-partner to **antagonist** (ch55–63's ex-Esper: removed as threat, kept as warning) or removed. The story does not farm humiliation.
7. **The cultivated cover is never costless** (Q386 solution 2's constraint): every chapter where Daichi *uses* undervaluation as cover (ch13+) must show its price — the cover burns at ch119–120 (Arc III's end), on schedule, invoiced.
8. **Civilian contempt never becomes worship** (ch104's guardrail): the pile-on converts to *contested attention*, not adoration. The story's contract with the reader is vindication, not coronation — he stays a clerk.
