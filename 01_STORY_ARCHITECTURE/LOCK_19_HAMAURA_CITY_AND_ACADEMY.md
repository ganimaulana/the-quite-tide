# LOCK 19 — HAMAURA CITY & HAMAURA ACADEMY

> **STATUS: AUTHOR-LOCKED — 2026-09-24**
> **Scope:** THE QUIET TIDE — current Academy branch naming and geographic presentation.

## 1. Official Names

- **City:** Hamaura City
- **Japanese rendering:** 浜浦市 (Hamaura-shi)
- **Academy:** Hamaura Academy
- **Common in-scene reference after introduction:** the Academy / Hamaura

## 2. Naming Rationale

**Hamaura (浜浦)** carries a natural coastal Japanese place-name impression: **浜 (hama)** evokes a shore or beach, while **浦 (ura)** evokes a cove, inlet, or sheltered coastal area.

The name should provide quiet coastal atmosphere without becoming an explicit symbolic mystery. Characters do not explain the etymology in ordinary dialogue.

## 3. Academy Presentation

Hamaura Academy is the principal Academy of the Arc I setting and the visible institutional center of Daichi's progression.

The Academy remains the **MAIN PLOT**. Records & Archives remains a **SUBPLOT / information-access method**.

Academy progression includes classes and practical training, examinations and tests, missions and field trials, division and status hierarchy, cadet social friction and recognition, and institutional records and consequences.

Records are Daichi's information advantage, not the story's primary setting.

## 4. Inter-Academy Rivalry

The wider setting contains multiple Academies and institutional rivalry may exist between them.

**Do not foreground this in the opening chapters.**

Arc I should establish Hamaura Academy naturally through Daichi's daily experience. Rivalry, exchange events, inter-Academy examinations, joint exercises, or institutional competition may emerge later when the plot naturally requires them.

No early exposition should require the reader to memorize other Academies merely to understand Daichi's immediate story.

## 5. Prose Usage Rule

The first clear institutional introduction should use **Hamaura Academy**. After the reader knows the institution, normal prose should prefer **the Academy** unless the full name is useful for emphasis, contrast, official paperwork, or introduction of an outside institution.

Avoid repeatedly writing **Hamaura Academy** in every paragraph. The name is an anchor, not a terminology burden.

## 6. Canon Safety

- Do not add a new Academy rivalry subplot to CH001–015 merely to establish the name.
- Do not add Academy-history exposition solely because the name has been locked.
- Do not make the coastal meaning of Hamaura a supernatural rule or hidden clue.
- Do not imply that Hamaura Academy is the world's only Academy.
- Preserve Academy-first hierarchy: Academy generates problems; Records helps Daichi understand them; evidence changes consequences.

## 7. Author North Star

> **Let the reader learn Hamaura Academy by watching Daichi live in it, not by being lectured about it.**

*End of LOCK 19 — Hamaura City & Hamaura Academy.*
