### §3A. Echo / Academy early implementation

The early Echo chain follows: **Observation → Hypothesis → Test → Result → Updated Hypothesis → Second Test → Resolution/Failure.**

Holding the Seal alone does not guarantee an Echo. The trigger follows the author-locked conditions in LOCK_15_ECHO_AND_WEAPON_CONTRACT: recognition of a genuine inconsistency/anomaly, proximity to the relevant anomalous trace/object/phenomenon, and active investigative intent. The trigger may still fail.

Echo side effects are sensory and temporary, not Depth costs or Contract tolls. Residual sensory overlap can create false certainty, so Daichi must verify fragments against the world.

The Academy preparation chain is **Weapon Knowledge → Weapon Familiarization → Weapon Mastery → later Weapon Contract**. Early Weapon Mastery is ordinary training. Daichi should be stronger at understanding weapon information than at physical execution; the later Contract elevates trained capability rather than revealing hidden innate mastery.

The early retention layer also carries cross-functional Operations Support exposure, division/status/rank segregation, intelligence-based face-slaps, social dismissal of Daichi's weapon knowledge as merely "Records" knowledge, institutional separation of artifact behavior from Esper Ability, and the concept that genuine SP solving can sometimes lead to a Contract.

These are supporting beats, not new plotlines. They must never displace the Academy main progression.

## §4. Payoff plan: the reader-trust schedule [RECOMMENDATION]

Trust is a *cadence*, not a promise. Two clocks (Q357), one schedule:

- **Every chapter**: story state moves (SOP §2 [KEEP]) — at least one tracked state changes.
- **Every 1–3 chapters**: a concrete, *priced* payoff (the anti-desert rule: no 10-chapter span without one; PACING file §4). Payoffs need not be combat — the non-combat catalog (FACE_SLAP file §3) is the primary instrument.
- **Every arc**: the Contract-anomaly mystery opens *and* closes (pre-paid debt, Q369) — the reader learns that *this story finishes what it starts*. One standing thread paid per arc minimum (M-04's identity at Arc II; the slot-cap at Arc I; the fragment's completion at Arc VI).
- **Every macro-phase** (~100–150 ch): a cosmology-clock servicing — a partial answer on the slow mysteries (M-01 deepened, M-05 asked, M-10 legible, M-06 posed) — the reader learns that *the deep water moves*.
- **Closable vs. unresolvable, labeled**: M-04 and M-10's grammar are *closable* (the schedule needs real answers); M-02, M-03, M-05, M-06 are *unresolvable by design* (the schedule needs permanent depth). The distinction is stated in the reboot 33-equivalent — never relabeled mid-stream (Q498's governance).
- **Partial-answer rule** (SOP §6 [KEEP]): every payoff is partial by default — QUESTION → INVESTIGATION → PARTIAL ANSWER → CONTRADICTION/CONSEQUENCE → NEW QUESTION. Full closures are budgeted events (M-04's horizons), never accidents.
- **The finale's form** [ADAPT] (Q486 solution 1, adopted as reboot law): **the cosmology is closed by phenomena, never by assertion.** No Contract may *assert* cosmological truth — Contracts grant capabilities and witness, never answers. The finale must be *demonstrated* (Q486 solution 2's participatory shape available: the final Contract puts him *in* the truth rather than telling it).
