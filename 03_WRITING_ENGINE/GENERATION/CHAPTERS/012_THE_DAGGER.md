# WEAPON EXPOSURE

The weapons room smelled of oil, old wood, and metal that had been handled by too many students.

Arthur stood before the instructor with a practice dagger in one hand.

Evelyn was testing a short sword at the far end of the room. Clara was learning how to keep a shield close to her body without locking her elbow. Iris was already moving through a basic sword sequence faster than Arthur could comfortably follow.

"You have already failed practical combat," the instructor said.

"Yes."

"So why do you want access to the full rack?"

"I want to know whether the failure belongs to me or to the weapon."

The instructor almost smiled.

"It belongs to you."

"Probably. I would still like to test it."

Permission came with rules: no live edge, no unsupervised combat, return every weapon to its marked place, report every injury.

Arthur accepted all of them.

He started with the dagger.

The dagger was fast. Motion Sense gave him information about wrist rotation and changes in grip, but the range was short enough that a correct read could still arrive too late.

He watched Evelyn's sword from the corner of his eye.

"You're watching the wrong weapon," she said.

Arthur looked at her.

"Why?"

"Because you are trying to decide what you should use by watching someone else use it."

Arthur considered that.

"That's a reasonable criticism."

"I know."

She returned to her drill.

The spear came next.

Its movement began farther from the body. A small adjustment at the rear hand changed the point at the other end.

The axe punished another mistake. Its momentum pulled Arthur's shoulders forward and nearly carried his foot with it.

The bow was stranger still. His body understood the pull quickly. His timing did not.

Clara watched him put the bow down.

"You look disappointed."

"I am collecting failures."

"That sounds depressing."

"It is efficient."

Clara laughed.

Then Arthur reached the sword.

He picked it up.

Nothing happened.

No voice.

No Contract offer.

No sudden knowledge.

The sword was simply a sword.

He began with the same drills.

The first ten minutes were ordinary.

Then he noticed something he had not noticed with the other weapons.

The sword made his own movement visible.

When his wrist turned too early, the blade drifted.

When his hips moved late, the blade lagged.

When his weight settled correctly, the weapon arrived where his body had already decided to be.

Iris stopped beside him.

"You're holding too tightly."

Arthur loosened his grip.

"Better."

"How did you know?"

Iris looked at the sword.

"Because you moved like someone trying not to lose it."

Arthur tried again.

This time the blade followed his movement more naturally.

That was not mastery.

It was a useful relationship.

He put the sword down.

The private interface was no longer empty.

```text
CONTRACT SYSTEM

Status: ACTIVE

Natural Ability
Motion Sense: ACTIVE

Contract
Benkei Seal: PARTIAL
```

Arthur looked at it for a moment.

Then he looked back at the weapon rack.

Tomorrow, he would test the sword again.
