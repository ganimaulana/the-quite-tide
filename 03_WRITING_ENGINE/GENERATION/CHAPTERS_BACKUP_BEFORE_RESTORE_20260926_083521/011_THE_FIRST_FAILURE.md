# THE FIRST FAILURE

The failure happened in twelve seconds.

Arthur remembered all twelve.

The exercise was simple: close distance, disarm the training opponent, reset.

Motion Sense caught the shoulder movement immediately.

Arthur moved too early.

The shoulder had been a feint.

The real attack came from the other side.

The padded baton hit his ribs.

Arthur recovered.

Then the opponent stepped inside his reach.

The baton stopped at his throat.

"Again?" the instructor asked.

"Again."

The second round lasted longer.

It ended the same way.

Arthur had read the movement correctly. He had used the information badly.

Motion Sense was not combat technique.

His body was strong enough to recover. His technique was not strong enough to capitalize on it.

After the exercise, Arthur opened the private interface.

________________________________
CONTRACT SYSTEM

Status: NO CONTRACT

NATURAL ABILITY
Motion Sense: ACTIVE
________________________________

Nothing else.

Arthur looked at the weapon rack.

He needed technique. Not another explanation.

He picked up a practice dagger.

His hand recognized the shape before his mind did.

A fragment returned.

A knife. A monster. Blood.

Nothing more.

He put the dagger down.

Then picked it up again.

He would test the connection rather than trust it.
