# THE PRICE OF WINNING

The first congratulations was polite.

The second was not.

A Combat cadet stopped Arthur outside the training hall.

"You got points for that?"

"The team did."

"Your name is on the record."

Arthur shrugged.

"Then the record is accurate."

The cadet stepped closer.

"You were Support."

"Yes."

"You gave the Combat lead instructions."

"He accepted them."

That answer made the other student angrier than an argument would have.

Arthur understood why.

His success did not fit the Academy's social expectation.

Support was supposed to make specialists more effective.

Arthur had done that.

But he had also noticed something the specialist had missed.

That made the boundary uncomfortable.

Later, Pram found him.

"They will test you again."

"I know."

"Good."

Arthur looked at him.

"Why tell me?"

"Because winning once is an event. Winning twice becomes evidence."

Arthur remembered the assessment terminal.

Evidence.

That was becoming a dangerous word.

The following morning, his name appeared on a new exercise notice.

**PARTNERED ASSESSMENT — REED / MERCER.**

Arthur stared at it.

He had never met Mercer.
