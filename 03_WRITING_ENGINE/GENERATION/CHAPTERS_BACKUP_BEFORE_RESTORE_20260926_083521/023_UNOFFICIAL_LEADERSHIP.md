# UNOFFICIAL LEADERSHIP

The next mission lost its lead before the first major decision.

The formal commander froze when the phenomenon changed its route.

Everyone waited.

Arthur watched the team.

Combat was ready to move.

Medical was waiting for a safe extraction path.

Tactical had the map open.

Arthur spoke.

"Do not enter yet."

The commander looked at him.

"Why?"

"The safe route is changing every thirty seconds."

"Then what do we do?"

Arthur pointed to Tactical.

"Mark the changes."

To Medical:

"Move the extraction point ten metres north."

To Combat:

"Hold."

The orders were simple.

They were also not his to give.

For one second, nobody moved.

Then the formal lead nodded.

"Do it."

The team moved.

Arthur did not become commander.

He became useful enough that the commander could make a decision.

The phenomenon was contained.

Afterward, the commander approached him.

"You should have said it sooner."

Arthur considered that.

"You were still deciding."

The commander almost smiled.

"Next time, decide faster."

Arthur nodded.

That was the first time someone had treated him as part of the operational chain rather than an extra pair of hands.

Then the commander lowered his voice.

"One problem."

Arthur waited.

"The next exercise has no designated commander."
