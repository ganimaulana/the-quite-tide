# THE REMAINDER

The first field plan failed.

They sealed the annex. The sound appeared outside it.

They moved the team outside. The sound appeared inside.

The Tactical cadet marked every recurrence. The Recon cadet searched the roof. Medical waited by the vehicles.

Arthur watched the sequence.

Three seconds. Same voices. Different location.

Then a generator started.

The phenomenon repeated the generator sound.

Arthur looked at the log.

The new sound lasted three seconds too.

"It copies the last sound."

The lead shook his head. "The old report says that."

"The old report says it repeats voices. It does not say what happens when the environment changes."

The generator stopped.

The false generator continued for three seconds.

Then silence.

Arthur looked at the team.

"We need to stop giving it new material."

The lead hesitated. The work crew wanted the equipment running. The field team wanted the phenomenon quiet.

The decision was not theoretical anymore.

The lead shut the generator down.

The false sound continued once.

Then stopped.

Arthur wrote the result.

The phenomenon was not merely repeating sound.

It was retaining the most recent sound available to it.

The distinction would matter in the next operation.

Then the team checked the recording.

The final three seconds were not the generator.

They were Arthur's voice.

He had not spoken during the recording.
