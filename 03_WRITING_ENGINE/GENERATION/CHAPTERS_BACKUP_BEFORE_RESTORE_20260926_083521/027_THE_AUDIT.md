# THE AUDIT

The audit began with one question.

"Why did you bypass the approved containment frame?"

Arthur sat across from an Academy reviewer.

"We did not have it."

"You had requested it."

"The request was denied because another Division had priority."

"Then why did you continue?"

Arthur placed the mission log on the table.

"Because the exercise was still active."

The reviewer read the first page.

"You changed the procedure."

"Yes."

"Without authorization."

"Within the exercise's safety boundary."

The reviewer looked up.

"That distinction matters."

"I know."

The reviewer turned to the second page.

The tool positions.

The timestamps.

The observers.

The test sequence.

Then he reached the final page.

His expression changed.

"Where did this old case reference come from?"

Arthur had not included the old case.

Or thought he had not.

He checked the report.

The cold-case catalogue number appeared in the appendix.

He had copied it without thinking.

The reviewer closed the file.

"You are not being audited for the ferry case."

Arthur waited.

"You are being audited because your field report connected a current exercise to a restricted historical case."

That was different.

"Is that prohibited?"

"Not automatically."

"Then what is the problem?"

The reviewer slid a second document across the table.

It was a correction notice.

The historical case had been classified as closed.

The current exercise had produced behaviour that matched one of its unresolved observations.

Two records disagreed.

The Academy wanted to know which one was wrong.

Arthur looked at the dates.

One of them had been altered recently.

He pointed at the margin.

"This correction was not part of the original file."

The reviewer went still.

"How can you tell?"

"The ink is newer. The transfer number is from a later filing system."

The reviewer took the page back.

The audit was no longer about Arthur's procedure.

It was about the record.

Arthur understood the change immediately.

Someone had touched the old case recently.

And now the Academy knew that Arthur had noticed.
