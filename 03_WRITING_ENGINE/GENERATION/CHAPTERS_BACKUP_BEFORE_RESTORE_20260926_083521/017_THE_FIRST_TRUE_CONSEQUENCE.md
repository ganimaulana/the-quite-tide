# THE FIRST TRUE CONSEQUENCE

The mark on Arthur's palm had not faded.

That was the first problem.

The second was that the thimble had stopped returning.

Arthur did not like either result.

He went back to the controlled storage room and repeated the last test.

The thimble was moved to another tray.

It stayed there.

Arthur waited until the next morning.

It stayed there.

He moved it again.

Nothing.

The Rule he had proved before was no longer behaving the same way.

Arthur opened his notebook.

Observation: return to first recorded intake location.

Correction: after Contract invocation, no return observed.

He stopped writing.

The easy conclusion was that the Contract had changed the phenomenon.

He crossed it out.

He had no proof.

The other possibility was worse: the Rule had never meant what he thought it meant.

He rebuilt the test from the beginning.

Different tray.

Different handler.

Different custody record.

No return.

Arthur stared at the thimble.

The confirmed sentence came back to him.

*It is bound to the hand, and not to the name.*

He had interpreted that as ownership.

That interpretation might be wrong.

He wrote:

**Confirmed:** the Contract distinguishes a hand from a name.

**Unconfirmed:** the phenomenon follows the first possessor.

He underlined the second line twice.

The private interface opened.

________________________________
CONTRACT SYSTEM

THE POLITE KNOCK

Integration: PARTIAL

Uses remaining for this phenomenon: 0

Known consequence: Depth expenditure detected

Unknown: full binding condition
________________________________

Arthur closed it.

The System had given him information.

It had not given him an explanation.

That distinction was becoming important.

He carried the thimble back to storage.

On the way out, a support instructor stopped him.

"You are on the field roster for tomorrow."

Arthur looked at the notice.

"Why?"

"Because your last report was useful."

That answer was more unsettling than praise.

Useful meant people could ask him to do it again.

Arthur folded the notice.

His first Contract had cost him something he could not yet measure.

His next field assignment would cost something more concrete if he made the wrong decision.

For the first time, the Contract was no longer just a mystery.

It was a responsibility.

The assignment sheet arrived before he could put the thought away.

**FIELD SUPPORT — ACTIVE SEEP.**

**Primary specialist unavailable.**

Arthur read the final line.

**Replacement required.**
