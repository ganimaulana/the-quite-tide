# THE FIRST SOLVE

The phenomenon was a brass thimble.

It returned to the same tray after every transfer.

Arthur moved it.

It returned.

He gave it to another student.

It returned.

He repeated the test while another instructor watched.

It returned again.

Arthur wrote the Rule:

**The thimble returns to its first recorded intake location despite later custody transfers.**

He checked the evidence again.

Then once more.

The private interface opened.

________________________________

Arthur did not accept immediately.

He looked at his notes.

Observation. Hypothesis. Test. Result.

The Rule had survived repeated testing.

He accepted the offer.

________________________________
CONTRACT ACCEPTED

THE POLITE KNOCK

Integration: PARTIAL

Unknown:

Complete consequences
________________________________

Nothing else happened.

Arthur looked at the thimble.

He had earned the Contract.

He had not yet learned what it could do.

That would come next.
