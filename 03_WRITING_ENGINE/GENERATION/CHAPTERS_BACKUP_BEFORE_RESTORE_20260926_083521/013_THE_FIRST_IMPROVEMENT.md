# THE FIRST IMPROVEMENT

Arthur built one drill.

Draw. Step. Turn. Stop. Return.

He changed one variable at a time.

At first, Motion Sense made him worse.

He saw movement and reacted before his body was ready.

He slowed down.

The next repetition lasted longer.

He failed again.

But the failure changed.

His foot landed closer to the correct position. His shoulder turned later. His blade stopped closer to the intended line.

That was improvement.

Not power.

Not a level.

Technique.

The instructor watched from the doorway.

"Why that weapon?"

Arthur answered honestly.

"It exposes my mistakes."

"Then keep using it."

Arthur repeated the drill.

By the end of the session, he could complete the sequence without thinking about every individual movement.

That was the first step toward technique.

The System remained silent.

Then the instructor placed a real field report on the table.

"You solved the movement problem," she said. "Now solve this one."

Arthur opened the report.

The phenomenon inside had already injured two cadets.
