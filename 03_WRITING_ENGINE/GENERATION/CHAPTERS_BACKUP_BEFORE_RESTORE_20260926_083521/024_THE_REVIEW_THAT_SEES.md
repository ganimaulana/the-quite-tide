# THE REVIEW THAT SEES

The review board watched the mission recording.

Then it read the report.

"Who made the route recommendation?"

The commander answered.

"Reed."

"Who authorized it?"

"I did."

The board nodded.

That distinction mattered.

Arthur had provided information and a recommendation.

The commander had made the decision.

The Academy recorded both.

His Cohort Performance Record increased again.

His Esper Rank remained F.

The review also added a note to his Support profile:

**Reliable under plan deviation.**

Arthur read the sentence twice.

It was not praise.

It was an operational assessment.

That was better.

Later, Owen found him outside the training hall.

"People are talking about you."

"Why?"

"Because you keep doing things the F-rank is not supposed to do."

Arthur looked at the Rank F badge.

"The F-rank is not supposed to do anything."

Owen laughed.

"Exactly."

Arthur looked toward the Academy board.

His Rank had not moved.

His points had.

His reputation had.

The three numbers were beginning to tell different stories.

He suspected that would become a problem.

The next line on the review sheet confirmed it.

**REQUEST FOR SECOND DIAGNOSTIC EVALUATION — APPROVED.**

Arthur stared at it.

The Academy wanted to know whether the machine would fail again.
