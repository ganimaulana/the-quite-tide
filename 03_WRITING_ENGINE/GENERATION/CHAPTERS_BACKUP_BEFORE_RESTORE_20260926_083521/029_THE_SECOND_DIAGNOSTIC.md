# THE SECOND DIAGNOSTIC

The re-examination request arrived without changing Arthur's Rank.

That was the first sentence on the form.

**DIAGNOSTIC REPEAT — NOT ANNUAL CLASSIFICATION.**

Arthur read it twice.

The Academy wanted another reading because his recent performance and his original classification did not sit comfortably together.

That did not mean the Academy could simply promote him.

The assessment terminal was connected to the annual classification system.

The instructors could test him.

They could document discrepancies.

They could not manually assign a different Rank.

Arthur entered the chamber.

The first test measured conventional output.

Low.

The second measured control.

Better than his first assessment.

The third measured practical movement under Resonance.

Better again.

The terminal paused.

Arthur watched the display.

The same three-beat hesitation.

Then:

**ERROR**

**UNDEFINED**

The room became quiet.

One examiner reached for the manual override.

The supervising instructor stopped him.

"No."

The examiner looked at him.

"We do not assign the classification ourselves."

They restarted the diagnostic.

The result was the same.

The official record remained:

**RANK F — IRREGULAR.**

Arthur left the chamber unchanged on paper.

But the instructors had more information than before.

His physical performance had improved.

His Resonance control had improved.

His conventional output remained low.

The classification system still could not produce a clean model of him.

Outside the room, the examiner spoke quietly.

"Next annual cycle."

Arthur nodded.

He did not feel disappointed.

The machine had done exactly what the institution had told it to do.

It had classified what it could.

And failed where it could not.

Arthur was beginning to suspect that the failure itself was information.

The review officer closed the file.

"Your Rank remains F."

Arthur nodded.

Then she added:

"The Institute has requested access to the original assessment data."
