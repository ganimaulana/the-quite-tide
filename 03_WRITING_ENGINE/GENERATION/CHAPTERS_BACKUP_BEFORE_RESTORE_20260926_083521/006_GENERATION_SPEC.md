# CHAPTER 006 — GENERATION SPEC

**Status:** ACTIVE STRUCTURAL REWRITE SPEC — 2026-09-26

## Function

CH006 introduces the Impossible Record and establishes Arthur's investigation method without turning him into an Archive clerk.

## Required beats

1. Arthur is operating as an Operations Support / Field Support cadet.
2. He encounters a record whose chronology is internally impossible.
3. He tests ordinary explanations before considering anything anomalous.
4. The institution confirms that the discrepancy cannot yet be resolved.
5. The record becomes a field-relevant lead.
6. No Contract is offered in this chapter.
7. The System does not solve the record.
8. No manual Esper Rank reassessment occurs.

## Mystery fence

The cause of the impossible record remains unresolved. Do not reveal artifact origin, previous-holder identity, Contract #2, or the meaning of Arthur's ERROR/UNDEFINED classification.

## Role fence

Arthur may consult Records & Archives when a Support problem requires historical information. He is not assigned there and must not be written as a records clerk, records body, or attached observer.

## Pacing gate

The chapter must end with the impossible record becoming a concrete lead into CH007, not with another passive filing sequence.
