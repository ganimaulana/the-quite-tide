# THE OFFER

The Institute did not send a letter.

It sent a person.

Dr. Miriam Kessler met Arthur after his diagnostic repeat in a quiet room beside the Academy research wing.

"You know why I am here."

Arthur sat down.

"The old ferry case."

"Partly."

She placed a thin folder on the table.

Inside was a copy of the transfer chain.

The private research office.

The missing subject.

The return to Academy custody.

Arthur looked at the final page.

The name of the research office had been removed.

"Why show me this?"

"Because the Institute has records the Academy does not."

"And what do you want?"

Kessler did not answer immediately.

"Access to your artifact."

Arthur closed the folder.

"No."

"You did not hear the terms."

"I heard the important one."

She smiled faintly.

"Fair. Then hear the rest. We would provide research access, protected storage, specialist consultation, and a stipend. You remain enrolled at Ravenscroft. The artifact remains yours."

"And you study it."

"Yes."

"Separately from me?"

"Ideally."

Arthur looked at the folder again.

"What happened to the previous holder?"

Kessler's expression changed.

"That is the question we cannot answer."

The offer suddenly mattered more.

Not because it was generous.

Because the Institute knew there had been another holder.

And because someone at the Institute had decided Arthur should hear about it now.

"I will not accept yet."

"I expected that."

"Why?"

"Because you read before you sign."

Arthur stood.

The Institute had not recruited him because he was Rank F.

It had noticed something else.

He did not know what that was yet.

But for the first time, the pressure around the artifact had moved beyond the Academy.
