# IRREGULAR VICTORY

The cooperative readiness test was supposed to be simple.

Contain the training phenomenon. Protect the team. Finish inside the time limit.

Arthur was assigned Support.

The scenario changed after six minutes.

The marked safe route became unsafe.

The Combat lead wanted to push through.

Arthur stopped him.

"Wait."

"Why?"

"The movement is repeating."

The floor marker shifted again.

Arthur pointed.

"Every time someone steps across that line, the safe lane moves two metres east."

The lead stared at him.

"You sure?"

"No. But we can test it."

They tested it.

The lane moved.

The team changed formation.

Arthur used the communication unit to relay the new route while the Combat students handled the front.

The exercise ended with thirty seconds remaining.

The board updated that afternoon.

Arthur's Cohort Points increased.

Not because he was F-rank.

Not because he was an Irregular.

Because the team had used his observation to complete the task.

A Combat student looked at the board.

"He actually helped."

Arthur heard him.

He did not turn around.

That was enough.

Then the cohort board refreshed.

Arthur's name had moved.

Not to the top.

But high enough for everyone to notice.

Someone had already written beside it:

**F-RANK?**
