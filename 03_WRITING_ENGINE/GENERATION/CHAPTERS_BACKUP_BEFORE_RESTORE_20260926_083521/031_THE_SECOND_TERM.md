# THE SECOND TERM

The Academy reopened with a new mission board.

Arthur stopped at the first notice.

**JOINT TEAM REQUEST — OLD FERRY DISTRICT**

He read the location twice.

The Institute had requested a controlled field exercise there.

Records support was required.

So was an Operations Support cadet.

Arthur's name was on the preliminary roster.

Owen read the notice beside him.

"You know that place."

"I know the records."

"Same thing?"

"No."

That was the problem.

The cold case was supposed to be closed.

The Institute had just reopened the district.

Arthur looked at the mission note.

**Objective: observe object-class Seep behaviour and determine whether prior containment assumptions remain valid.**

No mention of the previous holder.

No mention of the private research office.

Nothing about the transfer chain.

That omission was itself interesting.

Arthur folded the notice.

The Institute had made an offer.

Now it had arranged a mission that touched the same mystery.

He still had not accepted.

He suspected they knew that.

That meant the mission was either a coincidence.

Or a test.

Arthur preferred evidence.

He reported to the briefing room.

The mission packet was waiting on his seat.

One line had been highlighted.

**Artifact response expected.**

Arthur had not told the Institute about that response.
