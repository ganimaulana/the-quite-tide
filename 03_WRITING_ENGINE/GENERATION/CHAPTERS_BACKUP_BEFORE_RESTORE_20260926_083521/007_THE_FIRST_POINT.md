# THE FIRST FIELD TEST

The field roster arrived that evening.

Arthur was not listed as an observer.

He was listed as Support: communication, route observation, containment assistance.

The anomaly site was the old annex from the impossible record.

At the briefing, the Combat lead looked at Arthur's badge.

"F-rank."

"Yes."

"Support?"

"Yes."

The lead nodded. "Then keep the team supplied and tell me if the situation changes."

Arthur accepted the assignment.

The file said the phenomenon was stationary.

The team entered expecting a localized sound-bleed.

The first recurrence came from the stair.

The second came from the service road.

Arthur checked the time.

Three seconds. Same duration. Different location.

The team moved to the road.

The sound came from the building again.

The lead swore.

Arthur opened the field log.

"It is not moving."

The lead looked at him. "Then what is moving?"

Arthur listened.

"The location."

That answer changed the plan.

The team stopped treating the sound as an object.

They began recording where it appeared instead.

For the first time, Arthur was not reading the file after the fact.

He was helping build the next record while the phenomenon was happening.

Then the sound came from directly behind him.

Arthur turned.

Nothing was there.

The field recorder had captured something else.

Three seconds of his own voice.

Arthur had not spoken.
