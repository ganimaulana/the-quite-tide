# THE IMPOSSIBLE RECORD

The impossible record was found during field preparation.

Arthur was checking the old response history for the next Support exercise when the dates stopped making sense.

**Intake: later.**

**Response: after intake.**

**Closure: before intake.**

He checked the original scan. Same dates.

He checked the transfer record. Same dates.

The case had been closed before it had been opened.

Arthur did not assume anomaly.

He tested ordinary explanations first.

Clerical error? Possible once. Not twice on separate records.

Backfilled file? Possible, but the closure still preceded the intake.

Reused case number? The index showed only one assignment.

He brought the discrepancy to the Support supervisor.

"Do you want the case reopened?"

"I want to know whether it was ever valid."

The supervisor read the file.

"We cannot answer that from this record."

Arthur nodded.

That was the important part.

The institution did not pretend to know.

The file was flagged for controlled review.

Arthur copied the case number into his field notebook.

He did not know yet that the next assignment would take him to the site connected to it.

The impossible record had stopped being a curiosity.

It had become a lead.
