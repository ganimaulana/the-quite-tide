# THE SUPPORT TEST

The exercise failed before it began.

The assigned Combat cadet was injured during warm-up.

The Recon cadet had a scheduling conflict.

The remaining team looked at the roster.

Two specialists missing.

One Support cadet available.

Arthur.

The instructor changed the exercise.

"Reed, you are filling the gap."

Arthur looked at the equipment table.

Communication unit.

Containment straps.

Medical marker.

Practice dagger.

Short spear.

He picked up the communication unit first.

The team moved into the training structure.

The exercise phenomenon was simple: a room that shifted its safe route whenever a person crossed the wrong threshold.

The Combat position was empty.

Arthur was not going to pretend he could replace a Combat specialist.

He could, however, keep the team informed.

The first route failed.

The second route failed faster.

The team began arguing about which door was real.

Arthur watched the movement.

Motion Sense caught the change in the door mechanism before the visual shift completed.

"Stop."

Everyone looked at him.

"The left route is changing after the third person crosses."

"You sure?" Owen asked.

"No."

That answer made Owen hesitate.

Arthur pointed at the floor.

"But I can test it."

They sent one person through.

The route changed.

Arthur sent the second through a different threshold.

Nothing changed.

The team moved.

The room shifted again.

Arthur adjusted the route.

Not perfectly.

Twice he gave the wrong call.

Both times the team lost time.

But nobody was trapped.

When the exercise ended, the instructor reviewed the result.

"You did not solve the phenomenon."

Arthur nodded.

"You kept the team functional after the original plan failed."

That went into the Academy Performance Record.

Arthur looked at the board later.

The points were modest.

The result was not enough to make him a top student.

But his position moved.

He understood why.

The Academy had not awarded him points for being unusual.

It had awarded him points for doing a useful job under changed conditions.

That was different.

That was Support.

That evening, Arthur opened the Contract Interface.

________________________________
CONTRACT SYSTEM

THE POLITE KNOCK

Status: PARTIAL

No new Contract.
________________________________

He closed it.

Today had not been about the Contract.

It had been about discovering what he could do when the plan stopped working.

That was a question worth answering again.

The team leader stopped him at the exit.

"Next exercise, you are not backup."

Arthur waited.

"You are responsible for the fallback route."
