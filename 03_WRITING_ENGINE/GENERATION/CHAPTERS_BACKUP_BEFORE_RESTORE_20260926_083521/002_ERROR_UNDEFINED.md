# ERROR / UNDEFINED

One week after the assessment, Arthur Reed stood outside Ravenscroft Academy with his admission papers folded inside his jacket.

**Rank: F**

**Classification: Irregular**

**Division: Operations Support**

**Specialization: Field Support**

He read the four lines again.

The first three were ordinary enough.

The fourth was not.

A clerk had explained that Field Support covered equipment, containment assistance, route observation, communication, extraction, and other practical work around specialist teams.

Arthur had asked about the assessment.

"Why did the terminal stop?"

The clerk had looked at the screen.

"It returned an ERROR/UNDEFINED state before the Rank F classification."

"Does that happen often?"

"Not often enough for me to have an answer."

Arthur had liked that answer more than a confident one.

Now the Academy gates opened.

Hundreds of students moved through them.

Some wore Division pins already. Others were still carrying orientation papers.

Arthur entered with the crowd.

He did not know why the assessment had failed.

He did not know why he had survived Ravenhurst.

He knew he had killed a monster.

He knew his parents and sister were dead.

He knew the memory around the killing was incomplete.

Those facts were enough.

For now.

The orientation hall was louder than the hospital had been.

An instructor explained the Academy's basic structure: four years, seven Divisions, practical assessments, field readiness, research, support work, and professional pathways.

"Your Division is not your Rank," she said. "Your Rank is not your performance record. Your performance record is not a guarantee of what you will do in the field."

Arthur wrote that down.

A student behind him whispered.

"F-rank."

Arthur did not turn.

The whisper became another whisper.

"That is the survivor from Ravenhurst."

Then silence.

Arthur looked toward the front.

The instructor continued.

"Esper Rank is classified once per academic year by the Academy's assessment system. Faculty cannot simply change a student's Rank because they disagree with the result."

Arthur's pen stopped.

Annual.

So the F would remain.

That was fine.

He had four years to learn what the number actually meant.

That evening, after orientation, Arthur opened the private interface for the first time.

________________________________
CONTRACT SYSTEM

Status: NO CONTRACT

NATURAL ABILITY
Motion Sense: ACTIVE
________________________________

No explanation appeared.

No mission.

No reward.

No answer about the assessment.

Arthur closed the interface.

The next morning, his first-year assessment schedule appeared on the Academy board.

**FOUNDATION PRACTICAL — ALL NEW CADETS.**

Arthur read the last line twice.

Weapons were included.

He remembered a knife.

Not training.

Ravenhurst.

His hand tightened around the paper.

Tomorrow, he would find out whether his body remembered something his mind did not.
