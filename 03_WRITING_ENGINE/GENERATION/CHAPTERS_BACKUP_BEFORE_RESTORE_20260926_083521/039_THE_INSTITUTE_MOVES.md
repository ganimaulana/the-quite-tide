# THE INSTITUTE MOVES

The Institute's next contact did not ask for a meeting.

It sent a formal request to Ravenscroft.

**Controlled appraisal of Reed artifact.**

Arthur read the request in the Support office.

The Academy had approved the preliminary review.

Not the separation.

Only the review.

That distinction mattered.

Kessler arrived before Arthur could ask anyone else.

"They are moving faster."

"Why?"

"Because of the warehouse result."

Arthur looked at the request.

"The field test?"

"The fact that the artifact responded to you while the phenomenon was active."

Arthur frowned.

"That does not prove a Contract."

"The Institute knows that."

"Then what do they think it proves?"

Kessler hesitated.

"Recognition."

Arthur remembered the warning.

Do not separate the holder from the object before confirming recognition.

The Institute wanted exactly that test.

Arthur looked at Kessler.

"Who wrote the warning?"

She did not answer.

That was answer enough to make the question larger.

Then she placed a second document beside the request.

Arthur recognized the signature.

It was the same handwriting from the old warning.
