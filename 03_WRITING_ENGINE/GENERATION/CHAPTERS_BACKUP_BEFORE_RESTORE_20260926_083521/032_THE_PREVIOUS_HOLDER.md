# THE PREVIOUS HOLDER

Dr. Miriam Kessler arrived with the field packet.

She did not mention the offer.

Arthur noticed.

"You are avoiding a subject."

"So are you."

That was the beginning of their working relationship.

The warehouse was smaller than Arthur expected.

The old case had described it as a storage site.

The current team found something else.

The tools had been removed.

The shelves were empty.

Only one object remained.

A stone block sat beneath a sheet of glass.

It was not marked as anomalous.

Arthur crouched beside it.

The artifact in his pouch became warm.

He did not touch the stone.

Kessler saw the change in his expression.

"What happened?"

"Nothing I can prove."

She nodded.

They continued the inspection.

The stone had an old inventory number.

Arthur copied it.

Back at the Academy, Eleanor found the number in the transfer index.

The stone had belonged to the private research office.

It had been returned with the rest of the old case.

There was one additional note.

**Previous holder declined further contact.**

No name.

No address.

No explanation.

Arthur stared at the line.

"This is not enough."

Eleanor nodded.

"No."

"But it proves there was a previous holder."

"It proves someone wrote that there was one."

Arthur looked at her.

She tapped the page.

"Remember the difference."

The distinction bothered him.

It also kept the investigation honest.

Eleanor turned the page.

"There is one more note."

Arthur looked down.

The previous holder had not merely declined further contact.

Someone had marked the file:

**DO NOT REOPEN.**
