# THE CHAIN MOVES

Arthur returned to Eleanor with the corrected page.

She read it once.

"You found a later hand."

"Yes."

"Then stop looking for the missing page."

Arthur frowned.

"Why?"

"Because someone already answered your question."

She turned the page over.

On the back was a transfer code.

Not a case number.

An institutional movement code.

Eleanor checked the archive index.

"This file was transferred three times."

"Where?"

She followed the chain.

Academy archive.

Regional containment office.

Private research custody.

Then back to the Academy.

Arthur stared at the last transfer.

"Why would a private research office return it?"

"That is the useful question."

They found the return record.

The name was redacted.

The reason was not.

**Subject no longer available for continued observation.**

Arthur read the line again.

Not *case closed*.

Not *research completed*.

Subject.

Someone had been studying a person or an object.

The old case was no longer just about tools moving around a warehouse.

It was part of a longer custody chain.

Eleanor closed the screen.

"That is where you stop for today."

"Because it is restricted?"

"Because you now have enough information to make a bad assumption."

Arthur almost smiled.

"You really dislike conclusions."

"I dislike conclusions that arrive before evidence."

He left with three facts:

The case had moved between institutions.

A private research office had held it.

The person or object under observation had later become unavailable.

That was not an answer.

It was a direction.

And for the first time, the direction led outside Ravenscroft.
