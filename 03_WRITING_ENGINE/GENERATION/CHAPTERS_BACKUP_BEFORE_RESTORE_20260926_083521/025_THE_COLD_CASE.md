# THE COLD CASE

Arthur did not go looking for the old ferry case.

It found him.

The field-support preparation packet for the next exercise contained a location he recognized: the old ferry district.

He checked the current map.

The district was no longer used for ordinary Academy exercises.

The packet gave one reason.

**Historical anomaly density.**

Arthur asked Eleanor Price about it.

She looked up from the records request in front of her.

"You want the public history or the operational history?"

"Operational."

"Then you want the old case index."

She did not hand it to him.

She showed him where it was.

"You search."

Arthur searched.

The index contained hundreds of entries.

Most were ordinary closures.

One was not.

**FERRY DISTRICT — DORMANT SOURCE — CLOSED WITHOUT RESOLUTION.**

Arthur read the closure reason.

**No reproducible rule.**

He checked the original intake.

The case had been opened decades earlier after tools repeatedly appeared in the same place inside a warehouse.

The original team had moved the tools.

The tools returned.

They had sealed the room.

The tools appeared outside it.

They had changed custodians.

The same result.

Arthur stopped reading.

"This is the same shape."

Eleanor looked at him.

"Same as what?"

"The thimble."

She did not answer immediately.

"Then do not assume they are the same phenomenon."

Arthur nodded.

He kept reading.

The final page contained a handwritten note.

**Previous holder identified. Follow custody record.**

No name followed it.

Arthur turned the page.

Nothing.

The line had been written deliberately.

Someone had known there was another record.

The record was missing.

Eleanor closed the folder.

"That is enough for today."

"Why?"

"Because you have stopped reading the case and started chasing the missing page."

Arthur looked at her.

She smiled faintly.

"Those are different activities."

He put the folder back.

For once, he agreed.

The cold case was no longer merely interesting.

It was connected to a question he already carried.

And someone had removed the part of the record that might explain why.
