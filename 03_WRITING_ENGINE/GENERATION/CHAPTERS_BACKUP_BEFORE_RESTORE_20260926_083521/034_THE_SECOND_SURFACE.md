# THE SECOND SURFACE

The next field exercise was not supposed to involve Arthur's artifact.

It did anyway.

The warehouse phenomenon moved tools toward the last place a person had looked for them.

The team built a controlled observation zone.

Arthur stood outside it.

The artifact warmed against his side.

He ignored it.

The first tool moved.

The second followed.

Then a third object moved toward Arthur.

It stopped three metres away.

Arthur did not step forward.

The object moved again.

The private interface opened without being called.

________________________________
CONTRACT SYSTEM

Condition detected.

No new Contract available.

Recognition response: UNRESOLVED

THE POLITE KNOCK: ACTIVE
________________________________

Arthur closed it.

The phenomenon was reacting to his presence.

That did not mean the artifact had created the reaction.

He needed to separate the two variables.

"Everyone out," he said.

The team withdrew.

The tool remained.

Arthur stepped left.

The tool moved left.

He stepped right.

The tool moved right.

Recognition.

Not ownership.

Not a new Contract.

Arthur stopped.

The movement stopped.

He had learned one useful thing.

The artifact could affect what a phenomenon did without automatically creating a new Contract.

That distinction mattered.

Later, he wrote the field conclusion:

**Observed response is not proof of Contract formation.**

He filed it.

The System remained silent.

That was acceptable.

Then the artifact knocked once against the inside of his case.

Arthur froze.

He had not touched it.

One knock.

The same number as his first Contract.
