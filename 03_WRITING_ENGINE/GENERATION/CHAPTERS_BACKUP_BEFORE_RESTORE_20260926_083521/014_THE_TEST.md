# THE TEST

The next practical exercise paired Arthur with a more experienced cadet.

The objective was simple.

Touch the marked target three times.

Arthur failed twice.

On the third attempt, he stopped trying to react to every movement.

He watched the opponent's weight.

Motion Sense gave him the movement already happening.

Arthur waited until the information mattered.

Then he stepped.

The dagger touched the target.

Once.

The opponent adjusted.

Arthur adjusted too.

Twice.

The third exchange was messy.

Arthur almost lost his balance.

He recovered and landed the final touch.

The instructor marked the result.

"Better."

Arthur nodded.

He did not feel stronger.

He felt more deliberate.

That was better.

His first combat improvement had come from learning when not to trust the first movement he perceived.

The instructor turned to the next page.

"Tomorrow, you will use the dagger against an active phenomenon."

Arthur looked at the weapon in his hand.

This time, failure would not end with a padded baton.
