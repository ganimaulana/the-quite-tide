# SIX PLACES

The instructor put six blank markers on the table.

Arthur looked at them.

"Contract capacity," she said. "Do not think of it as a reward track. Think of it as a limit."

Arthur already disliked the idea.

"How many Contracts can I hold?"

"Six active slots."

"And after six?"

"You choose."

She pushed the markers toward him.

"Contracts can be cleared under their own rules. A cleared slot may eventually be reused. That does not mean the Contract itself is erased."

Arthur looked at the six markers.

"Can the slots increase?"

"Not as normal progression."

That answer mattered.

The Contract System was not a staircase where every milestone created more room.

Six was a constraint.

The instructor pointed at the pouch containing the artifact.

"And those physical markers?"

Arthur looked down.

The artifact had more than six faces.

"They are not slots."

"Correct. Physical binding faces and active Contract capacity are different measurements. Do not combine them."

Arthur wrote the distinction down.

Later that afternoon, he was given a choice.

A training exercise had produced an anomalous response from the artifact. The Academy's monitoring system recorded a possible Contract condition.

The interface opened.

________________________________
CONTRACT SYSTEM

Condition detected.

No new Contract available.

Existing Contract:
THE POLITE KNOCK

Active slots: 1 / 6
________________________________

Arthur stared at the final line.

One out of six.

It would have been easy to think of the remaining five as empty promises.

He did not.

An empty slot was not an invitation.

It was capacity he might regret filling.

He closed the interface.

The training exercise continued.

Arthur made the safer decision and refused to invoke THE POLITE KNOCK on a phenomenon he had not solved.

The exercise result was mediocre.

His teammate looked at him afterward.

"You could have used it."

"I could have."

"Why didn't you?"

Arthur looked at the artifact in his pouch.

"Because I only have six places."

That answer was not the whole reason.

The real reason was simpler.

He had learned the first Contract's cost.

He was not going to spend another unknown merely because the System offered him a chance to do so.

The decision earned no reward.

No notification appeared.

But the next time Arthur looked at the six markers, he no longer saw empty spaces.

He saw choices.

Then one marker changed.

Arthur had not touched the interface.

The System had recorded a new condition.

**CONTRACT SLOT RESTRICTION: ACTIVE.**

He had not known that condition existed.
