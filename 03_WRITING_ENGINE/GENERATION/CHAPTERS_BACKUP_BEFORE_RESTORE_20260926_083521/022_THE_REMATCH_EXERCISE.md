# THE REMATCH EXERCISE

The rematch began with a mistake.

The briefing map was wrong.

Arthur noticed the drainage channels were missing.

He raised the issue.

The lead rejected it.

"The map is official."

Arthur looked at the current site.

"The water does not care."

They entered.

The phenomenon activated on the eastern side.

The team had to cross the drainage channels to reach it.

Arthur changed position.

He did not take command.

He supported the person who had it.

"Move the medical point."

"Why?"

"If the channel floods, that is the only dry route back."

The lead moved it.

Five minutes later, the water rose.

The medical point stayed dry.

Arthur had been right.

But the phenomenon was still active.

This time he did not try to solve it alone.

He gave the team a sequence of observations.

The Combat cadet supplied force.

Tactical converted the observations into a route.

Medical maintained the fallback.

The phenomenon was contained.

Arthur's method had improved.

He was no longer trying to be the person who solved everything.

He was learning how to make other specialists more effective.

That was Support.

And it was harder than doing the work alone.

The instructor handed him the next roster.

Arthur scanned it.

His name was listed under **Joint Team Candidate**.

He looked at the assignment location.

It was outside the Academy.
