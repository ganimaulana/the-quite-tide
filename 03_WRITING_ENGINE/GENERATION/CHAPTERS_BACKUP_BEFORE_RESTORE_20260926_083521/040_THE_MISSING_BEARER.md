# THE MISSING BEARER

Arthur and Eleanor followed the transfer chain one step farther.

The private research office had not simply stored the artifact.

It had assigned a holder.

The holder's name was missing.

But the position was not.

The position existed for forty years.

Different people had signed adjacent documents.

The same position remained.

Arthur studied the signatures.

Then he stopped.

One signature appeared twice, decades apart.

Same name.

Different hand.

Eleanor noticed his expression.

"What?"

"Either the record is wrong, or someone carried the same name through two generations of custody."

"Which do you believe?"

Arthur shook his head.

"Neither yet."

They searched the public employment register.

The first person with the name had died before the second document.

The second person had been born years after the first appointment.

That should have solved it.

Instead, it created a worse problem.

The handwriting on the second document matched the first closely enough to require explanation.

Arthur wrote three possibilities.

Forgery.

Inherited identity.

Anomaly.

He did not choose one.

The next document did the choosing for him.

It was a resignation notice.

The signature was the same.

The resignation had been filed three years before the supposed holder's birth.

Arthur closed the folder.

"Now we have an impossible record."

Eleanor nodded.

"And now someone knows you found it."

The archive door opened.

A staff member stood outside.

"Reed?"

Arthur looked up.

"You have a visitor."

It was not Kessler.
