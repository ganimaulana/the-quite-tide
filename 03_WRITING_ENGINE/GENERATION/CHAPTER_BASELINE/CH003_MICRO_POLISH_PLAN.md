# CH003 MICRO-POLISH PLAN

## P-003-01
- Current wording: `Exceptional Aptitude Review. Two o'clock. Administration block, room two.` / `You have until two o'clock.`
- Problem: It is unclear whether two o'clock is the review start time, the deadline, or both.
- Minimal repair: Identify the EAR as scheduled for that morning and state that two o'clock is the completion deadline.
- Intended reader effect: The reader understands when Arthur reports and that the evaluation must be completed by two o'clock.
- Canon sensitivity: Timeline-sensitive; retain 4 September and the existing morning-to-early-afternoon review window. No event or time is added.

## P-003-02
- Current wording: `The third answer was right, but the test would record the second one he had written first, and he knew it.`
- Problem: The correct answer and the answer recorded by the test are difficult to distinguish on first reading.
- Minimal repair: State that the third answer was correct, but the test would record his second answer, which he had written first.
- Intended reader effect: The reader immediately understands which answer was correct and which answer the test records.
- Canon sensitivity: None beyond preserving the established Station Seven result and Arthur's over-hedging weakness; no new test rule is added.
