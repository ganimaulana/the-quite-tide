### P-02 — ¶60 + ¶68: remove the doubled expectation-subversion
- **Location:** Scene 2 — "The cohort was not what the word had made him expect…" and later "That was the cohort. Four of them… None of them secret. None of them waiting for a power to wake up and fix things."
- **Problem:** The "not secret / not waiting to become powerful" idea is stated in both ¶60 and ¶68.
- **Why it matters:** Repetition dilutes the chapter's key thematic correction (Irregular ≠ secret elite).
- **Required polish:** Keep the expectation frame in ¶60; keep the concrete peer details; fold the "none secret / none waiting" line into a single occurrence.
- **Preserve:** all three peer characterizations; the "word and a table near the bins" formulation.
- **Canon impact:** NONE. **Severity:** MINOR.

### P-03 — ¶68: clarify cohort scale (resolves I-1)
- **Location:** Scene 2 — "That was the cohort. Four of them, in a room of hundreds."
- **Problem:** Can be read as the total Irregular population being four, versus the locked ~36 academy-wide census.
- **Why it matters:** Continuity clarity only; no canon conflict exists (it is the four at this table).
- **Required polish:** Make explicit that these are **the four at this table** (or "the ones who ate here"), not the full classified population.

- **Canon impact:** NONE (clarity only; ~36 census untouched). **Severity:** MINOR.
