## EXACT ISSUES

**I-1 (MINOR — continuity clarity).** ¶68 "That was the cohort. Four of them, in a room of hundreds." can be read as the **entire** Irregular population being four, against the locked census (~36 academy-wide). The preceding ¶60 ("a room full of people the machine had decided about") slightly compounds the ambiguity. Compatible if read as "the four at this table," but a careless reader may under-count. *No canon change needed; clarity only.*

**I-2 (MINOR — cross-chapter beat ownership).** The annex, senior clerk, and Grade-3 clearance are staged in Ch 002, while the ch4 restructure skeleton describes ch4 as the chapter that "introduces the assignment (the Annex, the chief clerk…, Grade-3 clearance)." The queue authorizes ch2 = "the archive function," so this is not a canon conflict — but ch4 planning must treat the annex/Grade-3 as **already established**. Informational; requires no Ch 002 change, but should be noted in ch4 planning.

**I-3 (MINOR — naming latitude).** The three peers are unnamed but map closely onto locked roster entries (C-2 unstable, C-5 fiber-memory, C-4 dry channel). Compatible and arguably good continuity; flagged so future chapters either **name them consistently** with the roster or keep them unnamed, never both.

**I-4 (MINOR — optional).** Hayes's "Instruments hesitate for all kinds of reasons" adds a resonance hook with the Ch 001 hesitation. Ambiguous and fence-safe, but it is an added thread; retained or trimmed at the author's discretion during polish.
