## REVIEW 1 — OPENING
**PASS.**
Opens mid-line and mid-procedure: *"Arthur counted forty-one cadets through the assessment room before they called his name."* He is **acting** (timing the intake rate against the clock) within two sentences. Friction is immediate but quiet — the institution is processing him and he is already rationing information. Orientation lands by ¶2–3 (room, console, bay, queue). No waking-up, no travelogue, no gate description, no lore dump. Curiosity is planted before the assessment even begins (the warm stone; the withheld slips; *"a clock that runs fast"*).

## REVIEW 2 — ARTHUR CHARACTERIZATION
**PASS.**
Reader access comes through behavior: counting, reading the examiner's face instead of the console (*"trying would only have looked like trying"*), spending exactly one careful question, and a private rule against arguing. Crucially he is **not omniscient**: he holds two unranked hypotheses — *"It had been confused by him, and confusion was not the same thing, and he would rather know which of the two he was carrying."* Competent, information-poor, not a system-recipient, not passive.

## REVIEW 3 — SOCIAL UNDERVALUE
**PASS.**
Four distinct lenses, all understated and institutional:
- Examiner — *uncertainty* (frown at a console, not at the boy).
- Processing clerk — *neutral competence* (reads the file "like a shopping list").
- Gate cadet — *dismissal by word*, explicitly not cruelty: *"His voice was not cruel. It was tired."*
- Supervisor — *indifferent efficiency*, two readings and a stamp.
Rank F + ERROR + Irregular + Records & Archives registers as a *filing* consequence, not cartoon hostility. (No demand that all lenses appear in Ch1; varied response is achieved.)

## REVIEW 4 — ASSESSMENT SCENE
**PASS.**
The machinery is credible and legible without being explained: bay hum, faint pressure, console angled away. It is **not** an RPG HUD — output is three lines of institutional text plus a stamp box. The examiner reacts believably (frown → second examiner → countersign → *"like a man who had reached for a drawer and found it empty"*). F / ERROR / UNDEFINED / Irregular emerges **from the stall**, naturally. The scene creates tension (three beats nobody else noticed) rather than merely delivering a profile. Machine remains *honest about the measurement, incomplete about the person*.

## REVIEW 5 — INFORMATION DENSITY
**PASS (MINOR redundancy).**
Information arrives through action and dialogue. The one dense paragraph (¶84, "the tray marked otherwise") is Arthur's **inference in-voice**, not narration-lore, and it earns its length by characterizing his method.
**MINOR:** ¶142 (scene 3's walk-up) re-litigates material already carried by ¶84 and ¶100 (the tray logic and *"confused by him"*). See Review 15.

## REVIEW 6 — PROSE QUALITY
**PASS.**
Simple English with genuine variation; concrete verbs (*"A printer clicked"*, *"The line moved"*). No staccato cascade, no mechanical short-sentence runs, no repetitive openings, no gamer voice, no em-dash abuse, no fragment abuse, no rhetorical-question spam. **MINOR** repetition noted at ¶142/¶100 (see below).

## REVIEW 7 — POV
**PASS.**
Close third, Arthur-limited throughout. Examiner/second-examiner interiority is rendered **only** as external behavior. No head-hop. No accidental omniscience. The asymmetry is deliberate and load-bearing: the reader is shown the stall the room missed.

## REVIEW 8 — EMOTIONAL MOVEMENT
**PASS.**
Trajectory: expectation (braced for a "medical scan") → discrepancy (the stall) → discomfort/curiosity → processing (why he differs) → decision (find out). It advances; it does **not** reset to the opening state.

## REVIEW 9 — MYSTERY
**PASS.**
Primary question — *why did the instrument hesitate?* — is stated plainly (¶60–64, ¶102) and grounded in an on-page observation. Reader is given enough to form a hypothesis (the stone is warm since spring; the reading stalled then "answered a different one instead") without being handed a solution. Not too obvious, not too vague. ERROR fence approached at its edge only; no fence crossed.

## REVIEW 10 — PACING
**PASS.**
Scene 1 moves (assessment → hesitation → decision). Scene 2 moves (assignment logic → one rationed question → the reinterpreted stone). Scene 3 moves (gate friction → two readings and a stamp → arrival at the annex). No filler transitions; no redundant dialogue; each scene ends on a decision. Scene 3's walk-up ¶142 is the only beat that mildly slows the approach without stalling it.

## REVIEW 11 — CHAPTER ENDING
**PASS.**
*"The instrument had hesitated. He meant to find out why."* — evaluated as prose: it is concrete, earns its place from the earlier on-page observation, avoids a manufactured cliffhanger, and carries genuine forward pull. **Hook strength: genuine desire for Chapter 2.**

## REVIEW 12 — TNE ADAPTATION
**PASS.**
Uses approved mechanisms — information asymmetry, tactical cognition, underdog camera, functional dialogue, restrained emotional processing, hard scene movement, immediate consequence — with **no** TNE first-person monopoly, protagonist voice, interface, humor style, sentence imitation, or archetype copying.

## REVIEW 13 — QUIET TIDE IDENTITY
**PASS — strong.**
Identity signals present: bureaucratic classification as quiet dread (*"It was only very certain."*); cognition as characterization; the machine's *politeness* during an anomaly (*"the machine was being polite about it"*); the filing-metaphor spine (*"the tray marked otherwise"*); institutional neutrality rather than villainy. It does not read as generic Academy fantasy, generic LitRPG, generic TNE clone, or generic light novel.
