## 15. SHOULD FIX

**SF-1 — Scene-exit recap of the misfile cause (L86)**
- **Location:** L86.
- **Exact passage:** "He left the window with the correction form in his pocket and the zone name still in his head. East Beach Annex, Zone 11. A sound-bleed that played back the wrong day's conversations. **Filed under the wrong Rule because someone had ticked the box for a living entity and never read the description beneath it.**"
- **Issue:** the misfile cause is already shown at L16 ("The intake clerk had read the first box, not the description, and filed it under R-7") and is restated again at L94 ("filed under R-7 because the clerk who had logged it had checked the wrong box on the form"). L86 makes a third statement, immediately on the scene exit.
- **Why it matters:** mild but noticeable redundancy; it slows the transition out of the compliance scene without adding information.
- **Proposed repair direction:** trim the repeated cause clause at L86 (keep the zone-name beat and the exit), leaving L16 (discovery) and L94 (reflection + the new "one column over" detail) to carry the cause.

**SF-2 — Terminology overload of "Rule" (L12 / L14 / L24; chapter title)**
- **Location:** L12, L14, L24 (also the title, L1).
- **Exact passages:** "R-7 was the **response protocol** for living-entity Seep activity." (L12); "The correct **protocol** was R-4…" (L14); "changed the **Rule** header from R-7 to R-4." (L24); title "THE WRONG **RULE**."
- **Issue:** the chapter uses "Rule" to mean the archive's **response-protocol / filing code** (R-7, R-4), whereas the World Bible defines a Seep's **"Rule"** as the anomaly's own governing behavior ("Every Seep follows a **Rule** — alien, but fixed and discoverable," `03_SUPERNATURAL_SYSTEM.md` L13; cf. `02_WORLD_RULES.md` L25 "Anomaly Rules"). The chapter overloads a canon-load-bearing term.
- **Why it matters:** a reader holding the canon definition may read "the wrong Rule" as "the anomaly's behavior was misidentified," when the chapter means "the wrong response protocol was assigned." This is a canon-terminology tension, not a plot error.
- **Proposed repair direction:** clarify in-world wording so the R-codes read clearly as *response protocols / filing classes* assigned to a Seep's Rule (e.g., keep "Rule index"/"Rule header" but make the protocol sense explicit at first use), or confirm the intended usage. Because the spec itself equates "Rule" with the response protocol (spec §6), resolution may be a spec-level reconciliation — flagged, not repaired.
