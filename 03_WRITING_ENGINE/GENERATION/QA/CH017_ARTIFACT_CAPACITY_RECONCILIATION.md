The ruling resolved a conflict between:
- Worker H's CONTRACT_SYSTEM_MASTERS (proposed 7-knot cord = 7 slots)
- L1's RULESETS R2.3 ("seven-slot maximum")
vs.:
- Worker G's ARTIFACT_DESIGN (12 carvable faces)

The "seven" was family lore/mythology (seven generations of Reed holders), not the actual slot cap.
