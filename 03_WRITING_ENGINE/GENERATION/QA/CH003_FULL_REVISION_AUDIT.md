## 14. MUST FIX

**MF-1 — Station 8 / Station 9 order and label (vs LOCK 4)**
- **Location:** L106 and L108.
- **Exact passage:**
  - L106: "Station nine was the strangers. Five cadets who had never met were given a map and a route problem. … The examiner noted that the plan was sound and that the subject had not led."
  - L108: "At the final station, the degradation curve, Arthur was asked what would fail first if the task continued for another eight hours."
- **Issue:** The chapter presents the **strangers (station 9)** *before* the **degradation curve**, and calls the degradation curve "**the final station**." Per **LOCK 4** (`02_ACADEMY\07_EAR_DESIGN.md` L51/L65, citing `09_SPECIAL_CLASSIFICATION_EXAM.md`), the review has **nine stations**, with **station 8 = degradation curve** and **station 9 = the strangers**. The chapter also states the review "held nine tables" (L70) and that Arthur must "proceed in order… may not skip" (L76).
- **Why it matters:** it is internally self-inconsistent (nine tables, yet a station after station nine) and contradicts the locked EAR station design; a continuity check would flag it.
- **Proposed repair direction:** reconcile to LOCK 4 — e.g., place the **degradation curve as station 8** before the **strangers as station 9**, or relabel the degradation paragraph so it is not "the final station" and does not follow station nine. Do not add stations or change the nine-station count.
