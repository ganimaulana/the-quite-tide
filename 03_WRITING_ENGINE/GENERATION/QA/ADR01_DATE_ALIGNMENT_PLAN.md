> **Decision (author):** LOCK 2 T-08 governs — **2 September 2024 = assignment order / Records & Archives operational**.
> **Mode:** PLAN ONLY (pre-edit). No file modified until this plan is recorded.
> **Scope:** minimum bounded continuity repair to align CH001–CH002. No other file changed.
> **Frozen:** LOCK 1–14 (except prose continuity affected by this decision) · Writing Engine · Division/Curriculum/Assignment · mystery fences · CH003/CH004 · no new events or scenes.

## 1. Exact current contradiction

- CH001 (`CHAPTERS/001_ERROR_UNDEFINED.md`) stages the district-office assignment readout, then arrival at Ravenhurst **and** reporting in to the Records and Archives annex (locker, badge, first slip) all on **1 September 2024** (scene 3; L104–148). Its header reads "Late August – 1 September 2024."
- LOCK 2 **T-08**: "September 2, 2024 — Assignment order posted — reports to the … records annex 0600." LOCK 2 registry: "Records & Archives assignment — 2 September 2024 — LOCKED." Queue anchor: "2 September 2024 — Records & Archives operational."
- CH002 (`CHAPTERS/002_NO_TAKERS.md`) opens "**The next morning**, the backlog was in the basement…" (L8); with CH001 ending 1 Sep, that lands 2 Sep. If CH001's reporting moves to 2 Sep, "the next morning" drifts to 3 Sep — inconsistent with the "2 September operational" anchor.

**Required result:** 1 Sep = academy intake/arrival; the Records & Archives reporting = 2 Sep; CH002's first Records & Archives workday = 2 Sep.

## 3. Proposed minimal changes (smallest possible temporal bridge)

**CH001 — 1 prose edit + 1 header edit**
- **E1 (header):** "Late August – 1 September 2024" → "Late August – 2 September 2024".
- **E2 (L140):** "Then he opened the door, because standing in front of it was not going to answer anything." → "**He came back the next morning and opened the door**, because standing in front of it was not going to answer anything."
  - Effect: 1 Sep = arrival, gate, east block, quarters, stop at the annex door (unchanged); 2 Sep = reporting in, locker/badge, first slip, hesitation close (unchanged content, re-dated). No surrounding prose rewritten.

**CH002 — 1 prose edit**
- **E3 (L8):** "**The next morning**, the backlog was in the basement, and the basement was his." → "**His first morning on the job**, the backlog was in the basement, and the basement was his."
  - Effect: the first Records & Archives workday is unambiguously **2 Sep** (the morning of/after the report), not 3 Sep. All CH002 events and sequence preserved; no scene added.
