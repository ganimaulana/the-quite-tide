## CURRENT HEAD — end of Chapter 001

### TIME
- Arthur has been recovered from a mass-casualty incident and has spent a period under medical observation and placement review. During recovery, he independently notices, tests, and names his movement-detection ability **Motion Sense** before the formal Esper assessment.
- Exact calendar dates remain in the canon timeline, not in reader-facing prose.

### ARTHUR STATE
- **Name:** Arthur Reed.
- **Age:** 17.
- **Memory:** He retains ordinary autobiographical memory, including recognition of his parents, younger sister, home life, and basic procedural knowledge. The memory of the Ravenhurst Massacre is fragmented around the period immediately before and during the monster kill.
- **Family:** His mother, father, and younger sister died during the Ravenhurst Massacre. He has no immediate family placement to return to.
- **Status:** Sole surviving civilian from the incident; specialist assessment and institutional placement candidate.
- **Emotional state:** Arthur recognizes that his family is dead and remembers them clearly, but his grief response is muted and disproportionate to what he believes he should feel. The cause is unresolved.
- **Ability:** Before formal assessment, Arthur independently identifies and names **Motion Sense** through observation and repeated testing. It detects movement already in progress; it is not precognition.

### INCIDENT
- Public name: **The Ravenhurst Massacre**.
- Formal designation: **Ravenhurst Industrial District Anomalous Incident**.
- Local nickname: **The Ravenhurst Night**.
- Multiple anomalous attackers/monsters were involved.
- Arthur personally killed one monster. He remembers the act but not the complete sequence that made the kill possible.
- Major Elias Ward witnessed and formally attributed the kill to Arthur.
- Arthur was the only surviving civilian found inside the affected site.
- The exact casualty total, hostile-entity count, and full operational record remain unresolved at chapter end.

### PLACEMENT
- A social worker arranges specialist educational placement because Arthur is seventeen, has no guardian, and has an Esper-related assessment requiring a suitable institution.
- **Ravenscroft Academy** accepts the placement.
- Arthur travels toward Ravenscroft at the end of the chapter.

### READER CONTRACT
- The reader knows the event has a proper public name and institutional designation rather than being an anonymous "massacre."
- The reader knows Arthur survived, his family died, he killed one monster, and he does not know exactly how he did it.
- The reader does **not** yet know the complete cause, full scale, attacker count, or restricted truth of the event.
- The chapter does not require the reader to solve the incident; it establishes the event as a wider Ravenhurst mystery that existed independently of Arthur.

### OPEN THREADS
- The fragmented memory surrounding Arthur's monster kill.
- Why Arthur survived the Ravenhurst Massacre.
- What the official investigation is withholding or unable to explain.
- How much of the public Ravenhurst account is accurate.
- Arthur's future at Ravenscroft Academy.

### HARD CONTINUITY RULES
- Do not restore the old total-amnesia canon. Arthur remembers his family and ordinary life before the massacre.
- Do not write Arthur's parents or younger sister as alive after the Ravenhurst Massacre.
- Do not reveal how Arthur killed the monster unless the causal chain is earned through later observation, evidence, testing, or memory recovery.
- Motion Sense is discovered and named by Arthur before the formal assessment; the later assessment independently evaluates/classifies the ability rather than giving Arthur the idea for it.
- Do not invent casualty totals, hostile-entity counts, or restricted operational details merely for atmosphere.
- Do not turn the Ravenhurst Massacre into a mystery that exists only because Arthur is the protagonist; it must continue to exist as a civic and institutional event in the wider world.
- Do not imply Arthur chose Ravenscroft because of ambition or special status.
- His Academy placement is institutional support plus specialist evaluation, not a reward.
