# QUIET I

The Academy did not care that Arthur had settled on a dagger.

The Academy cared that he was still Rank F.

The notice remained on the board.

His name remained where it had always been.

Records.

Irregular.

Low operational priority.

Nobody moved it because a Records student had spent a week learning footwork.

Arthur preferred that.

So he trained quietly.

Morning: conditioning.

Afternoon: records work.

Evening: weapons.

Dagger most days.

Other weapons often enough to prevent himself from mistaking preference for proof.

The pattern became clear.

Knife taught economy.

Spear taught distance.

Axe taught momentum.

Bow taught preparation.

Sword taught connection.

Arthur kept the last word out of his official notes. It was too close to a conclusion.

His private notebook was different.

There he wrote:

*The dagger may be compatible with how I perceive movement.*

Then, below it:

*May be wrong.*

He underlined the second sentence.

That was the sentence that mattered.

That evening, he called the interface.

________________________________
CONTRACT INTERFACE

USER
ARTHUR REED

NATURAL ABILITY
Motion Sense
Status: ACTIVE

CONTRACT SYSTEM
Status: NO CONTRACT

No new condition detected.
________________________________

He looked at the empty Contract field.

The System was not a judge of effort.

It did not reward repetition.

It did not hand him a weapon because he had spent enough hours holding one.

Arthur closed the interface.

He picked up the dagger again.

The blade was cold.

The grip was familiar now, but not comfortable.

Good.

Comfort came later.

First came control.

Rain began after midnight.

Arthur woke once, listened to it against the residential block window, and flexed his right hand.

Nothing supernatural happened.

His muscles were sore.

His palm was warm.

The dagger had left no mark.

He went back to sleep.
