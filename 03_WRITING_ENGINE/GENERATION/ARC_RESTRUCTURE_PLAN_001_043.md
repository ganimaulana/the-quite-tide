## 3. Pacing Principles

1. No chapter exists only to transfer information from one file to another.
2. Every chapter must contain a concrete objective, resistance, and changed state.
3. Archive/records investigation must produce either action, risk, social consequence, or a materially different hypothesis.
4. Repeated `observe → file → request → denial` sequences must be compressed or converted into active conflict.
5. Contract UI confirms earned discoveries; it does not generate solutions.
6. Arthur's progression must be visible through decisions, technique, field usefulness, and consequences—not unexplained power jumps.
7. Rank F remains a system classification. APR remains dynamic. Actual combat capability remains contextual.
8. Arthur is Operations Support — Field Support Specialist, not an Archive clerk.
9. The dagger remains his primary weapon; multi-range competence is earned gradually.
10. The Ravenhurst Massacre remains an independent civic event and its unresolved details are not invented merely to create plot.

## 7. Acceptance Gate

An active chapter passes structural review only if its ending creates a changed state that materially affects the next chapter. A chapter may be quiet, but it may not be inert.

**Core target:** `Every chapter changes the situation.`

## 8. COMMERCIAL RETENTION GATE — AUTHOR LOCK 2026-09-26

The mandatory reader experience is: **I want to read the next chapter.**

Fast pace is mandatory. Raising quality must never mean adding filler, repeated exposition, artificial delays, or extra chapters solely to increase a score.

### Chapter retention test

Before LOCK, ask:

1. What does the reader want to know or see happen?
2. What concrete objective does Arthur pursue?
3. What resists him?
4. What changes by the end?
5. What payoff does the reader receive?
6. What new pressure makes the next chapter necessary?

If #6 has no concrete answer, the chapter fails retention review.

### Ending hook rotation

Rotate among **revelation, decision, threat, opportunity, consequence, and confrontation**. Do not repeat identical cliffhanger construction.

Prefer **payoff + consequence + question** over random shock without payoff.

### Anti-filler rule

If a beat can be handled in one scene, do not create two. If a discovery can happen during an existing field operation, do not create a separate archive chapter merely to deliver it. If dialogue can reveal information through conflict, do not use a lecture.

**Commercial quality = stronger movement per chapter, not more words per chapter.**

### Mystery retention

Use **question → evidence → partial answer → new question**. Do not use question → question → question without meaningful payoff.

### Arc retention

Every arc must contain a clear promise, early hook, escalating pressure, intermediate payoff, final payoff, visible progression, consequence, and bridge into the next arc.

### Hard lock rule

A chapter with a weak ending cannot be LOCKED merely because the prose is good. A chapter that is already fast must not be lengthened merely to manufacture a higher score.
