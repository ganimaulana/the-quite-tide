## SCENE 1 — "The Wrong Rule" (the low pile)
- **Want:** Find out why this folder does not belong where it is.
- **Friction:** The Seep report is filed under a Rule that does not match the incident class.
- **Information:** A Seep is an anomaly with a Rule and a response protocol; the Rule index exists; the wrong Rule sends the wrong responders.
- **Decision:** Re-file it under the correct Rule.
- **Consequence:** The corrected file is catalog #18 — a documentation victory.

## SCENE 2 — "Noted" (the compliance window)
- **Want:** Get the correction acknowledged and routed.
- **Friction:** The compliance clerk treats the correction as an inconvenience; the case is already closed / already responded to; the file is stamped "noted" but not acted upon.
- **Information:** The institution is correctable but inert; a correct file does not guarantee action.
- **Decision:** Arthur accepts the dismissal without arguing.
- **Consequence:** He leaves with the zone name in his head and the file number in his pocket.

## SCENE 3 — "East by Northeast" (the corridor / walk back)

- **Friction:** The Seal in his pocket warms — and for the first time, the warmth has direction.
- **Information:** The zone name triggers the Seal's directional warmth; it points toward the zone.
- **Decision:** He notes the direction and the name, copying both into his sheet.
- **Consequence:** The anomaly clue is planted; the chapter ends on quiet wonder, not resolution.
