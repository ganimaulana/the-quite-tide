## SCENE 1 — "The Low Pile" (Records and Archives annex)
- **Want:** Make himself useful; learn the actual job.
- **Friction:** The archive's unfileable backlog is dumped on the newest Irregular — low prestige work with Grade-3 routing limits.
- **Information:** The archive is the Records-Intelligence function; the backlog is real; the quartermaster penalty tier and routing are ordinary institutional facts, shown not explained.
- **Decision:** Arthur sorts the backlog by *rule*, not by date — an internal method he adopts.
- **Consequence:** He earns the mild, unimpressed notice of the archive's senior clerk.
- **Movement:** character (method) + institutional (function established).
- **Beats from function:** "the archive function"; Records-Intelligence.

## SCENE 2 — "Eat Last" (cohort window / mess)
- **Want:** Read the room; understand his peers.
- **Friction:** Combat cadets arrive and the Irregular cohort yields — eat last, old gym, no argument. Varied responses: Combat = procedural contempt; one Irregular peer = plain resignation; an admin clerk = practical disregard.
- **Information:** Irregular's real, varied composition (an unstable Ability; a specialist whose gift has no combat use and *stays* useless; a failed Awakening). Genuinely low status, not secretly strong.
- **Decision:** Arthur does not contest the exclusion. He records who moved and who did not — the same observational habit, applied socially.
- **Consequence:** He is seen reading faces; a Combat cadet marks him as the one who watches.
- **Movement:** relationship + institutional conflict + social undervalue (rank lens).
- **Beats from function:** "Irregular intake"; composition; Combat procedural contempt.

## SCENE 3 — "No Takers" (old gym → notice board; Hayes)
- **Want:** Get through the old-gym assignment without friction.
- **Friction:** Hayes — the Irregular faculty advisor — dismisses him on professional grounds ("paper-pusher auditing a war"). Not cruelty; authority bias.
- **Information:** Hayes's role; the old gym's notice board with a mission posting that has drawn no takers.
- **Decision:** Arthur reads the posting properly, forms a hypothesis about *why* nobody took it (possibly wrong), and files the detail away.
- **Consequence / plant:** The no-takers posting enters the world. Chapter ends cold.
- **Movement:** relationship (Hayes placed) + mystery (plant) + institutional.
- **Beats from function:** "first hierarchy contact"; Hayes's first appearance; the notice-board plant.
