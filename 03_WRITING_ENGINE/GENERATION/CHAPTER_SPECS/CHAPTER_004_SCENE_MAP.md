## SCENE 1 — "The Requisition" (basement → venue)
- **Want:** Understand why he is being pulled out of the basement mid-shift.
- **Friction:** The summons is automatic and impersonal; Margaret's reaction tells him it is not routine for the annex. At the venue, a Combat cadet's public mockery (evaluation spectacle) — the ERROR getting its own exam.
- **Information:** Formal name **Exceptional Aptitude Review**; slang "Tokurei"; no channeling at any station; board composition glimpsed (presiding officer, seconded examiners, Hayes as standing proctor).
- **Decision:** Sit it like a task, not a verdict — because it is not measuring what the machine measured.
- **Consequence:** He enters the room already knowing the charter line on the wall: *"Rank measures what the Tide does through you. Aptitude measures what you do with what you notice."*

## SCENE 2 — "Nine Stations" (the review itself)
- **Want:** Do the work well enough to be left alone.
- **Friction:** Stations 3 and 6 (Rule-extraction, kill-the-favorite) reward exactly what he does — and the examiner's reaction makes the room uncomfortable; Station 9 (unled group planning) exposes what he lacks — the group follows the plan, not him.
- **Information (shown through performance):** the planted discrepancy in the cart (flagged unprompted, fast); falsifiable Rules stated from redacted files; the cheapest test that kills the examiner's hypothesis; over-hedged calls under the clock. **Bands, never scores; tasks, never channeling.**
- **Decision:** Under the clock, he hedges — knows he is hedging, does it anyway.
- **Consequence:** He leaves the room knowing both the shape of what he is good at and the exact places he is not.

## SCENE 3 — "No Rank Effect" (results desk → annex)
- **Want:** Learn what the review concluded.
- **Friction:** The cadet receives **band summaries only**; the full profile is sealed by default. The slip prints the charter firewall: no rank effect. What he reads confirms aptitude without explaining him.
- **Information:** A/B/C bands on his summary (exceptional / high / exceptional-with-a-note); Rank F unchanged; classification unchanged; assignment unchanged. An instructor/examiner annotates something on the sealed side and files it — he sees the act, not the note.
- **Decision:** Return to the basement; file the exam the way he files everything.
- **Consequence / residue:** A sealed profile about him now exists in a quiet file; the anomaly in it has been noted and not acted on. He carries the doctrine sentence out with him.
