### ASSESSMENT
- Arthur's assessment instrument hesitates for three beats before producing his result.
- Result: **Rank F / Motion Sense / Irregular (Provisional)**.
- The hesitation is observed by Arthur but not explained.
- Motion Sense is a limited natural ability that detects movement and changes in motion. It does not reveal intent, meaning, or future events.
- Arthur discovered and named Motion Sense after the massacre, during the period in which he was rebuilding ordinary routines.

- He is placed in **Operations Support — Field Support Specialist**.
- Records & Archives is not his primary posting; archive access may remain available as a later side connection.
