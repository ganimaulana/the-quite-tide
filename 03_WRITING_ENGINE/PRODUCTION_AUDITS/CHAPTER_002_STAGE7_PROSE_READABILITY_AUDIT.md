## 3. Sentence-level audit

**Method:** quantitative pass over the frozen source (118 splitter-counted sentences; splitter merges sentences ending in quoted periods, e.g. `...word "office." Rent...`, so the true sentence count is ~110 and true maxima are lower than raw output; findings below use manually verified values where it matters), plus close reading against Module 03's detection checklist.

**3.1 The §39 band.** Average 11.6 words/sentence; 27% strictly inside 10–20, with the median (7.5) pulled down by dialogue shards and deliberate fragments. The band is honored as a *default*, not a quota: longer sentences appear exactly where the engine permits — institutional/inventory information (l. 27, 27w: apartment inventory), rhythm control (l. 96, 38w: fatigue accumulation), logical causality (l. 52's place-choice chain), deliberate emphasis (l. 48: `The math balanced.`). No sentence requires rereading for grammar. **No drift toward TNE's ~8.7 average.**

**3.2 Coordination over subordination.** Holds. The long sentences are coordinate chains (`and ... and ...`), not nested subordination. The 38-word fatigue sentence (l. 96) is one coordinate chain with a single em-dash pivot. No abstract-noun stacking; no parenthetical pile-ups; no complicated passives (zero approx hits).

**3.3 Em-dashes: 25 in 1,379 words — F-S7-02 (MINOR DRIFT, planner note).** Density ≈ 1 per 55 words. For comparison, ch1 FINAL carried 28 in 1,860 words (≈ 1 per 66) and was flagged MINOR DRIFT in Stage 14. Here the density is proportionally higher. Most do genuine pivot work (`The laugh was habit — and he didn't examine it, which was how the habit survived`, l. 19; `It accumulated — the way laundry did...`, l. 98). None are cut-off speech; all are pivots, so no functional violation — but the cadence is a recognizable mannerism. **Stage 10 direction:** thin toward ~1 per 80+ words in reconstruction; preserve the pivot function, vary the mechanism (periods, colons, commas).

**3.4 The `Not X. Y.` template — part of F-S7-02.** `Not frugality. Calibration.` (l. 27); `not the text, but the dinner` (l. 88, embedded). Module 03's checklist bans the `He did not think X. He thought Y.` template; this is its staccato cousin. Used twice in 1,379 words — not yet mannerism, but it is the same habit Stage 14 flagged in ch1 (`, not X` ×3). **Stage 10 direction:** ration to ≤1 per chapter; never let it become Arthur's default emphasis move.

**3.5 Semicolons: 5.** Two stacked in the bench-inventory sentence (l. 44: `groceries — more if good tofu; dryer; insurance; ...`). List-function semicolons, not clause-stacking. Acceptable; no action.

**3.6 Sentence openings — F-S7-01 (MINOR DRIFT, planner note).** `The` ×23, `He` ×18 = 35% of sentences. Examples: `The laundry bag sat by the door` (l. 27), `The show was a late-night Tokyo talk program` (l. 32), `The washer stopped.` (l. 42), `The monthly ceremony had rules.` (l. 44), `The supermarket was two stories...` (l. 54), `The receipt box lived on the shelf.` (l. 90), `The dailies reset at midnight...` (l. 96), `The fatigue did not lift.` (l. 98), `The room dimmed...` (l. 100). This is the source's inventory-taking camera — the single most portable prose-DNA item in the chapter — but at 23 occurrences it is also its most visible mannerism. **Stage 10 direction:** carry the inventory *function*, re-house with opening rotation (prepositional phrases, verbs, time markers, objects). The reconstruction's institutional payload (intake assembly, annex, mess, dorm) gives natural rotation material the civilian source lacked.

**3.7 Tic set:** `quietly / slowly / carefully / for a moment` — zero hits. Clean.

**3.8 Rhetorical questions:** 2 question marks, both genuine (dialogue question l. 64; indirect question l. 94). No rhetorical-question habit.

**3.9 Pronoun clarity:** one soft spot — l. 52: `he had arranged the apartment to be easily lost in, at a moment he had not named.` The dangling `a moment he had not named` is atmospheric but slightly under-specified; parseable, not broken. No action (the sentence's content is retired with the civilian era).

**3.10 Fragments:** earned and rationed. `One load, £300.` (l. 27); `Not frugality. Calibration.` (l. 27); `True, or would be — which amounted to the same thing.` (l. 38); `Sunday at six. The lotus root.` (l. 88). All follow complete-sentence stacks; none open consecutive beats; none appear in exposition. Compliant with Module 03 §3.

**Section verdict:** PASS, with F-S7-01 and F-S7-02 as MINOR DRIFT planner notes.

## 4. Readability audit

**4.1 Vocabulary levels (SOP §38).** L1 everyday dominant (`sat, fed, packed, typed, washed, photographed, filed, noted, adjusted`). L2 normal narrative (`hesitate` — absent here; `uneasy` — absent; the source's L2 is observational: `annotating`, `calibration`). L3 specialized: `calibration` (l. 27), `inventory` (l. 15), `externalized` (l. 90), `annotate` (l. 56) — all Arthur's records register, each introduced inside teaching context, never stacked, never shown off. **4.2 Idiom density:** low. `£230,000 and change` (l. 46) — the `and change` idiom is the single mildest trip-risk; context (salary figure) teaches it. `like a moon` (l. 46), `the weather of his working life` (l. 32) — transparent metaphors. No opaque idioms.

**4.4 Jargon.** No institutional jargon exists in the source (civilian chapter). The reconstruction's institutional terms (Irregular, Classification, Records & Archives, filing rotation) must arrive via the same contextual-teaching mechanism this source demonstrates — procedure and work, not glossary.

**4.5 Sentence dependency / paragraph transitions.** Hard cuts (`---`) reorient within ≤3 sentences every time: l. 15 (`His phone said 15:04.` — time, body-state, room), l. 52 (`Outside, Willowmere was doing Saturday afternoon in the sun.` — place, time, light). **4.6 `dailies` diction (l. 96) — watch-item.** `The dailies reset at midnight` is light gamer-meta voice. Module 09 §8 prohibits TNE gamer-meta voice in Arthur's register. In the clerk-era source it reads as 24-year-old civilian texture; it must not be carried into the Academy-era reconstruction as a standing habit. Flagged for Stage 10; no action now.

**Section verdict:** PASS.
