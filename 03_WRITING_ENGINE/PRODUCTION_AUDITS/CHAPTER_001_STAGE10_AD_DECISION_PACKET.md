- The examiners' function would be personalized around a character whose locked dramatic purpose is elsewhere (Irregular advisor / contempt / conversion due Arc II).

**Option C — consequences:**
- No identity is canonized; fully consistent with every locked description of the assessment and entry (all are document-, system-, and role-driven in locked sources).
- The chapter's institutional texture is carried by documents (the printout, the intake roll, the envelope, the posted lists) and Arthur-perceived functionaries — exactly the CONSEQUENCE → DOCUMENT/REPORT → DIALOGUE → CONCISE NARRATION grammar the revision plan's §9 prescribes.

- The Deputy Commandant (Sorting) board is [PROPOSED] and ch2-scoped; nothing in ch1 depends on it.

### Risks

- **Option A risks:** (a) unledgered identity entering canon through prose, bypassing character architecture; (b) reader-expectation debt for a one-scene functionary; (c) collision with L-09's advisor architecture or future casting of academy staff; (d) Stage 12/13 verification burden on a name the lock registry never authorized.
