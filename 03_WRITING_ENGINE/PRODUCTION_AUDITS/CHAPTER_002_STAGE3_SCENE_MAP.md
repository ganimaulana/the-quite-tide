## 4. Scene count rationale

**Scene count: 4.** The count emerges from the locked functional load, not from
any formula:

- The locked architecture stages **five beat groups**: (1) Irregular
  intake/composition, (2) Records-Intelligence function explained, (3) Combat
  procedural contempt, (4) Hayes dismissal, (5) F-01/F-03 glances + no-takers
  posting (Stage 2 §11).
- The two mystery glances and the no-takers posting are **residue-register**
  material: they ride inside driven beats rather than driving scenes of their
  own. No scene is built for a glance (that would inflate a microscopic beat
  into a plot event — the exact error the F-01/F-03 cadence forbids).
- The contempt beat and Hayes's dismissal share the **social-positioning**
  register: staging them as one movement (mess → old gym) lets the contempt
  arrive as *institutional behavior* and the dismissal as its *faculty voice*
  — two lenses of one pressure, not two scenes of the same lesson.
- The ending requires its own movement: lights-out dorm texture + lens beats
  beginning + EAR-forward pressure. Merging the ending into the contempt scene
  would collapse the chapter's two registers (institutional intake →
  social positioning) back into one, losing the gear change the skeleton's own
  structure implies (Stage 2 §11).

**Not 3** (ch1's count is ch1's count — not a quota; ch2's load is denser than
ch1's three-scene processing arc). **Not 5–6** (one scene per beat group would
make the glances/posting structural, violating the glance discipline).
**Not 6** (the old source's vignette count is retired architecture — Stage 1
§15/§17). **Not the TNE 3–5 rule** (no external numeric formula is imposed;
the planner's own cadence governs — Stage 2 §11).
