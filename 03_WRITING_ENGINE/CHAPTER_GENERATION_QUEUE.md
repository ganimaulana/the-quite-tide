## Active chapter contract

Every chapter must have:

1. A concrete objective.
2. Resistance or uncertainty.
3. At least one meaningful action or decision.
4. A changed state at the ending.
5. A reason the next chapter must happen.

A chapter may be quiet. It may not be inert.

## Structural anti-repeat rule

Do not repeat the same investigation engine in consecutive chapters. Rotate methods among:

**field action → interpersonal conflict → practical training → institutional pressure → archive discovery → experiment → consequence → public test.**

Records/Archive scenes must create an action, risk, changed hypothesis, or relationship consequence.
