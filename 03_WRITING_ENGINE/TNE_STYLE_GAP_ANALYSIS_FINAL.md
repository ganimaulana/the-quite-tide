## 1. Executive Summary

**Scope.** Read-only comparison of 87 discrete prose-style principles extracted from the external reference *STYLE-BIBLE-THE-NOVELS-EXTRA.md* (The Novel's Extra, Jee Gab Song; fan translation, 481 chapters) against THE QUIET TIDE's locked authorities, plus 3 Quiet Tide-side supplementary items. No file created or modified. No canon changed. No lock edited.

**Headline result.** 90 items classified: **15 ADOPT / 46 ADAPT / 4 REJECT / 5 DEFER / 20 ALREADY CANON.**

**Central finding.** TNE's *mechanisms* transfer well; TNE's *surface identity* must not be cloned. The highest-value imports are structural (scene cuts, paragraph discipline, no head-hopping, tactical thought, functional description, emotional displacement, documentation-first exposition) — all compatible with Quiet Tide's existing editorial system. The four rejections are all cases where a TNE absolute would break locked Quiet Tide architecture: first-person monopoly (breaks the institutional/documentary POV), terminal-hook absolutism (breaks the 12-ending-type rotation), blanket deletion of ordinary life (guts SOP §15), and the superpowered combat micro-sequence (violates Arthur's locked capability boundaries).
