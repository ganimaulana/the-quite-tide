## PART I — DIVISION DECISION (framing)

**Locked authority now:** LOCK 1 **L-17** — Division **INTELLIGENCE** · Curriculum **INTELLIGENCE OPERATIONS** · Assignment **RECORDS & ARCHIVES**.

**Current author direction (this task):** Arthur must **not** begin in Intelligence; he should initially belong to a **legitimate but low-prestige support / utility Division** that:

- is institutionally legitimate; has real cadets; performs useful military functions;
- is not a "garbage division," not a joke, not secretly special, not existing solely for Arthur;
- gives practical exposure useful later; keeps him socially low-status;
- does **not** fix his eventual military career; preserves the shared cohort/class structure;
- allows **Intelligence to notice him later** through demonstrated competence.

**Status:** the direction is **not locked** and **not present in any generation file**. Adopting it requires an explicit author lock decision and lock reopening (Part IV / `LOCK_REOPENING_MINIMUM_SET.md`).
