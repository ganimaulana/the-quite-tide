## 6. PROSE PROBLEM SUMMARY (detail in companion files)

The generator produced TNE-surface-overfit prose. Root causes, diagnosed in `PROSE_NATURALNESS_ROOT_CAUSE_AUDIT.md`:

1. **Mechanism-supply imbalance.** The TNE Gap Analysis imports 61 mechanisms (15 ADOPT + 46 ADAPT) as *prose devices*; the engine's modules read as prescriptive checklists, so the generator maximizes mechanism density (fragments, one-line paragraphs, hard cuts, clipped thought, attribution starvation) in scenes that do not need them.
2. **Numeric/threshold residue.** The engine repeatedly cites TNE's **8.7-word average** (even while disclaiming it) and adds an anti-drift rule ("non-tension scenes avg <10 words") — these function as de-facto short-sentence targets.
3. **Compression-first ordering.** "Fast locally" + Module 16 cut rules + concision guidance produce slogan-like compression ("Informed, not consulted. That was the whole conversation." / "The work was the work. It fit.").
4. **Metaphor substitution.** With description "rationed," the generator reaches for a striking comparison ("the way a punch clock takes its picture") — a synthetic metaphor rather than a functional detail.
5. **Dialogue economy over-applied.** The Module 09 compressed-exchange example trains clipped, data-entry dialogue ("Family keepsake... Appraised 1987 — the record's attached.").
6. **The engine's own calibration examples keep reappearing.** The engine names the exact failures (Module 01 §16) — yet the generator reproduces them, indicating the naturalness gate is treated as post-hoc QA rather than as the composition-time priority.

**Diagnostic corpus (10–15 examples) with problem type / cause / replacement rule:** in `PROSE_NATURALNESS_ROOT_CAUSE_AUDIT.md` §4.

## 8. DESCRIPTION RULE (AUTHOR DIRECTION — recorded)

Quiet Tide description audits against this priority: **1) what Arthur notices → 2) why he notices it → 3) what it tells him → 4) what changes because of it.** Description must not exist to sound atmospheric. (Full audit: `PROSE_NATURALNESS_ROOT_CAUSE_AUDIT.md` §5.)

## 9. DIALOGUE RULE (AUTHOR DIRECTION — recorded)

Dialogue must sound like people speaking — not database entries, exposition containers, institutional summaries, philosophy statements, artificial "cool" lines, or compressed TNE exchanges. For each exchange test: (1) would a real person say this? (2) is the speaker responding to what was said? (3) is it appropriate to the relationship? (4) is the information delivered naturally? (5) could the meaning be expressed more naturally? (6) does the speaker have a reason to speak? Military/institutional characters may be formal — **formal does not mean unnatural.** (Full audit: `DIALOGUE_NATURALNESS_ROOT_CAUSE_AUDIT.md`.)
